## Overview

Akal Dreek is a duergar adventurer and Bonebreaker closely tied to [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] and often paired with [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/del|Del]]. Chaotic and openly evil, he nonetheless has a strong sense of duergar justice. His readiness to kill has caused tension with his companions.

## Player

Deri.

## Class or role

Duergar adventurer and associate.

## Retainers or companions

- Often paired with [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/del|Del]].
- An unnamed wolf, killed during the jackalwere battle north of Ikiria in [[sessions/session-070|Session 70]].

## Important campaign events

- Present from the Blood Moon arc onward.
- Kills a lone rider in [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], provoking disgust from [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]].
- Still active in [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]].
- In [[sessions/session-069|Session 69 - A Cruel Bargain]], Akal sets fire to two carriages while the party is still debating ninja orcs' demand to exchange Lirina for a farming couple's daughter. His action triggers combat. The hostage-taker later kills the girl before Rose can reach him, and two attackers escape.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales]], Akal enlarges himself during a jackalwere encounter before the party enters the forest north of Ikiria. Arden enlarges Akal's wolf with Growth of Animal, but the wolf is killed in the battle.

## Relationships

## Current status

Active. His wolf is dead after the jackalwere battle north of Ikiria.
