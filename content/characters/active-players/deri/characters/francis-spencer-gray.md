## Overview

Francis "Frank" Spencer Gray is a thief who was raised on the streets after a noble took his mother away from him; his mother had worked in a brothel. His ambitions become increasingly social and political: he wants wealth, status, and influence among the elite, ideally by marrying into nobility and becoming the person powerful people turn to for quiet work. Many of his plans involve informants, deception, and leverage, though he also undercuts Agatha's secrecy by having Merrick spread word of the Bonebreakers' connection to her.

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]], Frank joins the party, then questions James the coffee merchant after the wererat attack at the Steamy Smithy, learning useful information about [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/index|Ikiria]] and the dangerous road east.
- In [[sessions/session-014|Session 14]], Frank learns sailing from the crew during the voyage to Fos Imeras and convinces Arden that Belenus's sign may point to a temple of Belenus.
- In [[sessions/session-016|Session 16]], Frank calls out that the temple is cleared, helps fight the blue figures rising from the lake, and—while charmed by the [[npcs/fos-imeras/nixies|Nixies]]—descends into their underwater city, sees its treasure, and tries to persuade the rest of the party to follow.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Frank cultivates the rescued nobles and [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], clarifying his ambition to gain status and influence among the elite; receives a potion of gaseous form; helps Arden distribute 15 gold pieces to the poorest people at Ikiria's docks in Belenus's name; begins seeking servants of noble households as informants; and repeats Arden's praise of Belenus when the princess is healed.
- In [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]], Frank scouts the Cathedral of the Crimson Flame's walls, repositions after the first attempt is compromised, identifies the bell plaza and [[npcs/mercy/sergeant-grimlock|Sergeant Grimlock]], then spikes a plaza door and takes an elevated firing position, firing on the counterattack.
- In [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], Frank frees two chained captives from the cathedral with his thieves' tools, though he cannot free a third, and helps bring the prisoners out during the retreat.
- In [[sessions/session-021|Session 21]], Frank poisons the kitchen stew and helps use stolen uniforms and the “Soup for the Abbott” ruse to infiltrate the cathedral; opens doors for mounted charges, attempts to flank the Sons of Bahal, tries to douse them with holy water, and rides [[characters/inactive-players/marco/characters/ehecat|Ehecat]]'s horse to safety when the party retreats; barricades the bishop's room with Ehecat, uncovering a hidden holy symbol and writings from the original bishop; and urges Captain Fardr to praise Belenus for the rescue, helping establish a possible Belenite allegiance if the undead are purged.
- In [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], Frank pulls the swords from the nearby statues to open the hidden descent beneath the altar and listens at the opening, hearing ghoulish chanting below; uses his potion of gaseous form to reach and grapple the airborne Crimson Bishop, preventing him from casting while the party brings him down, then gives Cedric a healing potion after the Bishop badly injures him; and is later nearly killed by rot grubs while scouting ahead before firing an arrow into library webs that releases grave spiders, which kill him before he is resurrected with Barny in Barrow Spa the following morning.
- In [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]], Frank notices Barny trying to return alone to the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|Barrow]], raises the alarm, and helps the party follow him back inside; offers an undead treant release by setting it alight; and recovers and questions the talking skull of Sir Gallant.
- In [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]], Frank hears movement during the orc raid on the Mercy, raises the alarm, wounds one attacker, nets five, burns the trapped orcs, and tackles another fleeing raider before stabbing it, although four priests are still taken hostage; he later helps interrogate the surviving captive by suspending him over the Abyss.
- In [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], Frank scouts the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|shrine]] while invisible, maps the enemy positions, lays tripwires that catch the changing guards, and recovers two [[items/party-inventory/gnomish-power-cores|gnomish power cores]], six automaton arms, and two automaton eyes; steals Krazak's war horn, ignites the prepared grease traps during the retreat, then scars [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh One-Tusk]] and lets him leave alive with a warning.
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], Frank rents a lower-city warehouse and establishes the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|Temple of Merciful Life]] as a public shrine with a hidden hub for spies, thieves, and informants, including a private shrine to Belenus; hires [[characters/active-players/deri/retainers-followers/merrick|Merrick]] to spread an embellished ballad about the Bonebreakers while concealing who commissioned it, drawing unwanted scrutiny from Commander Smith; questions guards and a weapon scavenger about Ikiria's sentient-weapon crisis, asks Helvian about Professor Longtooth and Arkeus Whizkeyrd, buys a potion of lesser restoration, and pickpockets 100 gold pieces from a passing noble; then crosses the hidden entrance to Arkeus's tower and opens the door onto its astral void.
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], a pale dagger tempts Frank with visions of ruling Ikiria and the world; he helps destroy the last crystal golem, receives a ring of protection, and calls in his duergar associates [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/akal-dreek|Akal]] and Del to join the Bonebreakers.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], Frank puts out a bounty for information about the trained giant hawks taking children, uncovering rumours that secluded elves may be involved while noting that the lead conflicts with other evidence.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], Frank clears the pit behind the Mercy, restoring a usable route into the depths, then discovers a polished black-basalt trapdoor leading to a colossal hall of ancient imperial arches; climbs through an inverted chamber to recover another map, lowers a rope for the party, and translates the pre-imperial creation myth in the Foundation Chamber.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], Frank adopts his noble persona, Francis Spencer Gray, to approach Prince Arthur's procession. When he warns that Princess Agatha is in danger and mentions the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]], the escort orders the Bonebreakers killed; Frank argues that the reaction itself suggests incriminating evidence.
- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], Frank reassures the frightened squire Pell with 100 gold pieces and helps send a warning letter to Commander Smith; takes a secretary's ledger proving meetings with the Grey Syndicate; extracts an oath from Akal to deliver the evidence to Princess Agatha if the party disappears; and develops an unexpected rapport with Prince Arthur at Barrow Spa.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], Frank gives 100 gold pieces to a displaced family in the Plats and pays for an informant at Madame Gorvetti's, learning that [[npcs/ikiria/nixx-pocketbrain|Nixx Pocketbrain]] resents Princess Agatha's influence.
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], Frank reads the imperial runes on the Arch VI gatehouse and works out enough of its opening mechanism for the party to memorise it.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], Frank finds an imperial schoolroom in Arch V containing civic lessons, children's objects, the name Cyril, and a disturbing drawing of the sun; he also reads the [[items/bloated-one-work-ledger|Bloated One work ledger]], learning that the district has undergone inspections for more than 2,000 years and that a recent warm-air anomaly behind the school was not to be reported to the Tribune.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], Frank is badly injured by zombies in Arch V, tries unsuccessfully to lure the orcs above the Abyss with cooking, and questions Kronk after the Mercy counterattack. Kronk reveals that the orcs came from Westmarsh, were following orders, and that the earlier priest kidnapping was also carried out for the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]. Frank later orders barbed, crescent-headed, and grappling-hook crossbow bolts by diagram in Barrow Spa.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], Frank commissions major defences for [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], including a moat and 30-foot walls, and later plays along with the zombie market in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]].
- In [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]], Frank scouts a domed chamber in the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]] using Luthien's recovered cloak and boots, examines a soul-bound chest secured by three locks, and helps work on it while the party fights its skeletal guards and a disenchanter, heavily wounding one. The chest remains unresolved.
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], Frank scouts the command quarter, finds its centurion's insignia, backstabs a minotaur, and helps recover 4,000 electrum pieces; takes corroded lockpicks from a prison cell, reads a warning about the vault and an untrustworthy halfling, and identifies a pressure plate beyond the portcullis that likely requires several people to trigger; then promises to lay [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] to rest, prompting Varro to leave the imperial armoury unlocked, where Frank opens its chest and recovers 800 gold pieces and a document before the party triggers the poison-gas trap.
- In [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]], Frank infiltrates the hobgoblin siege engines with Nila, badly wounds an ogre with a backstab and Kargaz with a crossbow shot, and helps eliminate the remaining archers. After the victory, he contributes 1,000 gold pieces to Westfort's rebuilding and asks Arden to lead burial rites for the fallen defenders.
- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], Frank gives [[npcs/ikiria/gilly|Gilly]] enough money to rent an upper-city apartment and dress for high society, asking her to gather information about eligible widows he might pursue. Her report that Agatha remains missing and Commander Smith has also disappeared helps prompt the party's investigation.

## Relationships

- Closely tied to Akal and Merrick.
- Often partners with Arden on public schemes, including the [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenite]] project, and with stealth-oriented party members on reconnaissance.
