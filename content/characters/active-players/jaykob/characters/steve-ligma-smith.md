## Overview

Steve "Ligma" Smith is a barbarian who joins the party in the Westmarsh arc. He is chaotic, abrasive, and openly disrespectful toward authority, but he also brings a wild, funny energy to the group and throws himself enthusiastically into the frontier's chaos. He survived [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s rotting disease after Ulrich cured him at the Mercy.

## Player

Jaykob.

## Class or role

Barbarian.

## Retainers or companions

- An unnamed bugbear follower first appears after Steve charms a bugbear in [[sessions/session-037|Session 37]] and later fights beside him in [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]].
- A charmed ogre ally is with Steve by [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]] and hurls him into the Tobold impostor during the night attack.
- Steve charms one of the sprites in [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]]. The sprite later spreads oil into the Ikirian grave pit during [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]]. Its name and current status: Needs review.

## Important campaign events

- Joins the Westmarsh strike party in [[sessions/session-034|Session 34]], antagonises Commander Valerius, and tortures captured gnolls for information, skinning one alive.
- In [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], Steve hauls Cyder out of the burning Cracked Shield, drags Sarla back for questioning, and puts her down when she bolts so Cyder can put her to sleep. He antagonises Valerius again, shows him one of the stolen swords, and kills one of the orc mercenaries captured at the bridge, taking its ears.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], Steve searches the orc mercenaries and finds four swords, releases Muzgash after questioning him, wears flayed orc skin as a disguise while watching Jeremy meet Ash in Orcish at the Spider Market, and buys the sword he feels compelled to own.
- In [[sessions/session-037|Session 37]], Steve and Akal kill two kobold miners after Selvar puts them to sleep and spare a third for questioning; when the mine yields little information, Steve helps move the carts deeper into the workings, where more kobolds work under two hobgoblin overseers. He charms one of four bugbears and uses the charmed bugbear to escape the cave, learning that a nearby base is stockpiling weapons for an imminent war. The party withdraws to the Spider Market, survives a gnoll attack, and turns toward the fortress whose weapons and armour Rose later scouts.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], Steve charges the hobgoblin officers with the party and helps kill three of them—including two alongside his charmed bugbear. The hidden passage then reveals hundreds of orcs, goblins, hobgoblins, and human mercenaries with siege weapons; Steve helps the party escape behind burning dragon grease and return to warn [[npcs/west-fort/commander-valerius|Commander Valerius]] that Westfort is about to be besieged.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], Mara Ironhand loans Steve a charm-deflecting shield on the condition that he helps defend Westfort. Under Invisibility and Flight, he sabotages the invasion army's siege engines, then scouts the emptied fort and opens the doors to the corridor where the children had been held; bugbears briefly attack him before recognising him from the mine.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], Steve is eager to accept Ash's offer from the Grey Syndicate, helps kill the orcs guarding the hidden complex, spares Jeremy, and asks the bugbears how to open the locked doors leading toward the children; their answer points the party to the key and the rescued children. The children are returned to Westfort, where Valerius takes custody and the Bonebreakers receive a 24,000-gold reward.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], Steve presses one of the Nexus eagle's gemstones, antagonises Cornelius Vane, clears a ceiling collapse while trying to free him, reopens a door that seals itself, fails to pull Valedictus's iron-bound treatise free, then breaks its chains, tests the Foundation Chamber skull with a thrown rock, and recovers 200 pre-imperial gold pieces and a black stone ritual knife.
- In [[sessions/session-043|Session 43]], Steve rides alone to Ikiria to verify the plague rumours, confirms that the sickness is real and worsening, avoids bandits on the road, and returns to the Mercy. He then descends with the party into the Necropolis and helps retrace their route toward the corridor marked by claw scars, setting up the discovery of the chimera's hoard.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], Steve accompanies the Bonebreakers through the chimera-hoard expedition and the roadside confrontation with Prince Arthur's retinue, where the party uncovers Arthur's ties to the Grey Syndicate and the threat to Princess Agatha.
- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], after two caravans flee and rumours spread that the Bonebreakers murdered [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]'s guards, Steve stabilises [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/tomas-wren|Tomas Wren]], one of the few witnesses to Arthur's confession. The Bonebreakers keep Arthur alive, send Pell to [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]], and secure [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]]'s ledger of [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] meetings. Steve commissions 1,000-gold armour in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/index|Barrow Spa]]; after the Syndicate contact through Gorm is cancelled and an eight-ogre ambush on the road, he reaches the [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|Spider Market]] with the party, where halflings approach the camp.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], a charmed ogre hurls Steve into the suspected Tobold impostor during the night attack. Ulrich's new holy symbol then identifies Steve as extremely evil. The party trades the Foundation skull for masks, enters the Spider Market, hears of a western Necropolis entrance, and reaches a 20,000-gold arrangement with Ash, who claims to be the Grey Khan.
- In [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]], Steve charms a sprite, completes his orc disguise by collecting new mail, and is sharply warned by Commander Valerius after killing prisoners in Westfort. The wider operation captures 20,000 gold and intelligence that the Grey Syndicate is spread across the region and supporting an invasion; the Bonebreakers carry their evidence through Westfort and Mercy to Ikiria, where Arthur is arrested.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], Steve charms a civilian inquisitor so the party can inspect his list, paddles himself across the raised Magistrate's Span in a box, accidentally hits Barny in the head when throwing the box back, cuts down the first zombie to emerge from the corpse pit, and strikes a ghoul before Frank finishes it. These incidents occur as the party investigates missing bodies in the quarantined Plats, discovers the burned Belenus temple, and faces the first signs of the undead outbreak.
- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], Steve is overcome by fear at the sight of the mummies, drinks his long-saved Potion of Flying, and flees over the sea while his sprite spreads oil into the grave pit. The wider fight escalates through skeletons, wights, mummies, and wraiths, and [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] is killed during the battle.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], Steve flies back into the fight after recovering from fear and obliterates one of the wights, helps destroy the remaining undead, and takes Maren Sollow's notebook. He draws the mob's attention, confirms that the Public Well is poisoned by drinking from it and curing himself, tests a snake-oil cure and discovers it is only alcohol, charms a guard without securing an exit from quarantine, hands Jim Selvar's letter for Captain Silvertread, and pushes a beggar into the well.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], Steve nearly pockets a child's toy horse, tries to kick a zombie child and hurts himself, crashes into the outer dome while attempting to shield-surf down, breaks the Arch V looping-street magic with a symmetrical floor arrow, helps disguise the party as foremen and confront the Bloated One's works, discovers the maintenance route, lands the final blow on the Bloated One, and is badly affected by the creature's necrotic death burst.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], Steve finds the Bloated One's Senior Administrator sash, pries the Nexus eagle's ruby eye-buttons out for loot, insults the orcs through the Abyss trapdoor, kills the other orc captives—including the squad leader and Kronk—during Frank's questioning, and is cured of the rotting disease by Ulrich after the party rests at the Mercy.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], during the party's recovery and preparations after Bran's death, Steve finishes reading the [[items/party-inventory/manual-of-strength|Manual of Strength]] and gains additional strength. He also plays along with a zombie merchant in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]], paying two gold pieces and receiving gems worth 380 gold pieces.
- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], Steve's sword proves ineffective against the pool serpents; he is badly bitten but resists their poison, fails to pull Fred free, and helps kill the creatures. He then smashes open the counting-house chests, kills one of the snake-men, and uses the chaotic tongue to tell the dark druids that the party does not want a fight.
- In [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]], he buys a [[items/party-inventory/massive-crossbow|massive crossbow]] that hits hard but is slow to use. During the journey toward Westfort, nine gryphons kill the party's horses, leaving the Bonebreakers to capture two of the creatures as possible mounts.
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], Steve tries and fails to lift the immovable soul-bound chest, forces open the barracks doors, and uses his strength to help kill both minotaurs after Micaelis turns the imperial eagle toward them; he also tests the prison portcullis without triggering its pressure plate, opens a hidden trapdoor by pulling a wall sconce, and asks Captain Varro for access to the imperial armoury.
- Steve takes the [[items/glowing-skull|glowing skull]] from Arch VII's Foundation Chamber in [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]] and trades it for masks at the Broken Crown in [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]]. He later gives the skull to a lizardfolk in the Sunken Market; in [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], the party strongly suspects it is one of Valedictus's phylacteries.
- In [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]], he uses his sword to cast Charm Person on [[npcs/kingsport/elspeth-crane|Mistress Elspeth Crane]] after she catches Selvar at a royal seal, then opens and reseals the archive cabinet. He blames [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] for the party's jewellery theft, tracks and captures him, and secures his arrest with Elspeth's support and Micaelis's planted evidence.

## Relationships

- Appears with the later Westmarsh roster and fellow late additions such as Rose and Cyder.
- [[npcs/kingsport/elspeth-crane|Mistress Elspeth Crane]] supports his accusation against Corvin under the Charm Person spell he casts through his sword, unaware that Steve and the party committed the theft.

## Current status

Active. No longer infected by the Bloated One's rotting disease, stronger after completing the Manual of Strength, and equipped with a massive crossbow.
