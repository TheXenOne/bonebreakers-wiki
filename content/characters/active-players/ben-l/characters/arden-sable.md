## Overview

Arden Sable is a founding Bonebreaker, a cleric of Belenus, and Bishop of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]. He is effectively the leader of the [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenites]] and the central figure in the party's public religious project.

## Player

Ben L.

## Class or role

Cleric of Belenus and Bishop of the Mercy.

## Retainers or companions

- [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] (dead)
- [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Sir Cedric Dawnmere]]
- [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess Tattercloak]]

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-001|Session 1]], Arden joins the expedition because of his hatred of undead, turns back a skeleton ambush, and taps a marked marble slab, drawing the carcass crawler into the party's first Barrow delve.
- In [[sessions/session-002|Session 2]], Arden helps free Alfwine, turns the skeletons that injure him and kill Ike, and triggers Yellow Mould while trying to remove a golden tapestry. He survives the spores, then reaches into the magical white flames to retrieve three blue gems and helps destroy the zombies during the retreat.
- In [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]], Arden and Barny read the fallen party members' wills and choose the cheaper Last Chance to restore Virien. Arden hires Bran "The Wolf", posts a notice offering seven gold pieces to adventurers who answer the Bonebreakers' alphorn call, and later lures rats away from the Barrow's tapestry room with rations so the party can continue exploring.
- In [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]], Arden argues that the Guildhall's 600-gold reward is too low but accepts the King's Road investigation. At the shrine, he pockets the brass automaton hands, tests the ornate sword in the foundry, and questions the gloop pilgrims, learning that travelers are being fed to the great serpent.
- In [[sessions/session-005|Session 5]], Arden bluffs gloop guards while the party rescues Eric, reactivates the shrine foundry with a brass lightning bolt, helps recover the ornate sword from the liquefied iron pool, and helps scatter the pilgrims by pretending to be a ghost. The sword is later identified as the Sword of Mulvis.
- In [[sessions/session-006|Session 6]], he stages an escape from the shrine by igniting the grease trail, spends his last spell saving Barny, briefly activates Auto-Mulvis before the final assault on Sootmurk, and extends the Goldmark deception to persuade a surviving gloop not to kidnap travelers.
- In [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]], Arden cures Neem after the thief is paralysed, discovers a hidden corridor behind King Bomfluke's casket, retrieves the crown after Gorfthistle's death, and survives the Auto-Mulvis explosion that kills Dreg.
- In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], he begins giving one gold piece a day to Barrow Spa's neediest residents in Belenus's name.
- In [[sessions/session-009|Session 9]], Bran and Arden lead the way into the silver-flute statue chamber beneath Tarantella Manor. While Ulrich distracts the giant spider, Arden finds a button on the south-west statue; after the spider and centipedes are defeated, the party retreats with the statues when several members are incapacitated.
- In [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]], Arden pockets silver, gold, and platinum ingots from the forge and tests a ruby ring by putting his finger in a lantern, discovering that it protects him from flame.
- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], Arden wears a purple glove, opens the checkered trap tome, approaches Morlan's mirror with a hand mirror, questions the trapped wizard, and smashes the mirror to free him.
- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], Arden trades Morlan a cursed lead band for his amulet, offers cursed rings to the Rat Queen, fights and kills her, then triggers Yellow Mould while trying to retrieve a matching purple robe before donning it and helping bottle the magical powders.
- In [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]], Arden heals a wounded customer after the party stops a wererat attack, publicly crediting Belenus for the recovery.
- During the voyage to Fos Imeras in [[sessions/session-014|Session 14]], Arden gives sailors gold and blessings in Belenus's name and receives a divine sign directing him toward a temple.
- In [[sessions/session-015|Session 15]], sustained visions call Arden to build a temple and provide healing on a much larger scale.
- In [[sessions/session-016|Session 16]], Arden blesses the party before the fight with Sebastaeia, drinks from the lake, and waves to the figures rising from it. Their song charms him; he removes his armour and wades toward them before the party escapes aboard the Eleftheria.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], he formalises his ambition to build a temple, find Mercy's Blade, and make Belenus a major force in East Agria. He and Frank distribute 15 gold pieces among the poorest people at Ikiria's docks, and Arden heals Princess Agatha under Belenus's blessing.
- In [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]], Arden casts Bless before the cathedral assault, knocks two trainees unconscious for questioning, and is slain by a Son of Bahal while proclaiming Belenus's fury. With his dying breath, he gives Bran a note and his holy symbol.
- In [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], Bran carries Arden's body back to Barrow Spa and pays for his resurrection with Arden's own money. Arden returns to life weak and spends two weeks recovering while the cathedral operation continues.
- After the cathedral is reclaimed, Arden is named Bishop of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] in [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], trains priests into clerics, and helps reshape the cathedral into a temple of Belenus and Coranth.
- In [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]], Arden frees Virien from paralysis, speaks with the undead treant, tests the opalescent healing water, and scrubs Quintius's glyph circle, purging the Barrow's active necromancy.
- During the Mercy hostage crisis in [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]], Arden blesses the defenders, helps stall Gruumsh with a partial ransom, rides into the marsh to exchange a priest, and tries to frighten the warlord with a false curse.
- In [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], Arden approaches the shrine with Luthien, Bran, and Cronk as a supposed tribute, then helps fight through the prison and embalming rooms, dispatching the messenger and sleeping guards. He rejects Gruumsh's offer to trade Gronk for treasure.
- During the sentient-weapons crisis in [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], Arden muffles his newly talking mace, persuades a frightened citizen to surrender a sentient weapon and visit the Temple of Merciful Life, helps subdue an attacking automaton, and takes a fine rug that he later unrolls to cross an astral void.
- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], Arden hands Barny a trembling inkwell whose opening releases a stunning wail, helps carry the shocked glass mallet, and pulls a spell-filled cage aside with a grappling hook, receiving a clairvoyant glimpse of the real Arkeus suspended in astral light.
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], Arden blesses the party during the crystal-golem fight, helps collect the golems' gems, claims a ring of protection, and uses the rug to escape the tower. He then rests and is temporarily replaced by Tess Tattercloak.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], Arden is serving as one of the Mercy's healers when the party returns to Ikiria with rescued villagers.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], poisonings at the Mercy put Arden's leadership under scrutiny as the party is called back to investigate.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], Arden sends an urgent plea reporting three dead priests and more failing, requesting the party's aid. Barny's crystal ball catches Arden's uncertainty and his reliance on Cedric.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], Arden's decision to interrogate villagers worsens the crisis. He addresses the angry crowd and apologises for his failures, but the anger only grows.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], Arden examines the partial plans of the Necropolis's Arch VII wing, identifying the mason's camp, unfinished hall, inverted room, Cartographer's Folly, and an exit. While guiding Cornelius Vane back toward the main chamber, he is caught in a ceiling collapse and helps clear the route.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], Arden blesses the party, claims a pre-imperial lion-faced shield, adopts his bishop's authority, and knocks Prince Arthur unconscious with non-lethal force.
- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], Arden smooths things over with Captain Fardr by presenting Arthur alive and contained.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], Arden raises the alarm when a halfling infiltrator approaches, bargains 2,000 gold pieces for the captive's information, confronts the watching Grey Syndicate orc, and buys an Amulet of Proof against Scrying.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], Arden puts on his bishop's hat, denounces the burning of the Temple of Merciful Life, and helps purge the undead outbreak, including a weakened wight.
- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], Arden continues turning the dead, is driven back by fear when he sees the mummies, cures Selvar's mummy rot, and helps finish the remaining mummy.
- After the corpse-pit battle in [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], Arden determines that fresh necrotic magic was cast in the graves, searches the abandoned Reefer's Row and Salting House, heals Selvar, comforts a guilt-ridden guard, and tries to calm the inquisitor-led mob by throwing 50 gold pieces and casting Silence on its instigator.
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], Arden opens the Mercy's trapdoor, uses turning, oil, and fire to help clear undead and ochre jellies, desperately heals Barny, finishes the Barny-faced undead after Ulrich turns it, and knocks an evil statue from a shrine, triggering the mechanism that reveals the Arch V map.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], Arden takes the hidden iron key, reverses and redirects the district's chalk arrows to test the looping routes, turns the undead in a side room, marks the party's path with Agria's symbol, casts Silence on the Bloated One, and tries unsuccessfully to blind it with Light.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], he destroys zombies with turning magic, tests the Nexus eagle's ruby eye-buttons again, helps hold orcs during the Mercy counterattack, offers rations to the unusually intelligent bear, identifies the hidden Arch V vial as holy water, and provides diagrams so Frank can order specialised bolts.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], Arden's Hold Person fails against the twelve insectoid humanoids, and he escapes with Micaelis while Bran covers their retreat with the bodies of the fallen. Arden later leads Bran's wake at the Mercy, praying for and gaining Bran's Heroic Aura as a spell in his honour.
- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], Arden destroys a zombie merchant with turning instead of paying, provoking the serpents beneath the market; he wades in with his mace, heals [[characters/active-players/jennifer/characters/quak-ii|Quak II!]] after the rescue, uses Bran's Heroic Aura and Light against the snake-men, and tries to preach kindness to druids who worship the Deep One.
- In [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]], Arden uses Bran's Heroic Aura to protect the party's final horse during a gryphon ambush, helps blind two attackers, heals Barny after a near-fatal blow, and gives his own dead horse a proper burial.
- In [[sessions/session-057|Session 57]], Arden casts Speak with Animals to stop thirteen starving dogs from attacking and persuades them to follow the party with a promise of food.
- In [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]], Arden casts Bran's Heroic Aura on himself in a desperate attempt to reach Luthien's remains, then destroys six skeletal legionnaires by turning and blinds a disenchanter.
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], Arden protects himself from the Imperial Hill Temple's unnatural cold with fire resistance, questions the ruin's undead inhabitants until the party establishes it is a hidden entrance to the Necropolis, asks where the high priest is, and joins Ulrich in turning ten skeletal soldiers.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], Arden buys Wistor's document, leads the party through the gas-filled route beneath the ruin, opens the blocked stone slab with a crowbar, survives paralysis and then cures Selvar, and leads the exchange with [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]]. His questions uncover [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus's]] three phylacteries and [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]]; a rust monster destroys his magical mace completely.
- In [[sessions/session-061|Session 61]], Arden commissions [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/michelangelo|Michelangelo]] to make a stone memorial statue of Bran and a provisionally wooden statue of Luthien. During the Westfort relief operation, he blinds a giant crab and casts Silence on the invisible Micaelis so he can lay the oil lines and sabotage a catapult undetected.
- In [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]], Arden paralyses the two surviving siege ogres with Hold Person. After the battle, he distributes gold among Westfort's people in Belenus's name and leads burial rites for the fallen defenders.
- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], Arden visits the family of a girl Ulrich cured during Ikiria's plague and recruits her sister, [[npcs/ikiria/elizabeth|Elizabeth]], into the Belenite faith. He trains and employs her as a non-combatant acolyte responsible for healing local people and running the rebuilt Temple of Merciful Life. After reaching Harrow, he loudly announces himself as a cleric of Belenus and tells the Anchor & Chain that the party is seeking work.

## Relationships

- Public leader of the [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenites]].
- Closely linked with Bran, Cedric, and Tess. Bran's death becomes a major personal and religious memory for Arden at the Mercy.
- Arden is now arranging permanent memorials for Bran and Luthien through Michelangelo's statues.
- Often works alongside [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] on public-facing schemes.

## Current status

Active. Arden remains Bishop of the Mercy and continues with the party in later arcs while carrying Bran's sacrifice into his magic and public memory.
