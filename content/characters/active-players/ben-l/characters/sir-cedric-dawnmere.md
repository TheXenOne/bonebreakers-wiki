## Overview

Sir Cedric Dawnmere is a paladin of Belenus and the last surviving member of a minor house destroyed by undead. He joined the Bonebreakers during their assault on the corrupted cathedral, later stayed behind to help tend the reclaimed church, and became one of the party's most visible defenders of the [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenite]] cause. His later actions include rescuing civilians in Westfort, arranging [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess's]] resurrection, and keeping the party focused on the kidnapped children rather than the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]'s offer.

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-021|Session 21]], Cedric introduces himself as the last of House Dawnmere, initially mistakes Virien for a Crimson Knight, and joins the assault on the corrupted cathedral. He nearly turns a Son of Bahal, joins the mounted charge against four more, declares his allegiance to Belenus, breaks a window to help the party escape with [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]], and later kills a Son of Bahal during the assault on the altar.
- In [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], Cedric leads the descent beneath the altar and rejects the [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]]'s offer unless the undead and plague are cleansed. When the bishop takes flight, Cedric attempts to turn him with the recovered holy symbol, then takes over the grapple from Frank and helps hold the creature down until it is destroyed. He later takes charge of a corridor beyond the bishop's chamber, drops a torch into an apparently endless stair, and is webbed by the grave spiders that attack the party in the library.
- During the church raid in [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]], Cedric kills orcs while defending the priests, including one he and Bran bring down as the raiders retreat. Four priests are nevertheless taken hostage.
- During the rescue of the priests in [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], Cedric kills one of the orcs who arrives during the change of guard as the party clears the shrine.
- After the shrine is reclaimed, the Bonebreakers leave Cedric with the priests to tend the church in [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]].
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], Cedric's rescue of the street urchin Tess Tattercloak brings her into the Bonebreakers as Arden's temporary replacement while Arden rests.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], Cedric is among the healers at the temple in Ikiria when the party returns with prisoners rescued from the Halls of the Blood King.
- After Tess is killed by the opening volley of a failed ambush in [[sessions/session-034|Session 34]], Cedric arrives at Westfort shaken by her death and arranges and pays for her body to be taken to Barrow Spa for resurrection. Akal contributes 500 gold pieces out of guilt for suggesting that Tess be used as bait.
- In [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], Cedric gives [[npcs/west-fort/private-jordan|Private Jordan]] his cape and a gold piece, then helps him to bed. When the [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/cracked-shield|Cracked Shield]] burns, Cedric wakes [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/osric-tam|Osric]], [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/lianna|Lianna]], and the other trapped residents, throws them to safety through a window, and jumps with Osric when the innkeeper refuses to leave. He later saves [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/sarla|Sarla]] after [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/veteran-borak|Veteran Borak]] cuts her throat and tears back the hood of [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/vex|Vex]]'s contact during the bridge ambush.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], Cedric buys a gleaming shield at [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|the Spider Market]] and directs the party's questions toward the trained hawks, prompting the hostile hobgoblins' attack.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], Cedric charges the hobgoblin officers with Steve, helps Luthien kill two of them, and uses Lay on Hands to heal Barny after the fight. He then writes urgently to [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Bishop Arden]] after learning that priests at the Mercy are being poisoned and dying.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], Cedric prays and makes a donation at the [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/temple-of-saint-lucia-the-merciful|Temple of Saint Lucia the Merciful]], receiving the [[items/party-inventory/book-of-sublime-holiness|Book of Sublime Holiness]] to study.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], Cedric rejects [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]'s offer to investigate [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]], keeping the party focused on rescuing the children. He tries to force open Ash's escape route, then calms and feeds the children when they are found. At the Mercy, he pushes through the angry crowd to reach the cathedral and insists on seeing the crisis for himself.
