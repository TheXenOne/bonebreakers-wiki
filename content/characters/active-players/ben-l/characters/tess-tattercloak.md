## Overview

Tess Tattercloak is an illusionist and street-urchin recruited by Cedric during the Blood Moon arc. She is not cruel and does not seem eager to do outright evil, but she is opportunistic, happy to steal, and quick to follow whatever path looks most profitable.

## Player

Ben L.

## Class or role

Illusionist.

## Retainers or companions

- Closely tied to [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Sir Cedric Dawnmere]].

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], Tess joins the active party after [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Cedric]] rescues her from life as a street urchin, while [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] rests. She asks whether the missing nobles' families are wealthy, casts Chromatic Orb on a castle guard, questions him about his enslavement, searches the bodies, and tries to force open the biting gate with a pole.
- In [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]], Tess investigates a melted breach in the castle fence and loots a charred priest, finding holy water, a ring, and 50 gold pieces. She enters the trapped chamber, helps defeat the armoured figures dragging prisoners, finishes one with her dagger, loots the bodies, helps topple an armory statue, and pries open a toothed armoire before claiming vampire-scented gothic clothes.
- In [[sessions/session-030|Session 30]], Tess's new clothing gives the party a vampire-like scent that helps their disguise. She is badly injured by the manor's giant spiders and turns invisible to survive.
- In [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]], Tess helps harvest arcane lotus flowers, finds a letter suggesting [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/lord-demetrius|Lord Demetrius]] supports the [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/princess-of-blood|Princess of Blood]], and steals an obsidian dagger while the Princess is distracted. She helps kill [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/circander|Circander]] by dragging her back and staking her, finds a magical crossbow for [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]], unlocks the prisoners, and uses Glamour to imitate Demetrius when confronting a fungus-inhabited vampire.
- In [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]], Tess strips the crystals from the vampire feast's chandelier, throws her dagger at a kitchen servant, and is nearly killed before the party wins. She helps lead the freed prisoners to safety, eats a black lotus and regains a spell slot, then turns invisible to retreat from the carnivorous orchid. In the observatory, she studies the multilingual tome and pulls the lever that activates the planetary model; the mechanism nearly kills her before exposing the Blood King's hidden heart. During the collapse, she takes Mercury and the tome, then returns with [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] to gather the prisoners.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], Tess is disturbed by Akal's killing of a lone rider and drags the body into the bushes. At [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], she explains the Bonebreakers' mission to [[npcs/west-fort/sergeant-dara|Sergeant Dara]], uses invisibility to overhear rumours at [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/cracked-shield|the Cracked Shield]], and unsuccessfully tries to steal the party's map payment back.
- In [[sessions/session-034|Session 34]], Tess volunteers to act as bait with Alison, posing as a traveller beside the party's cart. Nine gnolls ambush them first, killing Tess in the opening volley; [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Sir Cedric]] arranges for her body to be taken to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/index|Barrow Spa]] for resurrection.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], the Bonebreakers recover Tess from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/index|Barrow Spa]] after her resurrection, and she rejoins the party.
- In [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]], she secures entry to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]] by using Glamour to impersonate Wendell Prye and persuading Brother Sedge that she is secretly working to help Arthur's defence.

## Relationships

- Recruited by [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Sir Cedric Dawnmere]], who believes she wants to do good.
- Sees Cedric and the party as a strong opportunity for protection and money.
- Later travels with Tobold and the Westmarsh strike group.

## Current status

Active. After her death in Session 34, Tess is resurrected at Barrow Spa and rejoins the party in Session 40.
