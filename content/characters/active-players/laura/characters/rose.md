## Overview

Rose is an acrobat and Bonebreaker whose value shows most clearly in scouting, infiltration, and careful positioning. She has taken the lead in reconnoitring the Invasion Fort, setting the chimera trap, and exploiting openings during the Westfort siege.

## Player

Laura.

## Class or role

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]], Rose is with the Bonebreakers as they free prisoners from the Blood King's basement and burn the fungus-infected body resembling Lord Demetrius.
- In [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]], Rose vaults over and kills one of the kitchen servants while the party frees the caged prisoners. She later helps Tobold bring down the carnivorous plant with her crossbow and joins the escape party in holding off five guards.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], Rose joins the western expedition after Tobold is carried off by a giant hawk. The party's research links the abduction to trained hawks and Westmarsh, and they establish Westfort as a base for the investigation.
- In [[sessions/session-034|Session 34]], Rose joins the first Westmarsh push, survives the gnoll ambush that kills Tess, and returns to Westfort while the party questions the surviving gnolls about Grazka and the hawks.
- In [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], Rose is with the party during the Cracked Shield fire and rescue, the exposure of Vex's sabotage cell, and the capture of the hooded handler who points them toward the Spider Market.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], Rose takes the poisoned daggers hidden in the captured handler's shoes.
- In [[sessions/session-037|Session 37]], Barny makes Rose invisible and able to fly so she can scout the Invasion Fort. She slips through a high skylight and confirms that the fort holds large stockpiles of weapons and armour, with senior commanders studying maps.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], Rose finds the forge, invasion maps, and a hawk feather linking the fort to Tobold's abduction. She throws oil into the furnace, killing three hobgoblins in the explosion, steals the maps and documents, and opens the gate from inside for the party's assault.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], Rose trades information about the party's actions to [[npcs/west-fort/gorm-the-fence|Gorm the Fence]] for a [[items/party-inventory/necklace-of-strangulation|necklace of strangulation]]. She then helps hold Westfort against the approaching army and hawk-borne goblins before the party counterstrikes the Invasion Fort.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], Rose helps cut down the orcs guarding Ash's hidden complex and escorts the rescued children through the hidden exit back to Westfort.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], Rose presses one of the imperial eagle's gemstones in the Nexus, helping reveal the eight arches and release nine skeletons from Arch I before Ulrich destroys them. She remains with the party as it explores [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vii|Arch VII]], finds [[npcs/necropolis/cornelius-vane|Cornelius Vane]] and [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus's]] treatise, and recovers the Foundation Chamber skull.
- In [[sessions/session-042|Sessions 42–43]], Rose brings down a formidable enemy with a single sudden strike during the deeper [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vii|Arch VII]] delve. The foe's identity and the exact encounter remain unclear. Needs review.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], Rose climbs sixty feet up a shaft in the Necropolis and peers through a sealed grille into a domed chamber above. Later, under Silence and Invisibility, she places a poisoned pig, oil, and a lantern beneath the feeding shaft to lure the ancient chimera to its death. She skins the dragon head for possible armour, claims a `+3` imperial spear, and pins a fleeing squire through the leg with it.
- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], Rose accompanies [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|the prince's secretary]] to meet [[npcs/west-fort/gorm-the-fence|Gorm]] in another attempt to reach the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]], but the contact produces no information. She remains with the party as it keeps [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] alive, preserves evidence of his conspiracy, and abandons the plan after the meeting is cancelled.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], Rose accompanies the party at the Spider Market as it learns [[characters/active-players/david/characters/tobold-took|Tobold]] is dead, negotiates a 20,000-gold exchange for the prince's secretary, and hears [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]] claim to be [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|the Grey Khan]].
- In [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]], Rose accompanies the failed attempt to capture [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]] in the Spider Market. The party instead captures a recruiter, seizes 20,000 gold pieces, warns Westfort about the renewed invasion, and gets [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]] to testify against [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]]. Rose also collects her dragon-scale armour.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], Rose shoots a mummy and then helps [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] and [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] bring it down. She explores the abandoned _Drowned Pig_ and climbs the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/cartographers-hall|Cartographer's Hall]] wall to overhear complaints that [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] has fled.
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], Rose hears an unidentified soft male voice call her name in the Necropolis, asks [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] about [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]], and learns that it was sealed after something went wrong at the end of the empire. When twelve skeletal soldiers attack, she kills one of them.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], Rose tries to feed an unfamiliar armadillo-like creature before it proves to be a rust monster and eats her spear. A later incorporeal spirit overwhelms her with supernatural terror and ages her by ten years.
- In [[sessions/session-061|Session 61]], Wistor gives Rose a ring of fire resistance, which she passes to [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]]. Her opening arrow then deals minor damage to one of the ogres operating the Westfort siege engines as the relief attack begins.
- In [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]], Rose makes a vaulting attack that heavily wounds the northern ogre, then fires the enemy ballista and kills a blinded ogre standing in front of it.

## Relationships

- Regularly grouped with the later Westmarsh expedition roster.
- Often works alongside Barny on aerial scouting and covert operations, including the Invasion Fort reconnaissance and the chimera trap.

## Current status

Active. She has been supernaturally aged by ten years and now has dragon-scale armour; the `+3` imperial spear she claimed was later eaten by a rust monster.
