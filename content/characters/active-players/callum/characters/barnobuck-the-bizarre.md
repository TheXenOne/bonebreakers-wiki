## Overview

Barnobuck "Barny" the Bizarre is one of the party's defining magic-users. He insists he was once vastly more powerful, and much of his story revolves around recovering lost power, knowledge, and immortality. He is secretive by nature, keeps important things from the party, and appears to maintain undead servants in secret, especially away from the party's clerics.

## Player

Callum.

## Class or role

Magic-user.

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-001|Session 1]], Barny and Virien leave the rescued necromancer [[npcs/barrowshire/the-barrow/quintius|Quintius]] at the Red Herring, then Barny joins the first recorded expedition and harvests paralysis venom from a carcass crawler. The freed silk merchants later reward him and Virien with several thousand gold pieces in IOUs.
- In [[sessions/session-002|Session 2]], Barny fights beside Arden and Alfwine when two zombies ambush the party as they roll the Barrow entrance stone back into place. After the zombies kill Virien with his own alphorn, Barny helps destroy both attackers, allowing the survivors to recover Ike's body and escape with the magical sword and blue gems.
- In [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]], he reads the wills of the fallen, helps choose the cheaper reincarnation that restores [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]], negotiates the terms with the Last Chance, and hires [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/rhian|Rhian]] as a novice magic-user.
- In [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]], he prods a corpse at the collapsed shrine entrance, triggering the brass automaton hands and helping the party discover the shrine's machinery.
- In [[sessions/session-005|Session 5]], he tests the foundry bellows, helps remove two brass lightning bolts from the Mulvis statue, and throws darts at a captured gloop while the party learns that [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/sootmurk|Sootmurk]] is growing hungrier.
- In [[sessions/session-006|Session 6]], Barny wounds [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/sootmurk|Sootmurk]] with a brass lightning bolt, helps recover the party's weapons during the escape, and pursues one of the fleeing gloops. He is killed by the remaining gloops after the Grease Dragon falls, with Arden spending his last spell to save him before the final blow.
- In [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]], Barny is resurrected with resurrection sickness and spends his recovery studying new spells.
- In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], he embeds the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]] in a rat and scouts through its eyes, discovering the [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/rat-queen|Rat Queen]]'s deeper parlor.
- In [[sessions/session-009|Session 9]], Barny uses a mirror to scout the ruined library, finds books on spiders, traps, and unusual dungeon mechanisms, then uses Sleep to stop most of the centipedes overwhelming the party. He claims the silver collar from the giant spider.
- In [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]], he negotiates a one-time cure-poison price with Judas, hires Elon, turns invisible to shove a statue guardian into a pit, and studies the genie-skin research tome. He also uses the Seer's Dagger to locate the rat laboratory, casts Sleep when more centipedes attack, retrieves the dagger, and is cursed after putting on one of the rings from Morlan's casket.
- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], Barny tests a bone flute that produces no effect, reads another trap book, and fails to charm [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]], who responds by throwing a knife at him.
- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], he begins casting Sleep when the Rat Queen refuses to negotiate, helps defeat her guards, and puts on her ring, gaining the limited intelligence of a very clever rat through another curse.
- In [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]], Barny approaches the hostile hooded strangers at the Steamy Smithy as they attack two customers, helping the party identify them as wererats and capture one alive. He charms the survivor for a month, names him [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/rattius|Rattius]], and gains a strange follower who shadows him. During the journey east, Barny uses Sleep to neutralise the glowing beetles that attack the camp, allowing the party to harvest their luminous cores in jars.
- In [[sessions/session-014|Session 14]], Barny joins the expedition to [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]] after the party accepts Ulna Glitterpatch's investigation, and helps prepare for the voyage by brewing five healing potions. The party also accepts a parallel request from the [[factions/governments/ramen-empire|Ramen Empire]] to investigate Callea, Ossilicus, and the Eleftheria.
- In [[sessions/session-016|Session 16]], Barny casts Hold Portal to delay the harpies and physicians flooding into the Golden Asclepeion.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], he claims that he was once the level-100 wizard Barnobuck the Bizarre, explains his belief that a conspiracy stripped him of his power, and says he wants immortality and his lost past. He also asks [[npcs/flux-academy/alric-zexerus|Alric Zexerus]] for information, learning that formal Academy enrolment is required.
- In [[sessions/session-021|Session 21]], Barny fights the cathedral's guards, is briefly paralysed by a Son of Bahal's gaze, throws daggers, and uses Web to cover the retreat. He then confirms through the crystal ball that the route is clear, poisons the cathedral stew under the pretence of improving it, slips into the library while invisible, and takes twelve books on the dark arts.
- In [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], he throws a Fireball at the [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]], is killed by Finger of Death, and gives his Potion of Speed to Athlestan with his dying breath. After being restored, he studies the biography of Baron Bold the Brilliant and finds a lead to a stolen scroll of immortality and a burial mound.
- In [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]], Barny tries to return to the Barrow alone in pursuit of the immortality lead, but the party follows him. He uses Sleep against a baby carcass crawler, finds healing water and a hidden 500-gold coffer in Alfwine's old chambers, sneaks invisibly into [[npcs/barrowshire/the-barrow/quintius|Quintius]]' quarters for a grimoire and scroll, and persuades the party to annex the cleared dungeon as a magical research site.
- In [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]], he uses the [[items/party-inventory/crystal-ball|crystal ball]] to watch the attack on the Mercy, rallies Luthien and Selvar to bring help, and verifies that the kidnapped priests are alive. He then locates roughly one hundred orcs at the old Mulvis shrine, including Gruumsh and the bound priests.
- In [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], Barny casts Invisibility on Frank, gives Bran a Potion of Speed, and joins Selvar and Luthien in putting almost every orc in the shrine to sleep. He then secretly reanimates [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/krazak-the-silent|Krazak the Silent]] as a zombie thrall at personal cost, taking heavy damage when the final battle begins.
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], his dagger becomes one of the sentient weapons draining Ikiria's citizens. Barny asks who caused the crisis, dismisses an unhelpful witness, and argues for finding [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus]]'s secrets rather than simply purging the source, helping lead the party toward the tower investigation.
- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], Barny writes in a magical book and is turned invisible, opens the [[items/party-inventory/screaming-inkwell|Screaming Inkwell]] and releases a message that the entrance room was not searched, and takes a motionless candle that extinguishes every other light. He is shocked by a glass mallet but scoops it into Arden's pack, then scouts the green-vat laboratory while invisible, finds clone-Arkeus and the party's imperfect duplicates, and helps establish the bargain for the Harmonic Regulator.
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], Barny questions the crystal golems, reaches for an arrogant magical tome that first paralyses him and then casts Sleep on him, and later convinces clone-Arkeus to come to Athlestan. He helps restrain the golems with Web, tells an embellished account of the tower's "great sacrifice," and receives the [[items/party-inventory/staff-of-withering|Staff of Withering]] from [[npcs/flux-academy/screever-longtooth|Screever Longtooth]]. His subsequent dream of dying roses leads him to produce a matching decaying rose; [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/arun|Arun]] then notices two puncture marks on Barny's neck, leading into the Blood King arc.
- In [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]], Barny uses the Staff of Withering against undead hounds, tells a vampire that the party is there to kill vampires rather than join them, and ages several guards to death. He also stabs an orderly spider without effect, puts on a hat that gives him claws, and activates the manor mirror to meet [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/alvalus|Alvalus]], the Blood King's reflected guilt, regret, and shame.
- In [[sessions/session-030|Session 30]], Barny dons a vampire cloak to help the party pass within the manor's court, then joins the investigation of the drunken vampire at the trophy bar. He learns that the Blood King wears a neck relic associated with raising the dead and that Lord Demetrius never drinks blood. The vampire turns to mist, reforms as a wolf, and pins Barny before Del stakes it; Barny then shakes a bottle containing two blinking eyes, briefly disorienting them, and keeps it as a trophy.
- In [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]], Barny helps harvest the manor's arcane lotus, trips [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/circander|Circander]] as she tries to escape, receives the [[items/party-inventory/wand-of-daystars|Wand of Daystars]] from the Princess, and questions the manor guards. He kills one human guard with the Staff of Withering, secretly raises another guard as a zombie, and later discovers that the [[items/party-inventory/age-reading-enchanted-gloves|age-reading gloves]] reveal the age of anything he touches.
- In [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]], he turns invisible and poisons the goblets of blood prepared for the vampire feast. He tests the manor's [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|telescope]], discovers that it reveals a target's recent nightmares, gives healing potions to Tess and Del, nearly dies when the observatory's planetary mechanism activates, waylays five guards, and carries the telescope away when the Blood King's heart is destroyed.
- In [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], Barny stays up studying at the Cracked Shield, helps rescue its cat when the inn burns, and charms [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/vex|Vex]] into admitting that he burned the granary, poisoned the well, and stole the town's arms for an unknown hooded employer. Barny uses the Seer's Dagger to spy through Vex while the party prepares an ambush, then uses Sleep against the orcs guarding the road.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], he charms the captured handler and names him [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/jeremy|Jeremy]], learns that Ash is Jeremy's handler and that the next meeting is at the [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|Spider Market]], persuades Jeremy to accept the Seer's Dagger, and helps survive the fight with the hobgoblins at the cave.
- In [[sessions/session-037|Session 37]], Barny makes [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] invisible and able to fly so she can scout the hobgoblin fortress, revealing that weapons, armour, and senior commanders are being assembled for war.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], he puts the fortress's outer guards to sleep, is badly injured in the ensuing fight before Cedric heals him, and uses the crystal ball to confirm that the priests at the Mercy are sick and that Jeremy has returned to the cliffside fort.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], Barny improves the crystal ball and confirms that three priests at the Mercy are dead and more are seriously ill. He helps put thirteen hawks and their goblin riders out of action with Sleep, casts Fly on the invisible Steve during the counterattack, and spies on Jeremy and Ash with the Seer's Dagger. He keeps the crystal-ball evidence about the Mercy's illness from the party.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], Barny leans toward accepting Ash's [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] offer to investigate the Necropolis. He later uses the crystal ball to confirm Westfort's survival and the devastation of the battlefield, but lies about seeing gravely ill priests because he wants to return to Westmarsh. He gathers Rhian and Rattius before setting out for the Mercy.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], Barny sleeps alone in the lower area of the Mercy despite a voice trying to lure him onward, then uses the Seer's Dagger on Rattius to scout the impossible staircase. He examines Cornelius Vane's maps, identifies the necromantic implications of [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]]'s work, and reads the soul-binding treatise, gaining `+1` Intelligence.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], Barny casts Invisibility on Rose so she can place poisoned bait for the ancient chimera without being detected.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], he questions [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]], Prince Arthur's secretary, and investigates a captive with Tobold's face who shows no recognition of him. He uses the crystal ball to inspect the Invasion Fort and sees that its war preparations have resumed on a smaller scale, then buys another wand at the Spider Market.
- In [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]], Barny and Cyder put the decoy tent's occupants to sleep. He charms the captured recruiter, learning that the Grey Syndicate has no single headquarters, that another invasion is being prepared at the hobgoblin fort, that Gorm sometimes acts as a contact, and that the Necropolis lies roughly fifty miles west of the [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|Spider Market]]. He also learns that the secretary was meant to be killed after the planned exchange, then confirms the renewed but reduced invasion preparations at the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] with the crystal ball.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], Barny approaches the inquisitor among the dead, tries to communicate with the unresponsive undead, and identifies the corpse-pit wight as an ancient imperial soldier. He concludes that the undead were brought to Ikiria by magical means, then uses the returned box to cross the raised bridge after it strikes him in the head.
- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], he uses the Wand of Daystars to destroy one wraith, strikes a skeleton with the Staff, and is left near death by its counterattack. He takes to the air with Fly, then uses the wand again to badly damage a wight and blind both undead attackers.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], a wight grabs Barny by the foot and drains 15,000 experience—half a level of life force—from him. Ulrich destroys the wight with holy water, but Barny immediately flies toward Ikiria in panic.
- At East Agria Company Headquarters, Barny turns invisible, drinks a Potion of Speed, enters through an open window, and uses his everflame candle to search the secretarial offices. He steals an antique sextant and an antique gnomish clock as cultural artefacts, then flies back to the party before anyone realises he was gone. Ulrich later heals him after the theft.
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], two false Barnys appear inside [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. One imperfectly mimics him near [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]], while another claims that eternal life lies through faith before the party destroys it.
- The first false Barny even carries the same 280 gold pieces as the original. Barny tries to question the silent zombies, sends Frank to the roof to study the labourers' pattern, and is nearly killed by an ochre jelly before Arden heals him. He then bluffs the zombie foreman [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]] with an invented health-and-safety inspection, learning that the undead labourers are restoring [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain and asking how to reach it. When another Barny-faced zombie claims eternal life lies through faith, Barny mocks and rejects it before the party destroys it.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], Barny uses the crystal ball to spy on orcs occupying the Mercy and locate the lone guard above the Abyss trapdoor, charms that guard through the ball and persuades him that an outside invasion is beginning, then casts Invisibility on Frank. He secretly reanimates the dead orc squad leader for questioning, researches Bahal and the church's history, and finds a treatise describing the Abyss as a stairway to heaven.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], Barny improves the [[items/party-inventory/staff-of-withering|Staff of Withering]] and uses the [[items/party-inventory/crystal-ball|crystal ball]] to view the siege at Westfort's west gate, but does not share what he sees with the party.
- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], he tests the dead market's rules by pretending to steal from a zombie merchant and paying with imaginary money; the merchant still gives him a real imperial coin as change. Barny also drains hit points from another undead with the Staff of Withering.
- In [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]], Barny poisons the party's final horse during a gryphon attack, helping kill three of the creatures when they bring it down. He is nearly killed after stabbing another with the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]], and the wounded gryphon escapes with the blade still lodged in it.
- In [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]], Barny turns invisible during the ranged attack, kills the Krell that ate Luthien's body, and harvests its remains. He locates the escaped gryphon through the Red Seer's Dagger, discovers soul-binding magic in an imperial ruin, conceals the magic's true purpose from the party, and later turns invisible to try to bind a skeleton while pale-blue disenchanters approach. The [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] then shows him a soul held in a codex.
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], Barny sits with [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] and says the Bonebreakers came from Barrow Spa, then answers Varro's questions while exaggerating Agria's military strength. Varro leaves an imperial map unguarded. Barny later uses the [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] on the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]], seeing its twenty-four wight guardians dream of missing reliquaries and one sarcophagus occupant dream of power and wealth, and kills the last two skeletons in the chamber.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], Barny pries up a loose flagstone and climbs into a hidden pay room. He experiments with the funeral carvings, discovering that their eyes follow the nearest flame while the other lights dim, then uses the telescope on an incorporeal spirit and sees a glowing skull in lizardfolk hands, strengthening the party's belief that [[items/glowing-skull|the skull]] is one of [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]]'s phylacteries. He consumes a Potion of Control Undead, takes command of a wight and six skeletons in the Arch I drill yard, and orders them to perform handstands and dance.
- In [[sessions/session-061|Session 61]], Barny clarifies that the lizardfolk figure in the nightmare was a shaman from an unidentified clan studying the skull closely. He scries on Westfort, blinds a bugbear during the party's approach, then uses Fly and blinds one of the ogres operating the siege engines during the relief attack.
- In [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]], Barny incapacitates hobgoblin archers with Sleep and unsuccessfully tries to charm their king, [[npcs/westmarsh/kargaz-the-disciplined|Kargaz the Disciplined]]. After Wistor kills Kargaz, Barny secretly raises the king as a zombie, turns him invisible, and orders him to hide before travelling to the Barrow.

## Relationships

- Maintains the closest ties to Rhian, Rattius, and Krazak.
- Early partnership with [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] is central to the first arc.
- Keeps secrets from the party and seems to hide parts of his necromantic activity, especially from the clerics.
