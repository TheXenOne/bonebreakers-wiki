## Overview

Quak II! is Jennifer's current character, a paladin who arrives to aid the Bonebreakers during the corpse-pit battle in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. She is distinct from Jennifer's earlier bard character, Quak!.

## Player

Jennifer.

## Class or role

Paladin.

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], Quak II! arrives to reinforce the party during the corpse-pit undead battle.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], Quak II! helps finish off the zombies that arrive after the Bloated One's death.
- In [[sessions/session-057|Session 57]], Quak discusses chaos and faith with Cecil at the Red Herring and hires him as a retainer.
