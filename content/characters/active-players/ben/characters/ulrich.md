## Overview

Ulrich is one of the founding Bonebreakers, a long-standing cleric who serves no named god yet still receives divine magic. He is pragmatic, deeply connected to the party's history, and more focused on divine spellcasting than melee combat. His central goal is to find the "one true god" while studying faith more broadly.

## Player

Ben.

## Class or role

Cleric of none.

## Important campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]], Ulrich joins the rebuilt party after Virien's reincarnation. The new group recruits retainers, adopts the name [[factions/other/bonebreakers|the Bonebreakers]], and begins operating as a permanent adventuring company.
- In [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]], he buys [[characters/active-players/ben/retainers-followers/fred|Fred]], then tests the blackened grease trail in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]] by setting a sample alight; it ignites instantly. Inside the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]], he pulls a spike from the chained Mulvis statue and later presses a wall to reveal a second hidden route.
- In [[sessions/session-005|Session 5]], he gathers tools and dismantles the locked door beside the Mulvis statue. The room contains rotten robes, automaton parts, and a six-foot, six-armed automaton version of Mulvis with a lightning-bolt socket in its back; Ulrich also checks the shrine's caskets and confirms that the obvious ones are locked.
- In [[sessions/session-006|Session 6]], after the Bonebreakers' deception gets them released by the gloops, one fleeing gloop escapes with their weapons. Ulrich kills it and recovers the weapons, then purifies the shrine basin and retrieves the final brass lightning bolt before the party returns to confront Sootmurk.
- In [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]], when the party turns on Gorfthistle after he kills Neem, Ulrich remains passive rather than joining the attack. Later, when the head is cut from Auto-Mulvis and the automaton explodes, he dives clear while Bran, Arden, and Dreg are caught in the blast.
- In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], he crawls across the first checkered-floor trap, deliberately triggering a white tile so the wall blades swing out. He jams the blades with iron spikes and establishes that the safe route is made entirely of black tiles, allowing the party to reach the rat nest and the next trap.
- In [[sessions/session-009|Session 9]], he helps clear the giant centipedes in the ruined library, investigates the bookcases, and begins dismantling a hinged one before realising that it simply opens onto a concealed passage. In the flute-statue chamber, he whistles back at the Tarantella spider to distract it while the party searches for a mechanism; when another wave of centipedes bites him, his immunity leaves him unharmed.
- In [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]], he takes the two gemmed rings from the forge and studies the mural of fiery smiths, noting the hammer, tongs, and flames but finding no hidden mechanism. In the next chamber, he tests one ring against the sword-bearing statues' demand for a seal of approval; the ring and Virien's chalk sigil both fail, so the party lures the statues into the spike pits.
- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], he suspects that the still body of water described in the trap book is dangerous rather than a normal pool. The corroded dwarf corpse at the bottom and the footprints leading across the liquid vindicate his suspicion, although the party's attempt to cross still releases a grey ooze that kills Virien and forces a retreat.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], he explains that he has wandered from temple to temple while receiving divine magic without pledging himself to a named god. He is searching for the "one true god", hopes to establish a healing order, and wants to collect relics from many faiths for study; the city's alchemists also identify a potion of control gold dragon for him.
- In [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]], he sets the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|cathedral]]'s twisted alarm bell aflame during the opening assault, but a trainee rings it before the bell collapses. He then spikes one set of plaza doors while the party turns the courtyard into a defensive position against the Crimson Knights.
- In [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], he takes a kindly approach with the family of the rescued boys and convinces their frightened father that the Bonebreakers are not agents of the [[factions/religions-and-cults/church-of-the-crimson-flame|Crimson Flame]]. The family gives the party food and shelter, establishing Ulrich as the member who can defuse the suspicion created by the party's stolen enemy armour.
- In [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]], he claims Sir Ignacious' holy symbol, which strengthens his ability to Turn Undead, and hears its former owner speak telepathically about serving the one true god and being right and just. Ulrich answers that he is unsure but hopes so; he later uses the symbol to destroy undead in the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|Barrow]] and casts Light into the opalescent healing pool to inspect its clear depths.
- In [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]], the reclaimed church is divided between repairs, spying, research, and clerical work, with two priests assigned to train as researchers under Ulrich. When ninja-like orcs raid the church and abduct four priests, the party arranges a partial ransom and commits to rescuing the remaining hostages.
- In [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], he fills a barrel with leftover [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/sootmurk|Sootmurk]] residue and helps turn it into the main trap against [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh]]'s warband. During the assault, he freezes [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/krazak-the-silent|Krazak]] with Hold Person, kills an orc during the change of guard, and brings down the last orc in the final sweep before the grease corridors are ignited.
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], as weapons across Ikiria begin speaking and draining their bearers, his own mace complains about his lack of direction. He replaces it with a plain wooden club that does not fatigue him, warns [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] about Frank's hired bard and the dangerous story spreading through the city, and then helps Selvar solve the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/tower-of-arkeus-whizkeyrd|Tower of Arkeus]]' first resonance puzzle.
- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], he uses his shield to scoop the [[items/party-inventory/face-changing-golden-coin|Face-Changing Golden Coin]] out of its display case after failing to lift the case itself. He finds a hidden brass lever that opens a forcefield passage, tries to strike an invisible force attacking Arden's sling, and prays to a newly appearing divine face; the answer is, "Seek another path." He later blesses the party when the clones attack and pulls a spell-filled cage aside, triggering Ventriloquism.
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], he questions the crystal golems about Arkeus' role as a bridge anchoring the rift and notices that motes have inhabited the party's weapons. He and Athlestan refuse to cross the corridor of uncontrolled spells, avoiding the arcane burst that knocks down the others; after the crisis ends, Ulrich claims a `+1` mace as his reward.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], he confirms at a local pagan temple that the giant hawks are highly trained rather than acting as ordinary predators. He also meets [[npcs/west-fort/sister-aldrith|Sister Aldrith]], a cleric interested in healing, undead samples, ancient texts, and religious relics, giving Ulrich a new contact for his research.
- In [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], he escapes the burning Cracked Shield through a window, then blinds [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/veteran-borak|Veteran Borak]] with Light immediately after Borak cuts Sarla's throat. The opening lets the party capture Borak while Cedric keeps Sarla alive.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], he uses Hold Person against a hostile hobgoblin group whose answers about the hawks turn into an attack. He and Barny absorb heavy blows before Luthien's Sleep spell ends the fight and the party takes the jewellery found on the bodies.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], he blesses the raid on the invasion fort, helps defeat the hobgoblin officers, and heals himself after taking heavy injuries. When the Mercy reports that priests are being poisoned, he sends the [[items/party-inventory/crown-of-king-bomfluke|Crown of King Bomfluke]] there as protection against poison.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], he acquires `+1` plate armour from Mara Ironhand and spots the twenty-four giant hawks carrying goblins into [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. He blinds one hawk during the coordinated defence, then searches the invasion fort and finds the hidden passage leading underground.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], he blesses the Bonebreakers as Ash's orcs attack after the party refuses the Grey Syndicate's offer. The party defeats the guards, rescues the kidnapped children, and returns with documents showing that the Syndicate has been using poison, fire, and kidnapping to destabilise Agria.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], he casts Detect Magic after the party crosses into the inverted space beneath the Mercy and finds the entire area saturated with magic. When the imperial eagle opens Arch I, he destroys the nine skeletons that march out with Turn Undead; later, the same power has no effect on the chained skull in the Foundation Chamber.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], he casts Silence on Rose so she can place the poisoned pig without alerting the chimera. On the road to Ikiria, he follows Selvar's Sleep spell by holding [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]'s captain in place, helping the party capture the retinue and uncover the plot against Princess Agatha.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], he casts Light into the face of the halfling assassin who approaches the camp, blinding him long enough for the party to restrain him. In the Spider Market, Ulrich hears from three surviving adventurers about a lethal western entrance to the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|Necropolis]], then buys a holy symbol that detects evil and discovers that [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] radiates extreme evil.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], he uses Cure Disease on a visibly sick child in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] while the party moves through quarantine, burned buildings, inquisitor violence, and severe overcrowding. The successful cure shows that at least part of Ikiria's crisis behaves like ordinary disease rather than being purely necromantic.
- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], he repeatedly turns undead during the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/corpse-pit|corpse-pit battle]], uses Light to blind a mummy, and destroys a wraith with holy water. His evil-detecting symbol also shows that the automaton grave is neutral even though something inside it is moving.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], he identifies the ancient imperial armour taken from the undead as evil-aligned. He then descends into the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|Public Well]], casts Neutralise Poison to purify the suspected source of the first sickness, and recovers 400 pre-imperial gold pieces that appear to have been planted to stage the poisoning.
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], he asks [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] to press the [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]] to ease the quarantine because overcrowding is worsening the disease. In [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]], he tests the shrine idols, keeps the mostly non-evil examples for religious research, and distinguishes them from three altered idols that radiate evil.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], he destroys the zombie children in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v/childrens-quarters|Arch V's children's quarters]] rather than letting them remain a threat. After the party reaches a hidden shrine, he takes an unofficial god's idol from a domestic cache, adding a relic from the Necropolis to his growing collection.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], his Locate Object spell finds a chest hidden inside the Arch V shrine plinth, leading to an old locket and a vial of holy water. He turns the zombie labourers after the Bloated One's death, helps hold the Mercy's orc occupiers in place during the counterattack, and later casts Cure Disease on Steve and Micaelis after the Bloated One's necrotic explosion infects them.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], during the two-week recovery after Bran's sacrifice, he researches a spell for locating nearby treasure. He also asks for news of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] and learns that the renewed invasion has become a stalemated siege involving siege engines, hobgoblins, goblins, and ogres.
- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], he barters with a zombie merchant in the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]] for silver instruments worth 260 gold pieces. When serpents drag a companion underwater, Ulrich heals them after the party recovers them, then blesses the Bonebreakers before their fight with the snake-men.
- In [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]], the party uses his horse as poisoned bait during a gryphon attack, and the horse dies when the trap is sprung. Ulrich blinds two gryphons, pursues and subdues one of them, and helps secure it alive for the attempted [[quests/minor/active/gryphon-mounts|gryphon-breeding plan]].
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], he helps navigate the Imperial Hill Temple's eagle-controlled passages and notices funerary carvings that have been altered to depict a halfling. When skeletal soldiers attack, he turns ten of them, and his evil-detecting symbol finds no evil in the imperial armoury the party is allowed to loot.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], he detects a hollow space beneath the trapped armoury floor and supplies a Continual Light stone when the fumes make open flame unsafe. His healing magic removes gas-induced paralysis from Arden, his Sticks to Snakes spell helps kill the rust monster, and he collects sealed jars from the embalming workshop. When an incorporeal spirit cannot be turned and begins draining Wistor, Ulrich blinds it permanently with Continual Light so the party can survive and dismantle the gate.
- In [[sessions/session-061|Session 61]], he casts Sticks to Snakes during a bugbear ambush, turning fifteen sticks into snakes that poison and wound the attackers. Before the party enters the spreading fire behind the hobgoblin army, he casts Fire Resistance on himself and Selvar so they can operate in the blaze.
- In [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]], he fights through the burning battlefield during the relief of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], helping finish the hobgoblin archers while the party brings down the siege army. During the following downtime, he begins researching a Skeleton's Key spell.
- In [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]], he registers as a pilgrim in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]] and persuades the Queen's Guard to let Drakford stay armed as his bodyguard. The party follows the resulting referral to Brother Sedge and gains entry to Arthur's Palace. Ulrich’s mace remains in the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/guardhouse|guardhouse safe]], whose key Nila has secretly stolen.
