## Overview

Luthien was an elf adventurer raised in a sheltered village she disliked. Ambitious and openly hungry for power, she left home to seek a less constrained life and became a long-serving member of the Bonebreakers.

## Player

Marta.

## Class or role

Elf.

## Retainers or companions

An unusually intelligent bear begins following Luthien after she speaks with it on the road to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] in [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]]. Needs review on whether it remains a long-term companion.

## Associated locations

- [[locations/world/fos-imeras/index|Fos Imeras]]
- [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]

## Important campaign events

- Joins the Bonebreakers during the [[sessions/session-015|Golden Asclepeion]] expedition on [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]].
- Shares her hatred of her sheltered elven village and her hunger for power in [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]].
- In [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], she casts Web to trap incoming Crimson Knights and a Son of Bahal during the cathedral assault, claims a suit of the fallen Sons of Bahal armour, and charms a captured knight to learn that [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|the Crimson Bishop]] leads the church and is treated as a source of purity.
- In [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]], she helps rally twenty villagers in the name of Belenus to rescue the priests held by [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh One-Tusk]]. After an unsuccessful attempt to win over an orc prisoner, she uses Charm to make him cooperative.
- In [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], she sends the charmed orc toward the old shrine of Mulvis with wine as a supposed gift, hoping to bluff Gruumsh into lowering his guard. When the ploy fails, she sneaks invisibly to the shrine's prison and joins Barny and Selvar in putting almost the entire foundry and prison to sleep.
- Later in [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], she helps clear the remaining orcs and casts Sleep on Gruumsh's elite fighters, breaking the last organised resistance before Gruumsh surrenders.
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], she identifies [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]] as an old friend who brought her to Ikiria ten years earlier and taught her much, defending him when the party investigates the sentient-weapon crisis. Her own weapon becomes vocal and urges her to do something evil, which she quickly stifles.
- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], she takes the twitching [[items/party-inventory/the-laughing-blade|Laughing Blade]] from its display and immediately begins hearing its manic laughter and bad jokes in her head. She later gathers arcane fragments and catches a flying book containing a scroll of Protection from Lycanthropes.
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], she and Selvar repair the snapped lever at the Threshold Gate and operate the machinery, discovering another way to open the gate and helping resolve the sentient-weapon crisis.
- In [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]], she finds an iron gate leading to stairs descending into a dark, fungus-scented part of the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king|Halls of the Blood King]].
- In [[sessions/session-030|Session 30]], she dons one of the manor's vampire cloaks, allowing the party to smell like vampires and pass at a glance while infiltrating the Halls.
- In [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]], she harvests arcane lotus, helps kill [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/circander|Circander]], takes a magical crossbow, and finds a key, protective scrolls, enchanted gloves, and an unknown potion while searching the manor.
- In [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]], she uses Locate Object to track the lock tied to the [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/the-blood-king|Blood King's]] heart. She inserts the key into the observatory's planetary model, exposing the heart so the party can destroy it, then carries Selana's body and returns with Tess to gather the prisoners as the manor collapses.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], she slips away to a local library and reads about the gnolls, orcs, goblins, bugbears, ogres, and undead inhabiting Westmarsh.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], she ends the fight with the hostile hobgoblins near the Spider Market by casting Sleep, helping the party recover a large jewellery haul.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], she and Selvar translate the Invasion Fort's papers, confirming that the organised army plans to invade Agria and target Westfort.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], [[npcs/west-fort/elara-swiftstring|Elara Swiftstring]] equips her with Elven boots and a stealthy cloak. During the siege, Luthien combines Sleep with the party's other magic to bring down thirteen hawks carrying goblins.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], she suggests temporarily accepting Ash's offer to investigate the Necropolis for the Grey Syndicate, though the party ultimately refuses and prioritises rescuing the children.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], she identifies the ancient imperial numerals in the chamber beneath the Mercy as older than the forms used by the [[factions/governments/ramen-empire|Ramen Empire]], helping the party recognise the scale and age of the Necropolis.
- During the partly unattributed [[sessions/session-043|Sessions 42–43]] delve, she moves ahead alone to scout the route back toward the Necropolis's claw-scarred section. Exact scene attribution: Needs review.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], she scouts the claw-scarred passage and discovers an ancient sleeping chimera and its hoard, then searches a second cavern with a shaft leading toward a sealed domed chamber.
- Later in [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], she webs the drivers during the ambush of [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]'s procession and charms Arthur. Her questions reveal that he is travelling to meet [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|the Grey Khan]] for help becoming king and that the Grey Syndicate is moving against [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]].
- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], she uses Arthur's charm to send [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/pell|Pell]] to [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] with a message and orders Arthur's guards not to attack when they wake. She also gives Arthur a mystery ring to identify it, then takes back the [[items/party-inventory/ring-of-water-walking|Ring of Water Walking]] once its magic is revealed. Later, she secretly tails Gorm using the new cloak and boots.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], she tackles and restrains an assassin wearing [[characters/active-players/david/characters/tobold-took|Tobold]]'s face. She later walks ahead with Arthur into the Spider Market, where Ash immediately recognises that Luthien is controlling him.
- In [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]], she sneaks with Selvar to the orcs' tent, helps trigger the assault on the decoy operation, and helps the party seize 10,000 gold pieces. Arthur remains under her charm through his arrest in Ikiria.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], she investigates behind a waterfall in the Plats, finishes a fleeing ghoul during the corpse-pit battle, and discovers that at least some of the stronger undead are immune to mundane weapons.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], she helps bring down the last mummy in the corpse-pit battle, then boards the _Saint of Tides_ and meets an elf who knows [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]'s theories and believes someone is deliberately upsetting Ikiria's balance.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], she is caught in the Bloated One's necrotic explosion, though Micaelis and Steve suffer the worst of the rotting disease.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], she opens the hidden Arch V shrine cache, keeps the silver locket found inside, asks [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/aldrich|Aldrich]] about the accompanying vial later identified as holy water, puts the Mercy orcs to sleep during the counterattack, befriends an unusually intelligent bear, and orders a fine bow in Barrow Spa.
- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], she claims to be descended from the unknown person for whom the intricately carved mask in the Sunken Market is reserved. The merchant accepts the claim and tells her the mask is held on the lower floor.
- In [[sessions/session-057|Session 57]], she scouts the Invasion Fort alone through its secret entrance, finds the early chambers nearly empty, and is seized by a mimic disguised as a chest near the western secret entrance.
- In [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]], she escapes the mimic by slipping out of her glove, but a Krell paralyses and kills her before the party can reach her. The creature partly eats her body before it is killed, preventing Raise Dead from restoring her. Her belongings and what remains of her body are recovered.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], the Bonebreakers return to the Mercy and a waiting priest begins preparations for her funeral.
- In [[sessions/session-061|Session 61]], the party says that a tree has been planted for Luthien. Arden commissions Michelangelo to create a wooden memorial statue of her, although the final material may still change.
- Luthien's will explicitly states that she does not want to be reincarnated.

## Relationships

- Travels as part of the main Bonebreakers company from the mid-campaign onward.

## Current status

Dead. Her partly eaten remains are in the Bonebreakers' possession, preparations for her funeral have begun at the Mercy, and a memorial statue has been commissioned in Barrow Spa. Raise Dead cannot restore her, and her will explicitly refuses Reincarnation.
