## Overview

Selvar is a neutral elf who joins during the cathedral arc and remains active through the later Westmarsh and necropolis adventures. He is short and lithe, and favors charm, illusion, control magic, and magical investigation.

## Player

Alessandro.

## Class or role

Elf.

## Retainers or companions

Needs review.

- [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]

## Important campaign events

- Recruited in a flashback tied to [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]].
- During the cathedral withdrawal in [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], he puts two gate guards to sleep and later kills a returning Son of Bahal, helping free two prisoners.
- In [[sessions/session-021|Session 21]], Selvar charms [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]], allowing the party to extract him from the cathedral through a smashed window and hear his account of the Crimson Bishop.
- In [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], he remains with the Bonebreakers during the final assault on the [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]], after which the cathedral is reclaimed and its priests and Captain Fardr are freed.
- In [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]], Selvar drinks from an opalescent pool and is restored by its healing magic.
- In [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]], he helps rally twenty villagers to join the rescue of the priests held by [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh One-Tusk]].
- In [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], he casts Sleep with Barny and Luthien over the shrine's foundry and prison, leaving only Krazak, a messenger, and a guard awake. He helps clear the changing-of-the-guard and sleeping orcs, then takes Gruumsh prisoner after the final fight.
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], Selvar asks his crimson blade whether it has always been sentient; it replies that it has always been sentient, but was not always a weapon. At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/tower-of-arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus's tower]], he feels psychic pressure when he touches the exterior and solves the resonance puzzle with Ulrich: Selvar chooses “Crab”, Ulrich chooses “Repeat”, and they synchronise on the sixth attempt, opening hidden passages.
- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], he breaks open the display case holding [[items/party-inventory/drinkers-babel|Drinker's Babel]], drinks from it when the liquid disappears, and asks the animated book following him to draw a map. He then finds the [[items/party-inventory/astral-tuning-fork|Astral Tuning Fork]] and uses it to dissolve the Threshold Gate after presenting it to clone-Arkeus.
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], one of the tower's motes briefly enters Selvar and distorts his body and behaviour. He uses Knock to open a locked door, taps the tuning fork to open the forcefield guarding the Harmonic Regulator, and stays with Luthien to repair the Threshold Gate mechanism. He later follows the animated book's instruction to stand where the party entered, helping the group escape the tower, and takes a spell scroll as his reward.
- Across [[sessions/session-029|Sessions 29]]–[[sessions/session-032|32]], Selvar accompanies the party through the Halls of the Blood King, from the first infiltration and vampire-court encounters through the rescue of prisoners and the destruction of the Blood King's hidden heart.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], Selvar puts a lone rider to sleep before Akal kills him, then helps Tess explain the party's evidence to [[npcs/west-fort/sergeant-dara|Sergeant Dara]]. The rider's death disturbs the party.
- In [[sessions/session-034|Session 34]], he joins the Bonebreakers' first expedition into Westmarsh, including the failed bridge ambush and the gnoll attack that kills Tess, before the party returns to Westfort.
- In [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], Selvar escapes the burning tavern through a window, puts Vex to sleep when he bursts from the back door, and fires on orcs with Akal before Barny disables them.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], Selvar continues the investigation from Vex's warehouse to the Spider Market and the nearby hobgoblin cave, helping the party follow the trail from Vex through Jeremy and Ash to the organised forces in the hills.
- In [[sessions/session-037|Session 37]], he puts the kobold miners to sleep; the party kills two and keeps one alive for questioning.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], Selvar and Luthien translate invasion plans identifying Westfort as a target, and Selvar puts the Invasion Fort's main-hall hobgoblins to sleep during the raid. He later claims `+2` armour from the fallen officers.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], Selvar receives a Displacer Cloak and combines Sleep with the party's other magic to drop thirteen hawks carrying goblins during the siege of Westfort.
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], he leans toward accepting [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]'s offer to investigate the Necropolis for the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]], though the party ultimately refuses it.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], Selvar descends beneath the Mercy with the Bonebreakers and remains with them as they identify the Necropolis, choose Arch VII, meet [[npcs/necropolis/cornelius-vane|Cornelius Vane]], and explore the inverted chambers. The party reaches the Foundation Chamber and recovers [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]]'s necromantic treatise and the [[items/glowing-skull|glowing skull]].
- In [[sessions/session-042|Session 42]], Selvar continues deeper into Arch VII as the party investigates warped chambers and ancient halls where sound, distance, and geometry behave strangely. His individual contribution in this stretch remains unclear.
- In [[sessions/session-043|Session 43]], Selvar withdraws and regroups with the Bonebreakers, then returns with them to the Necropolis and retraces the route toward the claw-scarred section. His individual contribution remains unclear in this partly unattributed delve.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], Selvar puts ten guards to sleep during the ambush of [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]'s procession, making the capture possible.
- Across [[sessions/session-045|Sessions 45]] and [[sessions/session-046|46]], he travels with the party while Arthur's confession is turned into evidence, the attempt to contact the Grey Syndicate through Gorm fails, and the party reaches the Spider Market. He is present when the party learns Tobold is dead and when Ash claims to be the Grey Khan.
- In [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]], Cyder makes Selvar invisible, after which he persuades sprites to distract the Spider Market and sneaks with Luthien toward a decoy orc tent. He signals the assault with a Light-marked stone, then charms [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]], helping secure testimony against Arthur.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], he descends into quarantined Plats and takes part in the investigation at the Corpse Pit, where the plague crisis gives way to a battle with rising undead.
- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], Selvar uses Mirror Image during the corpse-pit battle, helps bring down the mummies, and is struck by mummy rot before Arden cures the wound.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], Selvar helps Luthien and Rose bring down a mummy, then claims one of the ancient imperial armour suits recovered from the undead. He uses ESP on [[npcs/ikiria/rufus|Rufus]] to uncover the guard's trauma after the inquisitor burnings, helps steady him while Arden comforts him, and writes to Vaessa for permission to leave the quarantine district.
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], Selvar finds four ancient painted panels, then cuts into an ochre jelly and splits it into three smaller jellies. During the fight he discovers that his newly worn ancient imperial armour is cursed and worsens his protection, and he is burned by acid.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], Selvar examines and erases a chalk mark pointing into unexplored Necropolis territory, then puts on one of the rings found in the ruins. He uses Mirror Image in the ambush that kills the Bloated One and is caught in its necrotic explosion, though Micaelis and Steve suffer the worst of the rotting disease.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], he helps finish the zombies attacking the Mercy, trades shouted insults with the orcs below the cathedral through a trapdoor, and remains behind to guard the Mercy while the rest of the party travels to Barrow Spa.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], he finishes reading the [[items/party-inventory/manual-of-strength|Manual of Strength]] and gains additional strength.
- In [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]], he uses Mirror Image during the gryphon ambush, then subdues and binds one gryphon alive for the party’s attempted [[quests/minor/active/gryphon-mounts|breeding and training plan]].
- In [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]], Selvar helps clear the temple's skeletons and listens at a sealed door before a disenchanter removes the enchantment from his sword. Whether the loss is permanent: Needs review.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], gas from a broken jar temporarily paralyses Selvar until Arden removes the effect with healing magic.
- In [[sessions/session-061|Session 61]], Selvar puts three of eleven attacking bugbears to sleep. During the Westfort relief attack, Ulrich protects him with Fire Resistance, but a missed shot alerts ten hobgoblin archers and his Mirror Image makes him their most conspicuous target.
- In [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]], he puts thirteen hobgoblin archers to sleep, then puts half of another archer group to sleep and fires on the survivors as the siege of Westfort is broken.
- In [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]], he uses ESP to discover that Hugh Malory's loyalty rests on job security and Arthur's position. Elspeth catches him trying to lift a royal seal, prompting Steve to charm her and preserve the party's access to the archives.

## Current status

Active. Selvar is affected by cursed ancient imperial armour, has gained strength from the Manual of Strength, and has had his sword disenchanted. Whether its magic can be restored: Needs review.
