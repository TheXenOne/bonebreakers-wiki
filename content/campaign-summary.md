The **Bonebreakers** began as a band of adventurers hired to clear undead from a Barrow near Barrow Spa. What started as a dangerous local job quickly became a pattern: delve into forgotten ruins, survive catastrophic losses, uncover powerful relics, and emerge richer and more infamous than before.

Their early exploits saw them slay the so-called **Grease Dragon**, plunder the trapped laboratories of a long-dead jeweller-magician, and follow a hidden map across the sea to an island where healing had become something monstrous. There they destroyed a corrupted cult, freed its prisoners, and rescued **Princess Agatha**, heir to the Agrial throne.

That rescue changed everything. Agatha recruited the Bonebreakers as covert agents of the crown, sending them against the **Church of the Crimson Flame**, whose undead bishop presided over the Deathless Plague. After destroying him and ending the church’s reign of terror, the party inherited its cathedral and transformed it into **the Mercy**, gaining land, influence, and a permanent base of power.

Their adventures grew stranger and more dangerous. They accidentally tore open a breach to the Astral Plane while stopping a magical catastrophe in Ikiria, then destroyed an otherworldly vampire domain by finding and crushing the enormous heart that sustained its king. Soon afterward, their companion **Tobold Took** was abducted by a giant hawk, drawing them west into a much larger conflict.

The search for Tobold uncovered a coordinated campaign of kidnapping, sabotage, poisonings, monster armies, and political manipulation orchestrated by the **Grey Syndicate**. The Bonebreakers disrupted a hobgoblin invasion and rescued the stolen children, but Tobold was already dead. Worse, they discovered that the conspiracy reached into the royal family itself: **Prince Arthur** was working with the Syndicate in hopes of taking Agatha’s throne.

At the same time, the party found an impossible staircase beneath the Mercy leading through a vast Abyss and into the **Imperial Necropolis**, the remains of the civilisation that ruled Westmarsh two thousand years ago. Its undead inhabitants still carry out the routines of a dead empire, while its ancient magic connects places separated by enormous distances.

As plague and undead outbreaks spread through Ikiria, the Bonebreakers learned that the Necropolis is tied to **High Priest Valedictus**, an ancient necromancer who once sought dominion across the continent. Valedictus possesses multiple phylacteries, and the party may already have unknowingly released one of them.

Meanwhile, the surviving hobgoblin commander **Kargaz the Disciplined** rebuilt the shattered invasion force and laid siege to Westfort. The Bonebreakers broke the siege, destroyed his army, and killed Kargaz. After Barny secretly reanimated him, Kargaz confirmed that the Grey Syndicate had supported the war and promised him control of Westfort.

[[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]] brings the Bonebreakers into a secret meeting at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow/anchor-and-chain|the Anchor & Chain]] in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow|Harrow]]. After testing their memory of the secrecy demanded following her rescue, [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] reveals herself alongside [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] and [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/admiral-nelson|Admiral Nelson]]. She is hiding from a continuing threat to her life. She believes Arthur could not have orchestrated the wider unrest alone and suspects involvement beyond even the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]. She commissions the party to investigate, giving this work priority over the Necropolis: her death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir despite his trial.

Agatha recommends beginning with [[npcs/harrow/count-drago-venn|Count Drago Venn]], whom she considers selfish but unlikely to be plotting her overthrow. She urges a consistent cover story, and the Bonebreakers retain their claim to be pilgrims. In Harrow's wealthy but disorderly upper city, they hire the justice-minded dwarf [[npcs/harrow/drakford|Drakford]] as muscle and seek information from [[npcs/harrow/lord-elron|Lord Elron]]. His blatant cheating at dice turns out to be a way of selling information. Their invented tale of rival pilgrims fails when Elron exposes it over breakfast. They admit they want the Count, and Elron tells them Venn is already waiting at the docks.

Back at the Anchor & Chain, the Bonebreakers pose as Arthur's sympathisers. Venn directs them to [[npcs/kingsport/brother-sedge|Brother Sedge]], a monk who formerly worked in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]] in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]]. His only price is secrecy about the referral. The party sails for three days along the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/salt-coast|Salt Coast]] to the wealthy city-state, where two gleaming palaces overlook a harbour crowded with domestic ships.

The Queen's Guard requires registration and the surrender of weapons. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] registers as a pilgrim and persuades Captain Sir Aldric Hale to let Drakford remain armed as his bodyguard. Hale directs them to Sedge, who cautiously denies political involvement but offers access to Arthur's supporters. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess]] uses Glamour to impersonate [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]] and demands entry to the palace to help Arthur's defence. Sedge accepts the deception, escorts them there, and unlocks the door. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] secretly takes the palace key before he leaves. The Bonebreakers enter Arthur's Palace to investigate his supporters under a false claim of loyalty.

The search of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]] in [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]] points toward [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of Velluria. Hugh Malory identifies her removed portrait and reports that she will be hosting a masquerade in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]], about a day from Kingsport. Arthur's correspondence reveals jealousy of Agatha and increasingly cautious political language; a hidden, incomplete letter anticipates Agatha's disgrace and a duke fit to be king, though its author and recipient remain unknown. Archives record increasingly frequent trips to Velluria and a payment for discretion in Harrow. Love letters and Lirina's warning, “Do nothing rash. Commit nothing to paper,” reinforce the personal connection without proving her political role.

Tess pockets the hidden letter before [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] arrives, claiming this is his first palace inspection. He seems disappointed with the other letters she hands over, but takes them. Elspeth reveals his repeated visits after Steve uses his sword to cast Charm Person on her to conceal the party's interference with a royal seal. The Bonebreakers take financial records and Agatha's letters, then leave with stolen valuables. Steve accuses Corvin of the jewellery theft, follows and captures him near the Royal Chamberlain's building, and Micaelis plants stolen jewels on him. Supported by the still-charmed Elspeth, the false accusation secures Corvin's arrest and the guards' gratitude. The guards take the party back to the dockside guardhouse to collect its weapons. When the guard cannot find his key, the Bonebreakers drop the stolen key behind him and say he dropped it. He shrugs and returns the weapons, which they may keep until leaving the following morning. The guards then pay for an inn stay.

Lirina's masquerade is the next lead, but the Bonebreakers have not visited Velur or reported back to Agatha. Corvin's lies, suspicious palace activity, and flight from the Royal Chamberlain's building lead the party to suspect he is not who he claims to be. His actual identity and purpose, the fate of the letters he took, and the meaning of a burned message reading “Smith already knows” remain unresolved. Rumours that Lirina abandoned Arthur and that the Queen intends to close his palace are unconfirmed.

Valedictus and his phylacteries remain an unfinished threat, but Agatha's investigation now takes priority over a return to the Necropolis.

And the Bonebreakers are no longer simply adventurers stumbling into danger. They have become wealthy, politically connected, and powerful enough that their own ambitions may now shape the fate of Agria.
