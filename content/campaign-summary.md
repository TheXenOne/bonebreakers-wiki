The **Bonebreakers** began as a band of adventurers hired to clear undead from a Barrow near Barrow Spa. What started as a dangerous local job quickly became a pattern: delve into forgotten ruins, survive catastrophic losses, uncover powerful relics, and emerge richer and more infamous than before.

Their early exploits saw them slay the so-called **Grease Dragon**, plunder the trapped laboratories of a long-dead jeweller-magician, and follow a hidden map across the sea to an island where healing had become something monstrous. There they destroyed a corrupted cult, freed its prisoners, and rescued **Princess Agatha**, heir to the Agrial throne.

That rescue changed everything. Agatha recruited the Bonebreakers as covert agents of the crown, sending them against the **Church of the Crimson Flame**, whose undead bishop presided over the Deathless Plague. After destroying him and ending the church’s reign of terror, the party inherited its cathedral and transformed it into **the Mercy**, gaining land, influence, and a permanent base of power.

Their adventures grew stranger and more dangerous. They accidentally tore open a breach to the Astral Plane while stopping a magical catastrophe in Ikiria, then destroyed an otherworldly vampire domain by finding and crushing the enormous heart that sustained its king. Soon afterward, their companion **Tobold Took** was abducted by a giant hawk, drawing them west into a much larger conflict.

The search for Tobold uncovered a coordinated campaign of kidnapping, sabotage, poisonings, monster armies, and political manipulation orchestrated by the **Grey Syndicate**. The Bonebreakers disrupted a hobgoblin invasion and rescued the stolen children, but Tobold was already dead. Worse, they discovered that the conspiracy reached into the royal family itself: **Prince Arthur** was working with the Syndicate in hopes of taking Agatha’s throne.

At the same time, the party found an impossible staircase beneath the Mercy leading through a vast Abyss and into the **Imperial Necropolis**, the remains of the civilisation that ruled Westmarsh two thousand years ago. Its undead inhabitants still carry out the routines of a dead empire, while its ancient magic connects places separated by enormous distances.

As plague and undead outbreaks spread through Ikiria, the Bonebreakers learned that the Necropolis is tied to **High Priest Valedictus**, an ancient necromancer who once sought dominion across the continent. Valedictus possesses multiple phylacteries, and the party may already have unknowingly released one of them.

Meanwhile, the surviving hobgoblin commander **Kargaz the Disciplined** rebuilt the shattered invasion force and laid siege to Westfort. The Bonebreakers eventually broke the siege, destroyed his army, and killed Kargaz—but the larger struggle remains unresolved.

The Grey Syndicate still operates in the shadows. Valedictus remains somewhere within the Necropolis. One of his phylacteries is missing.

And the Bonebreakers are no longer simply adventurers stumbling into danger. They have become wealthy, politically connected, and powerful enough that their own ambitions may now shape the fate of Agria.
