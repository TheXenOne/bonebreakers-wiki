See [[quests/index|Campaign Threads]] for active, completed, and retired quests.

The Bonebreakers have jailed [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]], identified as Ash and the Grey Khan, in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. They inspect the prison, warn of a likely breakout, and forbid contact with the imprisoned [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]]. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] assures them of its security. The leadership mystery in the [[quests/major/active/grey-syndicate-conspiracy|Grey Syndicate conspiracy]] is resolved, but Lirina says her plans can continue without her and the network has not been dismantled.

The journey towards [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] leads into the [[locations/world/agria/grey-syndicate-caverns|Grey Syndicate Caverns]], where the party finds page 19 of another [[items/poetry-cipher-book|poetry cipher book]]. It supplies the key to Lirina's matching letter, which has not yet been decoded. Seized correspondence and payment records concern nobles, merchants, and smugglers. Maps suggest a Northlands unification scheme without explaining it. The cave defenders remain undefeated, valuable contraband remains underground, and the outside orcs' allegiance and connection to the earlier hostage ambush remain unknown. The abbey is still unreached.

[[quests/major/active/princess-agathas-secret-patronage|Agatha's commission]] remains active. The Bonebreakers have firmly chosen her over Lirina, but have not delivered their findings. Lirina knows the party can contact the princess without knowing her hiding place. The new records and cipher page may strengthen the case against Lirina, but the Watch's response to the escape, Miranda's letter, and Corvin's true role remain unresolved.

The Bonebreakers' rejection of Lirina's alliance leaves the [[quests/major/active/necropolis-beneath-the-mercy|Necropolis]] and [[quests/major/active/valedictus-and-his-phylacteries|Valedictus]] as deferred threats. His phylacteries and the Stylus remain unsecured. Barny's open reanimation of a wyvern increases Arden's suspicions, without a confrontation.

Morel also identifies Fos Imeras as an ancient gnomish shrine and reports an island headquarters with a laboratory described in a decoded book. The island's identity and precise connection to Fos Imeras are **Needs review**; the [[quests/major/completed/fos-imeras-and-the-golden-asclepeion|original rescue expedition]] remains complete.

The party has promised the two surviving otyughs in the cave stronghold that it will feed the cave orcs to them in exchange for passage. That bargain and the outside orcs' request for help against dark orcs remain unfinished. The lethal diseases contracted by Akal and Michaelis are resolved: Ulrich cures Akal, and Arden cures Michaelis.
