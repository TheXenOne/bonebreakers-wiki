The Bonebreakers have escaped Velur and hold [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]] at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]. ESP identifies her as Ash, and her answers link Ash to the Grey Khan, resolving the leadership mystery in the [[quests/major/active/grey-syndicate-conspiracy|Grey Syndicate conspiracy]]. She says her plans can continue without her, so the network remains an active concern.

Lirina genuinely wants [[quests/major/active/valedictus-and-his-phylacteries|Valedictus]] destroyed and offers an alliance against the [[quests/major/active/necropolis-beneath-the-mercy|Necropolis's evil]]. The party remains divided and has agreed to no contract. His phylacteries and the Stylus remain unsecured, and exploration has not resumed.

[[quests/major/active/princess-agathas-secret-patronage|Agatha's commission]] remains active: the party has not reported back, and Lirina now knows it can contact the princess without knowing her location. The Watch's response to the escape, Miranda's letter, and the undeciphered correspondence remain unresolved. Corvin is still in custody on the party's planted evidence.
