The Bonebreakers have found Agatha and Smith alive and are pursuing [[quests/major/active/princess-agathas-secret-patronage|Agatha's covert commission]]. Barny's telescope reveals another noble's nightmare of Audrey's murder, but no attack or plot is established. The party has yet to report back to Agatha. Corvin remains in custody on the party's planted evidence; the Necropolis investigation is temporarily deprioritised.
