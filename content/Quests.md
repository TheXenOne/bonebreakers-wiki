The Bonebreakers have jailed [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], identified as Ash and the Grey Khan, in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. They inspect the prison, warn of a likely breakout, and forbid contact with the imprisoned [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]]. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] assures them of its security. The leadership mystery in the [[quests/major/active/grey-syndicate-conspiracy|Grey Syndicate conspiracy]] is resolved, but Lirina says her plans can continue without her and the network has not been dismantled.

[[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Morel]] traces the poetry book used as Lirina's cipher to monastic copying and identifies [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]]. The Bonebreakers are travelling there to seek another copy and the missing key. Their concealed forest route reveals recent orc traffic and traps, but the orcs' allegiance and any connection to the earlier hostage ambush remain **Needs review**. The abbey is unreached and the correspondence remains undeciphered.

[[quests/major/active/princess-agathas-secret-patronage|Agatha's commission]] remains active. The Bonebreakers have firmly chosen her over Lirina, but have not delivered their findings. Lirina knows the party can contact the princess without knowing her hiding place. The Watch's response to the escape, Miranda's letter, and Corvin's true role remain unresolved.

The Bonebreakers' rejection of Lirina's alliance leaves the [[quests/major/active/necropolis-beneath-the-mercy|Necropolis]] and [[quests/major/active/valedictus-and-his-phylacteries|Valedictus]] as deferred threats. His phylacteries and the Stylus remain unsecured. Barny's open reanimation of a wyvern increases Arden's suspicions, without a confrontation.

Morel also identifies Fos Imeras as an ancient gnomish shrine and reports an island headquarters with a laboratory described in a decoded book. The island's identity and precise connection to Fos Imeras are **Needs review**; the [[quests/major/completed/fos-imeras-and-the-golden-asclepeion|original rescue expedition]] remains complete.
