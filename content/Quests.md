The Bonebreakers are travelling towards [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] with [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]] captive, intending to leave her there and attempt contact with Agatha. ESP identifies her as Ash, and her answers link Ash to the Grey Khan, resolving the leadership mystery in the [[quests/major/active/grey-syndicate-conspiracy|Grey Syndicate conspiracy]]. She says her plans can continue without her, so the network remains an active concern.

Ninja orcs demand her release in exchange for a farming couple's daughter. Akal sets the carriages alight before the party agrees on a response; the girl is killed before Rose can rescue her. Most attackers die and two escape. Their allegiance is **Needs review**; Lirina remains captive.

The Bonebreakers have firmly chosen to support [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] and rejected Lirina's alliance against the [[quests/major/active/necropolis-beneath-the-mercy|Necropolis's evil]] because they cannot trust her. Lirina genuinely wants [[quests/major/active/valedictus-and-his-phylacteries|Valedictus]] destroyed, but remains their prisoner. His phylacteries and the Stylus remain unsecured, and exploration has not resumed.

[[quests/major/active/princess-agathas-secret-patronage|Agatha's commission]] remains active: the party has not reported back, and Lirina now knows it can contact the princess without knowing her location. The Watch's response to the escape, Miranda's letter, and the undeciphered correspondence remain unresolved. Corvin is still in custody on the party's planted evidence.
