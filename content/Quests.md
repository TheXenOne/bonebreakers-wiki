The Bonebreakers have found Agatha and Smith alive and are pursuing [[quests/major/active/princess-agathas-secret-patronage|Agatha's covert commission]]. At the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] in Velur they have captured [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]] during an assassination scare at her masquerade, and Rose is impersonating her. Lirina's thoughts confirm that she wants at least the ducal seat in Arthur's absence, and her rooms hold an assassin's armoury and the encoded letters of a spy network. She knows who the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]] is, and one of her messages is meant to warn the Khan about the party, tying her to the [[quests/major/active/grey-syndicate-conspiracy|Grey Syndicate conspiracy]]. A letter stolen from [[npcs/velur/lady-miranda|Lady Miranda]] urges her to meet Wendell Prye before he gives evidence. The party has yet to report back to Agatha. Corvin remains in custody on the party's planted evidence; the Necropolis investigation is temporarily deprioritised.
