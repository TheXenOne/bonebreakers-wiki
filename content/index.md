## Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales

After the fatal hostage ambush, [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] tries to help the grieving farmer and his wife by offering them money, following [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]]'s example of generosity towards followers of [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenus]]. They are offended: both are subscribers to the faith and feel betrayed because Belenus did not protect their daughter. Arden offers to take them and her body to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] for resurrection, explaining that death need not be the end. The parents refuse, wanting their daughter's body returned rather than accepting that magic. They agree to Steve's offer to pay for the funeral, but want nothing more to do with Belenus. Arden cancels their subscription.

The Bonebreakers continue to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] and take [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]] into the dungeons. [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] berates her over the hostage's death while the party inspects the prison carefully. The Bonebreakers warn the guards that a breakout attempt is likely. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] assures them that this is the safest place available, and they insist that the countess must never be allowed to speak with [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]], who is also imprisoned in the city. Lirina, exposed as Ash and the Grey Khan, is left in custody; her network's plans may still continue without her.

At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]], [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]] reports on the research into [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]] that the Bonebreakers commissioned long ago. He identifies it as an ancient gnomish shrine and has decoded a book they previously brought him. The book describes an ancient gnomish headquarters on an island at sea, containing some kind of laboratory. Its location and precise connection to Fos Imeras remain unclear.

The party also asks Morel about the half-orc poet whose work serves as the cipher for Lirina's spy correspondence. He has never heard of the poet, although he confirms the book was published in Agria and praises its writing. Investigating further, he establishes that monks copied it and identifies [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]], where literary publishing goes alongside brewing and other monastic produce. The Bonebreakers decide to seek another copy there, hoping it will provide the missing key to the letters. The correspondence remains undeciphered, and their findings have not yet reached [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]].

Hoping to avoid the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]'s orcs, the party chooses a concealed route through unfamiliar country. They head directly north from Ikiria into mountainous highlands and dense forest. [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm|Grainholm]] lies beyond the woods, and the Thorngate River continues north towards the sea. Shortly before entering the forest, they encounter jackalweres. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/akal-dreek|Akal]] enlarges himself, Arden enlarges his wolf, and [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] attacks with Fireball. The Bonebreakers win the fight, but Akal's wolf is killed.

After a night in the thick, gloomy deciduous woods, a huge winged creature approaches above the trees. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] takes to the air, Arden prepares Steve with Bran's Heroic Aura, and [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] blesses the party. The creature emerges as a wyvern. Arden's spell forces it to attack the waiting Steve, and it fails to strike him. Barny blinds it, the Bonebreakers bring it down, and Steve delivers the killing blow.

Barny then raises the dead wyvern openly in front of the party. Arden sees the strange magic and becomes extremely suspicious, but his trust in Barny keeps him from confronting him immediately. The Bonebreakers continue with the raised creature accompanying them.

Farther on, they find a giant boar that appears to have been dead for about a day, with wounds suggesting it was killed by swords. Arden uses Speak with Plants to question a nearby tree, which he names Trevor and befriends. Trevor says orcs fought the boar, damaged one of his branches, and hurriedly tried to hide the evidence. Arden removes the damaged branch at the tree's request and leaves the Barrow's glass eyes in a nook in its trunk, hoping to return someday. The party now has evidence of recent orc activity along the route it chose to avoid them, although these orcs' allegiance is unknown.

Steve trips over a cord trap and traces it to a long black piece of wood, without any obvious effect. Before sleeping at their next camp, Arden casts Detect Evil on the raised wyvern and confirms that it is undead. His suspicions about Barny grow considerably, but the matter remains unspoken.

Travelling northeast the next day, the party meets seven dire wolves. Ulrich speaks with them and explains that the Bonebreakers are passing through along the orcs' route. The wolves reluctantly grant a truce if the party leaves immediately. Beyond them, Steve spots a trail but does not want to follow it. Arden questions another tree, which rejects his attempt to name it Tybalt but confirms that many orcs use the trail and have passed recently. Arden heads up it and triggers a trap: something falls onto him and injures him. Thorngate Abbey remains unreached, the cipher key is still missing, and the trapped route lies ahead.

## Conspiracies

The [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] is a dispersed network behind sabotage, kidnappings, attacks on the Mercy, and support for the invasions of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. Kargaz confirms that it backed his war and promised him the town. Captured orcs tie it to the occupation of the Mercy and the kidnapping of its priests, although the purpose of their orders to delay the Bonebreakers remains unclear.

The investigation for [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]] has exposed its leader. Arthur's letters and visits led the party to [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of Velluria, whose private rooms concealed an assassin's equipment and a spy network's coded correspondence. Captured at her masquerade and taken to the Mercy, she is identified through ESP as [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]; her linked answers convince the party that Ash is the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]]. She mentally separates those identities, accounting for the earlier apparent denial.

Lirina says nobody directs her and that her plans can continue without her. She admits using Arthur, justifies the invasion as a way to put her own army in Westfort, and wants [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] destroyed. Her hatred gives substance to Lord Varrick's suggestion that the Syndicate might oppose the necromancer, but leaves the full scope of its plans unknown. The Bonebreakers reject cooperation with her because they cannot trust her and firmly side with Agatha. They know her responsibility for killing a companion, kidnapping children, arson, and invasions; her claims to serve Agria are her justification for those crimes.

Agatha remains hidden in Harrow, fearing for her life despite Arthur's arrest. With Queen Audrey reportedly unwell, Agatha's death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir. Lirina knows the party has contact with Agatha, but Micaelis has withheld her location. After a hostage ambush leaves a farming couple's daughter dead and two attackers at large, the Bonebreakers jail Lirina in Ikiria. They warn the guards of a likely breakout and forbid her from speaking with Arthur. Smith assures them of the prison's security; no report to Agatha has yet been delivered.

Morel traces the poetry cipher to monastic copying and identifies [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] as a lead. The Bonebreakers travel north from Ikiria to seek another copy and the missing key, taking a concealed forest route to avoid Syndicate orcs. Trees describe recent orc movements, but those orcs' allegiance remains unknown.

Other clues remain open: Lirina's undeciphered letters; the unknown writer urging [[npcs/velur/lady-miranda|Lady Miranda]] to meet Wendell before he gives evidence; a noble's unexplained nightmare of Audrey's murder; the burned message “Smith already knows”; and the payment for discretion in Harrow. [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] remains in custody on evidence the party planted, without his true role established.

## Ongoing threat: The Necropolis and Valedictus

After ending the immediate crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and descend through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], whose numbered arches open into different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] rises to a storm-lashed gatehouse overlooking [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and the Necropolis's central dome, confirming an exterior entrance on the western frontier.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] proves to be a residential district where zombie labourers have continued rebuilding for more than two thousand years. A work ledger warns that an anomaly must not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown person, title, or authority. The Bonebreakers kill the district's ruler, [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], resolving their immediate objective in Arch V.

When the party returns above, it finds orcs occupying the Mercy under orders to delay the Bonebreakers. The cathedral is retaken, but the following resupply journey costs [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] his life when he sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape. The Bonebreakers fortify the Mercy and hold a wake before descending again.

The Bonebreakers begin exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] and its flooded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but submerged routes prevent them from pressing farther. After breaking the siege of Westfort, they obtain eleven Potions of Water Breathing and can now continue the flooded delve.

The search for water-breathing potions takes the party west through Westfort and across Westmarsh. After sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] and losing Luthien, they continue northwest in search of another Necropolis entrance and discover the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its vault contains a locked iron chest beneath soul-binding sigils, and a magical vision shows a soul held in a codex. An imperial command quarter beyond the vault proves that the temple is a concealed Necropolis entrance.

There the Bonebreakers meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]], an undead imperial officer who says priests once conducted evil necromantic experiments in the vault. He refuses to speak the culprit's name, instead writing “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” The party recognises him as the author of a treatise found in Arch VII, while Varro identifies him as the high priest beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], warns that he “sees all,” and says [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] was sealed when the empire ended.

Secret passages lead into another district containing the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] and, by another route, [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]]. A document says that one passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders, confirming that the unknown authority extends beyond Arch V.

Its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], describes Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who sought dominion across the continent. He reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], though its nature and purpose remain unknown. The Bonebreakers now believe that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve recovered in Arch VII and later traded away is probably one phylactery; Barny's telescope shows it being studied by an unidentified lizardfolk shaman. The soul-bound chest in the Imperial Hill vault may hold another, but this is only a suspicion.

With Varro and Varrick, the Bonebreakers have now met two friendly undead within the Necropolis. Their willingness to talk and offer help has given the party—and its clerics in particular—reason to think more carefully about the undead they encounter.

On the way back, Barny permanently takes control of a wight and six skeletons from a much larger undead drill yard. The route reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], establishing another connection within the complex. The Necropolis remains active and unfinished: Valedictus has not been confronted, his phylacteries and the Stylus have not been secured, Arch IV remains partly explored, and the Tribune is still unknown. The party is equipped to return below, but its investigation for Agatha takes priority. The Bonebreakers have rejected Lirina's offer of cooperation against Valedictus because they cannot trust her, and firmly support Agatha. Lirina is jailed in Ikiria, and the party is pursuing the cipher lead towards Thorngate Abbey; exploration has not resumed.
