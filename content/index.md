## Session 50 - Coins from a Dead Empire

[[sessions/session-050|Session 50 - Coins from a Dead Empire]] covers the aftermath of the corpse-pit battle in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]]. The Bonebreakers destroy the last undead, but not before a wight drains [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] badly enough to shake him. The party recovers [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]'s notebook, strips ancient imperial armour from the dead, and learns from [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] that the armour carries an evil aura. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden Sable]]'s investigation also confirms that fresh necrotic magic was recently worked in the graves, even though the cemetery itself is not inherently evil.

The search through the southern Plats shows how badly the district is emptying out. [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/reefers-row|Reefer's Row]] and [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/salting-house|the Salting House]] are abandoned, the docks hold deserted vessels such as the *Drowned Pig*, and a friendly elf aboard the *Saint of Tides* knows of Maren's theories and believes someone is deliberately upsetting Ikiria's balance. Meanwhile, fear has turned political and dangerous: a mob, encouraged by inquisitors, now blames the Bonebreakers and other necromancers for the city's dead, while nearby citizens openly complain that [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] has vanished or fled.

The biggest advance comes at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]]. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] confirms that the water is poisoned, the tightened quarantine prevents the party from leaving freely, and the Bonebreakers find [[npcs/ikiria/rufus|Rufus]], a traumatised guard wracked with guilt after accompanying inquisitor burnings. Ulrich descends into the well, purifies it, and finds 400 pre-imperial Westmarsh gold pieces at the bottom, strongly suggesting that both the poison and the ancient evidence there were planted deliberately. The session ends with the Bonebreakers having solved at least part of the plague problem, though it remains unclear whether the wider outbreak has fully lifted, and with [[npcs/ikiria/jim|Jim]] returning permission for them to see [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]].

## The Rot of Agria

After [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] is handed over in Ikiria, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] sends the Bonebreakers down into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] to speak with [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]. The city is under quarantine, the lower districts are burned and overcrowded, and Vaessa needs help because the number of bodies in the corpse pits no longer matches the records. Maren's work points the party toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]] and a likely first wave of poisoning, followed by sickness spreading through crowding and broken civic systems.

On the way, the Bonebreakers see how fear has been turned against magic-users. Civilian inquisitors have burned [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|the Temple of Merciful Life]], blocked help, and helped make the streets hostile. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] curing a sick child shows that at least part of the crisis can be treated as disease, even as the inquisitors make that kind of aid harder to deliver. By the time the party reaches Maren and the corpse pits, the problem has widened from illness into missing dead, public panic, and signs of deliberate interference.

The corpse-pit investigation becomes a battle when undead rise from the graves. The Bonebreakers fight zombies, ghouls, wights, mummies, and wraiths, and Maren dies after confirming that the undead were conjured and do not belong there. In the aftermath, the party recovers her notebook, finds ancient imperial armour on the dead, and confirms that fresh necrotic magic was recently worked in the graves. The older markings and Westmarsh material suggest a possible link to the buried past below [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], though there is not yet concrete proof.

In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], the party confirms the Public Well is poisoned, purifies it, and discovers 400 pre-imperial Westmarsh coins planted at the bottom, making deliberate staging the clearest explanation. The immediate source of the poisoned water has been dealt with, and the plague may have been lifted or partly lifted as a result. What remains unresolved is whether the sickness, the missing bodies, the undead rising, and the planted ancient evidence are parts of one operation or overlapping crises. The Bonebreakers are still inside the quarantine, public trust is fragile, and their next step is to speak with Vaessa about what can be proven.

## Last arc: The Traitor Prince

The Traitor Prince arc begins when the party encounters [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] on the road back toward Ikiria in [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] and discovers that he is secretly working with the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] against [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]]. What first looks like a suspicious noble mission quickly becomes a succession crisis. Arthur admits he is trying to meet the Grey Khan, the party captures his secretary and documentary evidence, and later sessions reveal that Ash and the wider Syndicate network are entangled in the same plot. The Bonebreakers spend the arc trying to turn rumour into proof while also surviving Westmarsh pressure from kidnappings, raids, and Syndicate-controlled intermediaries.

The key developments are the secretary's ledger, Ash's offer to buy the secretary back, and the failed exchange plan that becomes the raid on the decoy Spider Market cell in [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]]. That raid gives the party cash, prisoners, and more testimony, but more importantly it keeps Arthur's side from recovering evidence or silencing witnesses. With [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]] finally willing to speak, the Bonebreakers bring Arthur, the records, and the witness to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. The arc ends when [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] accepts the case at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/east-agria-company-headquarters|East Agria Company Headquarters]] and orders Arthur arrested, leaving the larger Grey Syndicate threat unresolved but bringing the prince's betrayal into the open.
