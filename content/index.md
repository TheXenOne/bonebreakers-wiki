Welcome to the campaign wiki for **The Bonebreakers**.

## Session 60 - The Secret We Sold

[[sessions/session-060|Session 60 - The Secret We Sold]] begins with the Bonebreakers still trapped in the command-quarter armoury. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]], a thief who followed them inside, takes the document from the chest Frank opened and reads that [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]] ordered a secret passage sealed. An arrow scratched near the gas trap points down. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/wistor|Wistor]], a dragonborn who had approached invisibly, buys the document from Arden for fifteen gold pieces. He then turns the mannequin that once held the looted sword and opens a secret door, while Ulrich detects a hollow space beneath the floor.

Barny pries up a loose flagstone, allowing the party to descend into a hidden pay room containing unpaid wages and military valuables worth 1,980 gold pieces in total. The Bonebreakers then return to Wistor's passage. A strong chemical smell makes them cautious: Arden tests the ladder with a torch, extinguishes it when the vapour may be flammable, and uses one of Ulrich's Continual Light stones instead. The party climbs down, follows the passage, then climbs a second ladder. Arden uses a crowbar to slide aside the stone slab blocking the top.

The slab opens beneath a low, resin-scented embalming workshop filled with channelled stone tables, sealed jars, iron instruments, funerary carvings, and a basin of adhesive black resin. Opening one jar releases gas that nearly incapacitates the party, while a second broken jar paralyses Arden and Selvar until Ulrich cures Arden and Arden cures Selvar. Experiments with torches show that the carved mourners' eyes follow the nearest flame while other lights dim. A rust monster then enters the room, destroys Arden's magical mace, and eats Rose's spear before Ulrich's Sticks to Snakes helps the party kill it.

Continuing north brings the Bonebreakers to [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]], where twelve sarcophagi surround a raised black-marble coffin. Ulrich detects no evil intent, and Arden persuades the undead occupant to exchange information rather than fight. [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]] is devastated to learn that plague destroyed Westmarsh civilisation and erased his once-powerful house from memory. In return, he suggests the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] may seek [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus's]] necromantic power or may want him destroyed. He describes the high priest as an expansionist who ignored opposition, rejected Varrick's petitions three times, and sought dominion across the continent. Varrick warns that undead activity will worsen while Valedictus remains in power, reveals that he has three phylacteries, and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]]. The tomb carvings confirm the three rejected petitions.

The Bonebreakers connect this warning to [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] found among severed restraints in Arch VII and later traded away by Steve. They suspect it is one of Valedictus's phylacteries and that another may lie inside the three-locked, soul-bound chest at the Imperial Hill entrance. They then backtrack to the armoury, where Nila cannot pick the locked gate. Ulrich casts Silence while the party dismantles it with hammers, spikes, and crowbars.

The noise is suppressed, but an incorporeal spirit still wanders past. Its supernatural terror ages Nila and Rose by ten years and sends them fleeing, while its claws reach through the gate and drain four points of Wistor's Constitution. Neither cleric can turn it, and the party cannot harm it. Ulrich prevents a likely disaster by casting Continual Light directly upon the spirit and permanently blinding it. Barny's [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] shows the spirit dreaming of the glowing skull in lizardfolk hands, strengthening the phylactery theory. The party waits until the blinded spirit wanders away, then finishes dismantling the gate.

Beyond lies a vast drill yard where a wight and three sentries oversee 300 skeletons repeating the same exercises. The [[items/imperial-command-quarter-map|command-quarter map]] identifies the route as District One, Arch One. Barny drinks a Potion of Control Undead and permanently commands the wight and six skeletons while the party crosses the long bridge above. The far gallery leads through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]] into the Nexus. Wistor opens the spiked Mercy trapdoor with Knock, the party closes and spikes it again behind them, and the Bonebreakers return home. A waiting priest begins preparations for [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien's]] funeral.

## Current arc: The Necropolis Reopened

With the immediate danger in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] dealt with, the Bonebreakers return to the deeper problem beneath [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]: [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. They are descending again to purge more undead, seek treasure, and understand how the impossible buried complex connects to [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], ancient imperial power, and the older mysteries already found below.

Earlier delves establish that the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], the domed chamber of white archways beneath the Mercy, is not just an isolated dungeon hub. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] leads upward to a storm-lashed outer gatehouse and wall walk patrolled by skeleton archers. From there, the party can see Westmarsh beyond the walls and the Necropolis's central dome within, strongly suggesting that Arch VI is the western exterior entrance described by earlier explorers.

The deeper pattern is now clearer: the Necropolis is not only a tomb or treasure vault, but a preserved civic ruin with districts, labour systems, old administration, markets, shrines, schools, and undead routines that have continued long after ordinary life ended. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] shows this through its restoration works, zombie labourers, [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]], the [[items/bloated-one-work-ledger|work ledger]], and [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], while [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] now shows a flooded market district whose dead merchants still trade and whose counting house preserves a map of the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]].

The Bloated One's sash names it Senior Administrator of Residential District V, suggesting that the numbered arches may correspond to administrative districts. The Arch IV map now strengthens that pattern by naming another functional district and marking routes, hazards, and submerged areas rather than a simple dungeon corridor.

The Mercy itself remains under pressure. In another raid on the cathedral, Grey Syndicate-linked orcs seize the site and capture its inhabitants while the Bonebreakers are below. After the party retakes the Mercy, the surviving orcs say they were ordered to delay the Bonebreakers, but whether that delay was meant to keep them away from the Necropolis, [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], or some other objective remains unresolved. The renewed siege of Westfort is now active, though its exact relationship to the Syndicate is unproven.

The road back to the Mercy also costs the party dearly. On the road from Barrow Spa, Arden, Micaelis, Fred, Paul, and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] are attacked by twelve mantid-like warriors too fast to outrun. After Fred and Paul fall, Bran sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape with their bodies. The Bonebreakers return to the Mercy without Bran, fortify the cathedral, hold a wake in his honour, and descend again with his sacrifice fresh in memory.

The latest Arch IV exploration turns the dead market from a strange trading hall into a flooded and actively hostile district. What begins with the Bonebreakers testing zombie merchants and impossible payments becomes a fight for air when pool serpents erupt from the water, shrug off sharp weapons, and drag Fred and Quak under until healing and blunt-force attacks pull them back from drowning. The counting house then reveals the [[items/party-inventory/arch-iv-sunken-market-map|Sunken Market map]], but Steve's noisy hammering at the locked strongboxes brings three more dangerous snake-men into the chamber. The party survives them, gains treasure and the map, and now knows about the lower floor, fishermen's quarters, flooded routes, a dangerous after-dark channel, something in the sump, and [[factions/religions-and-cults/dark-druids-of-the-deep-one|dark druids]] who worship the Deep One. Arch IV is no longer just a market of harmless dead merchants; it is a drowned district with predators, cultists, and warnings that point deeper.

To explore those submerged routes, the Bonebreakers head for [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] to buy water-breathing potions. Nine gryphons ambush them on the road and kill every horse; the party kills six, captures two for a possible breeding and training plan, and returns to Barrow Spa. One gryphon escapes with Barny's [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]] still lodged in it. After commissioning secure harnesses, the party tries again on foot and reaches Westfort.

The town is visibly battered by the [[quests/major/active/renewed-siege-of-westfort|renewed siege]], with broken palisades, a no-man's-land bridge, and goblin raids stripping incoming food. [[npcs/west-fort/erasmus-vale|Erasmus Vale]] agrees to make six water-breathing potions, but hobgoblin envoys offer the town a one-year truce in exchange for surrendering the Bonebreakers. The party refuses and keeps the demand secret. That night, they ambush the food raiders and spare [[npcs/westmarsh/gobbo|Gobbo]], who leads them along the southern mountain route used against Westfort. The Bonebreakers detonate two [[items/party-inventory/gnomish-power-cores|gnomish power cores]], causing a landslide that buries the path.

At the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]], Luthien is paralysed, killed, and partly devoured by a Krell, leaving too little of her body for Raise Dead. The survivors sabotage the fort and continue toward the Necropolis, instead discovering the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its soul-bound vault opens into an imperial command quarter where [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] confirms the ruin is a concealed Necropolis entrance, identifies [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] as the high priest beyond Arch III, and reveals that Arch VIII was sealed when the empire ended. The party also discovers the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]].

Nila and Wistor join the party during its escape from the trapped armoury. Beyond a hidden workshop, the Bonebreakers enter a family tomb and negotiate with its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], a noble of a once-powerful imperial house. Varrick says the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] may be seeking [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus's]] necromantic power—or trying to destroy him—and reveals that the expansionist high priest has three phylacteries. This leads the Bonebreakers to suspect that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve traded away is one of them. A powerful incorporeal spirit ages Nila and Rose and drains Wistor's Constitution before Ulrich blinds it. Barny later gains control of a wight and six skeletons, and the party reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], confirming the command quarter as District One. Wistor opens the spiked Mercy trapdoor with Knock, allowing the Bonebreakers to return home for preparations for Luthien's funeral.

## Earlier arc: The Rot of Agria

After [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] is handed over in Ikiria, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] sends the Bonebreakers down into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] to speak with [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]. The city is under quarantine, the lower districts are burned and overcrowded, and Vaessa needs help because the number of bodies in the corpse pits no longer matches the records. Maren's work points the party toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]], and the party eventually confirms the crisis began with poison before later disease spread through crowding and broken civic systems.

The investigation becomes more dangerous when undead rise from the corpse pit and cemetery. The Bonebreakers destroy the undead, Maren dies after confirming the dead were conjured and did not belong there, and Arden later confirms that the necromantic energies are gone from the pit and cemetery after the battle. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] purifies the Public Well, ending the immediate poisoned-water danger, while the party's discovery of planted pre-imperial Westmarsh coins shows that someone tried to stage or frame part of the crisis.

The arc ends with the immediate danger over but consequences still moving. The quarantine has not yet been lifted, and the people responsible for the poison, planted coinage, missing bodies, and public panic remain unidentified. Still, the well is purified, the undead incursion has been purged, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa]] accepts testimony against the [[factions/other/civilian-inquisition|civilian inquisitors]], and the Bonebreakers are free to turn back toward the Necropolis.
