## Session 71 - The Nineteenth Page

The forest trap drops rocks onto [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]], and orcs appear in the trees with bows trained on the Bonebreakers. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] and [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/akal-dreek|Akal]] speak to them in Chaotic. Steve convinces them that he is an orc raised by humans. The orcs offer safe passage if the party helps them against dark orcs. Akal deliberately mistranslates the offer, telling the others that the orcs are frightened and are offering them somewhere to sleep.

Their leader shrugs off Arden's attempt to intimidate him and leads the party into a ravine, where a curtain of moss hides a [[locations/world/agria/grey-syndicate-caverns|cave entrance]]. Akal scouts ahead and finds an abandoned camp and a map of the caverns. When he suggests clearing the caves before resting, Arden grows uneasy about the change of plan and stays at the rear.

A wooden portcullis slams down behind the party in a narrow corridor, and arrows rain through murder holes overhead. Steve, Arden, and [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] are badly wounded. Arden sets the barrier alight, and the Bonebreakers flee to a ledge overlooking a deep cavern. Akal rigs a rope for the descent, then shrinks himself to examine one of the holes cut into the cavern walls. Drawing on his duergar knowledge, he identifies an acoustic system that could let someone overhear the party from another chamber.

Three shapes on the cavern floor, mistaken for refuse, rise as enormous tentacled otyughs. A voice in the party's minds accuses them of desecrating the creatures' home. One bites [[characters/active-players/john/characters/michaelis|Michaelis]], infecting him with a disease that will kill him within six days unless cured. Arden nearly dies and retreats to the ledge. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] blinds one creature with Light, and Steve lassoes Michaelis to pull him to safety. Akal contracts the disease during the retreat, and Ulrich barely survives.

The party attacks from the ledge until [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] kills one otyugh. The two survivors surrender and trade information for being left alone. They say ten orcs live in the caves and that barracks lie beyond either exit. Badly hurt, the Bonebreakers still have to cross the arrow corridor to reach the entrance camp.

Akal admits that he lied about the outside orcs being frightened, prompting Arden's suspicion. The Bonebreakers form a shield wall and sprint through the arrows. Nila is badly wounded, Ulrich withstands a barrage, and Steve smashes through the burning portcullis. Steve and Akal secure the outside orcs' agreement to leave the party alone while it rests. Arden sets a warning trap, and the night passes without incident.

Ulrich cures Akal's infection, and Arden cures Michaelis's. Both clerics spend their healing magic restoring the party. Akal is deeply grateful and credits Ulrich's magic to the underground gods. The next day, escorted by the outside orcs, the Bonebreakers cut timber and build a wooden covering shaped like an upside-down canoe. It shields them from the arrows on their way back in. The surviving otyughs demand a promise that the cave orcs will be fed to them in exchange for passage. The party nervously agrees and takes the eastern exit.

Nila climbs a barracks chimney and finds a second way out onto a high forest trail. Farther in, the Bonebreakers find enough provisions to feed a sizeable orc group for weeks, along with crates of contraband, rare dyes, silk, alchemical ingredients, jewellery, and silverware stripped of identifying marks. They leave the valuable crates in place because transporting them out will be difficult.

Familiar coded messages reveal a [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] lair. The party suspects it could be the Syndicate's main stronghold. They find another copy of the [[items/poetry-cipher-book|poetry cipher book]] they had hoped to find at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]]. The earlier pages are missing, but page 19 is intact and gives them the key to one of [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina's]] encoded letters. The letter has yet to be decoded and could provide evidence against the imprisoned countess. The missing pages prevent a complete reading of her correspondence.

Alongside the cipher, the party finds ten coded packets of emergency payments, as well as letters and payment records concerning nobles, merchants, and smugglers. Much of the material appears suitable for blackmail. The Bonebreakers take the payments and records as possible evidence or leverage. A map table marks roads, rivers, noble estates, and [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]] trade routes. The arrangement suggests a plan to unify the [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]], though how and to what end remain unclear.

Following running water from the map room, the Bonebreakers reach a huge natural cavern divided into three terraces among stone columns. Stepping stones cross the river at the lowest level, and three exits lead farther into the caverns. The party has yet to confront the cave orcs or fulfil its bargain with the otyughs. Lirina remains jailed in Ikiria, Thorngate Abbey is still unreached, and [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]] has yet to receive the findings.

## Conspiracies

The [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] is a dispersed network behind sabotage, kidnappings, attacks on the Mercy, and support for the invasions of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. Kargaz confirms that it backed his war and promised him the town. Captured orcs tie it to the occupation of the Mercy and the kidnapping of its priests, although the purpose of their orders to delay the Bonebreakers remains unclear.

The investigation for [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]] has exposed its leader. Arthur's letters and visits led the party to [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of Velluria, whose private rooms concealed an assassin's equipment and a spy network's coded correspondence. Captured at her masquerade and taken to the Mercy, she is identified through ESP as [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]; her linked answers convince the party that Ash is the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]]. She mentally separates those identities, accounting for the earlier apparent denial.

Lirina says nobody directs her and that her plans can continue without her. She admits using Arthur, justifies the invasion as a way to put her own army in Westfort, and wants [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] destroyed. Her hatred gives substance to Lord Varek's suggestion that the Syndicate might oppose the necromancer, but leaves the full scope of its plans unknown. The Bonebreakers reject cooperation with her because they cannot trust her and firmly side with Agatha. They know her responsibility for killing a companion, kidnapping children, arson, and invasions; her claims to serve Agria are her justification for those crimes.

Agatha remains hidden in Harrow, fearing for her life despite Arthur's arrest. With Queen Audrey reportedly unwell, Agatha's death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir. Lirina knows the party has contact with Agatha, but Michaelis has withheld her location. After a hostage ambush leaves a farming couple's daughter dead and two attackers at large, the Bonebreakers jail Lirina in Ikiria. They warn the guards of a likely breakout and forbid her from speaking with Arthur. Smith assures them of the prison's security; no report to Agatha has yet been delivered.

Morel's lead towards [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] draws the Bonebreakers through the wooded highlands north of Ikiria. Orcs there guide them into the [[locations/world/agria/grey-syndicate-caverns|Grey Syndicate Caverns]], where coded messages reveal a stronghold. The party takes correspondence and payment records concerning nobles, merchants, and smugglers. Page 19 of another [[items/poetry-cipher-book|cipher book]] gives it the key to Lirina's matching letter, but the letter has not yet been decoded. A pinned map table suggests a Northlands unification scheme without explaining it. The cave defenders remain undefeated, and the outside orcs' allegiance is unconfirmed.

Other clues remain open: the rest of Lirina's encoded correspondence; the unknown writer urging [[npcs/vellure/lady-miranda|Lady Miranda]] to meet Wendell before he gives evidence; a noble's unexplained nightmare of Audrey's murder; the burned message “Smith already knows”; and the payment for discretion in Harrow. [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] remains in custody on evidence the party planted, without his true role established.

## Ongoing threat: The Necropolis and Valedictus

After ending the immediate crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and descend through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], whose numbered arches open into different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] rises to a storm-lashed gatehouse overlooking [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and the Necropolis's central dome, confirming an exterior entrance on the western frontier.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] proves to be a residential district where zombie labourers have continued rebuilding for more than two thousand years. A work ledger warns that an anomaly must not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown person, title, or authority. The Bonebreakers kill the district's ruler, [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], resolving their immediate objective in Arch V.

When the party returns above, it finds orcs occupying the Mercy under orders to delay the Bonebreakers. The cathedral is retaken, but the following resupply journey costs [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] his life when he sacrifices himself so Arden and Michaelis can escape. The Bonebreakers fortify the Mercy and hold a wake before descending again.

The Bonebreakers begin exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] and its flooded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but submerged routes prevent them from pressing farther. After breaking the siege of Westfort, they obtain eleven Potions of Water Breathing and can now continue the flooded delve.

The search for water-breathing potions takes the party west through Westfort and across Westmarsh. After sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] and losing Luthien, they continue northwest in search of another Necropolis entrance and discover the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its vault contains a locked iron chest beneath soul-binding sigils, and a magical vision shows a soul held in a codex. An imperial command quarter beyond the vault proves that the temple is a concealed Necropolis entrance.

There the Bonebreakers meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]], an undead imperial officer who says priests once conducted evil necromantic experiments in the vault. He refuses to speak the culprit's name, instead writing “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” The party recognises him as the author of a treatise found in Arch VII, while Varro identifies him as the high priest beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], warns that he “sees all,” and says [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] was sealed when the empire ended.

Secret passages lead into another district containing the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] and, by another route, [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varek-tomb|House Varek Tomb]]. A document says that one passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders, confirming that the unknown authority extends beyond Arch V.

Its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varek|Lord Varek]], describes Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who sought dominion across the continent. He reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], though its nature and purpose remain unknown. The Bonebreakers now believe that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve recovered in Arch VII and later traded away is probably one phylactery; Barny's telescope shows it being studied by an unidentified lizardfolk shaman. The soul-bound chest in the Imperial Hill vault may hold another, but this is only a suspicion.

With Varro and Varek, the Bonebreakers have now met two friendly undead within the Necropolis. Their willingness to talk and offer help has given the party—and its clerics in particular—reason to think more carefully about the undead they encounter.

On the way back, Barny permanently takes control of a wight and six skeletons from a much larger undead drill yard. The route reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], establishing another connection within the complex. The Necropolis remains active and unfinished: Valedictus has not been confronted, his phylacteries and the Stylus have not been secured, Arch IV remains partly explored, and the Tribune is still unknown. The party is equipped to return below, but its investigation for Agatha takes priority. The Bonebreakers have rejected Lirina's offer of cooperation against Valedictus because they cannot trust her, and firmly support Agatha. Lirina is jailed in Ikiria, and the party's cipher investigation has uncovered a Syndicate cave stronghold north of the city. The Necropolis delve has not resumed.
