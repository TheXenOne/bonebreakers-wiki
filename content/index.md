## Session 68 - Ashes to Ashes

The interrogation of [[npcs/velur/lirina|Countess Lirina]] is interrupted by the Vellurian Watch searching the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]]. [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]] warns the party as the guards approach, and [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] carries the captive countess off a balcony, jumping two storeys into the gardens and hiding among the vegetation. [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] remains upstairs, still dressed as Lirina and continuing her impersonation.

The officer leading the search, [[npcs/velur/dame-silka-voss|Dame Silka Voss]], enters with eight guards and Lirina's attendant, [[npcs/velur/opal|Opal]]. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] reads their thoughts and learns that Opal recognises Rose as an impostor. Voss initially conceals her suspicion and asks whether the supposed Lirina is all right. Rose claims Opal attacked her the previous night, but the captain does not believe her. When Voss tests Rose by asking the attendant's name, Selvar supplies it from their thoughts. Rose tries to explain that Opal must have changed her name to deceive her, but the bluff fails and Voss orders the party's arrest.

The Bonebreakers overwhelm the arresting group with sleep, paralysis, and magical blindness, taking them captive. Voss remains conscious and blows an alarm whistle loud enough to summon guards throughout the palace. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] takes the whistle, flies to the opposite side of the building, and sounds a second alarm. Reinforcements change direction, allowing [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] to rejoin the others from the servants' quarters. Leaving their prisoners locked in a room, the party escapes through Lirina's secret passage while Barny recovers their equipment and finds Steve in the gardens.

Rose, still posing as Lirina, orders the stable servants to prepare another carriage. Micaelis and Nila drive the two carriages towards the exit, where elite guards stop them. Micaelis says the lady inside is unwell and should not be disturbed. When the guards insist on looking, Rose continues her impersonation of Lirina and pretends to be ill. Believing they have disturbed the countess herself, they apologise and let the first carriage through. Nila's attempt to avoid inspection makes the second carriage suspicious, but Steve uses his sword to charm the guard who opens its door and tells him he saw nothing. The guard accepts this and closes the door. The Bonebreakers escape [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]] with the real Lirina still in custody.

They travel south along the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kings-road|King's Road]] through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]], passing through Barrow Spa without stopping to avoid attracting attention. Back at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], Steve resumes the interrogation. Lirina remains evasive until Selvar casts ESP and asks whether she is [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]. Her thoughts answer yes. The countess is the same Syndicate enemy who negotiated with them in Westmarsh and repeatedly escaped their attempts to capture her.

Steve asks why she originally wanted them to enter the Necropolis. Lirina warns that they have no idea what they have become involved in, and Selvar senses numerous plans and forces at work. She names [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] as an arch-enemy and says [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]] adored her and deeply resented Agatha, while insisting his mistakes were his own. She says she answers only to herself, and her thoughts support that nobody is directing her.

The party asks again whether Lirina is the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]]. Her thoughts still distinguish Lirina from that identity while affirming that she is Ash. Steve instead asks whether Ash is the Grey Khan and receives a yes. She keeps the identities separate in her mind, accounting for her earlier apparent denial.

The Bonebreakers have finally identified the Grey Khan, and already hold her captive. The countess, their recurring enemy Ash, and the elusive leader of the Syndicate are one person. The breakthrough connects the frontier conspiracy and Arthur's royal dealings to Lirina's power in Velluria. Arden asks whether her plans will continue without her personal direction. She says they will; capturing her has not brought those plans to an end.

Pressed about her purpose, Lirina describes herself as a stabilising force acting for Agria's good and opposing the evil in the Necropolis. Barny uses the [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] and sees her nightmare of plague devastating the whole kingdom. It suggests that she fears this disaster and wants to prevent it, without proving that it will happen or what would cause it.

Barny challenges her account: why orchestrate the goblin invasion if she wants stability? Lirina argues that Westfort's defenders were weak and her own army would have garrisoned it better. She also admits treating Arthur as a useful tool. When Arden asks whether she knows what Valedictus truly is, she confirms that she knows he is a lich and wants him destroyed. Selvar confirms the sincerity of her hatred, and much of what she now says appears truthful. With her identities exposed, she is offering her own justification for the violence and suffering she has caused.

Micaelis suggests cooperation if she will work with [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]]. Lirina says a contract could be made, without promising one, and points out that the princess is missing. Micaelis reveals that the Bonebreakers have been in contact with her. Lirina immediately asks where Agatha is, but he refuses to answer.

Lirina renews her offer: help her destroy the Necropolis's evil and stabilise Agria, and keep all the treasure found there. She says she could offer much more as Duchess.

The offer provokes a fierce debate that divides the Bonebreakers almost evenly. Lirina has killed one of their companions, kidnapped children, set fire to an inn with the party inside, and orchestrated invasions that caused enormous suffering. Those opposed point to these crimes as grounds to reject any alliance with her. Others argue that she could be instrumental in destroying Valedictus and that the ends may justify working with someone so evil. Her usefulness against the lich is weighed against the harm she has already inflicted. No alliance or contract is agreed. Lirina remains captive at the Mercy, the Watch's response is unknown, and Agatha has yet to receive the party's findings.

## Conspiracies

The [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] is a dispersed network behind sabotage, kidnappings, attacks on the Mercy, and support for the invasions of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. Kargaz confirms that it backed his war and promised him the town. Captured orcs tie it to the occupation of the Mercy and the kidnapping of its priests, although the purpose of their orders to delay the Bonebreakers remains unclear.

The investigation for [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]] has exposed its leader. Arthur's letters and visits led the party to [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of Velluria, whose private rooms concealed an assassin's equipment and a spy network's coded correspondence. Captured at her masquerade and taken to the Mercy, she is identified through ESP as [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]; her linked answers convince the party that Ash is the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]]. She mentally separates those identities, accounting for the earlier apparent denial.

Lirina says nobody directs her and that her plans can continue without her. She admits using Arthur, justifies the invasion as a way to put her own army in Westfort, and wants [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] destroyed. Her hatred gives substance to Lord Varrick's suggestion that the Syndicate might oppose the necromancer, but leaves the full scope of its plans unknown. The Bonebreakers must decide whether that shared enemy warrants cooperation with a woman responsible for killing a companion, kidnapping children, arson, and invasions. Her claims to serve Agria are her justification for those crimes. The party has reached no agreement.

Agatha remains hidden in Harrow, fearing for her life despite Arthur's arrest. With Queen Audrey reportedly unwell, Agatha's death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir. Lirina knows the party has contact with Agatha, but Micaelis has withheld her location. The Bonebreakers have not reported their findings, and their resistance to the Vellurian Watch leaves new political consequences unresolved.

Other clues remain open: Lirina's undeciphered letters; the unknown writer urging [[npcs/velur/lady-miranda|Lady Miranda]] to meet Wendell before he gives evidence; a noble's unexplained nightmare of Audrey's murder; the burned message “Smith already knows”; and the payment for discretion in Harrow. [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] remains in custody on evidence the party planted, without his true role established.

## Ongoing threat: The Necropolis and Valedictus

After ending the immediate crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and descend through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], whose numbered arches open into different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] rises to a storm-lashed gatehouse overlooking [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and the Necropolis's central dome, confirming an exterior entrance on the western frontier.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] proves to be a residential district where zombie labourers have continued rebuilding for more than two thousand years. A work ledger warns that an anomaly must not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown person, title, or authority. The Bonebreakers kill the district's ruler, [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], resolving their immediate objective in Arch V.

When the party returns above, it finds orcs occupying the Mercy under orders to delay the Bonebreakers. The cathedral is retaken, but the following resupply journey costs [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] his life when he sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape. The Bonebreakers fortify the Mercy and hold a wake before descending again.

The Bonebreakers begin exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] and its flooded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but submerged routes prevent them from pressing farther. After breaking the siege of Westfort, they obtain eleven Potions of Water Breathing and can now continue the flooded delve.

The search for water-breathing potions takes the party west through Westfort and across Westmarsh. After sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] and losing Luthien, they continue northwest in search of another Necropolis entrance and discover the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its vault contains a locked iron chest beneath soul-binding sigils, and a magical vision shows a soul held in a codex. An imperial command quarter beyond the vault proves that the temple is a concealed Necropolis entrance.

There the Bonebreakers meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]], an undead imperial officer who says priests once conducted evil necromantic experiments in the vault. He refuses to speak the culprit's name, instead writing “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” The party recognises him as the author of a treatise found in Arch VII, while Varro identifies him as the high priest beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], warns that he “sees all,” and says [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] was sealed when the empire ended.

Secret passages lead into another district containing the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] and, by another route, [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]]. A document says that one passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders, confirming that the unknown authority extends beyond Arch V.

Its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], describes Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who sought dominion across the continent. He reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], though its nature and purpose remain unknown. The Bonebreakers now believe that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve recovered in Arch VII and later traded away is probably one phylactery; Barny's telescope shows it being studied by an unidentified lizardfolk shaman. The soul-bound chest in the Imperial Hill vault may hold another, but this is only a suspicion.

With Varro and Varrick, the Bonebreakers have now met two friendly undead within the Necropolis. Their willingness to talk and offer help has given the party—and its clerics in particular—reason to think more carefully about the undead they encounter.

On the way back, Barny permanently takes control of a wight and six skeletons from a much larger undead drill yard. The route reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], establishing another connection within the complex. The Necropolis remains active and unfinished: Valedictus has not been confronted, his phylacteries and the Stylus have not been secured, Arch IV remains partly explored, and the Tribune is still unknown. The party is equipped to return below and is back at the Mercy, where its captive Lirina offers cooperation against Valedictus. Her hatred is genuine, but no alliance has been agreed and exploration has not resumed.
