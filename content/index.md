## Session 64 - A Most Unconvincing Pilgrimage

[[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]] interrupts the Bonebreakers' revelry in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow|Harrow]] to lead them into a windowless back room at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow/anchor-and-chain|the Anchor & Chain]]. Three hooded figures ask what the person they rescued aboard the ship first requested of them. When the party recalls the demand to keep their association secret, the figures reveal themselves as [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]], and [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/admiral-nelson|Admiral Nelson]]. Agatha and Smith are alive, but Agatha must remain hidden because of a continuing threat to her life. She believes Arthur could not have orchestrated the wider unrest alone and suspects something beyond even the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]. With little direct information available while she is concealed, she asks the Bonebreakers to investigate. This takes priority over the Necropolis: her death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir despite the proceedings against him.

Agatha recommends beginning with [[npcs/harrow/count-drago-ven|Count Drago Ven]], Count of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere|Harrowmere]]. She regards him as selfish and effectively the ruler of a pirate town, but does not believe he is ambitious enough to overthrow her. She urges the party to establish a convincing cover and stick to it; they say they have already presented themselves as pilgrims. After accepting the commission, the Bonebreakers leave the meeting and climb a rickety staircase through the warehouse district into the upper city. The cargo suggests a port importing far more than it exports, including narcotics and exotic goods. They enter a wealthy inn resembling an upper-class hotel, only to find gambling and illicit activity flourishing inside. At [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess]]'s suggestion, they hire [[npcs/harrow/drakford|Drakford]], a dwarf struggling to enforce justice amid the disorder. He agrees to provide muscle but does not believe their pilgrimage story.

The party enters the gambling room to seek influential contacts and joins [[npcs/harrow/lord-elron|Lord Elron]] at a game of Deesix. He wins by revealing a seven on a six-sided die, provoking Drakford's anger. Elron hints that the loss has bought them information: his opponents expect assistance in return for letting him win. Meeting him privately, the Bonebreakers invent a story about needing help against rival pilgrims in the warehouse district. Elron claims to know everything happening in Harrow and arranges to see them again the following morning. After staying at the inn, they join him for breakfast at a café across the street. Following a polite meal, he exposes their lie: he would have known about these supposed rivals. The party claims it was testing his information network and admits it wants an introduction to the Count. Elron tells them Ven is already waiting at the Anchor & Chain and that they could simply have approached him directly. Despite their fumbling deception, he remains cordial and expects to meet them again.

The Bonebreakers return to the docks, where Fen admits them to Count Ven in the same private room. They now pose as Arthur's sympathisers, seeking political contacts through which to support him. Ven directs them to [[npcs/kingsport/brother-sedge|Brother Sedge]], a monk who formerly worked in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]] in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/kingsport|Kingsport]], where few people remain. His only price is that they never reveal he sent them. The party boards its ship and sails for three days past fishing villages along the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/salt-coast|Salt Coast]]. The journey clarifies the Northlands' geography: Harrowmere lies in the east, agricultural [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm|Grainholm]] occupies the centre inland, the Salt Coast lies to the north, and western [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]] contains the capital.

Approaching Kingsport, the party sees a compact, wealthy city overlooking the Tyne, with ornate buildings, elaborate gardens, and two gleaming palaces. Domestic ships crowd the harbour, but no naval fleet is visible. After the Bonebreakers disembark, friendly guards approach and explain the registration requirements. Sir Alric Hale, Captain of the Queen's Guard, takes them to a [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/kingsport/guardhouse|nearby guardhouse]], where they are required to surrender weapons. Tess remains invisible and [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] is sneaking, leaving [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] and Drakford to deal with the guards. Ulrich persuades them to let Drakford stay armed as his bodyguard, but his own mace is locked in a safe. Nila secretly steals its key. Sir Alric registers Ulrich as a pilgrim and directs him to Brother Sedge across the city; the mace remains behind.

Following those directions, the party reaches Sedge's simple home, and Tess becomes visible before they enter. Sedge wears plain monastic robes and calls himself a monk of the Furrow. He cautiously denies personal political involvement but says he can connect them with Arthur's supporters. Tess casts Glamour to take the exact appearance of [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]], startling Sedge, and demands immediate entry to the palace to help Arthur's defence. Once he agrees, she returns to her own appearance while maintaining that she is Wendell operating secretly. Sedge escorts the Bonebreakers to the palace and unlocks the door. Nila secretly takes the palace key before he leaves them alone inside. The party has gained entry under a false claim of loyalty to Arthur, but has yet to discover who remains in the palace or what evidence it holds.

## Resolved crisis: The Renewed Siege of Westfort

[[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], the fortified settlement guarding Agria's crossing into [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], came under siege by a smaller successor to the earlier invasion army while the Bonebreakers were seeking water-breathing potions. The crisis already cost the party dearly: while blocking a southern supply route and sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]], [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] was paralysed and killed by a Krell. Raise Dead could not restore her, and her will explicitly refused Reincarnation.

After returning from the Necropolis, the Bonebreakers found Westfort's fifty remaining defenders facing approximately ninety [[factions/military-and-orders/monsters-of-westmarsh/hobgoblins-of-westmarsh|hobgoblins]], three ogres, two catapults, and a ballista. Many inhabitants blamed the party for the settlement's suffering, and its officers were concealing a hobgoblin offer of a one-year truce in exchange for surrendering the Bonebreakers.

The party circled north through the marsh, crossed the West Flow River, and laid two oil lines behind the enemy. Micaelis ignited the northern line and sabotaged a catapult, opening a coordinated attack with Westfort's defenders. In [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]], the combined force destroyed the siege army and killed Kargaz, though the garrison suffered heavy losses.

The [[quests/major/completed/renewed-siege-of-westfort|renewed siege]] is complete. Westfort now faces rebuilding, but its public relationship with the Bonebreakers has changed from resentment to gratitude. Kargaz's secret reanimation remains a separate unresolved consequence. His testimony now confirms that the Grey Syndicate supported his war and promised him Westfort, although the Syndicate's objective remains unknown.

## Ongoing threat: The Necropolis and Valedictus

After ending the immediate crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and descend through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], whose numbered arches open into different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] rises to a storm-lashed gatehouse overlooking [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and the Necropolis's central dome, confirming an exterior entrance on the western frontier.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] proves to be a residential district where zombie labourers have continued rebuilding for more than two thousand years. A work ledger warns that an anomaly must not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown person, title, or authority. The Bonebreakers kill the district's ruler, [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], resolving their immediate objective in Arch V.

When the party returns above, it finds orcs occupying the Mercy under orders to delay the Bonebreakers. The cathedral is retaken, but the following resupply journey costs [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] his life when he sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape. The Bonebreakers fortify the Mercy and hold a wake before descending again.

The Bonebreakers begin exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] and its flooded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but submerged routes prevent them from pressing farther. After breaking the siege of Westfort, they obtain eleven Potions of Water Breathing and can now continue the flooded delve.

The search for water-breathing potions takes the party west through Westfort and across Westmarsh. After sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] and losing Luthien, they continue northwest in search of another Necropolis entrance and discover the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its vault contains a locked iron chest beneath soul-binding sigils, and a magical vision shows a soul held in a codex. An imperial command quarter beyond the vault proves that the temple is a concealed Necropolis entrance.

There the Bonebreakers meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]], an undead imperial officer who says priests once conducted evil necromantic experiments in the vault. He refuses to speak the culprit's name, instead writing “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” The party recognises him as the author of a treatise found in Arch VII, while Varro identifies him as the high priest beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], warns that he “sees all,” and says [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] was sealed when the empire ended.

Secret passages lead into another district containing the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] and, by another route, [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]]. A document says that one passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders, confirming that the unknown authority extends beyond Arch V.

Its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], describes Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who sought dominion across the continent. He reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], though its nature and purpose remain unknown. The Bonebreakers now believe that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve recovered in Arch VII and later traded away is probably one phylactery; Barny's telescope shows it being studied by an unidentified lizardfolk shaman. The soul-bound chest in the Imperial Hill vault may hold another, but this is only a suspicion.

With Varro and Varrick, the Bonebreakers have now met two friendly undead within the Necropolis. Their willingness to talk and offer help has given the party—and its clerics in particular—reason to think more carefully about the undead they encounter.

On the way back, Barny permanently takes control of a wight and six skeletons from a much larger undead drill yard. The route reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], establishing another connection within the complex. The Necropolis remains active and unfinished: Valedictus has not been confronted, his phylacteries and the Stylus have not been secured, Arch IV remains partly explored, and the Tribune is still unknown. The party is equipped to return below, but Agatha's investigation now takes priority.

## Connecting conspiracy: The Grey Syndicate

The [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] is the dispersed criminal network previously tied to sabotage in Westfort, kidnappings across Westmarsh, and Prince Arthur's conspiracy against his sister. Since the Plats investigation, its clearest direct move is the occupation of the Mercy while the Bonebreakers are below. The captured orcs say they came from Westmarsh under Syndicate orders to delay the party. They also reveal that the earlier kidnapping of the Mercy's priests was Syndicate work, suggesting the network had been targeting the cathedral long before the latest Necropolis delves.

What the delay was meant to accomplish remains unknown. It may have been intended to keep the Bonebreakers away from the Necropolis, prevent them from responding to Westfort, or serve another purpose entirely. Kargaz has now confirmed that the Syndicate supported his war and promised him control of Westfort, but he did not know what the organisation ultimately wanted from the campaign.

Lord Varrick adds a possible connection to Valedictus, but only as informed speculation. He believes the Syndicate may be seeking the high priest's necromantic power, or may instead be trying to destroy him. Agatha and Smith have now been found alive in hiding. Agatha suspects a wider force behind the Northlands unrest, possibly beyond the Syndicate, and has sent the party to investigate Arthur's supporters in Kingsport. That suspicion remains unconfirmed; the network's full purpose and any connection to Queen Audrey's reported illness are unknown.
