Welcome to the campaign wiki for **The Bonebreakers**.

## Session 61 - Through the Fire and Flames

[[sessions/session-061|Session 61 - Through the Fire and Flames]] begins with Barny clarifying the nightmare seen through the blinded Necropolis spirit: an unidentified lizardfolk shaman was closely studying [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]]. He then scries on [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] and finds the renewed siege still stalemated, though conditions inside appear improved.

The Bonebreakers travel first to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], sell their accumulated loot to [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/ruby-sunbeam|Ruby Sunbeam]], and meet [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/michelangelo|Michelangelo]]. Arden commissions him to create a stone memorial statue of [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]] and a provisionally wooden statue of [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]]. At the Minimarket, Wistor buys a random ring. Once identified, it proves to grant fire resistance; Wistor gives it to Rose, who passes it to Micaelis.

The guards stop the Bonebreakers at Westfort's gates. [[npcs/west-fort/captain-rodrick|Captain Rodrick]] comes outside and warns that many residents now blame the party for the town's troubles. [[npcs/west-fort/commander-valerius|Commander Valerius]] then meets the Bonebreakers beyond the walls and explains that his remaining fifty defenders face approximately ninety hobgoblins, three ogres, two catapults, and a ballista. He accepts the party's plan to circle north through the marsh, ignite a fire behind the siege line, and use it as the signal for Westfort to charge while the Bonebreakers disable the siege engines.

Carrying two oil barrels supplied by Valerius, the party travels through boggy wetlands, drives off four giant crabs, fords the West Flow River into [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], and kills eleven bugbears during an overnight attack. An easterly wind forces them to wait a day. When a northerly arrives, an invisible Micaelis—surrounded by Arden's Silence—lays two long oil lines behind the hobgoblin army.

At dawn, Micaelis ignites the northern oil line. Fire catches along the upper arc behind the hobgoblin army and begins advancing about ten feet every three rounds, while the southern line remains laid but unlit for later. Ulrich protects himself and Selvar from the flames, Barny takes flight, and most of the Bonebreakers advance directly into the burning ground. Rose's opening arrow clips one ogre, while Barny blinds another above the siege engines.

Still invisible and surrounded by Silence, Micaelis reaches the first catapult and sabotages its mechanism. The nearest ogre can neither see nor hear him, but the furthest ogre spots that something is wrong. Selvar's missed shot then alerts ten hobgoblin archers, and his Mirror Images turn him into an unmistakable cluster of targets for their volley. The session closes on the Bonebreakers standing in the heart of the blaze, then emerging through fire and flames beneath a storm of hobgoblin arrows. Behind them, the northern oil line burns while the southern line waits; ahead, one catapult lies disabled, two siege engines remain active, and Westfort's soldiers are still behind their gates.

## Immediate crisis: The Renewed Siege of Westfort

The first warning comes during the downtime after Bran's death. [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], the fortified settlement guarding Agria's crossing into Westmarsh, is under siege by a smaller successor to the earlier invasion army. The Bonebreakers head there while seeking water-breathing potions for the flooded Necropolis; a gryphon attack kills their horses and delays the journey, but they set out again on foot.

Westfort is badly damaged when they arrive, its defences patched and its food supplies threatened by nightly goblin raids. [[npcs/west-fort/captain-rodrick|Captain Rodrick]] and [[npcs/west-fort/commander-valerius|Commander Valerius]], the fort's senior officers, reveal a private hobgoblin offer: Westfort will receive a one-year truce if it surrenders the Bonebreakers. The party refuses, and the offer is kept secret to prevent local resentment from turning against them.

That night the Bonebreakers ambush the food raiders and force a survivor to reveal the southern mountain route used to bypass Westfort's defences. The party triggers a landslide that blocks the path, then sabotages the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] before continuing toward the Necropolis. During the operation, [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] is paralysed and killed by a Krell, which partly devours her body. Raise Dead cannot restore her, and her will explicitly refuses Reincarnation.

After returning from the Necropolis, Barny scries on Westfort and finds the siege still stalemated, although conditions inside appear better. The Bonebreakers travel west again, where Rodrick warns that many inhabitants now blame them for the settlement's suffering.

Valerius reports that Westfort has fifty defenders against approximately ninety [[factions/military-and-orders/monsters-of-westmarsh/hobgoblins-of-westmarsh|hobgoblins]], led by a commander wearing a broken crown, and three ogres operating two catapults and a ballista.

The Bonebreakers agree to circle north through the marsh, start a fire behind the siege line, and use it as the signal for Westfort's defenders to charge. Once a northerly wind arrives, Micaelis lays two oil lines behind the army, ignites the northern one, and disables a catapult. Most of the party begin emerging through fire and flames as hobgoblin archers respond. The southern line remains unlit, two siege engines are still active, and Westfort's soldiers have not yet charged. The [[quests/major/active/renewed-siege-of-westfort|battle to break the siege]] is underway.

## Ongoing threat: The Necropolis and Valedictus

After ending the immediate crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and descend through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], whose numbered arches open into different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] rises to a storm-lashed gatehouse overlooking [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and the Necropolis's central dome, confirming an exterior entrance on the western frontier.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] proves to be a residential district where zombie labourers have continued rebuilding for more than two thousand years. A work ledger warns that an anomaly must not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown person, title, or authority. The Bonebreakers kill the district's ruler, [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], resolving their immediate objective in Arch V.

When the party returns above, it finds orcs occupying the Mercy under orders to delay the Bonebreakers. The cathedral is retaken, but the following resupply journey costs [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] his life when he sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape. The Bonebreakers fortify the Mercy and hold a wake before descending again.

The Bonebreakers begin exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] and its flooded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but submerged routes prevent them from pressing farther. They retreat to rest and obtain water-breathing potions before continuing the delve.

The search for water-breathing potions takes the party west through Westfort and across Westmarsh. After sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] and losing Luthien, they continue northwest in search of another Necropolis entrance and discover the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its vault contains a locked iron chest beneath soul-binding sigils, and a magical vision shows a soul held in a codex. An imperial command quarter beyond the vault proves that the temple is a concealed Necropolis entrance.

There the Bonebreakers meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]], an undead imperial officer who says priests once conducted evil necromantic experiments in the vault. He refuses to speak the culprit's name, instead writing “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” The party recognises him as the author of a treatise found in Arch VII, while Varro identifies him as the high priest beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], warns that he “sees all,” and says [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] was sealed when the empire ended.

Secret passages lead into another district containing the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] and, by another route, [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]]. A document says that one passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders, confirming that the unknown authority extends beyond Arch V.

Its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], describes Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who sought dominion across the continent. He reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], though its nature and purpose remain unknown. The Bonebreakers now believe that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve recovered in Arch VII and later traded away is probably one phylactery; Barny's telescope shows it being studied by an unidentified lizardfolk shaman. The soul-bound chest in the Imperial Hill vault may hold another, but this is only a suspicion.

With Varro and Varrick, the Bonebreakers have now met two friendly undead within the Necropolis. Their willingness to talk and offer help has given the party—and its clerics in particular—reason to think more carefully about the undead they encounter.

On the way back, Barny permanently takes control of a wight and six skeletons from a much larger undead drill yard. The route reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], establishing another connection within the complex. The Necropolis remains active and unfinished: Valedictus has not been confronted, his phylacteries and the Stylus have not been secured, Arch IV remains partly explored, and the Tribune is still unknown.

## Connecting conspiracy: The Grey Syndicate

The [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] is the dispersed criminal network previously tied to sabotage in Westfort, kidnappings across Westmarsh, and Prince Arthur's conspiracy against his sister. Since the Plats investigation, its clearest direct move is the occupation of the Mercy while the Bonebreakers are below. The captured orcs say they came from Westmarsh under Syndicate orders to delay the party. They also reveal that the earlier kidnapping of the Mercy's priests was Syndicate work, suggesting the network had been targeting the cathedral long before the latest Necropolis delves.

What the delay was meant to accomplish remains unknown. It may have been intended to keep the Bonebreakers away from the Necropolis, prevent them from responding to Westfort, or serve another purpose entirely. The renewed siege uses coordinated food raids, mountain routes, and political pressure against the party, but there is still no proof that the Syndicate commands the hobgoblin army.

Lord Varrick adds a possible connection to Valedictus, but only as informed speculation. He believes the Syndicate may be seeking the high priest's necromantic power, or may instead be trying to destroy him. This means the siege, the attacks on the Mercy, and the Necropolis may belong to one wider plan—but they may also be separate struggles involving the same network. The confirmed position is narrower: the Syndicate remains active in Westmarsh, has ordered attacks against the Mercy, and has an unresolved interest in the Bonebreakers. Its control of the siege and its objective regarding Valedictus are both unproven.
