## Session 50 - Coins from a Dead Empire

[[sessions/session-050|Session 50 - Coins from a Dead Empire]] covers the aftermath of the corpse-pit battle in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]]. The Bonebreakers destroy the last undead, recover [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]'s notebook, strip ancient imperial armour from the dead, and confirm through [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden Sable]]'s investigation that fresh necrotic magic was recently worked in the graves. As the party searches southward, they find abandoned streets, emptied dockside buildings, and a friendly elf aboard the *Saint of Tides* who believes someone is deliberately upsetting Ikiria's balance.

The biggest advance comes at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]]. A mob, encouraged by inquisitors, now blames the Bonebreakers and other necromancers for the city's dead, while nearby citizens openly complain that [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] has vanished or fled. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] descends into the poisoned well, purifies it, and finds 400 pre-imperial gold pieces at the bottom, strongly suggesting that both the poison and the ancient evidence there were planted deliberately. The party ends the session with permission to see [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]], but the wider crisis is still deepening.

## Current arc: The Rot of Agria

The Rot of Agria is the current Ikiria arc, beginning in earnest once the party reaches the locked-down city and discovers that the Southlands crisis is not just political fallout from Arthur's arrest. Ikiria is sealed, the lower districts are partly burned, the Plats are unstable, and the city authorities are no longer dealing with a single clean problem. The Bonebreakers learn of rumours that the first outbreak may have come from poisoned water, find evidence that later sickness spread through crowding and failing infrastructure, and are drawn into a burial crisis in which the number of corpses no longer matches the official records. At the same time, civilian inquisitors are worsening the situation by attacking suspected magic-users, burning sites like [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|the Temple of Merciful Life]], and helping turn public fear into open mob violence.

What makes the arc more dangerous is that the plague story is now overlapping with older necromantic history. At the corpse pits, the dead rise not as ordinary plague victims but as stronger undead marked with ancient imperial signs, suggesting that Ikiria's suffering may be tied to the same buried past the party has already uncovered below [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]. In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], the party confirms the public well is poisoned, purifies it, and discovers 400 pre-imperial Westmarsh coins planted at the bottom, pushing the crisis even further toward deliberate staging. The arc now combines public health collapse, civic disorder, missing dead, planted evidence, and an apparently deliberate undead incursion centered on [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]].

## Last arc: The Traitor Prince

The Traitor Prince arc begins when the party encounters [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] on the road back toward Ikiria in [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] and discovers that he is secretly working with the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] against [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]]. What first looks like a suspicious noble mission quickly becomes a succession crisis. Arthur admits he is trying to meet the Grey Khan, the party captures his secretary and documentary evidence, and later sessions reveal that Ash and the wider Syndicate network are entangled in the same plot. The Bonebreakers spend the arc trying to turn rumour into proof while also surviving Westmarsh pressure from kidnappings, raids, and Syndicate-controlled intermediaries.

The key developments are the secretary's ledger, Ash's offer to buy the secretary back, and the failed exchange plan that becomes the raid on the decoy Spider Market cell in [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]]. That raid gives the party cash, prisoners, and more testimony, but more importantly it keeps Arthur's side from recovering evidence or silencing witnesses. With [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]] finally willing to speak, the Bonebreakers bring Arthur, the records, and the witness to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. The arc ends when [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] accepts the case at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/east-agria-company-headquarters|East Agria Company Headquarters]] and orders Arthur arrested, leaving the larger Grey Syndicate threat unresolved but bringing the prince's betrayal into the open.
