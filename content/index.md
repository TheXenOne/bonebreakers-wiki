Welcome to the campaign wiki for **The Bonebreakers**.

## Session 53 - Orcs Above, Dead Below

[[sessions/session-053|Session 53 - Orcs Above, Dead Below]] begins where the last Necropolis delve left the Bonebreakers: in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]], with eleven zombie labourers arriving after the death of [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]. The party destroys them, then finds only a sash on the Bloated One's remains naming it Senior Administrator of Residential District V. That title gives the group a new way to interpret the numbered archways as possible administrative districts.

Before leaving Arch V, [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]]'s Locate Object spell points the party back to the shrine, where [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] finds a hidden chest in the plinth. Inside are the [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-shrine-locket|Arch V Shrine Locket]], showing a family portrait, and a vial of holy water. In the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] tests the ruby eye-buttons in the eagle statue again and sees no obvious result; [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] then removes the rubies for loot.

The return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] turns into a second siege of the party's own home. The trapdoor over [[locations/world/agria/southlands/the-abyss|the Abyss]] cannot be opened from below, and [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] uses the [[items/party-inventory/crystal-ball|crystal ball]] to discover that Grey Syndicate-linked orcs have imprisoned the Mercy's inhabitants. After resting in the stairwell, Barny charms the orc above the hatch through the crystal ball, gets the trapdoor opened, and the Bonebreakers storm into the nave. Arden and Ulrich hold two orcs in place while Luthien's Sleep drops the rest, allowing the party to free the prisoners.

The interrogation changes an old assumption. Kronk, one of the surviving orcs, says the squad came from [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and was ordered to delay the Bonebreakers. Before Steve kills him, Kronk also says the first priest kidnapping from the Mercy was Grey Syndicate work, suggesting [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh One-Tusk]] may have been working for the Syndicate. Barny secretly reanimates the dead squad leader while Arden is distracted, but the only clearer answer is that the delay order was real; whether it was meant to keep the party from [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], the Necropolis, or something else remains unresolved.

After the party rests, Ulrich cures Steve and Micaelis of the Bloated One's rotting disease. Barny's library research then confirms that the Abyss predates the cathedral and that early Bahal worshippers built a stairway over it in the basement. They feared it, believed it held power, and one treatise even calls it a kind of stairway to heaven. That discovery sharpens the party's suspicion that something from the Necropolis may have possessed or corrupted the [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]], though the matter remains unproven.

The Bonebreakers split afterward. Steve, Frank, Selvar, and Barny remain at the Mercy while the others travel to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] to restock before descending again. On the road, Luthien befriends an unusually intelligent bear. In town, the party stays at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/red-herring|the Red Herring]], deposits loot at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/bean-counter|the Bean Counter]], collects and commissions equipment, and hears rumours that the Curia may be open. On the road back, the returning group is ambushed by twelve insectoid humanoids.

## Current arc: The Necropolis Reopened

With the immediate danger in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] dealt with, the Bonebreakers return to the deeper problem beneath [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]: [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The new arc begins as the party chooses to descend again, purge more undead, seek treasure, and understand how the impossible buried complex connects to [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], ancient imperial power, and the older mysteries already found below.

The renewed delve first establishes that the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], the domed chamber of white archways beneath the Mercy, is not just an isolated dungeon hub. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] leads upward to a storm-lashed outer gatehouse and wall walk patrolled by skeleton archers. From there, the party can see Westmarsh beyond the walls and the Necropolis's central dome within, strongly suggesting that Arch VI is the western exterior entrance described by earlier explorers.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] reveals a different kind of threat: an underground district where zombie labourers endlessly demolish and rebuild old streets for [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]. [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]], a zombie foreman, gives the party directions deeper in, while a hidden [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]] points toward the children's quarters, a foreman's station, and the Bloated One's domain. False Barnys also begin appearing around Barny's fears and ambitions, making the district feel personally hostile as well as undead.

Session 52 turns those clues into progress, but not cleanly. The hidden map carries the party into the children's quarters, where a schoolroom, zombie children, and a drawing of the sun show that Arch V preserved civilian life as well as labour. Beyond the school, chalk-marked streets start looping around the party; when the Bonebreakers alter one arrow to point upward, the route teleports them onto the storm-lashed outer dome near [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]]. After returning to the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]] and going back into Arch V, Steve opens the way forward by replacing the wall arrows with a symmetrical floor arrow.

That route leads to the foreman's station, where Frank reads the [[items/bloated-one-work-ledger|work ledger]] and finds records of restoration work continuing for more than 2,000 years. The ledger also notes a warm-air anomaly behind the school and orders that it should not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]]. The Bonebreakers follow that clue, find a hidden maintenance passage, and use it to come up behind the Bloated One. Their ambush kills it before it can properly respond, but its body explodes in necrotic force, infecting [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]] and [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] with a rotting disease; eleven zombies arrive as the party starts searching the remains.

By Session 53, Arch V has a clearer shape. The route through the district works, the chalk-marked streets can be escaped, the warm-air anomaly was probably connected to the Bloated One, and Section 22 was demolished because the restoration works have no fresh source of stone. The Bloated One is dead, its sash suggests Arch V is Residential District V, and Steve and Micaelis have been cured of its rotting disease. The Tribune's role, the purpose of the long restoration system, the sun drawing and Cyril, the hidden shrine locket, and the false Barnys still point beyond the dead Bloated One, keeping the Necropolis dangerous even after this victory.

The Grey Syndicate-linked orc raid shows someone still wants to delay the Bonebreakers around this route. Whether that delay is about the Necropolis, Westfort, or another Syndicate plan remains unresolved.

## Last arc: The Rot of Agria

After [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] is handed over in Ikiria, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] sends the Bonebreakers down into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] to speak with [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]. The city is under quarantine, the lower districts are burned and overcrowded, and Vaessa needs help because the number of bodies in the corpse pits no longer matches the records. Maren's work points the party toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]], and the party eventually confirms the crisis began with poison before later disease spread through crowding and broken civic systems.

The investigation becomes more dangerous when undead rise from the corpse pit and cemetery. The Bonebreakers destroy the undead, Maren dies after confirming the dead were conjured and did not belong there, and Arden later confirms that the necromantic energies are gone from the pit and cemetery after the battle. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] purifies the Public Well, ending the immediate poisoned-water danger, while the party's discovery of planted pre-imperial Westmarsh coins shows that someone tried to stage or frame part of the crisis.

The arc ends with the immediate danger over but consequences still moving. The quarantine has not yet been lifted, and the people responsible for the poison, planted coinage, missing bodies, and public panic remain unidentified. Still, the well is purified, the undead incursion has been purged, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa]] accepts testimony against the [[factions/other/civilian-inquisition|civilian inquisitors]], and the Bonebreakers are free to turn back toward the Necropolis.
