## Session 65 - Evidence, What Evidence?

Inside [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]], the Bonebreakers pursue [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]]'s covert investigation with a borrowed claim to authority: [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]] sent them. Since [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]]'s arrest, deliveries have dwindled and much of the household has disappeared beneath covers or official seals. [[npcs/kingsport/elspeth-crane|Mistress Elspeth Crane]], the mistress of the house, lets them proceed with a clear warning to leave those seals alone. In the kitchen, Steve cooks for [[npcs/kingsport/nessa|Nessa]], a nervous young chambermaid, and asks about Arthur. While serving drinks through the small door to Arthur’s private salon, Nessa overheard him telling someone else that Lirina understands what Agatha has taken from “him”; whom he meant remains unclear. The private salon offers another fragment: a burned letter reading only “Smith already knows,” with no explanation of what he knew or even which Smith is meant. The investigation also yields a bottle of fine brandy for Tess and loose sofa coins for Steve. The thefts begin modestly.

An empty space in the royal portrait gallery draws the party to the butler, [[npcs/kingsport/hugh-malory|Hugh Malory]]. Their claim to be acting for Wendell reassures him, while Selvar's ESP reveals that Hugh's loyalties rest largely with Arthur's position and the continued security of his own employment. In private, Hugh identifies the missing portrait as [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]]. Exceptionally charming, much discussed, and—in Hugh's suspicion—connected to Arthur's predicament, she will be hosting a masquerade at her palace in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/velur|Velur]], about a day's travel from Kingsport. The party has a name and somewhere to look for her.

In the writing room, Steve adds an inkwell to the haul while Tess finds an incomplete letter hidden beneath a drawer. Its approximate wording anticipates Agatha's disgrace and promises the Northlands a duke fit to be king, though its author, recipient, and the meaning of “con” remain unclear. Tess pockets the hidden letter. Other drafts trace years of Arthur's jealousy toward Agatha, gradually exchanging informal resentment for cautious political language. Then [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] arrives, claiming to document evidence for the Royal Chamberlain. He questions the visitors' authority and claims this is his first inspection. Tess hands over the other letters, keeping the one about “the con” in her pocket. Corvin seems disappointed with what she gives him, but takes the letters and leaves.

Elspeth returns just as Selvar is trying to lift a royal seal. Steve uses his sword to cast Charm Person on Elspeth, then supplies an explanation: they are repairing damage Corvin caused. Even charmed, Elspeth remains suspicious, and she supplies an awkward fact for Corvin's story—he has visited repeatedly. Once Steve persuades her to leave, the Bonebreakers open the cabinet. Records show Arthur travelling increasingly often to Veluria shortly before the party first encountered him, with payments for transport, hospitality, and discretion in Harrow. Agatha's letters urge her brother to take his duties seriously. The missing portrait is here too, finally giving Lirina a face. The party takes the financial records and Agatha's letters, leaves the portrait, and replaces the seal. Outwardly, at least, the cabinet is back in order.

By the ballroom, the thefts have outgrown pockets. Steve lowers the chandelier so the party can strip off its crystals, then raises the considerably less valuable fitting back into place. In the corridors, the Bonebreakers overhear rumours that Lirina abandoned Arthur after his arrest and that the Queen intends to close his palace permanently. Another rumour concerns someone magically controlling Arthur in the Southlands. That sounds uncomfortably familiar, although nobody names the Bonebreakers.

The music room contributes instruments before the party reaches Arthur's lavish bedroom. Steve takes all the jewellery. Rose puts on a fur cloak and bundles the expensive clothing into a sack. The party takes a coin pouch and a locket, removing its miniature portrait of Lirina and leaving the picture behind. Amid the disappearing possessions, love letters to Lirina reinforce the personal connection. Her own warning reads: “Do nothing rash. Commit nothing to paper.” Her precise involvement remains unproven; Arthur's attachment is becoming much harder to miss.

The Bonebreakers finally decide they have enough evidence—and enough of Arthur's belongings. They hand all the stolen loot to Tess, who struggles under its weight and casts Invisibility on herself. Meanwhile, Steve approaches the still-charmed Elspeth with alarming news: Corvin has stolen Arthur's jewellery. Elspeth insists on discretion and sends him to track the supposed thief. Steve follows Corvin to the Royal Chamberlain's building, where Corvin checks for pursuit and bolts. Steve catches and binds him, giving Micaelis the opportunity to plant some of the stolen jewels on him.

Back at the palace, jewels fall from Corvin's pockets, providing Elspeth with all the proof she needs. She summons [[npcs/kingsport/sir-aldric-hale|Sir Aldric Hale]] and backs Steve's account. Corvin protests that he has been framed, correctly, but the guards arrest him. His lies, suspicious palace activity, and flight from the Royal Chamberlain's building still leave the Bonebreakers suspecting he is not who he claims to be. They simply have rather less honest grounds for the jewellery charge.

The Queen's Guard thanks the Bonebreakers and escorts them to the dockside guardhouse to collect their weapons. One final complication awaits: the guard cannot find his key. The party drops the stolen key behind him and helpfully points out that he dropped it. With a puzzled shrug, he returns their weapons and permits them to keep them until leaving the following morning. The guards then pay for the party's inn stay, completing a remarkably hospitable conclusion to the palace robbery. Corvin is in custody, the fate of the letters he took remains unclear, and the Bonebreakers have yet to report to Agatha. Ahead lies Lirina's masquerade in Velur.

## Conspiracies

The [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] is a scattered criminal network behind sabotage, kidnappings, and support for the hobgoblin invasions of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], Agria's frontier stronghold. The defeated commander Kargaz confirms that it backed his war and promised him the town. Captured orcs also implicate it in the occupation of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], the Bonebreakers' cathedral base, and the earlier kidnapping of its priests. Their orders were to delay the party; why remains unknown.

The network may also be interested in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]], an undead imperial city reached through the depths beneath the Mercy. [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], an undead noble there, suspects it seeks either the power or the destruction of [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]], the ancient necromancer who still holds authority within the ruins. Neither possibility is confirmed.

The Syndicate's royal connection is firmer: [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]], Duke of the Northlands, sought its help against his older sister, [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], Agria's heir and the Bonebreakers' secret patron. The party exposed his dealings and secured his arrest, but his upcoming trial leaves open how much he directed or understood. Agatha remains in hiding in the northern port of Harrow, fearing a continuing threat to her life. She has sent the Bonebreakers to discover whether the Syndicate is manipulating Arthur or something separate lies behind the unrest. With Queen Audrey reportedly unwell, Agatha's death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir despite his arrest.

The investigation leads to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]], Agria's capital, where Arthur's palace records reveal his attachment to [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]]. Love letters, repeated visits, and her warning against rash action and written commitments make her upcoming masquerade in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/velur|Velur]] the next lead; her political role remains uncertain. Other clues include a burned message reading “Smith already knows,” an unexplained payment for discretion in Harrow, and [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]], a supposed royal evidence collector whose lies and flight make the party doubt his identity. His arrest rests on jewels the Bonebreakers planted themselves. Who directs the wider threat, and how to make Agatha safe, remain unanswered.

## Ongoing threat: The Necropolis and Valedictus

After ending the immediate crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and descend through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], whose numbered arches open into different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] rises to a storm-lashed gatehouse overlooking [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and the Necropolis's central dome, confirming an exterior entrance on the western frontier.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] proves to be a residential district where zombie labourers have continued rebuilding for more than two thousand years. A work ledger warns that an anomaly must not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown person, title, or authority. The Bonebreakers kill the district's ruler, [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], resolving their immediate objective in Arch V.

When the party returns above, it finds orcs occupying the Mercy under orders to delay the Bonebreakers. The cathedral is retaken, but the following resupply journey costs [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] his life when he sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape. The Bonebreakers fortify the Mercy and hold a wake before descending again.

The Bonebreakers begin exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] and its flooded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but submerged routes prevent them from pressing farther. After breaking the siege of Westfort, they obtain eleven Potions of Water Breathing and can now continue the flooded delve.

The search for water-breathing potions takes the party west through Westfort and across Westmarsh. After sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] and losing Luthien, they continue northwest in search of another Necropolis entrance and discover the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its vault contains a locked iron chest beneath soul-binding sigils, and a magical vision shows a soul held in a codex. An imperial command quarter beyond the vault proves that the temple is a concealed Necropolis entrance.

There the Bonebreakers meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]], an undead imperial officer who says priests once conducted evil necromantic experiments in the vault. He refuses to speak the culprit's name, instead writing “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” The party recognises him as the author of a treatise found in Arch VII, while Varro identifies him as the high priest beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], warns that he “sees all,” and says [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] was sealed when the empire ended.

Secret passages lead into another district containing the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] and, by another route, [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]]. A document says that one passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders, confirming that the unknown authority extends beyond Arch V.

Its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], describes Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who sought dominion across the continent. He reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], though its nature and purpose remain unknown. The Bonebreakers now believe that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve recovered in Arch VII and later traded away is probably one phylactery; Barny's telescope shows it being studied by an unidentified lizardfolk shaman. The soul-bound chest in the Imperial Hill vault may hold another, but this is only a suspicion.

With Varro and Varrick, the Bonebreakers have now met two friendly undead within the Necropolis. Their willingness to talk and offer help has given the party—and its clerics in particular—reason to think more carefully about the undead they encounter.

On the way back, Barny permanently takes control of a wight and six skeletons from a much larger undead drill yard. The route reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], establishing another connection within the complex. The Necropolis remains active and unfinished: Valedictus has not been confronted, his phylacteries and the Stylus have not been secured, Arch IV remains partly explored, and the Tribune is still unknown. The party is equipped to return below, but Agatha's investigation now takes priority.
