## Session 49 - Rot and Wraith

[[sessions/session-049|Session 49 - Rot and Wraith]] picks up in the middle of the corpse-pit battle in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], where the Bonebreakers are already facing an outbreak far worse than ordinary plague deaths. More skeletons climb from the pit, stronger undead close in from the cemetery, and fear magic throws parts of the battlefield into chaos. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] uses the Wand of Daystars from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king|the Halls of the Blood King]] to destroy one wraith and badly damage another, while [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden Sable]] and [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] repeatedly hold the line with turning, healing, holy water, and anti-undead magic. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] is nearly overwhelmed and suffers a rotting mummy wound before Arden cures it.

The most important story development is what the fight confirms about Ikiria's wider crisis. [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] tells the party that the undead do not belong in the pits and must have been conjured, which pushes the city's troubles beyond poisoning, disease, and civic collapse into deliberate necromancy. She also repeats her belief that the outbreak began as poison and then spread through overcrowding, but before she can say more a wraith drains away the last of her life and kills her. Maren's notebook survives her, preserving her conclusions, while the session ends before the full aftermath of the battle is shown.

## Current arc: The Rot of Agria

The Rot of Agria is the current Ikiria arc, beginning in earnest once the party reaches the locked-down city and discovers that the Southlands crisis is not just political fallout from Arthur's arrest. Ikiria is sealed, the lower districts are partly burned, the Plats are unstable, and the city authorities are no longer dealing with a single clean problem. The Bonebreakers learn of rumours that the first outbreak may have come from poisoned water, find evidence that later sickness spread through crowding and failing infrastructure, and are drawn into a burial crisis in which the number of corpses no longer matches the official records. At the same time, civilian inquisitors are worsening the situation by attacking suspected magic-users and burning sites like [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|the Temple of Merciful Life]].

What makes the arc more dangerous is that the plague story is now overlapping with older necromantic history. At the corpse pits, the dead rise not as ordinary plague victims but as stronger undead marked with ancient imperial signs, suggesting that Ikiria's suffering may be tied to the same buried past the party has already uncovered below [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]. In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], that fear hardens into open confirmation that the outbreak is unnatural: the undead assault continues, [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] says the dead do not belong there and must have been conjured, and she dies before the cause can be identified. The arc now combines public health collapse, civic disorder, missing dead, and an apparently deliberate undead incursion centered on [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]].

## Last arc: The Traitor Prince

The Traitor Prince arc begins when the party encounters [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] on the road back toward Ikiria in [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] and discovers that he is secretly working with the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] against [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]]. What first looks like a suspicious noble mission quickly becomes a succession crisis. Arthur admits he is trying to meet the Grey Khan, the party captures his secretary and documentary evidence, and later sessions reveal that Ash and the wider Syndicate network are entangled in the same plot. The Bonebreakers spend the arc trying to turn rumour into proof while also surviving Westmarsh pressure from kidnappings, raids, and Syndicate-controlled intermediaries.

The key developments are the secretary's ledger, Ash's offer to buy the secretary back, and the failed exchange plan that becomes the raid on the decoy Spider Market cell in [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]]. That raid gives the party cash, prisoners, and more testimony, but more importantly it keeps Arthur's side from recovering evidence or silencing witnesses. With [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]] finally willing to speak, the Bonebreakers bring Arthur, the records, and the witness to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. The arc ends when [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] accepts the case at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/east-agria-company-headquarters|East Agria Company Headquarters]] and orders Arthur arrested, leaving the larger Grey Syndicate threat unresolved but bringing the prince's betrayal into the open.
