Welcome to the campaign wiki for **The Bonebreakers**.

## Session 51 - Barnys All the Way Down

[[sessions/session-051|Session 51 - Barnys All the Way Down]] turns the Bonebreakers' findings in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] into civic consequences. [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] examines the pre-imperial coin from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]] and says it does not fit Ikiria's known history, since the city was first built by elves and later expanded by gnomes. She accepts witness testimony against the [[factions/other/civilian-inquisition|civilian inquisitors]], promises to deal with them, agrees to raise the quarantine problem with the [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]], and says the city will pay to rebuild [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|the Temple of Merciful Life]].

After storing their own pre-imperial coin cache in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]'s vault, the party returns to [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. They find their commissioned trap door over the stairwell complete, and descend into the Nexus. They first enter [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]], finding it leads to a rain-lashed gatehouse and wall walk patrolled by skeleton archers, with [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] visible outside, the large dome inside the walls, and imperial runes explaining the gate mechanism. The discovery strongly supports the idea that Arch VI is an outer entrance into the Necropolis. Before the party returns to the Nexus, a false [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] appears, fails to mimic him properly, and is killed.

The Bonebreakers return to the Nexus before entering [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]], which reveals a roofed underground street where zombie labourers endlessly dismantle and rebuild ancient homes. The Bonebreakers loot old apartments, survive an ochre jelly, and learn that [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]]'s ancient imperial armour is cursed. They then meet [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]], a zombie foreman directing the disarrayed zombie workers inside. Barny then bluffs him into treating the party as health and safety inspectors. Mortimer names the labourers' purpose as the restoration of [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain, and a hidden [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|map]] recovered from a shrine confirms routes toward the children's quarters, the foreman's station, and the Bloated One's domain.

## Current arc: The Necropolis Reopened

With the immediate danger in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] dealt with, the Bonebreakers return to the deeper problem beneath [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]: [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The new arc begins as the party chooses to descend again, purge more undead, seek treasure, and understand how the impossible buried complex connects to [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], ancient imperial power, and the older mysteries already found below.

The first renewed push changes the map. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] leads upward to a storm-lashed outer gatehouse and wall walk patrolled by skeleton archers, strongly suggesting that this arch reaches the western exterior entrance described by earlier explorers. The party can see Westmarsh beyond the walls and the Necropolis's central dome within, making the Nexus feel less like an isolated dungeon chamber and more like one controlled access point in a much larger ruin.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] points the arc toward a different kind of threat: an underground district where zombie labourers endlessly restore [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain. [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]] gives the party directions deeper in, false Barnys begin appearing around Barny's fears and ambitions, and the [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]] names the children's quarters, the foreman's station, and the Bloated One's domain as the next major destinations.

## Last arc: The Rot of Agria

After [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] is handed over in Ikiria, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] sends the Bonebreakers down into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] to speak with [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]. The city is under quarantine, the lower districts are burned and overcrowded, and Vaessa needs help because the number of bodies in the corpse pits no longer matches the records. Maren's work points the party toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]], and the party eventually confirms the crisis began with poison before later disease spread through crowding and broken civic systems.

The investigation becomes more dangerous when undead rise from the corpse pit and cemetery. The Bonebreakers destroy the undead, Maren dies after confirming the dead were conjured and did not belong there, and Arden later confirms that the necromantic energies are gone from the pit and cemetery after the battle. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] purifies the Public Well, ending the immediate poisoned-water danger, while the party's discovery of planted pre-imperial Westmarsh coins shows that someone tried to stage or frame part of the crisis.

The arc ends with the immediate danger over but consequences still moving. The quarantine has not yet been lifted, and the people responsible for the poison, planted coinage, missing bodies, and public panic remain unidentified. Still, the well is purified, the undead incursion has been purged, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa]] accepts testimony against the [[factions/other/civilian-inquisition|civilian inquisitors]], and the Bonebreakers are free to turn back toward the Necropolis.
