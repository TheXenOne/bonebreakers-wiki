Welcome to the campaign wiki for **The Bonebreakers**.

## Session 54 - The Last Charge of Bran

[[sessions/session-054|Session 54 - The Last Charge of Bran]] begins with part of the party leaving [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] for [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], joined by two new player characters: [[characters/active-players/marie/characters/fred|Fred]] and [[characters/active-players/valerio/characters/paul|Paul]]. On the river road, [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]], [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]], [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]], Fred, and Paul encounter twelve four-armed insectoid humanoids with polearms and crystal discs.

The travellers try to flee when they realise the odds are poor, but the creatures are too fast. Arden's Hold Person fails, missiles prove difficult to land, and Fred and Paul are cut down. With the party collapsing and no clear path to victory, Bran chooses to sacrifice himself, throwing himself into the whole enemy group and holding their attention long enough for Arden and Micaelis to escape with Fred and Paul's bodies. Bran dies in the last stand, and his body cannot be recovered.

Back in Barrow Spa, Fred and Paul are resurrected. The party buys a riverboat, hires eight oarsmen, sends word to the Mercy by river trader, and rests for two weeks while the resurrected companions recover. During the same downtime, [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] commissions a moat and 30-foot walls for the Mercy, Arden gains Bran's Heroic Aura as a spell in Bran's honour, [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] researches treasure-locating magic, [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] improves the [[items/party-inventory/staff-of-withering|Staff of Withering]], and [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] and [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] gain strength from the [[items/party-inventory/manual-of-strength|Manual of Strength]].

The wider campaign pressure does not stop. Ulrich learns that the renewed invasion of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] has become a stalemated siege. The enemy force is much reduced but still has siege engines and includes hobgoblins, goblins, and ogres. Barny views the west gate through the [[items/party-inventory/crystal-ball|crystal ball]], but keeps what he sees to himself.

At the Mercy, the Bonebreakers hold a wake for Bran and plan a commemorative statue. Arden speaks of Bran as the party's shield, sword, and protector through many trials. After the ceremony, the party descends again through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/the-abyss|the Abyss]] into the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]] and chooses [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]].

Arch IV opens into a vast damp hall covered in lichen and sloping downward. Deeper in, the Bonebreakers find a dead market street where zombies act out ordinary trade with rotted stalls and goods that often are not really there. Frank plays along with a seller of invisible art supplies, while Steve pays two gold pieces to another zombie and receives real gems worth 380 gold pieces from a locked display case.

## Current arc: The Necropolis Reopened

With the immediate danger in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] dealt with, the Bonebreakers return to the deeper problem beneath [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]: [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. They are descending again to purge more undead, seek treasure, and understand how the impossible buried complex connects to [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], ancient imperial power, and the older mysteries already found below.

Earlier delves establish that the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], the domed chamber of white archways beneath the Mercy, is not just an isolated dungeon hub. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] leads upward to a storm-lashed outer gatehouse and wall walk patrolled by skeleton archers. From there, the party can see Westmarsh beyond the walls and the Necropolis's central dome within, strongly suggesting that Arch VI is the western exterior entrance described by earlier explorers.

The deeper pattern is now clearer: the Necropolis is not only a tomb or treasure vault, but a preserved civic ruin with districts, labour systems, old administration, markets, shrines, schools, and undead routines that have continued long after ordinary life ended. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] shows this most strongly. Its zombie labourers endlessly demolish and rebuild streets for [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], while [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]], the foreman's station, the [[items/bloated-one-work-ledger|work ledger]], and the [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]] reveal a restoration system that has operated for more than 2,000 years. The Bonebreakers eventually find and kill the Bloated One, ending the district's most obvious authority but not explaining the system it served.

The Bloated One's sash names it Senior Administrator of Residential District V, suggesting that the numbered arches may correspond to administrative districts. The work ledger also mentions [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown entity or authority within the restoration system.

The Mercy itself remains under pressure. In another raid on the cathedral, Grey Syndicate-linked orcs seize the site and capture its inhabitants while the Bonebreakers are below. After the party retakes the Mercy, the surviving orcs say they were ordered to delay the Bonebreakers, but whether that delay was meant to keep them away from the Necropolis, [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], or some other objective remains unresolved. The renewed siege of Westfort is now active, though its exact relationship to the Syndicate is unproven.

The road back to the Mercy also costs the party dearly. On the road from Barrow Spa, Arden, Micaelis, [[characters/active-players/marie/characters/fred|Fred]], [[characters/active-players/valerio/characters/paul|Paul]], and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] are attacked by twelve mantid-like warriors too fast to outrun. After Fred and Paul fall, Bran sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape with their bodies. The Bonebreakers return to the Mercy without Bran, fortify the cathedral, hold a wake in his honour, and descend again with his sacrifice fresh in memory.

The latest descent takes the Bonebreakers into [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]], opening a different face of the same buried world. Its damp, lichen-covered route leads to a dead market street where zombie merchants still perform the motions of trade. Steve's exchange of two gold pieces for real gems suggests that the market has rules the party may be able to use, but its purpose, risks, and connection to the wider Necropolis economy remain unknown.

## Last arc: The Rot of Agria

After [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] is handed over in Ikiria, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] sends the Bonebreakers down into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] to speak with [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]. The city is under quarantine, the lower districts are burned and overcrowded, and Vaessa needs help because the number of bodies in the corpse pits no longer matches the records. Maren's work points the party toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]], and the party eventually confirms the crisis began with poison before later disease spread through crowding and broken civic systems.

The investigation becomes more dangerous when undead rise from the corpse pit and cemetery. The Bonebreakers destroy the undead, Maren dies after confirming the dead were conjured and did not belong there, and Arden later confirms that the necromantic energies are gone from the pit and cemetery after the battle. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] purifies the Public Well, ending the immediate poisoned-water danger, while the party's discovery of planted pre-imperial Westmarsh coins shows that someone tried to stage or frame part of the crisis.

The arc ends with the immediate danger over but consequences still moving. The quarantine has not yet been lifted, and the people responsible for the poison, planted coinage, missing bodies, and public panic remain unidentified. Still, the well is purified, the undead incursion has been purged, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa]] accepts testimony against the [[factions/other/civilian-inquisition|civilian inquisitors]], and the Bonebreakers are free to turn back toward the Necropolis.
