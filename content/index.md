## Session 66 - Masquerade

The morning after searching [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Prince Arthur's palace]] in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]], the Bonebreakers plan to infiltrate [[npcs/velur/lirina|Countess Lirina]]'s masquerade in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]]. Arthur is under arrest for plotting against his sister, [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], the Bonebreakers' secret patron. She has sent them to investigate who else may be involved. Arthur's love letters to Lirina and his repeated visits to her county, [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria|Velluria]], have given them a reason to investigate the countess. The masquerade will let them approach her and mingle with her guests.

The party spends a week preparing. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] chooses to attend openly as a [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenite]] bishop, wearing long robes decorated with gold, a tall gilt hat, and an eye mask shaped like Belenus's sunburst. His ceremonial sceptre can also serve as a weapon. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] will accompany him as a fellow cleric. He polishes his armour, incorporates the [[items/party-inventory/crown-of-king-bomfluke|Crown of King Bomfluke]] into his helmet, and casts Continual Light on the symbol of Belenus atop Arden's hat.

[[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] poses as a foreign noble from the [[locations/world/ramen-empire|Ramen Empire]], choosing a formal toga with a gleaming pin, a slightly military appearance, and a lion mask. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] will be his bodyguard. He hunts for skins and fashions an animal-skin toga, completing his cultured Ramen barbarian costume with a bear's head for a mask. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/captain-winston|Captain Winston]] introduces Frank to a forger, who supplies two invitations to the masquerade.

[[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] prepares an acrobatic act called “The Crimson Lotus”, with a silken outfit, ribbons, and concealed daggers. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] dresses in professional evening wear to pass as a server. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] plans to perform under his formal stage name, Barnobuck the Bizarre, but tells the others little about his act. He goes grave robbing and raises a minor undead as his female assistant, then dresses them both in multicoloured robes. The party rents an expensive carriage, while Rose, Barny, and Nila hire horses and travel ahead.

The three reach Velur, a landlocked city of wealthy residents and celebrities, with estates, entertainment venues, and elaborate gardens. It is smaller than Kingsport but considerably more fashionable. Lirina's [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] dominates the city, looking part fortress and part entertainment venue. Servants and performers queue at the city checkpoint, where guards record each visitor's name and occupation.

Once inside the city, Nila goes straight to the Gilt Palace. Her uniform is convincing enough that she is immediately put to work. Rose initially struggles to convince the organisers that she belongs there, but persuades them to let her open the entertainment. Barny claims that the scheduled final act cannot attend and that he has been sent as their replacement magician. The organisers accept his story.

Later, the carriage carrying Frank, Steve, Arden, and Ulrich arrives outside the Gilt Palace for the masquerade. Spectators have gathered to watch the guests make their entrances. Frank steps out first and immediately draws attention with his foreign appearance. He presents his invitation and a gift of fine bear hide and leatherwork made by Steve, then enters the palace with his bodyguard.

Arden and Ulrich step down next, moving slowly and ceremonially. The crowd falls quiet with a degree of reverence, even though many may not know who Arden is. Arden presents his invitation, blesses the spectators, and offers a gem enchanted by Ulrich with Continual Light. With the four guests admitted, all seven Bonebreakers are inside the masquerade.

In the palace's lavish interior, the master of ceremonies introduces Lirina Kartashan, Countess of Velluria. Tall, slender, and poised, she appears at the top of the stairs in a golden half-mask that does little to hide the face the party recognises from her portrait in Arthur's Palace. She descends gracefully, pauses beside an ornate, empty ducal chair, and speaks about Agria's political uncertainty. As she surveys the gathering, her gaze pauses on each Bonebreaker. She appears to recognise them despite their disguises and cover stories.

Lirina claps her hands to begin the festivities. The Bonebreakers spread through the enormous ballroom, remaining close enough to work together. Its alcoves offer places for private conversations, while attendants and eager guests surround the countess. Frank finds an unmarried noblewoman and flirts with considerable success. She appears to be a distant cousin of the royal family. Arden approaches a physician to promote Belenite healing, but alarms him by mentioning that a companion is almost capable of resurrecting the dead. The physician quickly withdraws.

Rose opens the entertainment by climbing and pirouetting on long silken ribbons suspended above the ballroom. She fumbles and falls flat onto the floor, then manages to play off the embarrassing accident. Meanwhile, Nila circulates among the guests and learns that the woman Frank is flirting with is related to the royal bloodline. She steals a letter from her and hears rumours that Lirina is trying to take Arthur's place. The letter's contents remain unknown.

Barny performs grotesque knife tricks with his undead assistant, then turns the [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] on a noble. It shows him a nightmare of [[npcs/agria/queen-audrey-ii|Queen Audrey II]] being murdered in the streets. Barny incorporates the dream into his act, prompting the noble to deny it vehemently. He then cuts his assistant into pieces and puts her back together, leaving the assembled guests shocked and horrified.

## Conspiracies

The [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] is a scattered criminal network behind sabotage, kidnappings, and support for the hobgoblin invasions of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], Agria's frontier stronghold. The defeated commander Kargaz confirms that it backed his war and promised him the town. Captured orcs also implicate it in the occupation of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], the Bonebreakers' cathedral base, and the earlier kidnapping of its priests. Their orders were to delay the party; why remains unknown.

The network may also be interested in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]], an undead imperial city reached through the depths beneath the Mercy. [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], an undead noble there, suspects it seeks either the power or the destruction of [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]], the ancient necromancer who still holds authority within the ruins. Neither possibility is confirmed.

The Syndicate's royal connection is firmer: [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]], Duke of the Northlands, sought its help against his older sister, [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], Agria's heir and the Bonebreakers' secret patron. The party exposed his dealings and secured his arrest, but his upcoming trial leaves open how much he directed or understood. Agatha remains in hiding in the northern port of Harrow, fearing a continuing threat to her life. She has sent the Bonebreakers to discover whether the Syndicate is manipulating Arthur or something separate lies behind the unrest. With Queen Audrey reportedly unwell, Agatha's death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir despite his arrest.

The investigation leads to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]], Agria's capital, where Arthur's palace records reveal his attachment to [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria|Velluria]]. Love letters, repeated visits, and her warning against rash action and written commitments lead the party to her masquerade at the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]]. They gain entry, but she appears to recognise them. Nila hears rumours that Lirina wants Arthur's place and steals a letter from a noblewoman with royal connections. Barny's telescope reveals another noble's nightmare of Audrey's murder; the noble denies it, and no actual attack or plot is established. Lirina's political role remains uncertain. Other clues include a burned message reading “Smith already knows,” an unexplained payment for discretion in Harrow, and [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]], a supposed royal evidence collector whose lies and flight make the party doubt his identity. His arrest rests on jewels the Bonebreakers planted themselves. Who directs the wider threat, and how to make Agatha safe, remain unanswered.

## Ongoing threat: The Necropolis and Valedictus

After ending the immediate crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and descend through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], whose numbered arches open into different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]] rises to a storm-lashed gatehouse overlooking [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] and the Necropolis's central dome, confirming an exterior entrance on the western frontier.

[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] proves to be a residential district where zombie labourers have continued rebuilding for more than two thousand years. A work ledger warns that an anomaly must not be reported to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]], an unknown person, title, or authority. The Bonebreakers kill the district's ruler, [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]], resolving their immediate objective in Arch V.

When the party returns above, it finds orcs occupying the Mercy under orders to delay the Bonebreakers. The cathedral is retaken, but the following resupply journey costs [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]] his life when he sacrifices himself so Arden and Micaelis can escape. The Bonebreakers fortify the Mercy and hold a wake before descending again.

The Bonebreakers begin exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] and its flooded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but submerged routes prevent them from pressing farther. After breaking the siege of Westfort, they obtain eleven Potions of Water Breathing and can now continue the flooded delve.

The search for water-breathing potions takes the party west through Westfort and across Westmarsh. After sabotaging the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]] and losing Luthien, they continue northwest in search of another Necropolis entrance and discover the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Its vault contains a locked iron chest beneath soul-binding sigils, and a magical vision shows a soul held in a codex. An imperial command quarter beyond the vault proves that the temple is a concealed Necropolis entrance.

There the Bonebreakers meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]], an undead imperial officer who says priests once conducted evil necromantic experiments in the vault. He refuses to speak the culprit's name, instead writing “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” The party recognises him as the author of a treatise found in Arch VII, while Varro identifies him as the high priest beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], warns that he “sees all,” and says [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] was sealed when the empire ended.

Secret passages lead into another district containing the heavily guarded [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] and, by another route, [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]]. A document says that one passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders, confirming that the unknown authority extends beyond Arch V.

Its undead occupant, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], describes Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who sought dominion across the continent. He reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], though its nature and purpose remain unknown. The Bonebreakers now believe that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] Steve recovered in Arch VII and later traded away is probably one phylactery; Barny's telescope shows it being studied by an unidentified lizardfolk shaman. The soul-bound chest in the Imperial Hill vault may hold another, but this is only a suspicion.

With Varro and Varrick, the Bonebreakers have now met two friendly undead within the Necropolis. Their willingness to talk and offer help has given the party—and its clerics in particular—reason to think more carefully about the undead they encounter.

On the way back, Barny permanently takes control of a wight and six skeletons from a much larger undead drill yard. The route reaches the Nexus through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]], establishing another connection within the complex. The Necropolis remains active and unfinished: Valedictus has not been confronted, his phylacteries and the Stylus have not been secured, Arch IV remains partly explored, and the Tribune is still unknown. The party is equipped to return below, but Agatha's investigation now takes priority.
