## Overview

The Necropolis is an ancient imperial ruin older than the Ramen Empire and tied to the civilisation that once ruled Westmarsh before its fall. Although it lies in [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], the Bonebreakers first reach part of it from beneath [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]].

## Geography and layout

- The first known entry from the campaign descends through inverted passages beneath the Mercy.
- That route opens into a vast chamber of eight numbered archways.
- Arch VII leads to a stoneworking quarter, unfinished halls, a chained necromantic book, the Foundation Chamber, a chimera-haunted section, and Cartographer's Folly.

## The Arches

- Arch I: dark beyond, with the sound of marching and a buried street in the distance.
- Arch II: a narrow corridor carved with what look like family or ancestral records.
- Arch III: violet-lit, incense-scented, and marked by a mosaic of imperial rise and divine fire.
- Arch IV: green-tinged, damp, and sloping toward a flooded fork.
- Arch V: dry and dusty, opening onto a broad underground boulevard.
- Arch VI: smoky and misted, said by Cornelius Vane to be his original route in from Westmarsh.
- Arch VII: the explored route, including the stonemason's camp, Foundation Chamber, chimera section, and Cartographer's Folly.
- Arch VIII: sealed when first discovered. Needs review.

## Associated people and groups

- Cornelius Vane is the first trapped explorer the party finds here.
- Skeleton patrols, unnatural geometry, and necromantic relics all mark the site.
