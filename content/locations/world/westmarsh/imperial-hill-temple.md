## Overview

The Imperial Hill Temple is a monumental ruin in the barren northwestern reaches of [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], found by the Bonebreakers while travelling toward [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] in [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]]. Its true name and original purpose are unknown. It resembles an imperial temple built into an enormous hill and is important because its central chamber contains magic intended to bind a particular person's soul.

## Geography and layout

The visible structure is approximately thirty metres high. Its entrance opens into a broad domed chamber with a ceiling about forty feet above the floor. Twelve alcoves around the chamber originally held skeletal legionnaires facing inward.

An iron box stands on a central plinth. It is secured by three locks that can be picked but must be opened simultaneously. A far door bears soul-binding sigils connected to the box, with an open corridor visible beyond it. The door slammed shut when the skeletal guards activated.

## Known dangers

- Twelve skeletal legionnaires guarded the central chamber until the Bonebreakers destroyed them.
- Two transparent, pale-blue disenchanters approached from behind while the party worked on the box. One was killed, and the blinded survivor was effectively dealt with.
- One disenchanter removed the enchantment from Selvar's sword.

## Campaign events

- Frank scouts the chamber using Luthien's recovered cloak and boots.
- Barny identifies the sigils as magic concerned with binding a specific person's soul, but tells the party only that the box contains treasure.
- Barny's attempt to bind one skeleton with rope alerts all twelve guards. Arden and Quak turn most of them, and the party destroys the survivors.
- The Nightmare-Revealing Telescope shows the disenchanters dreaming of a soul contained within a codex.
