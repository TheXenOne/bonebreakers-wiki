## Overview

The Imperial Hill Temple is a monumental ruin in the barren northwestern reaches of [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], found by the Bonebreakers while travelling toward [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] in [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]]. Its true name is unknown, and its temple-like exterior conceals a back entrance into the Necropolis. The first domed chamber is marked as a vault on an ancient [[items/imperial-command-quarter-map|map]] and contains magic intended to bind a particular person's soul.

## Geography and layout

The visible structure is approximately thirty metres high. Its entrance opens into a broad domed vault with a ceiling about forty feet above the floor. Twelve alcoves around the chamber originally held skeletal legionnaires facing inward.

An iron chest stands on a central plinth. It is secured by three locks that can be picked but must be opened simultaneously, is attached to the floor, and cannot simply be carried away. A far door bears soul-binding sigils connected to the chest, with a corridor beyond it.

Beyond the vault, glowing lichen lights old barracks and a four-way intersection built around a rotatable imperial eagle standard. An inscription orders travellers to proceed in the direction the eagle faces. The east and west passages loop back to the same barracks, while the northern route leads through a prison, guardroom, mess hall, altered funerary chambers, soldiers' quarters, and an armoury.

The [[items/imperial-command-quarter-map|Imperial Command-Quarter Map]] labels the entry chamber as the vault, marks secret passages in the barracks and mess hall, and confirms that the party entered the Necropolis through a concealed back route.

Secret passages also connect the command quarter to [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|an imperial family tomb]]. [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] says the tomb belongs to a separate district, so the Bonebreakers appear to have discovered a hidden connection between two parts of the Necropolis.

## Connection to the Nexus

The command quarter is connected to one of the eight arches in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|the Nexus]], but the Bonebreakers have not established which one. The known evidence makes Arch V through Arch VIII unlikely matches, Arch IV leads to a flooded market district, and Varro places the temple district and its high priest, [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]], beyond Arch III. This leaves [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]] or [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-ii|Arch II]] as the strongest possibilities, but neither has been confirmed.

## Known dangers

- Twelve skeletal legionnaires guarded the central chamber until the Bonebreakers destroyed them.
- Two transparent, pale-blue disenchanters approached from behind while the party worked on the chest. Both have now been killed.
- One disenchanter removed the enchantment from Selvar's sword.
- Unnatural cold in the barracks can induce freezing and early hypothermia.
- Two minotaurs approached the eagle crossroads and were killed by the party.
- A pressure plate beyond the prison portcullis may trigger when several people cross together.
- Twenty-four wights guard the Imperial Family Tomb.
- Twelve skeletal soldiers attacked the party in a later sleeping hall and were destroyed.
- Looting the armoury triggered a poisonous-gas trap that remains active.

## Campaign events

- Frank scouts the vault using Luthien's recovered cloak and boots.
- Barny identifies the sigils as magic concerned with binding a specific person's soul, but tells the party only that the chest contains treasure.
- Barny's attempt to bind one skeleton with rope alerts all twelve guards. Arden and Quak turn most of them, and the party destroys the survivors.
- The Nightmare-Revealing Telescope shows the disenchanters dreaming of a soul contained within a codex.
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], the Bonebreakers follow the eagle north through the command quarter and meet [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] in its mess hall.
- Varro allows the party to inspect a map that confirms the site is a concealed entrance into the Necropolis.
- A prison warning says, “Three days. They know about the vault. Do not trust the halfling.” Nearby funerary carvings have been reworked to depict a halfling.
- Varro says the priests did something evil in the vault while meddling with necromancy. When the party asks who the priest involved was, he refuses to speak the answer and instead writes [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]], implying a connection that he does not explain.
- Frank promises to lay Varro to rest. Varro then leaves the armoury unlocked, allowing the party to take weapons, armour, and 800 gold pieces before a gas trap activates.
