## Overview

House Varek Tomb is a family burial chamber in a Necropolis district reached through a secret passage from the command-quarter armoury. It contains [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varek|Lord Varek]], an undead imperial noble who exchanged information with the Bonebreakers and revealed that [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] has three phylacteries.

This is separate from the larger [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|Imperial Family Tomb]] guarded by twenty-four wights. House Varek Tomb is rectangular rather than domed, contains a raised black-marble sarcophagus and twelve other coffins, and did not register as evil when Ulrich examined it with his holy symbol.

## Geography and layout

The chamber's walls are carved with the history of [[factions/necropolis/house-varek|House Varek]]. Twelve sarcophagi stand in two rows of six along the walls. A distinctive black-marble sarcophagus rests slightly open on a raised dais at one end.

## Known occupants

Lord Varek speaks from the black sarcophagus. The identities and condition of the other twelve occupants remain unknown.

## Current status

Lord Varek has closed himself back inside his sarcophagus. The Bonebreakers left without disturbing the other tombs.
