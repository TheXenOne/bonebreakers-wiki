## Overview

Arch III is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It has not been entered by the Bonebreakers. From the Nexus, it appears as a violet-lit, incense-scented passage with a wide corridor and a mosaic showing the rise of the empire, with gods descending to give fire to humanity. [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] says it leads to the temple district, where the high priest [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] “sees all.”

## Nexus view

Arch III glows violet and carries the scent of incense. The visible passage is wide and decorated with a mosaic showing the rise of the empire, with gods descending to give fire to humanity.

## Known contents

- Violet-lit corridor.
- Incense smell.
- Mosaic of imperial rise and divine fire.
- No known explored rooms beyond the visible corridor.
- [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] is the high priest within the temple district. His present condition is unknown.

## Status

Unexplored. Varro's account confirms that the route continues into the temple district.
