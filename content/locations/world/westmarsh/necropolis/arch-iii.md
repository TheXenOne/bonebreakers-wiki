## Overview

Arch III is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It has not been entered by the Bonebreakers. From the Nexus, it appears as a violet-lit, incense-scented passage with a wide corridor and a mosaic showing the rise of the empire, with gods descending to give fire to humanity.

## Nexus view

Arch III glows violet and carries the scent of incense. The visible passage is wide and decorated with a mosaic showing the rise of the empire, with gods descending to give fire to humanity.

## Known contents

- Violet-lit corridor.
- Incense smell.
- Mosaic of imperial rise and divine fire.
- No known explored rooms beyond the visible corridor.

## Status

Unexplored.
