## Overview

Arch VIII is the northern arch in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It was sealed when the Bonebreakers first discovered the chamber, and no scent or interior view is recorded. [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] calls it the Eighth Wing and says it was sealed after something went wrong when the empire ended.

## Known contents

No contents are known. The party has not opened or entered Arch VIII.

## Status

Sealed and unexplored.
