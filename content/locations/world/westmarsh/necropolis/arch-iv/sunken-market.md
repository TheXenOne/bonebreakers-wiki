## Overview

The Sunken Market is the market district reached through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]] of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The Bonebreakers first encounter it as a damp, lichen-covered route into a dead market street where zombie merchants continue the motions of trade. In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], a hidden map in the counting house names the area and reveals that the market extends into flooded, partly submerged, and lower-level spaces.

The area matters because its dead merchants can still produce real goods, its trade rules are only partly understood, and its flooded routes now appear to contain both physical hazards and active intruders.

## Known areas

- The market street, lined with rotted stalls and zombie merchants.
- Nearby pools from which serpents erupt when Arden destroys a zombie merchant.
- A counting house off to the side of the market, containing collapsed ledgers, overturned bookcases, locked chests, and the hidden [[items/party-inventory/arch-iv-sunken-market-map|Arch IV Sunken Market Map]].
- A lower floor where the [[items/reserved-carved-mask|reserved carved mask]] is supposedly held.
- Fishermen's quarters farther ahead. Needs review.
- Flooded areas, some of them entirely underwater, marked on the map.
- A channel that the map warns should not be approached after dark.
- A sump containing something that is not a fish.

## Known inhabitants and hazards

- Zombie merchants continue to barter, accept strange or imaginary payments, and sometimes provide real goods.
- Pool serpents respond violently after the party breaks the market's routine by destroying a merchant.
- Snake-men arrive after the Bonebreakers loudly break open chests in the counting house.
- [[factions/religions-and-cults/dark-druids-of-the-deep-one|Dark druids of the Deep One]] move through the market chanting, claim they are not from here, and do not guarantee safe passage.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], the Bonebreakers first enter Arch IV and find the dead market street.
- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], the party barters with several zombie merchants, triggers a serpent attack, explores the counting house, recovers the Sunken Market map, defeats three snake-men, and avoids a fight with the dark druids.

## Current status

Partially explored. The party has the map and knows several important hazards, but the lower floor, fishermen's quarters, underwater areas, after-dark channel, sump, and dark druids remain unresolved.
