## Overview

Arch I is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It connects the Nexus to a vast skeletal drill yard and the command quarter reached through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]], proving that the hill ruin is a concealed entrance into District One.

## Nexus view

When the arch curtains are lifted, Arch I appears dark. The party hears the sound of marching and sees an image of a buried city street glowing beyond the arch.

## Known contents

- Nine skeletons march out of Arch I when the Nexus is first activated.
- [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] destroys the skeletons with turning.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], the party reaches Arch I from the opposite side after escaping the command quarter. An immense drill yard before the arch holds 300 skeletons repeating exercises under a wight and three sentries.
- Barny consumes a Potion of Control Undead and permanently takes command of the wight and six skeletons while the party crosses a long bridge above the yard.
- The route through Arch I returns the party to the Nexus.

## Status

The Bonebreakers have crossed into the Nexus through Arch I, confirming its connection to the Imperial Hill command quarter. Most of the drill yard and District One remain unexplored.
