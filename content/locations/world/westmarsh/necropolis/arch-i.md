## Overview

Arch I is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It has not been explored beyond what the Bonebreakers saw from the Nexus: darkness, the sound of marching, and a glowing image of a buried city street beyond the arch. No scent is recorded. Needs review.

## Nexus view

When the arch curtains are lifted, Arch I appears dark. The party hears the sound of marching and sees an image of a buried city street glowing beyond the arch.

## Known contents

- Nine skeletons march out of Arch I when the Nexus is first activated.
- [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] destroys the skeletons with turning.
- No further exploration through Arch I has been recorded.

## Status

Unexplored beyond the Nexus activation.
