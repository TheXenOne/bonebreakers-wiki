## Overview

The Arch V children's quarters are a smaller-scaled residential section inside [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The Bonebreakers reach the district while following the [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]] toward [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain.

The area matters because it contains undead children, an imperial schoolroom, a strange drawing of the sun, and the maintenance route that lets the party emerge behind the Bloated One.

## Known contents

- Smaller apartments, apparently built for children or smaller folk.
- Zombie children destroyed by [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]].
- A schoolroom with imperial civic lessons written on the wall.
- A child's drawing of the sun, despite the district being buried and sunless.
- A teacher's desk containing a clay marble, a carved wooden horse, and a paper naming Cyril.
- A small empty play area behind the school.
- A drain cover leading to a maintenance corridor.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], the Bonebreakers explore the children's quarters, find the sun drawing, and discover a maintenance passage behind the school.
- The maintenance passage emerges behind the Bloated One, revealing that the ledger's warm-air anomaly was probably connected to the Bloated One's own gas or breath.

## Current status

Partially explored. The Bonebreakers know the maintenance route, but the meaning of the sun drawing and the identity of Cyril remain unresolved.
