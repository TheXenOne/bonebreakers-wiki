## Overview

Arch IV is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. From the Nexus, it glows white with a faint green tint and smells of stagnant water. The Bonebreakers enter it in [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]] and find a vast damp hall covered in lichen, followed by a dead market street where zombies act out the motions of trade.

In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], the party discovers that this district is called the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]]. It contains a counting house, lower levels, flooded and underwater areas, active water hazards, snake-men, and strange druids who worship the Deep One.

## Nexus view

Arch IV glows white with a faint green tint and smells of stagnant water. The first route slopes downward through a vast hall dripping with moisture and covered in lichen.

## Known contents

- A vast moisture-dripping hall covered in lichen and sloping downward.
- The [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], a market district of rotted stalls, zombie merchants, flooded routes, and lower floors.
- Zombies moving as if preparing for a market day, handling goods that often do not appear to exist.
- Zombie merchants capable of speaking simple market phrases and completing transactions.
- A display case containing real gems worth 380 gold pieces, received by Steve after he paid two gold pieces to a zombie merchant.
- Other market goods include silver instruments, ancient wine, an intricately carved reserved mask, ivory statues, and imperial crystal vials. What remains available after Session 55 needs review.
- Pools that release serpents when the market routine is violently broken.
- A counting house containing collapsed ledgers, locked chests, the [[items/party-inventory/arch-iv-sunken-market-map|Arch IV Sunken Market Map]], and loot guarded or interrupted by snake-men.
- Flooded areas, entirely underwater sections, fishermen's quarters, a dangerous after-dark channel, and a sump containing something that is not a fish, all marked or implied by the map.
- [[factions/religions-and-cults/dark-druids-of-the-deep-one|Dark druids of the Deep One]] moving through the market.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], the Bonebreakers enter Arch IV, find the damp hall and market street, and learn that some zombie trades can yield real treasure.
- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], the party barters more widely with the merchants, triggers a serpent attack after Arden destroys a zombie merchant, explores the counting house, recovers the Sunken Market map, defeats three snake-men, and avoids a fight with the dark druids.

## Status

Partially explored. The Bonebreakers have identified the district as the Sunken Market and recovered a map, but the lower floor, fishermen's quarters, flooded areas, after-dark channel, sump, dark druids, and deeper rules of the market remain unresolved.
