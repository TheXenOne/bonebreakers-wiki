## Overview

Arch IV is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It has not been explored beyond its visible threshold. From the Nexus, it glows white with a faint green tint, smells of stagnant water, and shows a cracked lichen-covered hall sloping toward a flooded fork.

## Nexus view

Arch IV glows white with a faint green tint and smells of stagnant water. It shows a cracked hall covered in lichen and sloping toward a flooded fork.

## Known contents

- Cracked hall.
- Lichen.
- Stagnant-water smell.
- Flooded fork visible farther in.
- No known explored rooms beyond the visible hall.

Unexplored.
