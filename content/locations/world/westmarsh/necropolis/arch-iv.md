## Overview

Arch IV is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. From the Nexus, it glows white with a faint green tint and smells of stagnant water. In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], the Bonebreakers enter it and find a vast damp hall covered in lichen, followed by a dead market street where zombies act out the motions of trade.

## Nexus view

Arch IV glows white with a faint green tint and smells of stagnant water. The first route slopes downward through a vast hall dripping with moisture and covered in lichen.

## Known contents

- A vast moisture-dripping hall covered in lichen and sloping downward.
- A street lined with rotted market stalls.
- Zombies moving as if preparing for a market day, handling goods that often do not appear to exist.
- Zombie merchants capable of speaking simple market phrases and completing transactions.
- A display case containing real gems worth 380 gold pieces, received by Steve after he paid two gold pieces to a zombie merchant.
- A flooded fork was visible from the threshold in earlier observation, but the exact connection between that view and the explored market route needs review.

## Status

Partially explored. The market's purpose, the nature of its zombie merchants, and whether its trade rules can be used safely remain unresolved.
