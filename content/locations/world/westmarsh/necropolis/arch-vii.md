## Overview

Arch VII is the only Nexus arch the Bonebreakers have explored in depth. From the Nexus, it glows white but flickers, has no scent, and appears to lead into a stone-cutting or construction area. When the party enters, ash settles on their shoulders.

## Stonemason's camp

Arch VII first leads to an abandoned stonemason's camp and cutting yard. The site appears untouched for more than two thousand years: old tools, a dried waterskin, boots, half-finished stone blocks, chiselled dust, and plans pinned beneath a mallet remain in place.

One unfinished block reads, "Work suspended by order, see attached," though the attachment is gone. The space is sterile, faintly luminous, and free of the lichen seen elsewhere.

The local geometry behaves badly. Sound carries too far, voices seem to arrive too quickly, and the party feels distant thumps through the floor from an impossible distance. Plans recovered here show only part of a larger wing, including the mason's camp, an unfinished hall, an inverted room, and a chamber labelled Cartographer's Folly.

## Cornelius Vane's room

[[npcs/necropolis/cornelius-vane|Cornelius Vane]] is found in a nearby room with a bedroll and maps naming places such as the Vault, the Collapsed Nave, the Twins, and the Chamber. He describes the builders as master architects and administrators who were once lawful before later corruption by chaos and the worship of chaotic gods.

## Inverted room

Past the camp, the party finds an inverted room with an imperial eagle set into the wall. Furniture is bolted to what should be the ceiling, including a stone table, chairs, an open cabinet, and a scroll case. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] retrieves a scroll from the case and finds another map.

## Valedictus's chamber

Deeper in is a level chamber with smooth walls and a stone plinth. Chained to the plinth is an iron-bound book covered in soul-binding sigils, surrounded by eight iron plates marked with compulsion runes and numbered one to eight.

[[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] reads the book, a necromantic treatise by Valedictus, and gains increased intelligence from it.

## Foundation Chamber

The Foundation Chamber appears to be one of the deepest known points reached from Arch VII. Its walls are smooth through some older force rather than normal craft. At the centre is a shallow depression containing [[items/glowing-skull|a glowing skull]], surrounded by iron stakes and severed chains.

Writing around the chamber is pre-imperial and preserves a creation myth in which something made the world, regretted doing so, and has sought to undo it ever since. Frank identifies the script as older than the other imperial material the party has seen.

The skull does not respond when [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] attempts to turn undead. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] takes it from the chamber and later trades it away at the Broken Crown. In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], Lord Varrick's warning about Valedictus's three phylacteries and a spirit's nightmare of the skull in lizardfolk hands strongly implicate it as one of those phylacteries. Treasure found in the chamber also includes pre-imperial coinage and a black stone ritual knife.

## Chimera section

Beyond the explored halls, the party finds a broad natural passage marked by huge claw scars and a heavy animal smell. To the north is a vast cavern containing an ancient sleeping chimera with lion, goat, and dragon heads. The creature guards a hoard gathered over centuries and appears to have lived there for an extremely long time.

To the south is a smaller cavern with a vertical shaft and rung-like handholds. Above it, [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] glimpses a sealed low-domed chamber built from darker, older stone than the surrounding necropolis, with writing inlaid into the floor and no visible exits.

The Bonebreakers later kill the chimera with poisoned bait rather than a direct fight. The hoard includes pre-imperial and imperial coinage, gemstones, jewellery, a pre-imperial lion-faced shield, an imperial spear, a magical ring, a manual of strength, and a red crystal vial.

## Status

Partially explored. The full route, side chambers, and connection to other Necropolis entrances remain unresolved.
