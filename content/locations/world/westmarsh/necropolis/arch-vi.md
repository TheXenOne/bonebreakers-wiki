## Overview

Arch VI is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It has not been explored by the Bonebreakers, but [[npcs/necropolis/cornelius-vane|Cornelius Vane]] says he originally entered the Necropolis through this route from Westmarsh. From the Nexus, it glows white, smells of old smoke and mist, and shows a corridor sloping gradually upward to a staircase.

## Nexus view

Arch VI glows white and smells of old smoke and mist. It shows a corridor sloping gradually upward to a staircase.

## Known contents

- Sloping corridor.
- Staircase farther in.
- Old smoke and mist.
- Cornelius Vane's reported entry route from Westmarsh.

## Status

Unexplored by the Bonebreakers. Known indirectly from Cornelius Vane's account.
