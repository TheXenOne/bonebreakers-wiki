## Overview

Arch VI is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus and now appears to be the route to the ruin's outer entrance. From the Nexus it glows white, smells of old smoke and rust, and climbs through a corridor, staircase, and trapdoor into a rain-lashed gatehouse. From the ramparts, the Bonebreakers see skeleton archers patrolling the wall, the Necropolis and its great dome inward, and [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] beyond the outer doors. This strongly supports [[npcs/necropolis/cornelius-vane|Cornelius Vane]]'s claim that he entered through Arch VI from Westmarsh.

## Nexus view

Arch VI glows white and smells of old smoke and rust. It shows a corridor sloping gradually upward to a staircase.

## Known contents

- Sloping corridor.
- Staircase farther in.
- Old smoke and rust smell.
- Cornelius Vane's reported entry route from Westmarsh.
- A trapdoor opening into a rain-drenched gatehouse.
- Stairs up to a forty-foot-wide wall walk.
- Skeleton archers patrolling in pairs.
- A wall guardhouse with undead guards.
- A storage room containing rations, rope, iron spikes, signal flares, and an imperial pay chest.
- A view inward toward the Necropolis and its great central dome.
- A view outward toward [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]].
- Great iron doors marked with imperial runes and an opening mechanism worked out by [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]].

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], the Bonebreakers open Arch VI, climb into the gatehouse, and identify the route as a likely outer entrance to the Necropolis.
- A false [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] appears as the party prepares to return to the Nexus and is killed.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], a teleportation effect from Arch V briefly drops the party onto the outer dome and wall. The Bonebreakers destroy skeleton archers, clear a wall guardhouse, and return to the Nexus through the same outer-route area.

## Status

Partially explored. The party knows the gate mechanism and has cleared at least one wall guardhouse, but has not pushed beyond the guarded wall route.
