## Overview

Arch V is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. It has not been explored beyond its visible threshold. From the Nexus, it glows white, smells of dry dust and ancient cloth, and opens onto a broad underground boulevard lined with windows and doors.
