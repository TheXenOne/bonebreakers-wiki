## Overview

Arch V is one of the eight archways in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. From the Nexus, it glows white, smells of dry dust and ancient cloth, and opens onto a broad roofed underground boulevard lined with old homes. The Bonebreakers now know it contains zombie labourers, looping restoration work, false Barnys, and a route toward [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain.

## Nexus view

Arch V glows white and smells of dry dust and ancient cloth. It opens onto a broad underground boulevard lined with windows and doors.

## Known contents

- Broad underground boulevard.
- Windows and doors lining the route.
- Dry dust and ancient-cloth smell.
- Multi-storey homes with old apartments and hidden civilian caches.
- Silent zombie labourers carrying stone and wood in and out of buildings.
- An ochre jelly that ambushed the party from above and split during the fight.
- [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]], a zombie foreman wearing an imperial sash.
- A crossroads shrine with many divine idols, including three evil altered idols.
- A hidden shrine mechanism containing the [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]].
- Marked destinations beyond the explored route: the children's quarters, the foreman's station, and [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], the Bonebreakers enter Arch V, search old homes for treasure, fight an ochre jelly, and discover that [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]]'s ancient imperial armour is cursed.
- Barny convinces Mortimer that the party are inspectors, learning that the undead labourers endlessly restore the Bloated One's domain.
- At the shrine, the party destroys a false Barny and recovers a hidden map.

## Status

Partially explored. The route toward the Bloated One's domain remains open.
