## Overview

The Spider Market is a neutral monster market in [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]]. Orcs, hobgoblins, bugbears, lizardfolk, and other dangerous groups gather here under an uneasy truce to trade, bargain, and exchange information.

## Geography and layout

- The market is built among tents and old crumbling stone.
- It functions as both a trading site and an intelligence hub.
- Three rules govern the place: no violence, no broken deals, and no loose secrets.

## Associated people and groups

- Ash uses the market as a meeting point.
- Jeremy is first traced here.
- Adventurers, mercenaries, and monster factions all use the market.

## Campaign events

- The Bonebreakers first reach the market in [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]] while tracking Jeremy and Ash.
- Rumours gathered here point them toward [[locations/world/westmarsh/hobgoblin-mine|the Hobgoblin Mine]] and later war preparations.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], the market becomes the setting for tense negotiation with Ash and renewed Grey Syndicate intrigue.
