## Overview

Westmarsh is a monster-ridden wilderness west of the Southlands. It was once fertile and civilised, but a necrotic plague ruined the land more than two thousand years ago. In the present campaign, it becomes the centre of kidnappings, invasion plans, and ancient buried ruins.

## Geography and layout

- The frontier crossing near [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|West Fort]] controls one of the main routes in.
- The region contains monster markets, mines, invasion staging grounds, and later known links to [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]].
- Travel is consistently described as dangerous and poorly controlled.

## Associated people and groups

- Gnolls, goblins, bugbears, hobgoblins, kobolds, orcs, and undead all operate here.
- The Grey Syndicate uses the region as a covert base of operations.
- Dark legends place the King of Ghouls somewhere within Westmarsh.

## Campaign events

- The western frontier arc begins in [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]].
- [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]] through [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]] reveal coordinated military activity across the region.
- By [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]] and later, the marsh is also tied to Necropolis exploration and rumours of a second entrance.
