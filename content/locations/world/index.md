## Overview

This page gathers the major realms and regions currently known to the party. Most of the campaign so far takes place in [[locations/world/agria|Agria]], especially [[locations/world/agria/southlands|the Southlands]], but the Bonebreakers' adventures have also touched the wider frontier and the seas beyond.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]], the party pieces together the broader geography around Agria, Ikiria, the West Marches, and the road east.
- In [[sessions/session-014|Session 14]] and later, the campaign opens onto the wider world through the voyage to [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]].
- Political tensions between [[locations/world/agria|Agria]] and [[locations/world/ramen-empire|the Ramen Empire]] become explicit in [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]].
