## Overview

The Elven Lands are a secluded realm south of the hills beyond [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. They are not part of Agria and have remained politically and culturally isolated for centuries.

## Geography and layout

- Travel into the region is rare.
- The current state of the realm is poorly documented.

## Associated people and groups

- Elves are known for mastery of nature and beasts.
- Later rumours connect unusual hawk training to elves, but no proof is established.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], the party considers whether trained giant hawks might point toward elven involvement.
