## Overview

Vex's Warehouse is a warehouse in [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|West Fort]] used by Vex as both a criminal base and a hidden cache for weapons stolen from the fort.

## Geography or layout

- The warehouse lies within the fort settlement rather than out in Westmarsh.
- It contains concealed arms, working space, and later enough privacy to serve as a temporary covert base.

## Associated people and groups

- Vex uses the site as his operations base.
- The Grey Syndicate's local sabotage network is tied to the warehouse.
- The Bonebreakers later use it as a base while monitoring Jeremy and trying to reach the Syndicate.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], the party catches Vex here and uncovers the stolen barracks weapons.
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], the Bonebreakers use the warehouse as a base while tracking Jeremy to [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|the Spider Market]].
- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], they again use the warehouse while trying to reopen contact with the Grey Syndicate.
