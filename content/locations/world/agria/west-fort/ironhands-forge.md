## Overview

Ironhand's Forge is a smithy in [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] known for sturdy work and, on rare occasions, access to magical equipment for trusted defenders of the fort.

## Geography or layout

- The forge serves the practical needs of a frontier stronghold.
- It can also act as a source of exceptional equipment when the fort faces major danger.

## Associated people and groups

- Mara Ironhand is associated with the forge.
- Defenders of Westfort may receive gear here under conditions tied to the fort's survival.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]], Steve is loaned a charm-deflecting shield here on condition that he helps defend Westfort.
- Ulrich also secures enchanted plate during the same preparation phase.
