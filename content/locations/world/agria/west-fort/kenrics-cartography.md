## Overview

Kenric's Cartography is a mapmaker's shop in [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. Kenric's work is regarded as uneven by locals, but he possesses valuable knowledge of old roads, ruins, and [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]].

## Geography or layout

- The shop provides maps and local route knowledge useful to frontier expeditions.
- It becomes a practical resource for parties entering Westmarsh.

## Associated people and groups

- Kenric runs the shop.
- Sergeant Dara's bargain helps direct the Bonebreakers here.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], the party receives a Westmarsh map here and commissions another for 100 gold pieces.
