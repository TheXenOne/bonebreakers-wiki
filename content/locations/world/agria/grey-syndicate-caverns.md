## Overview

The Grey Syndicate Caverns are a concealed cave stronghold in the wooded highlands north of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], on the Bonebreakers' route towards [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]]. Orcs encountered on a trapped forest trail guide the party to its moss-hidden ravine entrance after offering passage in exchange for help against dark orcs. Familiar coded messages identify the caves as a [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] base. The Bonebreakers suspect it may be the network's principal lair, but its rank and full extent remain unconfirmed.

The stronghold holds provisions, contraband, correspondence, payments, and maps of a wider Northlands scheme. Its surviving cipher page gives the party a way to decode one of [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]]'s letters. The party has penetrated the complex and taken records, but has not defeated its defenders or secured control.

## Entrances and defences

The main entrance lies behind easily overlooked moss in a ravine. Arden calls the moss Morvald Moss after questioning it with Speak with Plants. Inside are six bedrolls, a campfire that had been cold for roughly a week, and a map of the caves.

A passage beyond the camp forces travellers into single file beneath murder holes. A wooden portcullis traps the party inside during its first approach. Arden sets it alight, and Steve later breaks through it during the retreat. A large wooden overhead covering lets the Bonebreakers pass under the arrows safely when they return, and it remains beyond the corridor.

A chimney above the eastern barracks opens onto a high forest trail with a wide view. Nila climbs it and establishes a second entrance. Listening holes in the stone carry sound between chambers, allowing possible surveillance. Who uses them, and whether the party has been overheard, remain unknown.

## Known areas

### Otyugh chamber

The trapped passage reaches a ledge above a natural chamber about forty feet across and deep, with exits on either side. Three otyughs occupy its floor. The Bonebreakers kill one, and two surrender, including one blinded by Ulrich. The survivors report ten orcs within the cave system and say both exits lead to barracks and deeper routes. They later demand a promise that the party will feed the cave orcs to them in exchange for passage.

### Eastern barracks

A sparsely furnished room beneath the chimney entrance holds plain black cloaks, rope, caltrops, arrows, and other equipment. One onward passage smells of dried meat and herbs, while running water can be heard from the other.

### Storeroom

Locally prepared meat and herbs could feed about twenty orcs for six weeks. Commercial crates contain rare dyes, silk, alchemical ingredients, jewellery, and silverware whose identifying marks have been removed. The Bonebreakers value the contraband at about 12,000 gold pieces and leave it here because removal will be difficult. The crates bear a trade label reading “Harromir.” Its place of origin is **Needs review**.

### Communications room

Familiar coded messages establish the Syndicate connection. Another copy of the [[items/poetry-cipher-book|poetry cipher book]] has its earlier pages removed but retains page 19, matching Lirina's corresponding encoded letter. A central brazier appears suited to burning messages after reading.

Ten packets marked with coded identifiers contain emergency payments worth about 10,000 gold pieces, probably intended for couriers. Messages, letters, and payment records concern nobles, merchants, and smugglers and appear usable for blackmail. The party takes the payments and records as evidence or possible leverage.

### Map room

A plain wooden table holds maps pinned at roads, rivers, noble estates, and [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]] trade routes. The arrangement suggests a scheme to unify the [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]], but no explanatory message establishes the purpose or method.

### Terraced river cavern

A natural chamber about eighty feet long, sixty feet wide, and thirty feet high contains three terraces and numerous stone columns. A river crosses the lowest level, with stepping stones across it and three exits from the chamber. The river's inlet is too low for useful transport. The Bonebreakers reach this chamber without establishing where its exits lead.

## Occupants and relationships

- The outside orcs negotiate against dark orcs, agree to leave the party alone while it rests, and escort its timber-cutting. Their identities, allegiance, and exact relationship to the cave's defenders remain unknown.
- The otyughs report ten orcs inside. Arrow fire remains a danger, and no individual cave defender has been identified.
- Two otyughs survive under an unfulfilled food bargain. One is blinded.
- The cave documents connect this site to the wider Syndicate network, but do not establish it as the sole or principal headquarters.

## Current status

Partly explored and contested. The cave orcs remain undefeated.
