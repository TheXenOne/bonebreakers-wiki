## Overview

The Anchor & Chain is the only inn on [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrow|Harrow's]] cramped docks. It is the Bonebreakers' first base in the town and the place where they publicly announce their presence while beginning the search for Princess Agatha.

## Geography or layout

- The inn is squeezed into the limited space available on Harrow's docks.
- Its common room is seedy, noisy, and filled with loud, brash patrons; fights break out among the customers.

## Associated people and groups

- [[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]] is the landlady.
- Pirates and other suspicious-looking visitors frequent the inn.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], Arden announces that the Bonebreakers are adventurers seeking work. Fen agrees to spread the word, while Barny asks where contraband can be sold.
