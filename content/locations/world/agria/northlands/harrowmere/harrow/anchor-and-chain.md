## Overview

The Anchor & Chain is the only inn on [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow|Harrow's]] cramped docks. Its noisy common room attracts pirates and rough travellers, while Fen Blackwater's private back room offers a place for discreet meetings. The Bonebreakers use it to meet both their hidden patron Agatha and Count Drago Ven.

## Geography or layout

- The inn is squeezed into the limited space available on Harrow's docks.
- Its common room is seedy, noisy, and filled with loud, brash patrons; fights break out among the customers.
- A windowless back room contains a single table and has a lockable door; Fen controls access.

## Associated people and groups

- [[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]] is the landlady.
- Pirates and other suspicious-looking visitors frequent the inn.
