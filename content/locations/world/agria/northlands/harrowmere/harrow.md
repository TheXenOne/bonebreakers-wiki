## Overview

Harrow is a fogbound port in the county of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere|Harrowmere]], within [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Agria's Northlands]]. Its pirate-filled docks and prosperous upper city support a busy trade in exotic and illicit imports. The Bonebreakers find Agatha hiding here and use local contacts to pursue her covert investigation into the Northlands conspiracy.

## Geography or layout

- Harrow clings to steep cliffs above cramped docks and smells strongly of brine.
- Ships approach through [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/narrows|the Narrows]], a confined route of fjords, steep cliffs, narrow waterways, and some artificial canals.
- The docks are noisy, dirty, crowded, and visibly dangerous, with pirates and other suspicious visitors moving through the area.
- [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow/anchor-and-chain|The Anchor & Chain]] is the docks' only inn and the Bonebreakers' first base in town.
- A rickety stair climbs from the docks through warehouses to the more developed upper city. Cargo suggests imports greatly exceed exports, including narcotics and exotic goods.
- A wealthy upper-city inn hosts gambling and illicit business; Lord Elron receives contacts there and at a café opposite.

## Associated people and groups

- [[npcs/harrow/rickart-the-reeve|Rickart the Reeve]] collects entry payments for the County of Harrowmere and demands 10,000 gold pieces from *Agatha's Favour* before accepting 7,500.
- [[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]], the landlady of the Anchor & Chain, is missing two fingers and agrees to spread word that the Bonebreakers are seeking work.
- [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/captain-winston|Captain Winston]] brings the party to Harrow under an East Agria Company charter.
- [[npcs/harrow/count-drago-ven|Count Drago Ven]] is Count of Harrowmere; [[npcs/harrow/lord-elron|Lord Elron]] claims broad knowledge of the town and brokers information.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], the Bonebreakers enter Harrow while trying to locate Princess Agatha. They make themselves conspicuous at the Anchor & Chain but do not yet find her.
- In [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]], the party meets Agatha in hiding, hires Drakford, and follows introductions through Elron and Count Ven to Brother Sedge in Kingsport.
- In [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]], financial records found in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]] include a payment for discretion in Harrow. Its recipient and purpose remain unknown; the party does not revisit Harrow.
