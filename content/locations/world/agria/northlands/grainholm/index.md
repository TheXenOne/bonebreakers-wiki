## Overview

Grainholm is the central agricultural county of the [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]]. It lies inland and consists largely of farmland, distinguishing it from the fishing economy of the northern Salt Coast.

## Geography and the Bonebreakers

The Bonebreakers learn where Grainholm lies while travelling from Harrow to Kingsport, but do not visit it directly.

The wooded highlands north of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] lie south of Grainholm. The Thorngate River continues north towards the sea. The Bonebreakers enter those woods while seeking [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]], without an established arrival in the county.

## Notable locations

- [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] — a monastic community and the party's destination in the poetry-cipher investigation.
