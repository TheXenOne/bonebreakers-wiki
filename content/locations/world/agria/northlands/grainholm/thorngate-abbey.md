## Overview

Thorngate Abbey is a monastic community in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm|Grainholm]], [[locations/world/agria|Agria]]. Its monks publish literary works, brew alcohol, and make other produce. It is the Bonebreakers' destination in their effort to decipher [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]]'s spy correspondence: [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]] identifies the abbey after tracing the poetry book used as her cipher to monastic copying. The party hopes to find another copy and the missing key there.

## Geography and approach

The Bonebreakers choose a concealed overland route north from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] to avoid the Grey Syndicate's orcs. Their route enters mountainous highlands and dense deciduous forest, with [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm|Grainholm]] beyond the woods and the Thorngate River continuing north towards the sea.

The abbey lies in Grainholm. Its exact site within the county is **Needs review**. The party has not reached it.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales]], Morel identifies the abbey as a lead for the cipher investigation. The Bonebreakers leave Ikiria to seek another copy of the poetry book, but encounters and traps interrupt their passage through the forest.

## Current status

The Bonebreakers are travelling towards the abbey. Whether it holds another copy of the poetry book, and whether that copy would supply the cipher key, remain unconfirmed.
