## Overview

Velur is a city in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]], approximately one day's travel from [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]]. [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of Veluria, has a palace here and will be hosting a masquerade at the start of the social season. Hugh Malory's account gives the Bonebreakers a lead for investigating her connection to Arthur.

## The masquerade

The gathering offers a possible opportunity to approach Lirina. The Bonebreakers have not yet visited Velur or secured admission. The date and admission arrangements: **Needs review**.
