## Overview

Kingsport is Agria's capital, in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]] in the western [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]]. Compact and conspicuously wealthy, it overlooks the nearby Tyne and is dominated by the Queen's Palace and [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]]. The Bonebreakers reach it by sea from Harrow to investigate Arthur's supporters for Agatha.

## Geography and appearance

Ornate royal architecture, elaborate gardens, and well-maintained buildings characterise the city. The party sees numerous domestic ships in the harbour and no naval fleet. Its merchant vessel attracts attention.

## Entry and authority

The Queen's Guard requires visitors to register and surrender weapons. Sir Alric Hale, Captain of the Queen's Guard, registers Ulrich as a pilgrim. He permits Drakford to remain armed as Ulrich's bodyguard. At the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/kingsport/guardhouse|guardhouse near the harbour]], the guards confiscate Ulrich's mace and lock it in a safe. Nila secretly steals the safe's key; the weapon remains there when the party leaves to find Sedge.

## Associated locations and people

[[npcs/kingsport/brother-sedge|Brother Sedge]] lives in a simple building across the city from the harbour. His access to Arthur's Palace gives the Bonebreakers their first route inside the prince's circle.

## Campaign events

In [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]], the Bonebreakers arrive after three days of sailing from Harrow. Tess impersonates Wendell Prye to secure Sedge's help entering Arthur's Palace.
