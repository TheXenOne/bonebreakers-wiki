## Overview

Harrow is a fogbound port in the County of Harrowmere, within [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Agria's Northlands]]. The Bonebreakers sail there aboard *Agatha's Favour* after receiving a letter apparently from [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]]. They arrive without knowing where Agatha is, making the port the starting point of their search.

## Geography or layout

- Harrow clings to steep cliffs above cramped docks and smells strongly of brine.
- Ships approach through [[locations/world/agria/northlands/narrows|the Narrows]], a confined route of fjords, steep cliffs, narrow waterways, and some artificial canals.
- The docks are noisy, dirty, crowded, and visibly dangerous, with pirates and other suspicious visitors moving through the area.
- [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrow/anchor-and-chain|The Anchor & Chain]] is the docks' only inn and the Bonebreakers' first base in town.

## Associated people and groups

- [[npcs/harrow/rickart-the-reeve|Rickart the Reeve]] collects entry payments for the County of Harrowmere and demands 10,000 gold pieces from *Agatha's Favour* before accepting 7,500.
- [[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]], the landlady of the Anchor & Chain, is missing two fingers and agrees to spread word that the Bonebreakers are seeking work.
- [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/captain-winston|Captain Winston]] brings the party to Harrow under an East Agria Company charter.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], the Bonebreakers enter Harrow while trying to locate Princess Agatha. They make themselves conspicuous at the Anchor & Chain but do not yet find her.
