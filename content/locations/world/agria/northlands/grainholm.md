## Overview

Grainholm is the central agricultural county of the [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]]. It lies inland and consists largely of farmland, distinguishing it from the fishing economy of the northern Salt Coast.

## Geography and the Bonebreakers

The Bonebreakers learn where Grainholm lies while travelling from Harrow to Kingsport, but do not visit it directly.
