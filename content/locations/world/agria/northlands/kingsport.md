## Overview

Kingsport is an independent city-state within the [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]] and the capital of Agria. Separate from neighbouring [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]], it is compact and conspicuously wealthy, overlooks the nearby Tyne, and is dominated by the Queen's Palace and [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]]. The Bonebreakers reach it by sea from Harrow to investigate Arthur's supporters for Agatha.

## Geography and appearance

Ornate royal architecture, elaborate gardens, and well-maintained buildings characterise the city. The party sees numerous domestic ships in the harbour and no naval fleet. Its merchant vessel attracts attention.

## Entry and authority

The Queen's Guard requires visitors to register and surrender weapons. Sir Aldric Hale, Captain of the Queen's Guard, registers Ulrich as a pilgrim. He permits Drakford to remain armed as Ulrich's bodyguard. At the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/guardhouse|guardhouse near the harbour]], the guards confiscate Ulrich's mace and lock it in a safe. Nila secretly steals the safe's key; the weapon remains there when the party leaves to find Sedge.
After [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]]'s arrest on evidence planted by the Bonebreakers, the guards take the party back to the dockside guardhouse to collect its weapons. The Bonebreakers return the stolen key by dropping it behind the confused guard and claiming he dropped it. The guards permit the party to remain armed until departure the following morning, then pay for an inn stay.

## Associated locations and people

[[npcs/kingsport/brother-sedge|Brother Sedge]] lives in a simple building across the city from the harbour. His access to Arthur's Palace gives the Bonebreakers their first route inside the prince's circle.

## Campaign events

In [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]], the Bonebreakers arrive after three days of sailing from Harrow. Tess impersonates Wendell Prye to secure Sedge's help entering Arthur's Palace.

In [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]], the palace search reveals Arthur's ties to Lirina and Veluria. Steve tracks Corvin to the entrance of the Royal Chamberlain's building, catches him when he runs, and returns him to the palace. Micaelis plants stolen jewellery on him, and Elspeth's support persuades [[npcs/kingsport/sir-aldric-hale|Sir Aldric Hale]] to arrest him.
