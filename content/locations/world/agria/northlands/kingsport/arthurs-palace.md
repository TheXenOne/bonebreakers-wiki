## Overview

Arthur's Palace is one of the two gleaming palaces dominating [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]]. It is the lavish household of [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]], now sparsely occupied and partly sealed for investigation following his arrest. Its servants, correspondence, and archives give the Bonebreakers evidence of Arthur's ties to [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]] and a lead toward Velluria.

## Access

[[npcs/kingsport/brother-sedge|Brother Sedge]], who formerly worked here, can unlock the entrance. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess]] impersonates Wendell Prye and claims an urgent need to help Arthur's defence, persuading Sedge to admit the party.

The party continues to claim that Wendell sent it, gaining limited cooperation from the household without producing written authority.

## Household and discoveries

- **Servants' quarters and kitchen:** a low-ceilinged, beeswax-scented entrance leads past a delivery ledger showing a sharp decline in activity after Arthur's arrest. [[npcs/kingsport/nessa|Nessa]], a nervous young chambermaid, recalls Arthur discussing Lirina while entertaining guests behind the private salon's serving door.
- **Private salon:** an ashtray holds a burned letter preserving only “Smith already knows.” Ulrich considers questioning a plant with magic but does not do so.
- **Gallery:** royal family portraits include Arthur, Agatha, and Audrey. Butler [[npcs/kingsport/hugh-malory|Hugh Malory]] identifies a recently removed portrait as Lirina. Selvar's ESP reveals that Hugh values the security of his employment more than Arthur personally. He provides the lead to Lirina's masquerade.
- **Writing room and archives:** a false-bottomed drawer holds an incomplete letter anticipating Agatha's disgrace and a duke fit to be king. Other drafts show Arthur's jealousy becoming more politically cautious. Tess pockets the hidden letter before Corvin arrives. He takes the other correspondence she hands over, seeming disappointed with it. A sealed cabinet holds travel and financial records, Agatha's correspondence, and Lirina's removed portrait. The party takes records and Agatha's letters, leaves the portrait, and replaces the seal.
- **Arthur's bedroom:** love letters addressed to Lirina and her warning, “Do nothing rash. Commit nothing to paper,” reinforce their personal connection. Her miniature is left behind when the party takes its locket.

The party also steals valuables from the ballroom, music room, and bedroom. Some of Arthur's jewellery becomes the planted evidence used against Corvin.

## Current status

The Bonebreakers leave several rooms unexplored when they end their search, concealing their interference with a royal seal. [[npcs/kingsport/elspeth-crane|Mistress Elspeth Crane]] remains in charge of the household and is still charmed when she supports the false accusation against [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]]. He is arrested for jewellery the party stole and planted on him. Nila retains the secretly taken palace key. Rumours that the Queen intends to close the palace permanently are unconfirmed.

## Open questions

The purpose of Corvin's repeated visits, the extent of Lirina's involvement, and the meaning of “Smith already knows” remain unresolved. The fate of the letters Corvin took: **Needs review**.
