## Overview

Arthur's Palace is one of the two gleaming palaces dominating [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]]. Associated with [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] and his household, it is the Bonebreakers' first destination in their covert investigation of his supporters. Count Venn says few people remain there; the party has entered but has not yet explored it.

## Access

[[npcs/kingsport/brother-sedge|Brother Sedge]], who formerly worked here, can unlock the entrance. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess]] impersonates Wendell Prye and claims an urgent need to help Arthur's defence, persuading Sedge to admit the party.

## Current status

The Bonebreakers are alone inside after Sedge's departure. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] has secretly taken the palace key. No remaining occupants or evidence within have yet been identified.

## Open questions

Whether the palace contains Arthur’s supporters or evidence of the wider conspiracy remains unresolved.
