## Overview

The Gilt Palace is [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]]'s palace in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]], dominating the city with an exterior that resembles both a fortress and an entertainment venue. The Bonebreakers infiltrate Lirina's masquerade here to investigate her relationship with [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] and whether she is involved in the threat to [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]]. Her private rooms upstairs hide evidence of her covert activities.

## Known layout

The interior is exceptionally rich and luxurious. A staircase descends into an enormous ballroom with numerous alcoves suited to private conversations. An ornate ducal chair stands empty beside the place where Lirina addresses her guests. Long silks suspended above the ballroom allow aerial performances. Besides its main doors, the ballroom has a door into the servants' quarters.

A balcony outside the private quarters overlooks densely planted gardens two storeys below. The grounds also contain stables and a guarded carriage exit.

## Lirina's private quarters

The countess's rooms lie upstairs. Her study contains encoded letters and the poetry book used as their cipher. Her bedroom wardrobe has a false back hiding a cupboard of dark clothing suited to covert work, and a secret exit leads from the bedroom into a back alley behind the palace. A locked closet barely large enough for one person serves as a small armoury of daggers, poisons, a disguise kit, and other assassin's equipment.
