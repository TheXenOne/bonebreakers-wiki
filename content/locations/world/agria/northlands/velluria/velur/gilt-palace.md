## Overview

The Gilt Palace is [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]]'s palace in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]], dominating the city with an exterior that resembles both a fortress and an entertainment venue. The Bonebreakers infiltrate Lirina's masquerade here to investigate her relationship with [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] and whether she is involved in the threat to [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]].

## Known layout

The interior is exceptionally rich and luxurious. A staircase descends into an enormous ballroom with numerous alcoves suited to private conversations. An ornate ducal chair stands empty beside the place where Lirina addresses her guests. Long silks suspended above the ballroom allow aerial performances.
