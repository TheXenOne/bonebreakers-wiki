## Overview

West Flow River is the ancient river beside [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|West Fort]]. Its crossing is strategically important enough that the settlement was once known as West Ford.

## Geography or layout

- The river marks a defining frontier crossing between Agria and [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]].
- The bridge and flanking towers control one of the main routes into the western frontier.

## Associated people and groups

- West Fort's garrison depends on controlling the crossing.
- Traders, soldiers, and raiders all value the route.
