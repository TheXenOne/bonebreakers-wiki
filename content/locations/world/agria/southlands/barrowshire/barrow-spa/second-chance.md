## Overview

The Second Chance is a resurrection service in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] run by Judas the Cleric. It offers *Raise Dead* for 1000 gold pieces.

## Geography or layout

- The establishment provides formal clerical resurrection magic.
- Judas charges extra for urgent night-time service.

## Associated people and groups

- Judas the Cleric runs the Second Chance.
- The Bonebreakers also use the site for poison treatment and other clerical aid.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]], Judas cures poison for party members after the Jeweller's Sanctum expedition.
- In [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], Arden is raised here after the retreat from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]].
- The site continues to serve as a dependable source of resurrection magic in later returns to Barrow Spa.
