## Overview

The Red Herring is an opulent, upmarket inn in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. It is run by Calley O'Toole and serves as one of the Bonebreakers' earliest recurring bases.

## Geography or layout

- The inn is presented as a high-end place to stay within Barrow Spa.
- It offers notably comfortable food and lodging by local standards.

## Associated people and groups

- Calley O'Toole owns the inn.
- Early party members and retainers repeatedly stay here between expeditions.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-001|Session 1]], the campaign begins here after the party's first Barrow outing.
- In [[sessions/session-002|Session 2]], Calley's reputation helps reassure the party that [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/bean-counter|the Bean Counter]] is trustworthy.
- In [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], Bran keeps Arden here overnight before returning to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/second-chance|the Second Chance]] for resurrection magic.
