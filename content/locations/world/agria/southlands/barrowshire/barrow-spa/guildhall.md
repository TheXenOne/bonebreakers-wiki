## Overview

The Guildhall is the grand seat of the Tourism Merchants' Guild in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. It is led by the self-important Guildmaster Reginald.

## Geography or layout

- The building is described as lavish and grandiose.
- It functions as a civic and commercial meeting place for contracts and local patronage.

## Associated people and groups

- Reginald leads the Tourism Merchants' Guild from here.
- The Guildhall serves as a contact point between the town's merchant interests and adventuring parties.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]], Reginald hires the Bonebreakers to investigate disappearances near [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]].
- In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], the Guildhall brings the party into contact with Jessum Tarantella and the troubles at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor|Tarantella Manor]].
