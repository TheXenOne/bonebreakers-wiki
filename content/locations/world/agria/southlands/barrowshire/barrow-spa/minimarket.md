## Overview

The Minimarket is a general store in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] run by Amadeus Thud. It stocks most ordinary adventuring goods.

## Geography or layout

- The shop serves as one of the party's regular resupply stops in town.
- It is associated with Spat, Amadeus Thud's one-eyed rat companion.

## Associated people and groups

- Amadeus Thud owns and runs the store.
- Spat is regularly present there.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-001|Session 1]], the party buys modest supplies here for its next Barrow expedition.
- In [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]], the Bonebreakers return for more gear while regrouping after resurrection.
- In [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]], Amadeus buys trade goods from the party and helps support the next marsh expedition.
