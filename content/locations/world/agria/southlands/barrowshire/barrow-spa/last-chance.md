## Overview

The Last Chance is a chaotic, cluttered resurrection service in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. It is run by Aldrich, an absent-minded magic user, and offers reincarnation magic.

## Geography or layout

- The shop is notably messy and haphazard.
- It offers *Reincarnate* for 500 gold pieces, or 250 gold pieces after undesirable results.

## Associated people and groups

- Aldrich runs the establishment.
- The Bonebreakers use it when lower-cost resurrection suits their situation better than formal clerical magic.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]], Virien is successfully reincarnated here and returns in his original body.
- Around [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]], the party returns here to restore Barny after the Shrine expedition.
