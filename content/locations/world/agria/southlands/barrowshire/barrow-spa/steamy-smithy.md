## Overview

The Steamy Smithy is a combined spa and smithy in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. It is run by Agar and Esme.

## Geography or layout

- The establishment combines relaxation facilities with smithing services.
- It includes changing rooms and spa space used by visitors and tourists.

## Associated people and groups

- Agar and Esme run the Steamy Smithy.
- Tourists and travelers use it as a place to rest, shop, and exchange local information.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]], the Bonebreakers visit the Steamy Smithy while gathering information before heading east.
- During that visit, two wererats attack patrons there and are exposed by the party.
