## Overview

Tarantella Manor is a decayed estate about an hour from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. Once built by the jeweller-magician Draxas Tarantella, it is overgrown, spider-themed, and hides the far more dangerous [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|Jeweller's Sanctum]] below.

## Geography and layout

- The visible manor is only part of the site.
- Beneath it lies a concealed workshop-dungeon full of traps, magical experiments, and sealed chambers.

## Notable sublocations

- Overgrown manor grounds
- Hidden access to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|the Sanctum]]
- Upper ruined rooms used as staging space between delves

## Associated people and groups

- Draxas Tarantella built the manor and the dungeon below it.
- Jessum Tarantella is the last known heir and hopes to restore the family name.

## Campaign events

- The Bonebreakers first reach the manor in [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]].
- The manor remains the surface approach for the multi-session delve through the dungeon below.
