## Overview

King's Road is a main travel route through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire|Barrowshire]], leading from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]] and beyond. Despite its traffic, it is considered dangerous because of disappearances.

## Geography or layout

- The road connects Barrow Spa with the marsh approaches and onward travel.
- Sections near Kelfrek Marsh become tied to monster attacks and missing travelers.

## Notable sublocations

- Approaches to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]]
- Nearby trails leading toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|the Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]]

## Associated people and groups

- The Tourism Merchants' Guild has a direct interest in keeping the road safe.
- Travelers, merchants, and adventurers all rely on the route.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]], attacks and disappearances along King's Road lead the Bonebreakers into Kelfrek Marsh.
