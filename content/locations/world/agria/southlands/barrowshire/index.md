## Overview

Barrowshire is the western barony of the Southlands. It is best known for [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], but the surrounding region also includes old burial sites, dangerous marshland routes, and estates with long-buried magical secrets.

## Geography and layout

- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] is the local capital and the party's first long-term base.
- The route east eventually leads toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]].
- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kings-road|King's Road]] runs through the barony toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]] and beyond.
- The region also includes [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]], [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor|Tarantella Manor]], [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]], and the road network around them.

## Associated people and groups

- The Tourism Merchants' Guild is influential in Barrow Spa.
- The Tarantella family legacy shapes one of the barony's most dangerous estates.
- The Bonebreakers repeatedly return here for trade, resurrection magic, and staging.

## Campaign events

- Most early sessions take place in and around Barrowshire, beginning with the delve into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]].
- The party later crosses the region on the way to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] in [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]].
- In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]] and [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], Barrowshire again serves as a safe commercial stop while the campaign focus shifts west.
