## Overview

Kelfrek Marsh is a dangerous bogland east of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kings-road|King's Road]]. It is known as the home of the Gloops and later becomes tied to Gruumsh's warband and the recurring expeditions around [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|the Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]].

## Geography and layout

- The marsh is difficult ground with bogs, sandstone cliffs, and hidden approaches.
- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kings-road|King's Road]] runs nearby but is considered unsafe because of disappearances.

## Notable sublocations

- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]]
- Routes along [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kings-road|King's Road]]
- Gloop-held marsh terrain

## Associated people and groups

- The Gloops are a known marsh people.
- Gruumsh One-Tusk and his orc warband later operate from the shrine complex.

## Campaign events

- The Bonebreakers first enter the marsh in [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]].
- They return repeatedly while dealing with Sootmurk and the shrine.
- Later sessions connect the old shrine to Gruumsh's hostage operation.
