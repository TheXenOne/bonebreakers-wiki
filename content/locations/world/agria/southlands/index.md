## Overview

The Southlands are a broad temperate region in eastern Agria. Much of the campaign has taken place here, especially around [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire|Barrowshire]], [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], and the frontier routes leading toward [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]].

## Geography and layout

- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] is the region's major coastal metropolis.
- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire|The Barony of Barrowshire]] lies farther west and includes [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]].
- The north-western countryside around [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] is marked by plague, pilgrimage, and later church conflict.

## Associated people and groups

- Princess Agatha holds the title Duchess of the Southlands and governs the region.
- The East Agria Company is one of the region's major power centres.
- The deathless plague shapes much of the region's recent history.

## Campaign events

- The party pieces together the geography of the Southlands while preparing for the journey east in [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]].
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Princess Agatha sends the Bonebreakers to end the plague centred on [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]].
- By [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], the campaign widens from Southlands politics into the western frontier.
