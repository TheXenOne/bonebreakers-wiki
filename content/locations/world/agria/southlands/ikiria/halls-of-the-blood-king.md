## Overview

The Halls of the Blood King are a vampiric manor that appeared outside [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] under the Blood Moon. The site is a shifting castle-manor full of imprisoned humans, vampire courtiers, hidden passages, and a hidden heart that sustains its ruler.

## Geography and layout

- The outer grounds lead into a layered manor with upper chambers, lower passages, prison cells, gardens, and an observatory.
- The site behaves like a hostile domain rather than a static noble house.

## Associated people and groups

- The Blood King rules the manor.
- The Princess of Blood pursues her own succession plot within it.
- Selana becomes an uneasy ally during the delve.
- Arun and other missing prisoners link the manor back to Ikiria.

## Campaign events

- The manor first becomes the party's focus at the end of [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]].
- [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]] through [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]] cover the full infiltration, prisoner rescue, and destruction of the Blood King's hidden heart.
- The halls begin collapsing once the heart is destroyed.
