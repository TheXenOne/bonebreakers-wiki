## Overview

The White Horse Inn is a rowdy tavern in the lower docks of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. It is associated with sailors and rougher dockside company.

## Geography or layout

- The inn is in the docks rather than the upper city.
- It serves as a place to find local fighters and hired help.

## Associated people and groups

- Virien visits the inn in a flashback.
- Kargor "Viperhand" is recruited here.
- Selvar also joins the party during this flashback sequence.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]], Virien visits the White Horse Inn to recruit help before the next stage of the cathedral assault.
