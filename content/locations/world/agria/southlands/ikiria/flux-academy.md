## Overview

Flux Academy is a prestigious institution of learning in the upper city of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. Entry is competitive, its grounds are grand, and its faculty includes several of the most respected scholars in Agria.

## Geography and layout

- The College of Wizardry studies arcane theory, spellcraft, and magical history.
- The Institute of Health focuses on healing, surgery, herbal medicine, and public health.
- The College of Practical Invention focuses on machines, potions, and magical design.
- The Historium studies history, philosophy, literature, and art.

## Associated people and groups

- Alric Zaxerus leads the College of Wizardry.
- Ulna Glitterpatch leads the Institute of Health.
- Herbert Nuel leads the College of Practical Invention.
- Illitharien Morel leads the Historium.
- Screever Longtooth is a key scholar tied to the Arkeus crisis.
