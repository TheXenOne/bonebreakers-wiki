## Overview

The Precisely Measured Scroll is a shop in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] that sells scrolls, inkwells, potions, and related goods. It is run by Helvian.

## Geography or layout

- The party finds the shop in the upper city during the sentient weapon crisis.
- It functions as both a merchant shop and an information source.

## Associated people and groups

- Helvian runs the shop.
- The shop provides information about [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]], Professor Screever Longtooth, and [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/tower-of-arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]].

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], Frank buys a potion of lesser restoration here.
- In the same session, Athlestan buys a map of the tombs of Westmarsh.
- Helvian tells the party that Longtooth is shielding Arkeus and that the city's merchant council is led by Nixx Pocketbrain.
