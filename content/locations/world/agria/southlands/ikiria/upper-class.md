## Overview

The Upper Class is a lavish inn on the edge of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]'s upper city. It is associated with nobles, merchants, and influential scholars.

## Geography or layout

- The inn sits near the upper district rather than the docks below.
- It is presented as a fine establishment suited to wealthier visitors.

## Associated people and groups

- The Bonebreakers stay here during multiple visits to Ikiria.
- Commander Smith later approaches the party here while in disguise.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]], the party stays here after first arriving in Ikiria.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], the Bonebreakers return here after the [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]] expedition.
- In [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]], Commander Smith meets the party here at breakfast before the mission against [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] continues.
