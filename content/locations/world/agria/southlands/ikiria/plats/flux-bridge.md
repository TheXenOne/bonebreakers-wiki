## Overview

The Flux Bridge is the northern internal crossing between sections of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]].

## Geography or layout

- It connects the commercial quarter to the central residential district.
- A waterfall flows beneath it.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], the party crosses the Flux Bridge heading south.
- Later in the same session, Luthien searches behind the waterfall beneath the northern bridge and finds nothing.
