## Overview

Madame Gorvetti's is a brothel in the central Plats known to Frank. It also serves as one of his informant contact points in Ikiria.

## Geography or layout

- It stands near burned blocks in the central district.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], Frank visits Madame Gorvetti's, pays 500 gold for information-gathering help, and hears that [[npcs/ikiria/nixx-pocketbrain|Nixx Pocketbrain]] dislikes [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]].
- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], Frank meets [[npcs/ikiria/gilly|Gilly]] here and finances her entry into upper-city society. She reports that Agatha remains missing and Commander Smith has also disappeared.
