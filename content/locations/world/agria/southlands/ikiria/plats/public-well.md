## Overview

The Public Well in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] is the main suspected origin point for the first wave of Ikiria's plague crisis.

## Geography or layout

- It stands in the northern commercial section of the district.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] says rumours quickly spread that the well was the source of the initial outbreak.
