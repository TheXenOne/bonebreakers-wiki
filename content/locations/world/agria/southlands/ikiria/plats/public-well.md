## Overview

The Public Well in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] is the confirmed origin point of Ikiria's poison crisis. In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], the party confirms that the water is poisoned, [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] purifies it, and ancient Westmarsh coinage is found planted at the bottom. The well is no longer an active danger, but the planted coinage still points to deliberate staging.

## Geography or layout

- It stands in the northern commercial section of the district.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] says rumours quickly spread that the well was the source of the initial outbreak.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve "Ligma" Smith]] confirms that the water is poisoned.
- In the same session, [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] descends into the well, casts Neutralise Poison, and recovers 400 pre-imperial gold pieces from Westmarsh.
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], Vaessa says the coin should not naturally be there and suggests the poisoning and planted coin may be separate staged acts.
