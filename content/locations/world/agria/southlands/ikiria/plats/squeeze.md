## Overview

The Squeeze is a crowded housing tenement in the central Plats that becomes a symbol of the district's dangerous overcrowding during the plague.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], a scavenger says displaced families are being forced into the Squeeze.
- Later the party sees a family pushed out because one child appears ill.
