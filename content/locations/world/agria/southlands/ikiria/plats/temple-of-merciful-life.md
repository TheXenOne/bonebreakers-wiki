## Overview

The Temple of Merciful Life is a dockside warehouse-front at the very north tip of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], established by Frank as a front-facing public temple for the [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenites]]. Behind that front, it also serves as a base for intelligence-gathering and informants. In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], it is burned down by civilian inquisitors.

## Geography or layout

- The temple stands at the northern tip of the Plats near the dockside commercial stretch.
- The front room is arranged as a simple temple space with a wooden altar, yellow cloth, and a painted sun symbol.
- A smaller shrine to Belenus stands in the back.
- The rear of the warehouse is used as a hidden operational base.

## Associated people and groups

- Frank establishes and decorates the site.
- Merrick is recruited here to spread Frank's preferred version of recent events.
- The site becomes a Bonebreakers base in Ikiria.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], Frank rents the warehouse in the lower city and establishes the Temple of Merciful Life.
- The party uses it as a base during the sentient weapon crisis in Ikiria.
- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], the party finds that the site has been burned down by civilian inquisitors during the plague unrest.
