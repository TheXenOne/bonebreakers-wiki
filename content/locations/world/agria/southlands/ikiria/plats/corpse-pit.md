## Overview

The Corpse Pit is the main burial site in the southern Plats and one of the key flashpoints of Ikiria's plague crisis.

## Associated people and groups

- [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] performs last rites here and keeps records of the dead.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], Maren tells the party that the number of bodies in the pit no longer matches the number delivered.
- The same session ends mid-fight after undead claw their way out of the pit, including zombies, ghouls, mummies, and other stronger undead.
- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], the battle continues, Maren says the undead do not belong here, and a wraith kills her before the aftermath is shown.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], the party ends the battle, recovers ancient imperial armour from the dead, and confirms that necrotic magic was cast in the graves recently.
