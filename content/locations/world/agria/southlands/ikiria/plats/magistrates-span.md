## Overview

The Magistrate's Span is the southern internal bridge in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]].

## Geography or layout

- It is an older mechanical bridge design.
- In Session 48 it is raised and stuck, with no operators present to lower it.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], the party improvises a crossing by passing a box back and forth over the span.
