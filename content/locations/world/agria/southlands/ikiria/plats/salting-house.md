## Overview

The Salting House is a named industrial site in the southern Plats. By [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], it is standing empty during the quarantine.

## Geography or layout

- It stands near the corpse pit and dockside work areas.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], the party notes the Salting House while moving through the southern district.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], the party finds the Salting House empty and recovers a fishing log from inside.
