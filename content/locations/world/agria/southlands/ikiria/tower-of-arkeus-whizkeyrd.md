## Overview

The Tower of Arkeus Whizkeyrd stands on the cliffs outside [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. Its architecture is impossible even before one enters: skewed geometry, shifting rooms, astral corridors, and magical devices all suggest prolonged instability around its master.

## Geography and layout

- Entry requires solving puzzle-like access points rather than forcing a simple door.
- The interior includes exhibit rooms, laboratories, damaged passages, detainment spaces, and an astral chamber at the heart of the crisis.
- The Harmonic Regulator and a translucent tuning fork are key objects tied to the tower's workings.

## Associated people and groups

- Arkeus Whizkeyrd built and occupied the tower.
- A clone-like Arkeus and several assistants are found within.
- Screever Longtooth directs the party here during the crisis.
