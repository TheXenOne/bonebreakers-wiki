## Overview

The East Agria Company Headquarters is a grand mercantile office in the upper city of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. It serves as a major political and commercial power centre and is closely tied to Princess Agatha's rule in the Southlands.

## Geography or layout

- The headquarters is described as a large, stately building decorated with fine mercantile goods.
- It functions as both an administrative centre and a place for high-level meetings.

## Associated people and groups

- Princess Agatha governs the East Agria Company.
- Commander Smith serves as head of security.
- Admiral Nelson later appears here after the Fos Imeras expedition.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], the Bonebreakers are summoned here and formally meet Princess Agatha in her full political role.
- The headquarters becomes the place where the party receives secret patronage and the mission to reclaim [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]].
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], the party again attempts to seek answers here during the Ikirian weapons crisis.
