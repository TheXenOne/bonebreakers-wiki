## Overview

House Varek was a powerful family of the ancient imperial city now preserved within [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The house has been forgotten in modern Westmarsh after plague destroyed most of the region's civilisation, a revelation that deeply upsets its surviving undead lord, [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varek|Lord Varek]].

## Known history

Carvings within [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varek-tomb|House Varek Tomb]] record the family's history and confirm that Lord Varek petitioned [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|High Priest Valedictus]] three times. Each petition was rejected. What Varek sought remains unclear.

## Current status

No living members or modern influence are known. Lord Varek and twelve other family sarcophagi remain within the tomb.
