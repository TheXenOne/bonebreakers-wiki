## Overview

The Kingdom of Agria is the main human kingdom tied to the Bonebreakers' travels. It is a feudal monarchy ruled by [[npcs/agria/queen-audrey-ii|Queen Audrey II]], with major power also held by nobles, cities, and mercantile interests.

## Purpose or role

Agria is home to the Bonebreakers and the setting for many of the kingdom's most important political struggles, especially the authority of [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], the frontier importance of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], and the succession crisis around [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]].

## Leadership

- [[npcs/agria/queen-audrey-ii|Queen Audrey II]] reigns as monarch.
- [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] governs the Southlands and is heir to the throne.
- [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] governs the Northlands and is later exposed as part of a treasonous plot.

## Allies and enemies

- Princess Agatha's bloc is closely tied to the [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]].
- Agria is politically threatened by the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]].
- The [[factions/governments/ramen-empire|Ramen Empire]] remains an external rival power.

## Campaign involvement

- The kingdom's structure becomes much clearer after the return from [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]].
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] and [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], the Bonebreakers uncover direct evidence that Prince Arthur is working with the Grey Syndicate.
- By [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], Arthur's conspiracy has become a major political lever, though its outcome is still unresolved.
- In [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]], Agatha remains in hiding and warns that her death would leave Arthur the only surviving heir despite his trial. The Bonebreakers investigate his supporters in the capital.
- In [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]], Arthur’s household yields an incomplete letter anticipating Agatha’s disgrace and a duke fit to be king. Servants repeat an unconfirmed rumour of permanent palace closure. The Queen’s Guard arrests [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] on evidence secretly planted by the Bonebreakers.
- In [[sessions/session-068|Session 68 - Ashes to Ashes]], the Bonebreakers identify Lirina as Ash and the Grey Khan. She claims her invasions were intended to stabilise Agria, admits using Arthur, and offers cooperation against Valedictus. The party has agreed to no alliance and has yet to report to Agatha.
- At the start of [[sessions/session-069|Session 69 - A Cruel Bargain]], the Bonebreakers reject Lirina's alliance because they cannot trust her and firmly commit to Princess Agatha.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales]], Lirina is jailed in Ikiria, where Arthur is also imprisoned. The Bonebreakers prohibit contact between them, warn of a likely breakout, and depart to pursue the poetry cipher at Thorngate Abbey without having reported to Agatha.
- In [[sessions/session-071|Session 71 - The Nineteenth Page]], the [[locations/world/agria/grey-syndicate-caverns|Grey Syndicate Caverns]] yield communications and payment records concerning nobles, merchants, and smugglers. A map table suggests a scheme involving Northlands unification without establishing its purpose, method, or participants.

## Current status

Active, with the royal succession threatened by Arthur's conspiracy and the Grey Syndicate.
