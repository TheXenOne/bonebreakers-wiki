## Overview

Flux Academy is a prestigious centre of scholarship in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. It is one of the city's most important institutions and repeatedly acts as a source of research, funding, and expert knowledge.

## Purpose or role

The Academy supports magical, historical, medical, and practical study. In the campaign, it helps launch the Fos Imeras expedition and later becomes central to the Arkeus crisis.

## Leadership

- [[npcs/flux-academy/ulna-glitterpatch|Ulna Glitterpatch]] is a leading medical scholar and the Academy figure most directly tied to the Fos Imeras mission.
- [[npcs/flux-academy/alric-zexerus|Alric Zexerus]], [[npcs/flux-academy/herbert-nuel|Herbert Nuel]], and [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]] represent major academic disciplines.
- [[npcs/flux-academy/screever-longtooth|Screever Longtooth]] becomes important during the sentient weapon crisis.

## Allies and enemies

- The Academy operates within the same civic world as [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] and the Merchant Lords of Ikiria.
- It becomes effectively opposed to [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]] during the sentient weapon crisis.

## Campaign involvement

- In [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]], the party begins working with Academy contacts after reaching Ikiria.
- In [[sessions/session-014|Session 14]], Ulna Glitterpatch identifies [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]] and funds the expedition.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], the Academy remains part of the party's post-expedition research and introductions.
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], it becomes a main institutional response point to the city's magical emergency.
