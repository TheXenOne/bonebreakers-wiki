## Overview

The Bonebreakers are the adventuring company at the centre of the campaign. The party formally adopts the name in [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]] and later grows into a force with property, followers, patrons, and political significance.

## Purpose or role

They are explorers, monster-hunters, problem-solvers, and increasingly power brokers. Over time they gain control of sites such as [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] and [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]].

## Leadership

Leadership is shared and situational rather than strictly formal.

## Allies and enemies

- The company often works with [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|the East Agria Company]] and Princess Agatha's bloc.
- It has direct conflicts with the [[factions/religions-and-cults/church-of-the-crimson-flame|Church of the Crimson Flame]], the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]], the [[factions/military-and-orders/orcs-of-the-marsh|Orcs of Kelfrek Marsh]], and the [[factions/ancient-powers/halls-of-the-blood-king-court|Halls of the Blood King Court]].

## Campaign involvement

- The name is adopted in [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]].
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]] to [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], the Bonebreakers become Agatha's covert agents and reclaim the Mercy.
- In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]] through [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], they become central defenders of West Fort and opponents of the Grey Syndicate.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] to [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], they hold politically dangerous evidence tied to Prince Arthur and the Syndicate.

## Current status

Active and increasingly influential.
