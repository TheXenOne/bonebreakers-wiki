## Overview

The Civilian Inquisition is the informal name for the inquisitors and supporters operating in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] during Ikiria's plague crisis. They target suspected magic-users and necromancers, encourage public panic, and act as if they hold policing authority despite [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] later stating that only the civil militia under her command are lawful.

## Purpose or role

The group claims or performs anti-necromancer enforcement during the plague crisis, but its leadership, doctrine, and backers remain unclear. Its actions have included burnings, obstruction, mob agitation, and claims against the Bonebreakers.

## Associated locations

- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|The Plats]]
- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|The Temple of Merciful Life]]

## Allies and enemies

- The inquisitors are hostile to suspected spell-users and increasingly hostile toward the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]].
- Vaessa Silvertread now treats their assumption of policing authority as unlawful.

## Campaign involvement

- In [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]], inquisitors are active around the burned Temple of Merciful Life and the wider unrest in the Plats.
- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], a mob encouraged by inquisitors accuses the Bonebreakers of necromancy.
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], witnesses back the Bonebreakers' account, and Vaessa promises to deal with the inquisitors.

## Current status

Active but politically exposed. Vaessa has accepted testimony against them and intends to act.
