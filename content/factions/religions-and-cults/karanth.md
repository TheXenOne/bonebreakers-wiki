## Overview

Karanth is known mainly through [[characters/inactive-players/matt/characters/athlestan-ghoulsbane|Athlestan Ghoulsbane]] and the hidden priesthood he describes. The faith appears less as a public church and more as a secret anti-undead order bound to oppose the King of Ghouls.

## Purpose or role

Karanth's clearest role is the struggle against undead, especially ghouls and the returning King of Ghouls.

## Leadership

- [[characters/inactive-players/matt/characters/athlestan-ghoulsbane|Athlestan Ghoulsbane]] is the only clearly identified priest.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Athlestan describes a hidden brotherhood of one hundred priests sworn to destroy the King of Ghouls.
- He also states that these priests have no temple, no home, and can never meet. The broader structure therefore remains mostly unseen.

## Allies and enemies

- Karanth's priests work alongside the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] through Athlestan.
- Their clearest enemies are undead forces, especially ghouls and the still-feared King of Ghouls.
- The faith proves compatible enough with the [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenites]] for Athlestan and Arden to reshape the Mercy together.

## Campaign involvement

- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Athlestan reveals the secret background of his order.
- After the cathedral arc, the Mercy is reshaped around both Belenus and Karanth. Needs review on the exact timing.
- In [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]], Athlestan raises a holy symbol of Karanth against undead during the Blood Moon manor expedition.

## Current status

Active, but mostly hidden from public view. Only Athlestan's place in the order is clearly established.
