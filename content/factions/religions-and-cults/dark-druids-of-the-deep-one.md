## Overview

The Dark Druids of the Deep One are strange druids encountered chanting in the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]] during the Bonebreakers' exploration of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]]. They say they worship the Deep One and that they are not from the market.

The group matters because they are an active, moving presence inside an already dangerous Necropolis district. They do not immediately attack the Bonebreakers, but they also refuse to guarantee safe passage.

## Known interaction with the Bonebreakers

In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], chanting draws [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] out of the counting house to investigate. Arden tries speaking in Common and preaching kindness, but the druids react oddly. Steve then speaks in the chaotic tongue and smooths over the interruption, avoiding an immediate fight.

## Beliefs and purpose

The druids say they worship the Deep One. Nothing else about the Deep One, the cult's doctrine, or the reason for their presence in the Sunken Market is confirmed.

## Current status

Active and at large in the Sunken Market. The Bonebreakers avoided a fight and returned to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] to rest, so the druids remain a possible hazard if the party re-enters the area.
