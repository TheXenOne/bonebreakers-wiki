## Overview

The Church of the Crimson Flame was the corrupted religious power centred on [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]. Once a sanctuary of Bahal, it became the heart of the deathless plague before the Bonebreakers destroyed its leadership.

## Purpose or role

The Church served as both a cultic faith structure and an armed stronghold. Its inner hierarchy included the Crimson Knights and the Sons of Bahal.

## Leadership

- [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]] led the church during its corrupted phase.
- [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]] commanded the Crimson Knights beneath him.
- [[npcs/mercy/sergeant-grimlock|Sergeant Grimlock]] helped enforce discipline among initiates and troops.

## Allies and enemies

- The Church included the Crimson Knights and Sons of Bahal as subordinate groups.
- It was opposed by the [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]] and later destroyed by the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]].

## Campaign involvement

- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], the party is secretly tasked with ending the plague centred on the church.
- [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]] through [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]] cover the assault, infiltration, interrogations, and final fall of its leadership.
- In [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]] and [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], the aftermath of its fall still shapes events at the reclaimed cathedral.

## Current status

Broken. Its leadership was destroyed, its cathedral was taken, and surviving personnel were either killed, captured, or absorbed into the Mercy's later order.
