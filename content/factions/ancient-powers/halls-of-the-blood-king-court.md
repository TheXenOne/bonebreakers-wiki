## Overview

The Halls of the Blood King Court was the vampiric ruling circle inside the shifting manor outside [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. It mixed open monstrosity with internal court politics and succession schemes.

## Purpose or role

The court defined the power structure of the Blood Moon manor arc, with the Blood King at its centre and several rival or subordinate figures manoeuvring around him.

## Leadership

- [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/the-blood-king|The Blood King]] ruled the manor.
- [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/princess-of-blood|Princess of Blood]] acted as an internal rival and aspirant successor.
- [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/lord-demetrius|Lord Demetrius]] and [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/circander|Circander]] were major court figures.

## Allies and enemies

- The court is internally divided, especially between the Blood King and his daughter.
- It is opposed by [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/selana|Selana]] and later by the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]].

## Campaign involvement

- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], the party enters the manor and confirms its threat.
- [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]] to [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]] reveal the court's rivalries, captives, and hidden weaknesses.
- In [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]], the Bonebreakers destroy the Blood King's heart and the manor collapses.

## Current status

Broken. The Blood King is dead, Demetrius and Circander are dead, and the court as a functioning power collapses with the manor.
