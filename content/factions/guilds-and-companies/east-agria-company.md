## Overview

The East Agria Company is one of the main mercantile and political powers in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. Under [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], it also serves as a base for covert action on behalf of the realm.

## Purpose or role

The Company combines trade power, political influence, and private operational reach. In the campaign, it is the organisation most directly responsible for employing and directing the Bonebreakers during the Mercy arc.

## Leadership

- [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] is Governor of the Company.
- [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] serves as head of security and field liaison.
- [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/admiral-nelson|Admiral Nelson]] commands its maritime arm in the published material.

## Allies and enemies

- Closely tied to [[factions/governments/kingdom-of-agria|the Kingdom of Agria]], especially Agatha's political bloc.
- Works with the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] during several major arcs.
- Indirectly opposed to the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] through the later Arthur conspiracy.

## Campaign involvement

- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], the party meets Agatha in her full political role and begins working under her secret patronage.
- Through [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]] to [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], Commander Smith helps drive the mission against the [[factions/religions-and-cults/church-of-the-crimson-flame|Church of the Crimson Flame]].
- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], the party again seeks answers through Company channels during the Ikirian weapons crisis.
- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], Pell is sent to Smith with warning about Prince Arthur.
