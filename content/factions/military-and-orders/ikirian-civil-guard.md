## Overview

The Ikirian Civil Guard is the city's organised security force. It is the main uniformed authority shown enforcing order during the sentient weapon crisis.

## Purpose or role

The guard maintains public order in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] and, during emergencies, appears to operate under martial law.

## Leadership

- [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] is captain of the civil guard in the published material.

## Allies and enemies

- The guard operates within the same city power structure as [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] and the [[factions/governments/merchant-lords-of-ikiria|Merchant Lords of Ikiria]].
- It becomes practically opposed to [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]] during the tower crisis.

## Campaign involvement

- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], Vaessa authorises the party to enter Arkeus's tower and offers payment for proof of his involvement in the city's magical emergency.
