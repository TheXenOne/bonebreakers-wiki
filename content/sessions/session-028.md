# Session 28 - From Stars to Blood

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-027|Session 27 - Reflections of the Mind]], [[sessions/session-029|Session 29 - The Blood Moon Rises]]

## Narrative recap

The tower arc ends with the Bonebreakers finally learning what Arkeus was doing: he had been anchoring a dangerous rift, and the magical disorder in Ikiria was fallout from that effort breaking down. To stabilise the situation, the party retrieves the Harmonic Regulator, navigates more bizarre objects and temptations inside the tower, and forces a meeting between clone-Arkeus and the real mage.

The resolution is messy rather than clean. Real Arkeus is freed, both versions of him vanish into the astral disturbance, and the crystal golems have to be destroyed before the party can escape. Ikiria's weapon crisis ends, the party is rewarded, and almost immediately the campaign pivots toward a new problem: a rotting rose, missing noble children, and the Blood Moon castle beyond the city.

## Important events

- The crystal golems explain that Arkeus is anchoring a rift.
- Selvar is distorted after touching one of the motes.
- The Harmonic Regulator is recovered from the tower's secured area.
- Barny is overcome by a rude tome, and Frank is tempted by visions tied to a pale dagger.
- Selvar and Luthien repair the snapped lever at the Threshold Gate.
- Clone-Arkeus is dragged before the real Arkeus.
- Real Arkeus is freed, and both Arkeuses are pulled into an astral portal.
- The crystal golems are destroyed.
- The party escapes the tower by retracing the rug-based exit.
- Professor Longtooth rewards the party for ending the crisis.
- Arun arrives with a rotting rose and news of missing noble children.
- The party heads for the castle and fights its gate guards.
