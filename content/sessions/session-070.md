## Narrative recap

After the fatal [[sessions/session-069|hostage ambush]], [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] tries to help the surviving farmer and his wife. Following [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]]'s example of generosity towards followers of [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenus]], he offers them 500 gold pieces. The parents are offended. They explain that they are subscribers to Belenus and feel betrayed because their faith did not protect their daughter.

Arden approaches them and explains that death need not be the end. He offers to take both parents and their daughter's body to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], where she could be resurrected. The farmer refuses: he does not believe in that kind of magic and simply wants his daughter's body returned. Arden repeats the offer, but neither parent accepts. Steve instead offers to pay for the funeral, which they agree to. This softens their anger somewhat, but they want nothing more to do with Belenus. Arden agrees to cancel their subscription and waive the cancellation fee.

The Bonebreakers continue to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] and take [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]] into the dungeons. [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] berates the countess over the hostage's death while the party thoroughly inspects the prison to satisfy itself that she can be held securely.

The Bonebreakers warn the guards to expect an attempt to break her out. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] assures them that this is the safest available place to hold her. They specifically order the guards never to allow Lirina to speak with [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]], who is also imprisoned in Ikiria. Having rejected her proposed alliance and committed themselves to [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]], they leave the exposed Grey Khan in custody rather than continue travelling with her.

At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]], the party visits [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]]. He reports on the research into [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]] that they commissioned long ago, explaining that it was once an ancient gnomish shrine. He has also decoded a book they previously brought him. It describes an ancient gnomish headquarters on an island at sea, containing some kind of laboratory. The island's identity and the laboratory's location remain unclear.

The Bonebreakers ask Morel about the half-orc poet whose work provides the cipher for Lirina's encoded correspondence. He has never heard of the poet, despite confirming that the book was published in Agria, and considers the poetry extremely well written. Further examination reveals that monks copied it. Morel identifies [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]], where monks publish literary works, brew alcohol, and make other produce.

The party decides that the abbey may hold another copy of the poetry book and potentially the missing key to Lirina's letters. To avoid the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]'s orcs, they choose the most concealed route they can through unfamiliar parts of Agria. They head directly north from Ikiria towards mountainous highlands covered in dense forest. [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm|Grainholm]] lies beyond the woods, with the Thorngate River continuing north towards the sea. The abbey remains their destination; they have not reached it.

Shortly before entering the woods, the party encounters several jackalweres. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/akal-dreek|Akal]] enlarges himself, and Arden casts Growth of Animal on Akal's wolf. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] casts Fireball into the attackers. Steve and [[characters/active-players/john/characters/michaelis|Michaelis]] are caught at its edge but dodge the worst of the blast. The Bonebreakers defeat the jackalweres and recover gems and money, but Akal's wolf is killed in the fighting.

The party enters the thick, gloomy deciduous forest and camps overnight. During the following day's travel, a huge winged creature approaches above the trees. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] casts Fly and takes to the air. Arden places Bran's Heroic Aura on Steve and casts Striking on Michaelis, who braces his spear. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] blesses the party, Rose climbs a tree, and [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] hides to gain an advantage.

As the creature emerges, the Bonebreakers identify it as a wyvern. Bran's Heroic Aura forces it to attack Steve, who is ready and avoids the blow. Barny blinds it, Michaelis strikes with his spear, and the others join the attack. Steve delivers the killing blow. The party finds 2,000 electrum pieces on the creature.

Barny then raises the dead wyvern in full view of his companions. Arden sees the strange magic and becomes deeply suspicious. He still trusts Barny enough to withhold an immediate confrontation, but the creature's apparent return to life leaves him uneasy.

Farther through the forest, the Bonebreakers find a giant boar's corpse, apparently about a day dead. Its wounds suggest that swords killed it. Arden casts Speak with Plants on a nearby tree and names it Trevor. The two become friendly, and Arden asks about a damaged hanging branch.

Trevor explains that orcs damaged the branch while fighting the boar, then hurriedly tried to conceal the evidence of the encounter. At the tree's request, Arden cuts off the injured branch and has Steve fashion it into a cudgel. Hoping to return someday, Arden leaves the glass eyes from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]] in a small nook in Trevor's trunk.

Continuing north, Steve trips over a cord trap. He pulls on the cord and traces it to a long black piece of wood, but nothing obvious happens. The party camps again. Before sleeping, Arden investigates the raised wyvern with Detect Evil and confirms that it is undead. His suspicions about Barny grow considerably, although no confrontation follows.

The following day, the party heads northeast and encounters seven dire wolves. Ulrich uses Speak with Animals to explain that the Bonebreakers are passing through and following the same route as the orcs. The wolves begrudgingly agree to a truce, provided the party leaves immediately. The Bonebreakers move on without fighting them.

Steve notices a trail ahead but does not want to follow it. Arden consults another tree with Speak with Plants and tries to name it Tybalt. The grumpy tree rejects the name and asks to be left alone, but tells him that many orcs use the nearby trail and have passed along it recently.

Arden heads up the trail. A trap triggers, dropping something onto him and injuring him. The Bonebreakers have found evidence of recent orc movement along their concealed route, but have not established who those orcs serve. The object that falls on Arden and the trap's wider purpose are **Needs review**.

## Important events

- The bereaved parents refuse resurrection, accept Steve's offer to fund the funeral, and leave the Belenite faith.
- Lirina is placed in Ikiria's prison. The party warns of a likely breakout and forbids contact between her and Arthur.
- Morel identifies Fos Imeras as an ancient gnomish shrine and reports an island headquarters with a laboratory described in a decoded book.
- The poetry cipher leads the Bonebreakers towards Thorngate Abbey in search of another copy and the missing key.
- Akal's wolf is killed during the jackalwere battle.
- The party kills a wyvern; Barny openly raises it, and Arden later confirms that it is undead.
- Trevor describes orcs killing a giant boar and concealing the evidence. Another tree confirms recent orc traffic on a nearby trail.
- Ulrich negotiates passage past seven dire wolves. Arden then triggers a trap on the orc-used trail.
