## Narrative recap

With [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] under arrest, the Bonebreakers are pulled straight into Ikiria's plague crisis. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] still does not know where [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] is, and the city is sealed into controlled zones. [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]], already stretched thin, sends the party down into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] because the burial records no longer match the bodies reaching the pits. What sounds like an accounting problem quickly becomes a walk through a lower city buckling under quarantine, fire, hunger, and fear.

Moving through the district, the Bonebreakers find barricades, overcrowded streets, ruined blocks, and the burned [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|Temple of Merciful Life]], a direct blow to their public religious foothold in Ikiria. [[factions/other/civilian-inquisition|Civilian inquisitors]] are acting as if they have lawful authority, targeting suspected magic-users and making desperate people more afraid of help, with reports of people being burned around [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/pyre-square|Pyre Square]]. Ulrich cures a visibly sick child, proving that at least some cases behave like ordinary disease, and Frank gives the displaced family enough gold to matter. At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/madame-gorvettis|Madame Gorvetti's]], Frank pays for an informant and hears that [[npcs/ikiria/nixx-pocketbrain|Nixx Pocketbrain]] dislikes Princess Agatha and resents her influence. Even the city's infrastructure is failing: the Bonebreakers have to cross the stalled Magistrate's Span by passing a box back and forth over the raised gap.

At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/corpse-pit|the Corpse Pit]], [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] gives the clearest theory so far. Her records show the dead in the pits no longer matching the number of bodies delivered, and she believes the crisis has more than one layer. The first wave looks like poisoning, likely connected to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]], while the later wave looks like disease spreading through overcrowding after the quarantine trapped people together. The party finally has a working model: deliberate contamination may have started the crisis, but civic panic and containment have made it much worse.

Before the party can finish sorting disease from sabotage, the dead rise around them. Zombies and ghouls come first, followed by a wight, mummies, and finally a shroud wraith from the cemetery. The bodies are not simply fresh plague dead: many are wrapped in sailcloth, marked with ancient imperial script, or carved with glowing necromantic inscriptions. The Bonebreakers fight them back, but the evidence now points beyond a normal plague and toward deliberate necromancy layered on top of Ikiria's civic collapse. The session ends with the battle still escalating and Maren's investigation suddenly becoming a fight for survival.
