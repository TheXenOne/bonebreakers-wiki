## Narrative recap

With [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] under arrest, the Bonebreakers are pulled immediately into Ikiria's deeper crisis. [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] sends them down into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], where the dead no longer match the burial records, civilian inquisitors are burning property, and the lower city is buckling under plague, fear, and overcrowding.

What begins as an investigation becomes a tour through a collapsing district. The party finds barricades, ruined blocks, the burned [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|Temple of Merciful Life]], displaced families, and signs that the plague may not be one single illness at all. By the time they reach the corpse pit and meet [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]], the picture is even worse: she believes an initial wave of poisoning was followed by ordinary disease spread through crowding, while the dead themselves may now be tied to something necromantic and older.

That suspicion turns into immediate danger when undead begin clawing up from the pit in front of them. The Bonebreakers destroy zombies, ghouls, a wight, and several mummies, but the details only grow stranger. The corpses bear ancient imperial markings, necromantic inscriptions, and signs that they may have reached Ikiria by magical means rather than burial alone. The session ends with the fight still escalating as a shroud wraith rises in the cemetery.

## Important events

- At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/east-agria-company-headquarters|East Agria Company Headquarters]], the party confirms that [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] is not considered missing even though [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] does not know her whereabouts.
- [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] asks the party to investigate missing bodies in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] and directs them to [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]].
- The party descends into the quarantined lower district and witnesses barricades, inquisitor activity, and severe overcrowding.
- The Bonebreakers find that [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|the Temple of Merciful Life]] has been burned out by civilian inquisitors.
- [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] cures a sick child, and [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] gives the displaced family 100 gold pieces.
- Frank pays for an informant at Madame Gorvetti's and hears that [[npcs/ikiria/nixx-pocketbrain|Nixx Pocketbrain]] dislikes Princess Agatha and resents her influence.
- The party crosses the stalled infrastructure of the Plats by passing a box back and forth over the raised Magistrate's Span.
- [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] explains her belief that Ikiria's crisis began with poison, then worsened through ordinary disease and civic panic.
- Undead rise from the corpse pit and cemetery, including zombies, ghouls, a wight, mummies, and finally a shroud wraith.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]]'s Cure Disease works on one visibly ill child, supporting [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]]'s claim that at least part of the crisis now behaves like ordinary disease rather than a purely necromantic affliction.
- Maren's records suggest the dead in the pits no longer match the number of bodies delivered there.
- Maren believes the plague has two causes: an initial poisoning, likely tied to the well in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]], followed by disease spread through overcrowding.
- The undead rising from the pit are wrapped in sailcloth marked with ancient imperial script and bear glowing necromantic carvings.
- The wight's ancient imperial dress and the markings on the corpses suggest a link to old Westmarsh or the [[quests/major/active/necropolis-beneath-the-mercy|Necropolis Beneath the Mercy]] rather than a normal city burial.
- [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] discovers that at least some of the stronger undead are immune to mundane weapons.
