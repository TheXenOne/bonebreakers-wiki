# Session 1 - Into the Barrow

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-002|Session 2 - Bones, Fairy Deals, and Fallen Friends]]

## Narrative recap

After escorting Quintius out of the Barrow in the previous expedition, Virien and Barny return to the Red Herring with his promised ruby amulet and a few new leads. Quintius claims there are still secret chambers in the Barrow and mentions a fairy who has long lived within it. The party also receives substantial IOUs from the freed silk merchants, which helps fund another expedition.

Arden, Malakai, Ike, and Karsin join Virien and Barny for a renewed push into the Barrow. The group finds the entrance inscription changed again, fights off skeletons, and investigates a marble slab marked with an X, only to draw the attention of the carcass crawler. Karsin is paralyzed in the fight, but the party survives and pushes deeper.

Later, the group burns a giant black widow in its web before it can wake and discovers a narrow natural tunnel that could serve as an escape route. A final zombie attack is handled quickly, and the party makes it back to town alive. Despite surviving several threats and learning more about the Barrow's layout, they return with no treasure.

## Important events

- Quintius gives the party a ruby amulet and speaks of secret chambers and a resident fairy in the Barrow.
- The freed silk merchants reward Virien and Barny with valuable IOUs.
- Arden, Malakai, Ike, and Karsin join the expedition.
- The Barrow entrance seals behind the party after they enter.
- The entrance inscription has changed from its earlier wording to "All who enter are doomed."
- The party is ambushed by skeletons and later defeats the carcass crawler.
- Barny harvests paralysis venom from the carcass crawler.
- The group burns a giant black widow in its web before it can attack.
- The party discovers a narrow tunnel that appears to offer another way out of the Barrow.
- A zombie is killed during the retreat, and the group returns to town without loot.
