## Narrative recap

After escorting [[npcs/barrowshire/the-barrow/quintius|Quintius]] out of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]], [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] and [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] regroup at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/red-herring|the Red Herring]] in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. Quintius pays them with his promised ruby amulet and adds two useful leads: the Barrow still hides secret chambers, and a fairy has long lived somewhere inside. The freed [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/silk-merchants|silk merchants]] also reward Virien and Barny with valuable IOUs, giving the group enough backing to consider another delve.

The expedition grows when [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden Sable]], [[characters/inactive-players/don/characters/malakai|Malakai]], [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ike|Ike]], and [[characters/inactive-players/vince/characters/karsin|Karsin]] join Virien and Barny, drawn by treasure, work, and in Arden's case a particular hatred of undead. After buying supplies from [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/amadeus-thud|Amadeus Thud]] at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/minimarket|the Minimarket]], they return to the Barrow. The entrance seals behind them again, and the warning over the antechamber has changed from Virien's altered wording to "All who enter are doomed," showing that someone or something is still reshaping the place.

Inside, the party tries to push beyond the routes already known from the first rescue. Skeletons ambush them from behind, but Arden turns them long enough for the group to survive and continue. A rough marble slab marked with an X proves dangerous to investigate: Arden's tapping draws the carcass crawler, which paralyzes Karsin before Virien and Ike help bring it down. Barny harvests the creature's paralysis venom, turning the fight into at least one useful gain.

The party then follows the crawler's route and finds a giant black widow sleeping in its web. Rather than risk a direct fight, they burn it with oil before it can wake, then press on to a crossroads and discover a narrow natural tunnel that may serve as another way out of the Barrow. A final zombie catches the rear of the party while Ike and Malakai scout, but Malakai drops it with a clean crossbow shot. The group escapes alive and with a better understanding of the Barrow's layout, though the expedition ends without treasure and leaves several mysteries active: the changing inscription, the marked slab, Quintius' secret chambers, and the fairy he mentioned.

- The Barrow entrance seals behind the party after they enter.
- The entrance inscription has changed from its earlier wording to "All who enter are doomed."
- The party is ambushed by skeletons and later defeats the carcass crawler.
- Barny harvests paralysis venom from the carcass crawler.
- The group burns a giant black widow in its web before it can attack.
- The party discovers a narrow tunnel that appears to offer another way out of the Barrow.
- A zombie is killed during the retreat, and the group returns to town without loot.
