## Narrative recap

Back in [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] finally catch hold of the conspiracy weakening the fort from within. [[characters/inactive-players/connor/characters/cyder|Cyder]], a newly graduated magic-user, joins the party at [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/cracked-shield|the Cracked Shield]], where the serving girl [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/sarla|Sarla]] watches them too closely. That night, after Akal scouts the kitchen in tiny form and Barny stays awake studying, the inn catches fire. The blaze has clearly been set near the kitchen logs.

Akal raises the alarm while Barny runs back in to save the tavern cat. The party and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Sir Cedric]] rescue [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/osric-tam|Osric]], [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/lianna|Lianna]], merchants, and other trapped guests, with Cyder's Web helping cushion people thrown from the upper floor. The first floor collapses, but the inn is not lost completely, and Osric promises the Bonebreakers free food, drink, and lodging.

Suspicion falls on Sarla, who breaks under pressure and admits that [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/vex|Vex]] forced her to start the fire. The Bonebreakers race to [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/vexs-warehouse|Vex's warehouse]] and catch him trying to flee. Inside, they find the stolen swords and shields from Westfort's barracks. Under Barny's charm, Vex confesses to burning the granary, poisoning the water, stealing the arms, and ordering the inn fire on behalf of an unknown hooded employer. He also reveals that [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/veteran-borak|Veteran Borak]] has been ordered to kill Sarla.

The party reaches Sarla just as Borak slits her throat. Ulrich blinds him, Cedric saves her life, and Borak is captured. Rather than expose everything immediately, the Bonebreakers convince [[npcs/west-fort/commander-valerius|Commander Valerius]] to leave the warehouse as bait and wait for Vex's handler. Barny uses the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]] to spy through Vex while the ambush is prepared.

After several nights, the hooded contact arrives at the bridge with four orcs. Cyder webs him, Cedric reveals an unfamiliar human face beneath the hood, and the party disables or captures the orcs. By session end, Westfort's stolen arms and local sabotage cell have been exposed, but the captured handler points to a larger network still operating beyond Vex.
