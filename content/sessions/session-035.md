## Narrative recap

Back in Westfort, the Bonebreakers finally catch hold of the conspiracy destabilising the fort. What starts as a tavern fire at the Cracked Shield becomes a chain of confessions, arrests, and emergency rescues that tie arson, theft, and sabotage back to a handler network operating inside and around the settlement.

By the end of the session the party has exposed Vex, saved Sarla from an attempted silencing, recovered the stolen arms cache, and baited out an even more important contact at the bridge. The immediate crisis is contained, but only by revealing that the real organisers are still at large and working with hired orc muscle.

## Important events

- Cyder joins the party in Westfort.
- The Cracked Shield is set on fire during the night.
- The Bonebreakers rescue Osric, Lyanna, merchants, and others from the burning inn.
- Osric promises the party free food, drink, and lodging in future.
- Sarla confesses that Vex forced her to start the fire.
- Vex is caught at his warehouse after trying to flee.
- The warehouse is found to contain the weapons stolen from Westfort's barracks.
- Under Charm, Vex admits to arson, poisoning the well, and theft on behalf of a hooded employer.
- Veteran Borak tries to silence Sarla and cuts her throat before Cedric saves her life.
- The party convinces Valerius to leave the warehouse in place as bait.
- After several nights of waiting, the hooded handler appears with four orcs at the bridge.
- Cyder webs the handler, and the party captures him and the orcs.
