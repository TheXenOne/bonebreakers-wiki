# Session 38 - The Siege Yet to Come

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-037|Session 37 - Drums Beneath the Hills]], [[sessions/session-039|Session 39 - Ashes of the Siege]]

## Narrative recap

Rose's reconnaissance pays off immediately. Her closer look at the fortress reveals invasion plans, a hawk feather matching the kidnappers' trail, and a partial garrison preparing openly for war. The Bonebreakers translate the captured documents and confirm that Westfort is a near-term target.

Rather than simply warn the fort, the party strikes. They break into the visible section of the stronghold, kill its officers, and nearly commit to holding it, only to discover a hidden passage opening onto a far larger invasion force with siege engines and allied troops waiting beyond. They escape with only seconds to spare and return to Westfort bearing two urgent warnings at once: a siege is coming, and the Mercy is facing a poisoning crisis of its own.

## Important events

- Rose scouts the fortress through a high skylight.
- She discovers a forge, command room, invasion map, and a hawk feather matching earlier evidence.
- Rose sabotages the forge with oil and escapes with the plans and documents.
- Luthien and Selvar translate the papers and confirm a planned invasion of Agria with Westfort as a target.
- The party assaults the outer fortress after sleeping its guards.
- Several hobgoblin officers are defeated in heavy fighting.
- The Bonebreakers loot gold and powerful equipment from the fallen.
- A crowned hobgoblin opens a hidden passage and reveals a much larger army beyond.
- The party blocks pursuit with its last jar of dragon grease and rides back to Westfort.
- Commander Valerius begins preparing for siege.
- News arrives from the Mercy about poisonings and unrest around Bishop Arden's leadership.
- Barny uses the crystal ball and confirms both the priestly sickness and Jeremy's presence near the cliffside fort.
