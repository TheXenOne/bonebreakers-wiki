## Narrative recap

After the shrine expeditions, the Bonebreakers spend two weeks recovering in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] recovers from resurrection sickness and studies, the party upgrades gear and wagons, and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] begins publicly preaching Belenus by giving money to the town's poorest residents. The group also brings its stranger trophies to [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/aldrich|Aldrich]], who identifies [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|the Red Seer's Dagger]], [[items/party-inventory/sword-of-mulvis|the Sword of Mulvis]], a potion of plant control, and the indestructible, poison-proof [[items/party-inventory/crown-of-king-bomfluke|Crown of King Bomfluke]].

Soon afterward, [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/reginald|Reginald]] summons the Bonebreakers to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/guildhall|the Guildhall]] with another problem suited to treasure-hunters. [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum Tarantella]], the last heir of a declining family, wants help restoring [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor|Tarantella Manor]]. Town rumors describe shrieks, scratching, and eerie humming from the house, and connect the estate to Jessum's grandfather, [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/draxas-tarantella|Draxas Tarantella]], a jeweller and magician said to have left behind wealth, traps, and trouble beneath the manor.

At the manor, Jessum proves humble and desperate. He tends the party's horses himself, shows them the faded grandeur and spider motifs of the house, and explains that the noises come from Draxas's old workshop below. A dwarf he previously sent into the place lasted only an hour before screaming. Jessum offers the Bonebreakers whatever treasure they can recover if they clear the danger and help him reclaim the estate.

The party descends into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|the Jeweller's Sanctum]], arranging the knock signal "Shave and a Haircut" so Jessum can let them out again. The dungeon is a damp spiral of limestone, cobwebs, giant rat droppings, footprints, carved black and white faces, and trap design. At the first checkered-floor trap, [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] tests the mechanism, jams the blade slits with spikes, and confirms that the black tiles are safe.

Past the trap, manic laughter and scratching lead the Bonebreakers into a giant-rat nest. They clear the rats, and Barny uses the Red Seer's Dagger on a fleeing rat to see through its eyes as it reports to [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/rat-queen|the Rat Queen]] in a deeper parlor. The nest also contains [[items/party-inventory/morlans-marvelous-magic|Morlan's Marvelous Magic]], a spellbook with Sleep and Light, making the name Morlan important long before the party meets him. The session ends with the group standing before a second checkered-floor trap, now committed to the manor's deeper pattern of traps, vermin, and hidden magical legacies.

- The party recovers Morlan's Marvelous Magic from the rat nest.
- The session ends at a second checkered trap deeper in the sanctum.
