# Session 8 - The Jeweller’s Sanctum

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-007|Session 7 - The Price of Greed]], [[sessions/session-009|Session 9 - Tarantella's Tune]]

## Narrative recap

After resting in Barrow Spa, the Bonebreakers spend part of their new wealth on equipment and cosmetic upgrades, while Arden openly begins preaching Belenus to the poor. The party also visits Aldrich, who identifies some of the stranger trophies from the shrine: the Red Seer's Dagger, the Sword of Mulvis, a potion of plant control, and the indestructible, poison-proof Crown of King Bomfluke.

Soon afterward, Reginald sends the Bonebreakers to meet Jessum Tarantella, the last heir of a ruined family manor plagued by shrieks, scratching, and humming from below. Rumors about Draxas Tarantella, Jessum's jeweller-magician grandfather, suggest that the estate hides both treasure and deadly traps. Jessum confirms as much and offers the party whatever riches they can recover if they clear the danger beneath the house.

The Bonebreakers descend into the Jeweller's Sanctum, a damp spiral dungeon full of checkered-floor traps, giant rats, and signs of old magical work. Barny uses the Red Seer's Dagger to spy through a fleeing rat's eyes, glimpsing a rat queen in a deeper chamber. By the session's end, the party has crossed the first deadly floor, looted Morlan's Marvelous Magic from a rat nest, and reached a second checkered trap deeper in the sanctum.
