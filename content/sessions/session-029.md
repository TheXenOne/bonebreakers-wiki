## Narrative recap

Under the Blood Moon, the Bonebreakers enter the castle grounds and begin working room by room through a hostile, shifting stronghold. Their early exploration reveals undead hounds, barricaded chambers, a letter tied to Arun, and a hidden way downward that confirms the manor is not simply a noble house gone wrong.

The session's biggest gains come from information rather than direct victory. The party meets Selana, who explains how the Blood King is protected, where the key to his heart may be found, and how dangerous the manor's vampire court is. They also secure the rose-crested key, learn that the Blood King can be shaken by his own guilt and shame, and leave with a much clearer sense of how this nightmare domain might eventually be broken.

## Important events

- The party enters the castle grounds beneath the Blood Moon.
- Undead hounds are fought and turned back.
- Tess finds a dead priest carrying holy water, a ring, and coin.
- The Bonebreakers scout several rooms before breaking in through a window.
- A letter from Elara to Arun is found in a dormitory.
- A hidden stair leads to deeper, fungus-scented passages.
- Athlestan's divination points to the strongest undead presence above.
- The party meets Selana, who opposes the Blood King.
- Guards dragging prisoners are attacked, and a blood-drinking carpet is encountered.
- The armory is looted.
- A mirror encounter with Alvalus reveals the Blood King's regret, guilt, and shame.
- The party recovers a rose-crested key and an ivory comb.
