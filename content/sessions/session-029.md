## Narrative recap

Under the Blood Moon, the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] enter the grounds of the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king|Halls of the Blood King]] and begin testing the manor's defenses. Tobold scouts ahead through decaying roses and draws the attention of three undead hounds. The fight reveals how hostile the grounds are: the hounds do not bleed, one nearly crushes Tobold, Barny's new [[items/party-inventory/staff-of-withering|Staff of Withering]] proves poorly suited to undead, and Athlestan has to drive the creatures back with the holy symbol of [[factions/religions-and-cults/karanth|Karanth]]. Nearby, Tess finds a dead priest by a melted fence and recovers holy water, a ring, and coin.

The party circles the manor, peering through red stained-glass windows into feasting murals, a ruined armory, and a barricaded room full of moans. When the main door refuses to open, Athlestan smashes a window and the group climbs into a cramped dormitory. A letter from [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/elara|Elara]] to [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/arun|Arun]] confirms that the missing noble boy has personal ties inside, and Athlestan's divination reveals that the strongest undead presence lies above them. The Bonebreakers also find stairs descending through red-veined stone and fungal air.

The most important discovery is [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/selana|Selana]], a pale armoured vampire-hunter barricaded in a trap-filled room with a bound vampire. She is not undead, but she has been trapped in the shifting manor since her own party was wiped out, surviving by feeding from the prisoner. Selana tells the Bonebreakers that the manor holds at least a dozen vampires and worse creatures, that ordinary violence may only make them reform, and that [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/the-blood-king|the Blood King]] can only be killed by destroying his hidden heart. The key may be somewhere in the southeast wing, and the manor itself may move through the world. The party accepts an uneasy alliance with her.

The manor gives them no quiet time to plan. The Bonebreakers attack armoured guards dragging prisoners, kill them, and discover that the red carpet drinks blood like a living tongue. Tobold stakes Selana's bound vampire, and the party explores the ruined armory before seeing a procession of unnervingly orderly spiders. In the entry chamber they find bloodstained garments, a waiting dwarven statue, and sealed central doors leading toward greater danger.

In the southeast, the party finds a room full of hats and mirrors. Barny triggers the central mirror and meets [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/alvalus|Alvalus]], the Blood King's reflected guilt, regret, and shame. Alvalus says the Blood King would tremble if shown the mirror, though his servants would try to destroy it first. The session ends with another practical gain: in a torture-reeking guest room, Athlestan smashes a tooth-lined armoire and recovers a rose-crested key and an ivory comb, while Tess claims vampire-scented gothic clothes that may help the party pass deeper into the manor.
