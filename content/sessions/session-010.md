# Session 10 - Pentagrams and Pitfalls

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-009|Session 9 - Tarantella's Tune]]

## Narrative recap

After the retreat from the spider chamber, the Bonebreakers regroup in Barrow Spa. Judas cures Virien and Barny of poison for a negotiated fee, the silver statues are sold through the Bean Counter, and Barny hires a new thief named Elon. With fresh supplies and Jessum's continued cooperation, the party returns to the Jeweller's Sanctum almost immediately.

This expedition opens up several new parts of the dungeon. In a forge room decorated with efreet imagery, the party claims rings, ingots, and a sigil-etched crucible. In a separate chamber with a pentagram and elemental murals, two statue guardians demand a "seal of approval." The Bonebreakers outmaneuver them by retreating into the spike-pit room and shoving them into the traps before looting the chamber's contents.

Among the new finds are a ruby ring that seems to grant fire protection, a sealed envelope for Jessum, a large tome of magical research, a rat-haunted workshop with uncut gems and a key, and finally an anti-magic chamber containing a chained iron casket. Inside are five mysterious rings and a note reading "Morlan, hands off!" Barny puts on one of the rings and is immediately cursed, ensuring that whatever Morlan once wanted to keep away from this room remains dangerous.
