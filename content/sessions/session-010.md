## Narrative recap

After the retreat from the spider chamber, the Bonebreakers regroup in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] rather than letting the sanctum sit untouched. At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/second-chance|the Second Chance]], [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/judas|Judas the Cleric]] cures [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] and [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] of poison for a negotiated one-time payment, while Rhian is left to recover in bed. The party sells the silver statues through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/bean-counter|the Bean Counter]], restocks, and Barny hires a new thief named Elon after smoothing over an awkward negotiation as a "wizard's jest."

With fresh support, the party returns to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor|Tarantella Manor]] sooner than [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum]] expects. They re-enter [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|the Jeweller's Sanctum]] and head back toward the spider chamber, using it as a hub for unexplored side rooms. The first new chamber is a forge decorated with fiery efreet and jeweller's work. There the Bonebreakers claim a sigil-etched crucible, gemmed rings, and ingots of silver, gold, and platinum, adding immediate treasure while also deepening the elemental pattern of Draxas's workshop.

South of the spider room, the party finds a more dangerous chamber: elemental jewel murals, a pentagram inlaid in the floor, a desk with a ruby ring, a large tome, and a sealed envelope, and two greatsword-wielding statue guardians. The guardians demand a "seal of approval," and the Bonebreakers' attempted tricks fail. Rather than fight them on their terms, the party retreats into the spike-pit room. Barny turns invisible and shoves one statue into a pit, while Bran risks the swinging blades to topple the other, turning an old trap into the solution to a new guardian.

The chamber's loot advances several threads at once. Barny skims the genie-skin tome and finds magical research. Virien pockets an envelope marked "for my grandson, Jessum," clearly intended for the manor's heir. Arden tests the ruby ring and discovers it protects him from flame. Barny also uses the Red Seer's Dagger to check on the rat he marked earlier, seeing a deeper laboratory strewn with dead rats and giving the party another destination to pursue.

The Bonebreakers continue through a Zorn-themed workshop, where seven centipedes and more rats attack before Barny's Sleep spell helps stabilize the fight. They recover 37 uncut gems and a key, then follow the western route to a room of corroded scrap metal and a locked iron casket chained inside a glowing purple salt circle. Virien steps into the circle and feels his magic items suppress, revealing an anti-magic effect. The newly found key opens the casket, which contains [[items/party-inventory/five-cursed-rings|five rings]] and a note reading "Morlan, hands off!" Barny puts one on and is immediately cursed, turning Morlan's name from a spellbook clue into a clear warning that the sanctum's old owner or assistant left dangerous things behind.
