## Narrative recap

Falling rocks strike [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] as he triggers the trap on the forest trail north of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. Orcs appear in the surrounding trees with bows trained on the Bonebreakers, taking the party completely by surprise. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] and [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/akal-dreek|Akal]] address them in Chaotic. Steve convinces them that he is an orc raised by humans, although they remain confused about why he and Akal travel with humans.

The orcs offer passage through their territory if the party helps them against the dark orcs. Akal deliberately gives his companions a different account: he claims that the orcs are frightened of the Bonebreakers and are offering a cave for the night. When their leader descends from the trees, Arden tries to look boastful and intimidating, but the orc shows no fear. He leads them into a ravine and pulls aside a curtain of moss that conceals the entrance to a [[locations/world/agria/grey-syndicate-caverns|cave network]]. The party begins to feel uneasy.

Akal scouts inside alone and finds six bedrolls around a campfire, with a map of the caverns beyond. Outside, Arden casts Speak with Plants on the moss, which mostly answers, “I am moss.” It says only one person has entered recently, referring to Akal, and says none of those entering were green. Arden names it Morvald Moss. The others enter, and Akal estimates that the fire has been cold for about a week. Arden relights it.

Akal says there is an exit at the back and proposes clearing the caverns before resting. Arden remains wary: they supposedly came here to sleep. He stays at the rear as the party enters a passage so narrow that they must walk in single file. A large wooden portcullis slams down behind them, and arrows pour through murder holes above. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] hides while Steve, Arden, and [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] take heavy wounds. Arden throws oil onto the portcullis and sets it alight as the party runs deeper into the caves.

The corridor opens onto a ledge overlooking an enormous natural chamber, roughly forty feet across and forty feet deep, with exits to the left and right. Large holes have been made in its stone walls. Akal secures a rope to the ledge while Nila hears dripping water. As the party starts descending, Steve climbs along the walls and later pulls Akal back up. Akal shrinks himself to investigate one of the holes. Drawing on his duergar knowledge, he recognises listening holes designed to carry sound between chambers: someone may be overhearing the party.

Three shapes below, initially mistaken for piles of refuse, begin to move. Huge tentacles rise, and a voice enters the Bonebreakers' minds, accusing them of desecrating the creatures' home. The creatures are otyughs. One attacks [[characters/active-players/john/characters/michaelis|Michaelis]] twice and infects him through its bite with a disease that will kill him in six days unless cured.

The fight quickly becomes a retreat. Arden is almost killed and climbs back to the ledge, beyond the otyughs' reach. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] strikes one hard and blinds it with Light, while Steve lassoes Michaelis and pulls him to safety. Akal and Ulrich are both struck as they try to escape. Akal contracts the lethal disease as well, and Ulrich barely survives. The entire party reaches the ledge before anyone is killed.

From above, Arden casts Striking on Akal, Steve fires his enormous bow, and Michaelis throws the Imperial spear he recovered from the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|Necropolis]], missing his target. Nila kills one otyugh. The two survivors, including the blinded creature, surrender and offer information if the Bonebreakers leave them alone. They say ten orcs live in the cave system and explain that barracks lie beyond either exit, with both routes leading deeper underground.

The party decides to retreat to the campsite, but must pass through the murder-hole corridor again. Akal admits that he lied about the outside orcs being frightened, drawing a suspicious look from Arden. After drinking healing potions, the Bonebreakers form a shield wall and run through the passage. The archers inflict further heavy wounds, especially on Nila, while Ulrich withstands an extraordinary number of arrows. Steve leads the charge and smashes through the burning portcullis, bringing everyone back to the entrance camp.

Steve and Akal go outside and tell the original orcs that the party intends to rest. The orcs agree to leave them alone. Arden rigs a noisy warning trap across the dangerous corridor, and the night passes without incident. Ulrich cures Akal's disease and helps heal the party. Deeply grateful, Akal tells him that he must follow the underground gods to be so blessed. Arden cures Michaelis's disease and spends all his available spells healing his companions. Both infections are removed before they can kill their victims.

The following day, the party cuts down a tree under the outside orcs' escort and spends the day constructing a large wooden covering, shaped like an upside-down canoe. Carrying it overhead, the Bonebreakers run through the trapped passage again. This time the arrows strike harmlessly into the wood. They leave the covering beyond the corridor and return to the otyughs. The survivors demand a promise that the party will feed the cave orcs to them in exchange for passage. The Bonebreakers nervously agree and take the eastern exit. The promise has not yet been fulfilled.

The route reaches a sparsely furnished barracks with daylight falling through a chimney-like opening above. Black cloaks, rope, caltrops, arrows, and other supplies lie around the edges. Arden takes a plain black cloak, and Steve gathers the remaining equipment. Nila climbs the chimney and emerges onto a forest trail on high ground with a wide view, discovering another entrance to the caves. She jumps back down, and Steve and Akal catch her in a cloak. Of the barracks' two onward passages, one smells of dried meat and herbs, while Akal hears running water from the other. The party follows the smell.

The storeroom beyond holds enough locally prepared meat and herbs to feed about twenty orcs for six weeks, or a larger group for less time. Sealed commercial crates bear trade markings reading “Harromir.” The place behind that name is **Needs review**. Opening them reveals contraband, rare dyes, silk, alchemical ingredients, jewellery, and silverware with identifying marks deliberately removed. The Bonebreakers estimate the goods at about 12,000 gold pieces, but leave the crates in place because carrying them out will be difficult.

Two passages continue from the storeroom, one towards running water and one silent. Following the silent route, the party enters a communications room. Familiar coded messages lie in a desk, and the Bonebreakers realise that they have entered a [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] lair, perhaps even its principal stronghold. Its place in the wider network is not established.

They find another copy of the [[items/poetry-cipher-book|poetry cipher book]] they have been seeking. Its earlier pages are missing, but page 19 survives intact. It matches the encoded letter recovered from [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]], previously identified as number 19 and titled “On Love and Power.” The Bonebreakers can now decode that particular letter, potentially gaining evidence against the imprisoned countess. The letter has not yet been decoded, and the discovery does not supply the missing keys for every letter.

A brazier in the centre of the room appears to have been used to burn messages after reading. The party also finds ten packets marked with coded identifiers. Arden is cautious, and Nila checks them for traps before Steve tears one open. It contains coins, gems, and what appear to be noble banknotes. The Bonebreakers open the rest, finding emergency payments worth about 10,000 gold pieces in total, probably intended for couriers. Further messages, letters, and payment records concern nobles, merchants, and smugglers, with much of the material apparently suitable for blackmail. The party takes the payments and records as potential evidence or possible leverage.

A passage from the communications room leads to another cavern with a plain wooden table covered in maps. Pins mark roads, rivers, noble estates, and [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]] trade routes. The arrangement suggests a wider plan involving the unification of the [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]], but no accompanying explanation reveals its purpose or intended means. The party's interpretation remains a suspicion.

Of two further exits, the Bonebreakers follow the one towards running water. It opens into a natural cavern about eighty feet long, sixty feet wide, and thirty feet high, divided into three terraces among stone columns. A river cuts across the lowest level, with stepping stones providing a crossing. Three exits lead away, while the opening through which the river enters is too low to offer a useful transport route. The party reaches this chamber without yet confronting the cave orcs. [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] remains unreached, and the new evidence has not been delivered to [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]].

## Important events

- The outside orcs offer safe passage in exchange for help against dark orcs. Akal conceals the terms, then admits his deception after the party is badly hurt underground.
- A portcullis and murder-hole corridor force the Bonebreakers deeper into the cave network. Steve later breaks through the burning barrier during their retreat.
- The party kills one otyugh and accepts the surrender of two. The survivors report ten orcs in the caves and later demand that those orcs be fed to them.
- Ulrich cures Akal's lethal infection, and Arden cures Michaelis's. Akal's gratitude prompts him to credit Ulrich's magic to the underground gods.
- An overhead wooden covering defeats the corridor's arrow fire, allowing exploration to resume. Nila discovers a second entrance through the barracks chimney.
- Coded messages identify a Grey Syndicate stronghold. Page 19 of another cipher book supplies the key for Lirina's corresponding letter.
- The Bonebreakers seize emergency payments and records concerning nobles, merchants, and smugglers. Valuable contraband remains in the storeroom.
- A map table suggests a wider Northlands scheme without explaining it. The party reaches a terraced river cavern, leaving the cave's defenders and further routes unresolved.

## Locations visited

- [[locations/world/agria/grey-syndicate-caverns|Grey Syndicate Caverns]] — the moss-hidden ravine entrance, entrance camp, murder-hole corridor, otyugh chamber, eastern barracks, storeroom, communications room, map room, and terraced river cavern.
- **The high forest trail above the barracks** — a second cave entrance reached by Nila through the chimney.
- [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] — intended destination, not reached.

## NPCs and characters involved

- **Bonebreakers:** Arden, Steve, Akal, Nila, Michaelis, Ulrich, and Barny.
- **The outside orcs and their leader** — negotiate in Chaotic, guide the party to the cave, permit its rest, and escort its timber-cutting. Their names and allegiance are unknown.
- **Morvald Moss** — Arden's name for the moss hiding the ravine entrance, questioned with Speak with Plants.
- **Three otyughs** — one killed and two alive after surrender, with one survivor blinded. The survivors provide information and bargain for the cave orcs as food.
- **The cave orcs** — ten reported by the otyughs. Arrow fire threatens the party, but no individual defender is identified or confronted face to face.
- [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]] and [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] — absent. The recovered cipher and records bear on the investigation for Agatha.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- **[[items/poetry-cipher-book|Poetry cipher book, page 19]]:** intact in the cave copy, matching Lirina's numbered letter. The preceding pages are removed. The letter has not yet been decoded.
- **Syndicate communications and payment records:** messages concerning nobles, merchants, and smugglers, taken for evidence or possible blackmail. Ten coded emergency-payment packets contain about 10,000 gold pieces in valuables, probably for couriers.
- **Contraband crates:** about 12,000 gold pieces in goods, left in the storeroom. The trade label “Harromir” has no confirmed identification: **Needs review**.
- **Pinned maps:** roads, rivers, noble estates, and Company routes suggest a Northlands scheme. Its objective and method remain unknown.
- **Listening holes:** Akal identifies an acoustic means of monitoring conversations between chambers. Whether anyone overhears the party is unknown.
- **Disease and cures:** otyugh bites infect Michaelis and Akal with lethal diseases. Arden cures Michaelis, and Ulrich cures Akal. Neither infection remains active.
- **Improvised protection:** the wooden overhead covering blocks the corridor's arrows and is left beyond the passage. The cavern map found at the entrance and the barracks equipment aid exploration without establishing control of the lair.
