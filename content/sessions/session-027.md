## Narrative recap

Inside [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/tower-of-arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus' tower]], the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] follow the newly opened passages into a gallery of magical curios. Several objects are taken or triggered: [[items/party-inventory/drinkers-babel|Drinker's Babel]], the [[items/party-inventory/screaming-inkwell|Screaming Inkwell]], the [[items/party-inventory/the-laughing-blade|Laughing Blade]], the [[items/party-inventory/face-changing-golden-coin|Face-Changing Golden Coin]], and Athlestan's [[items/party-inventory/the-memory-glove|Memory Glove]]. The inkwell warns them that they missed something in the entrance room, while the glove gives Athlestan the clearest long-term clue of the night: the [[npcs/westmarsh/king-of-ghouls|King of Ghouls]] is in Westmarsh.

The tower keeps resisting ordinary exploration. A melted half-humanoid thing retreats from Athlestan's holy symbol, a hidden lever reveals a sealed forcefield chamber, and the party splits between a psychic fissure and a longer route through abandoned rooms. They find a dead half-elf, a frozen furniture workshop, identical corpses, and a glass mallet dangerous enough to shock several party members. Obeying the inkwell's message, Selvar searches the entrance room again and finds the translucent [[items/party-inventory/astral-tuning-fork|Astral Tuning Fork]] that [[npcs/flux-academy/screever-longtooth|Longtooth]] described.

The fork confirms that the tower's planar machinery is central to the crisis, but it does not immediately solve it. Deeper inside, Barny scouts a laboratory full of green vats and half-formed bodies, including figures resembling the party. There the Bonebreakers meet an ink-stained version of [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]], who appears to be a clone or copy of the original. He offers a bargain: bring him the Harmonic Regulator from the forcefield-protected detainment room, and he will explain how to cross the Threshold Gate.

The bargain becomes more complicated when Athlestan pulls a failsafe lever and the clones attack, but the party survives and pushes onward through a library of animated books and loose spells. Moving the spell-cage barring the way triggers uncontrolled magic and gives Arden a clairvoyant glimpse of the real Arkeus suspended in astral light, watched by two crystal golems. Selvar then uses the tuning fork to dissolve the Threshold Gate. The session ends with the true Arkeus hanging as a living anchor, the Harmonic Regulator still unrecovered, and clone-Arkeus still expecting the party to honour their bargain.
