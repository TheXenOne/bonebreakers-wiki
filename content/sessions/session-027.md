## Narrative recap

Inside Arkeus' tower, the Bonebreakers move through a sequence of magical exhibits, dead-end puzzles, and half-stable laboratories. Harmless curiosities quickly give way to unsettling clues: a speaking inkwell, relics that show visions, hidden levers, abandoned corpses, and evidence that whatever happened in the tower fractured both its architecture and its master.

By searching more carefully, the party finds the translucent tuning fork Professor Longtooth wanted. They also uncover clone-like versions of Arkeus and learn that one of them wants the Harmonic Regulator from a detainment room in exchange for help crossing the Threshold Gate. The session ends with the Bonebreakers reaching the astral chamber, where the real Arkeus hangs suspended beyond the tower's last defenses.

## Important events

- The party investigates a room full of magical curios.

- The group splits to explore separate routes through damaged parts of the tower.
- The entrance room search uncovers the translucent tuning fork.
- The party finds clone-Arkeus and his assistants in a laboratory.
- Clone-Arkeus offers information in exchange for the Harmonic Regulator.
- Athlestan pulls a failsafe lever that destroys one of the vats.
- The Threshold Gate is dissolved with the tuning fork.
- The Bonebreakers reach the chamber where the real Arkeus hangs suspended beside two crystal golems.
