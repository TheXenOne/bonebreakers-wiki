## Narrative recap

The [[quests/major/completed/renewed-siege-of-westfort|battle for Westfort]] continues with the Bonebreakers already behind the hobgoblin lines and the fire from their oil trap spreading across the field. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] and [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] slip into position behind the siege engines, while [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/wistor|Wistor]] sets a catapult ablaze with his fiery breath. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]], [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]], and [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] engage the northern ogres as [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] puts thirteen hobgoblin archers to sleep.

One ogre manages to fire the northernmost catapult, but its shot misses Westfort's troops and lands harmlessly in the [[locations/world/agria/west-flow-river|West Flow River]]. The hobgoblin archers are more effective: one massed volley kills a substantial portion of Westfort's remaining archers. Hobgoblin soldiers then begin crossing the narrow bridge two abreast. Arden paralyses the two surviving ogres with Hold Person, allowing Frank to land a devastating backstab, while [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] and Selvar incapacitate more archers with Sleep.

As the fire spreads, Frank badly wounds the broken-crowned hobgoblin king. Rose turns the enemy ballista on the blinded ogre standing before it and kills him, while [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]] leaps onto another ogre and repeatedly stabs it. Frank, Nila, Wistor, Ulrich, and Selvar finish the remaining hobgoblin archers. With all three ogres and the archers dealt with, the Bonebreakers converge on the centre while the [[factions/military-and-orders/west-fort-garrison|Westfort Garrison]] destroys several sections of soldiers advancing across the bridge.

Barny flies over the battlefield and attempts to charm the hobgoblin king, but the spell fails. Wistor then sends a Lightning Bolt through the enemy ranks, killing [[npcs/westmarsh/kargaz-the-disciplined|Kargaz the Disciplined]], the king and commander of the siege army, and badly damaging the soldiers around him. The remaining hobgoblins fight on, wiping out an entire section of Westfort's soldiers and killing several more defenders before the militia moves up to replace the fallen. Under sustained attacks from the Bonebreakers, soldiers, militia, and surviving archers, the final two hobgoblins are killed. The renewed siege of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] is broken.

The victors recover 37,000 gold pieces and 3,000 silver pieces from the hobgoblin army, then pile the enemy dead where the spreading fire can consume them. During the commotion, Nila tries and fails to pick Frank's pocket. Their exchange draws attention away from Barny, who secretly raises Kargaz as a zombie and turns him invisible.

The Bonebreakers re-enter Westfort beside the surviving defenders to cheers from townspeople whose attitude toward the party has markedly improved. Celebration is tempered by the garrison's heavy losses. [[npcs/west-fort/commander-valerius|Commander Valerius]] publicly thanks the party and presents them with a key to the city. Frank gives 1,000 gold pieces toward rebuilding and relief, while Arden distributes money in the name of [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenus]] and, at Frank's suggestion, leads burial rites for the fallen soldiers.

Before beginning a period of downtime, the party collects eleven Potions of Water Breathing from [[npcs/west-fort/erasmus-vale|Erasmus Vale]] for 75 gold pieces each. These give them the means to return to the flooded routes of the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]]. They also buy ten riding horses released from wartime service. During downtime, Ulrich begins researching a Skeleton's Key spell and Nila commissions several daggers.

## Important events

- The Bonebreakers eliminate the ogres, archers, and remaining soldiers of the renewed hobgoblin siege army while disabling, burning, or turning its siege engines against it.
- Hobgoblin volleys and melee attacks inflict heavy losses on Westfort's already depleted defenders.
- Wistor kills Kargaz the Disciplined, the broken-crowned hobgoblin king, with Lightning Bolt.
- Barny secretly raises Kargaz as a zombie and makes him invisible.
- The renewed siege of Westfort is broken, and local sentiment toward the Bonebreakers changes from resentment to public gratitude.
- Commander Valerius presents the Bonebreakers with a key to the city.
- Frank donates 1,000 gold pieces to Westfort's recovery, and Arden distributes further money in Belenus's name before leading burial rites for the fallen.
- The party obtains eleven Potions of Water Breathing and ten riding horses, then begins downtime.
