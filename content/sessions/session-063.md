## Narrative recap

After the victory at [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] questions the secretly reanimated [[npcs/westmarsh/kargaz-the-disciplined|Kargaz the Disciplined]]. Kargaz says he knew little of the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]'s larger plan, but confirms that the organisation supported his war and promised him control of Westfort if the hobgoblins captured it. The answer turns the suspected link between the Syndicate and the renewed siege into direct testimony, without revealing what the Syndicate hoped to gain.

The Bonebreakers travel east for two days and enter [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. The city is still untidy after the plague and unrest, but is functional again, and the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|Temple of Merciful Life]] has been rebuilt. The party spends a week resting, recuperating, and gathering information. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] visits [[npcs/ikiria/gilly|Gilly]] at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/madame-gorvettis|Madame Gorvetti's]], a brothel in the Plats, funding an apartment and suitable clothing so she can enter upper-city society and identify eligible widows for him. Gilly also brings more serious news: [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] remains missing, [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] disappeared around the time [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] was returned, and [[npcs/agria/queen-audrey-ii|Queen Audrey II]] is reportedly unwell. The overlap with the Grey Syndicate's royal conspiracy convinces the party to investigate.

During the same week, [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] visits the family of the girl [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] cured during the plague. Her sister, [[npcs/ikiria/elizabeth|Elizabeth]], agrees to serve Belenus. Arden trains her as an acolyte and hires her to run the rebuilt temple, gather followers, and heal local people without entering combat. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] constructs an improved set of thieves' tools, while Barny secretly researches magic that could let him command and temporarily possess undead after attacking them. Frank's attempt to make poison fails.

The party takes its recovered coinage to [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]] at the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]]. He separates it into old and very old Imperial currency, which the Academy buys at 110 percent of face value, and much rarer pre-Imperial coins, which it buys at twice their normal gold value. This also confirms that the oldest coins have exceptional historical value.

At the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/east-agria-company-headquarters|East Agria Company Headquarters]], a secretary insists that Agatha is safe but indisposed and gives the Bonebreakers a letter apparently written by her. It says little beyond summoning them to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrow|Harrow]], a northern port. With Gilly's report still unresolved, the party leaves quickly. Their former navigator from the Fos Imeras expedition is now [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/captain-winston|Captain Winston]], master of the Company-chartered carrack *Agatha's Favour*. He sails them north from the Plats through [[locations/world/agria/northlands/narrows|the Narrows]], a confined route of fjords, steep cliffs, and artificial canals.

The ship approaches Harrow through heavy fog and briny air. A local official, [[npcs/harrow/rickart-the-reeve|Rickart the Reeve]], intercepts it in a rowboat, welcomes the crew to the County of Harrowmere, and demands an entry tax of 10,000 gold pieces. Winston prevents the party from disrupting the negotiation, reduces the demand to 7,500 gold pieces, and pays it. At Harrow's cramped, dangerous docks, he admits that he does not know where Agatha is; the Bonebreakers must find her themselves.

The party begins at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrow/anchor-and-chain|the Anchor & Chain]], the docks' only inn, where fights break out among a crowd of pirates and other rough patrons. Its landlady, [[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]], is a stocky woman missing two fingers. Arden loudly presents himself as a cleric of Belenus, drinks the inn's filthy-looking rum, and announces that the Bonebreakers are adventurers seeking work. Fen agrees to spread word of their arrival. Barny immediately asks where he can sell contraband and learns that Harrow offers many possibilities. The session ends with the party conspicuous in an unfamiliar port, uncertain whether Agatha truly summoned them or where she can be found.

## Important events

- Kargaz confirms that the Grey Syndicate supported his war and promised him Westfort if he captured it.
- The party returns to a functional Ikiria and finds the Temple of Merciful Life rebuilt.

- Elizabeth becomes a Belenite acolyte and takes responsibility for the rebuilt temple's local ministry.
- Illitharien Morel identifies and buys the party's old, very old, and pre-Imperial coins.
- An East Agria Company secretary gives the party a letter apparently from Agatha summoning them to Harrow.

- [[npcs/harrow/rickart-the-reeve|Rickart the Reeve]] extracts a 7,500-gold entry payment before allowing the ship into Harrow.
- The party begins seeking Agatha from [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrow/anchor-and-chain|the Anchor & Chain]] on Harrow's docks.
