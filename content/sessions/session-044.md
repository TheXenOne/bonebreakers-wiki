## Narrative recap

Following the claw marks deeper into [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vii|Arch VII]], Luthien scouts ahead and finds the source: an ancient chimera asleep on an enormous hoard. The Bonebreakers also investigate a southern chamber with a vertical shaft, where Rose climbs to a sealed metal grille and glimpses a domed chamber above, marked with writing and no obvious exits. Rather than fight the chimera directly, the party withdraws, acquires a pig, poisons it, and returns with a plan.

The trap works. With Ulrich's Silence, Arden's blessing, and Barny's invisibility on Rose, the poisoned bait is placed where the creature will find it. The chimera devours the pig and dies without the party having to meet it in open battle. Rose takes the dragon head for possible armour, and the Bonebreakers claim a major hoard: Arden's pre-imperial lion-faced shield, Rose's imperial spear, a magical ring, the [[items/party-inventory/manual-of-strength|Manual of Strength]], a red crystal vial, and a great mass of coin, gems, and jewellery.

After hauling the treasure back through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|The Mercy]] and onward to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/index|Barrow Spa]], [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/ruby-sunbeam|Ruby Sunbeam]] warns that some of the imperial coinage may be worth more to the right buyer. The party then heads toward Ikiria, but the road brings a different prize: [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] and his guarded coach.

At first the procession treats the Bonebreakers as an inconvenience. The guards haughtily demand the road for the Duke of the Northlands, Frank is shoved aside despite trying to warn them that [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] is in danger, and [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]] stays inside the carriage rather than answer directly. The tone only changes when Frank names the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]. The captain goes back to the carriage, speaks with Arthur, and returns with the order to kill the party.

The Bonebreakers strike first, disabling the retinue with sleep, hold, webbing, and controlled force. Once Luthien charms Arthur, he admits he is travelling to meet [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|the Grey Khan]] for help becoming king. He also confirms that the Syndicate has been moving against Princess Agatha and staging attacks under false colours. By the end of the session, the chimera hoard is won, but the party has uncovered a royal conspiracy in motion.
