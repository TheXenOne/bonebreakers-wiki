# Session 44 - Gold Beneath the Beast, Blood Above the Crown

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-043|Session 43 - Echoes Beneath the Empire]], [[sessions/session-045|Session 45 - The Syndicate's Silence]]

## Narrative recap

The necropolis delve pays off dramatically when Luthien's scouting reveals a sleeping chimera guarding an enormous hoard at the end of the claw-scarred section. Rather than meet it head-on, the Bonebreakers withdraw, prepare carefully, and kill the creature with poison and patience instead of a direct fight.

The treasure haul is enormous, but the session does not stay underground. On the road afterward, the party collides with Prince Arthur and his escort and uncovers the most direct proof yet that the Grey Syndicate reaches into the royal succession. By session end Arthur is alive, charmed, and openly admitting that he seeks the Grey Khan's help in becoming king while the Syndicate moves against Princess Agatha.

## Important events

- Luthien scouts the claw-scarred passage and discovers a sleeping chimera and its hoard.
- Rose climbs the vertical shaft in the southern chamber and spots a sealed domed room above.
- The party withdraws, acquires a pig, poisons it, and returns to set a trap.
- Ulrich casts Silence, Arden blesses the party, and Barny makes Rose invisible.
- The chimera devours the bait and dies from poison.
- Rose skins the dragon head for later use.
- The Bonebreakers recover a major hoard of coin, gems, jewellery, and magic items.
- The treasure is hauled back to the cathedral and then to Barrow Spa.
- Ruby Sunbeam advises that the imperial coinage may be worth more elsewhere.
- On the road to Ikiria, the party meets Prince Arthur and his escort.
- The escort turns hostile once Frank mentions the Grey Syndicate.
- Selvar, Ulrich, Luthien, and Arden neutralise much of Arthur's retinue with sleep, hold, web, and non-lethal force.
- Luthien charms Prince Arthur and learns he is travelling to meet the Grey Khan in order to become king.
- Arthur admits that the Grey Syndicate has been moving against Princess Agatha and staging attacks under false colours.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- Arden claims a pre-imperial lion-faced `+2` shield.
- Rose claims an imperial `+3` spear.
- The hoard also includes a magical ring, a manual of strength, and a red crystal vial.
- The treasure includes 3,000 gold pieces in gemstones, 2,500 gold pieces in jewellery, 2,000 gold pieces worth of pre-imperial coinage, and 4,000 gold pieces worth of imperial coin.
- Rose keeps the chimera's dragon head for possible armour crafting.
- Arthur's agenda proves he is travelling to meet the Grey Khan.
- Arthur confirms that the Grey Khan is an orc and leads the Grey Syndicate.
