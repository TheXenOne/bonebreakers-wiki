## Narrative recap

Back at the Mercy, the Bonebreakers finally test the poisoned well and learn that the danger there is natural rather than the result of sabotage. That relief is short-lived. Barny again hears Tobold's voice from below the cathedral, and this time the warning only drives the party deeper into the hidden spiral descent beneath the church.

What they find is far larger and stranger than expected: an inverted passage saturated with magic, a vast chamber of numbered imperial arches, and a section of the legendary necropolis somehow lying beneath the cathedral instead of out in Westmarsh. By the end of the session the Bonebreakers have chosen Arch VII, met the trapped scholar Cornelius Vane, uncovered maps of an impossible wing, and reached chambers holding necrotic lore and a disturbing pre-imperial creation myth.

## Important events

- The party tests the Mercy's poisoned well and finds the poison is natural.
- Rumours of plague in Ikiria continue, and Tobold remains missing.
- Barny hears Tobold's voice below the cathedral again.
- Barny uses the Seer's Dagger on Rattius to scout the spiral descent.
- The party descends into a magically inverted passage where the whispers stop.
- Frank finds a trapdoor leading into a colossal chamber of eight archways.
- The imperial eagle statue opens the arches when its gemstone eyes are pressed.
- Skeletons emerge from Arch I and are destroyed by Ulrich's turning.
- The party studies the seven visible archways and chooses Arch VII.
- The Bonebreakers enter an abandoned stonemason's camp and recover partial plans of the wing.
- Cornelius Vane is found alive but trapped in the section.
- Frank retrieves another map from an inverted chamber.
- Barny reads Valedictus's iron-bound necromantic treatise and gains `+1` Intelligence.
- The party reaches the Foundation Chamber and studies its skull and pre-imperial writing.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- The Mercy's well poison is found to be natural rather than deliberate.
- The arch chamber uses ancient imperial numerals older than those used by the Ramens.
- Arch VII contains a preserved stonemason's camp and plans for part of a larger wing.
- Cornelius's maps name places such as the Vault, the Twins, the Collapsed Nave, and the Chamber.
- Frank finds another map in the inverted room.
- Barny reads Valedictus's treatise on necrotic arts and gains `+1` Intelligence.
- Steve recovers 200 gold pieces of pre-imperial coinage and a black ritual knife worth 600 gold pieces.
- The Foundation Chamber's text preserves a pre-imperial creation myth that conflicts with modern religions.
