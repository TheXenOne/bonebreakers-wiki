## Narrative recap

Back at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|The Mercy]], the Bonebreakers finally test the poisoned well and learn that its danger is natural rather than deliberate sabotage. That answers one local crisis, but it does not make the cathedral feel safer. Rumours of plague in Ikiria continue, Tobold is still missing, and Barny again hears Tobold's voice from somewhere below the church, warning him not to come down.

Rather than leave the warning alone, Barny uses the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]] on [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/rattius|Rattius]] to scout the hidden spiral staircase. The route begins as a descent below the church, but Rattius's view confirms that it is not behaving like normal architecture: the walls are strangely blue, and at some point he seems to be ascending while still moving away from the cathedral. When the party follows in person and crosses into the inverted space below, the whispering presence around the cathedral fades and Ulrich finds the lower area saturated with magic.

Frank discovers a polished black-basalt trapdoor in the ceiling of the upside-down chamber and pushes through into the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], the large domed hub chamber that controls access to different parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|The Necropolis]]. The Bonebreakers emerge into a vast hall arranged around eight white archways: seven glow behind ashen curtains, while the northern arch is sealed. A giant imperial eagle statue stands ahead with dull red gemstones set into its eyes, and the archways are marked with ancient imperial numerals older than the Ramen forms Luthien knows. The markings run from [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]] at the north-east around to [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] at the sealed northern gate.

When Rose and Steve press the eagle's gemstone eyes, the ash-curtains vanish and images appear within the arches: buried streets, carved corridors, incense-lit halls, flooded stone, ancient boulevards, smoke-stained stairs, and a flickering construction area. Nine skeletons march out from [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]] and are destroyed by Ulrich's turning, making the danger of the nexus clear before the curtains fall again. After studying the options, the Bonebreakers choose [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vii|Arch VII]] and step through into a preserved stonemason's camp from a much older imperial project. The plans there show only part of a larger impossible wing.

Deeper inside, the party finds [[npcs/necropolis/cornelius-vane|Cornelius Vane]], a scholar who entered from Westmarsh six months ago but has experienced only eleven days. His maps name further chambers and suggest the section connects to [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|The Necropolis]] proper. The Bonebreakers also recover another map from an inverted room, break open the chains around Valedictus's iron-bound treatise, and reach the Foundation Chamber, where pre-imperial writing presents a creation myth at odds with modern faith. By the end of the session, the poisoned well is explained, but the Mercy has become the entrance to a much larger and stranger delve.
