## Narrative recap

The [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] turn their bridge capture from [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]] into the first clear path up the conspiracy. Akal searches the hooded handler and finds poisoned daggers hidden in his shoes, while the captured orcs admit they were hired mercenaries and repeat that the hawks went south. Steve releases one orc, Muzgash, after getting information, and the rest are handed to [[npcs/west-fort/sergeant-dara|Sergeant Dara]].

Using [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/vexs-warehouse|Vex's warehouse]] as a base, Barny charms the handler and names him [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/jeremy|Jeremy]]. Jeremy admits he pays [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/vex|Vex]] but is only a middleman. His handler is [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]], whom he must meet at dawn at [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|The Spider Market]] west of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. Barny also persuades Jeremy to accept the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]], giving the party a way to watch his movements.

Disguised in cloaks from the warehouse, the party rides hard and reaches the Spider Market just after dawn. The market is neutral ground where orcs, hobgoblins, bugbears, lizardfolk, and other Westmarsh factions trade under tense rules. There the Bonebreakers watch Jeremy meet Ash, a female orc, and learn that the connection is real even if the conversation remains partly hidden by language. They also use the market themselves: Steve buys a sword he feels compelled to own, Akal buys a vicious shortsword and a wolf, and Cedric buys a gleaming shield.

Trusting the market's neutrality long enough to rest, the party pushes into the nearby hills and finds a cave with heavy traffic. Nine hobgoblins inside are willing to talk only if the Bonebreakers help raid gnolls; when asked about the hawks, they turn hostile. The fight is dangerous but short, ending with Luthien's Sleep spell and a large haul of jewellery. By session end, the party knows Vex reports through Jeremy to Ash, Ash uses the Spider Market as a contact point, and the hobgoblins near the market are guarding something organised enough to connect the hawks, raids, and coming violence in Westmarsh.
