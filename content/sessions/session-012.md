## Narrative recap

Morlan quickly decides that cooperation is safer than defiance. Arden tricks him into taking the cursed lead ring in exchange for his amulet, then frees him from the cage and lets him join the search for treasure. The party soon reaches the parlor previously glimpsed through the Seer’s Dagger and meets the so-called Rat Queen, a giant rat with a magical ring and several guards.

Negotiation fails. Barny’s patience runs out, Sleep starts flying, and the chamber turns into a brawl. Arden kills the Rat Queen, Morlan survives only long enough to help carry off loot, and Barny makes matters worse by trying on the Queen’s ring and discovering it is yet another curse. On the way back, the Bonebreakers burn out the grey oozes in the flooded trap room, but a fresh rat ambush kills Morlan before the party can leave.

The final chamber of the sanctum proves to be a ruined laboratory full of colored powders, a matching purple robe, and Yellow Mould. The party clears the danger, loots the room, and makes one last search for secret doors before accepting that Draxas’s dungeon is effectively finished. Back in town, the encoded gnomish tome yields a hidden map to the Golden Asclepion on a distant island, pointing the campaign toward a new destination.
