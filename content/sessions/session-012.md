## Narrative recap

[[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]] quickly realizes that defiance is less useful while he is still trapped in a cage. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] turns the moment into a bargain, trading him the cursed lead ring from Morlan's own casket for his fine amulet without revealing the curse. Once freed, Morlan joins the Bonebreakers for treasure, though the alliance is obviously transactional and uneasy.

Heading west, the party reaches the dusty parlor [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] had glimpsed earlier through [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|the Red Seer's Dagger]]. There they meet [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/rat-queen|the Rat Queen]], a giant rat wearing a magical ring and guarded by five rat attendants. She demands tribute before negotiation. Arden offers cursed rings as a possible trade, but Barny's patience runs out and he begins casting Sleep.

The parlor becomes a brawl. Some rats fall to magic, others swarm from both directions, and [[characters/inactive-players/dom/retainers-followers/grog|Grog]] and [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] hold the rear while Arden duels the Queen. Arden eventually kills her, and the party claims a large golden bowl that Morlan wrongly assumes is meant for him. Barny then puts on [[items/party-inventory/rat-queens-ring|the Rat Queen's ring]] and suffers another curse, gaining the limited intellect of a very clever rat rather than the benefit a rat would receive.

The Bonebreakers next settle old business in the flooded trap chamber by dousing the grey oozes with oil and burning them out. The retreat is interrupted by mourning rats enraged over their queen's death. Morlan is slain in the ambush, and Grog is badly hurt but helps the party survive. Morlan's brief freedom ends almost as quickly as it began, and the sanctum's remaining dangers are now mostly vermin, traps, and lingering magical refuse.

The final unexplored chamber is a ruined laboratory with a chemical stink, shelves of multicolored powders, and a purple robe matching Arden's glove. Arden tries to snag the robe with a grappling hook and releases Yellow Mould, but the party disperses it safely from a distance. With the robe claimed and the powders bottled, the Bonebreakers search the complex for secret doors, find no more, gather the remaining treasure and furnishings, and leave the sanctum effectively cleared. Jessum thanks them at the surface and even trades his magic ring for several gems.

Back in Barrow Spa, the party cashes in its haul and begins working through Jessum's encoded gnomish tome. A hidden map falls from it, showing a golden Asclepeion on an island and a lake marked with an X. The Jeweller's Sanctum is finished, but [[items/party-inventory/asclepeion-map|the map]] turns Draxas's legacy into a new lead far beyond Barrowshire.
