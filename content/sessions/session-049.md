## Narrative recap

The corpse-pit fight in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] continues with the Bonebreakers surrounded by undead from both the pit and the cemetery. [[characters/active-players/jennifer/characters/quak-ii|Quak II!]] arrives to help, but the battle keeps escalating: skeletons climb from the grave pit, ghouls and zombies press in, mummies bring rotting wounds, and wraiths spread terror across the field.

The party survives by leaning hard on its anti-undead tools. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden Sable]] and [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] repeatedly turn or hold back the dead, while holy water and focused magic keep stronger undead from overrunning the group. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] uses the [[items/party-inventory/wand-of-daystars|Wand of Daystars]] from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king|the Halls of the Blood King]] to destroy one wraith outright and badly wound another. Steve, overwhelmed by fear, finally drinks his long-saved fly potion and flees out over the sea.

The fight also turns personal and costly. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] is nearly overrun and struck with mummy rot, though Arden cures the wound before it can take hold. Ulrich's evil-detecting holy symbol shows that the automaton grave itself is neutral, even though something inside it is twitching, adding another strange thread to the burial ground.

[[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] gives the clearest answer before she dies: the undead do not belong here and must have been conjured by a necromancer. A wraith then drains away her remaining life. Her notebook survives, preserving her working model of the crisis: poisoning first, overcrowding and disease second, and now an undead incursion layered on top. The fight is not fully settled by the end of the session, but the outbreak is clearly deliberate rather than a simple consequence of plague.
