## Narrative recap

After the losses of the previous expedition, Arden and Barny sort through the wills of their fallen companions. Virien's request for a second chance leads them to the Last Chance, where he is successfully reincarnated back into his own body. With Virien restored, the group regathers its strength, recruits Ulrich and several retainers, and formally adopts the name the Bonebreakers.

The new company returns to the Barrow with a clearer plan, better support, and a promise of paid aid to any adventurers who answer the alphorn. This time the expedition is disciplined and productive. The party defeats more skeletons, uses stealth and trickery to manage the dungeon's dangers, and discovers two glass eyes that explain the unfinished idol puzzle from the prior delve.

Pushing farther than before, the Bonebreakers reach Quintius' old quarters and then the Barrow's largest chamber. Virien talks a lone skeleton into destroying itself with holy water, allowing the party to secure the room and carry out a major haul of gold, silver, silk, gemstones, wine, and an ivory drinking horn. For the first time, the Barrow yields the kind of treasure the party had been chasing.

## Important events

- Virien is reincarnated at the Last Chance and returns in his original body.
- Ulrich joins the party.
- Bran "The Wolf," Grog, Rhian, and Dreg are hired as retainers.
- The party adopts the name "The Bonebreakers."
- Arden posts a notice promising 7 gold to adventurers who answer an alphorn call for aid at the Barrow.
- The Bonebreakers defeat another group of skeletons in the Barrow.
- Virien sets a bell-and-string warning trap against attacks from behind.
- The party finds two glass eyes, revealing the likely solution to the idol chamber puzzle.
- The Bonebreakers reach Quintius' room and then the Barrow's largest chamber.
- Virien convinces a skeleton to drink holy water, destroying it.
- The party hauls out a large cache of treasure and returns safely to town.

- The lingering necromancy tied to Quintius' activities is finally addressed in Session 23, when Arden cleanses Quintius' chamber and the Barrow is scoured of necromancy. Status: Resolved in Session 23.
