## Narrative recap

After the disastrous second delve, [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] and [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] sort through the wishes of the dead. Stan Nice wanted to remain dead, [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ike|Ike]] left no will, and [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] asked for a second chance so he would not disappoint his parents. Arden and Barny choose [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/last-chance|the Last Chance]] over the more expensive [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/second-chance|Second Chance]], and an unusually lucky reincarnation restores Virien in his original body.

With Virien alive again, the group rebuilds. They reunite with [[characters/inactive-players/vince/characters/karsin|Karsin]] and [[characters/inactive-players/don/characters/malakai|Malakai]], bring in [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]], and hire several retainers: [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran "The Wolf"]], [[characters/inactive-players/dom/retainers-followers/grog|Grog]], [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/rhian|Rhian]], and Dreg. They restock at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/minimarket|the Minimarket]], formally adopt the name [[factions/other/bonebreakers|the Bonebreakers]], and Arden posts a notice promising payment to any adventurers who come running if the alphorn sounds at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]].

The return to the Barrow is far more controlled than the previous two expeditions. Virien chalks over the ominous entrance warning with a Bonebreakers boast, the party destroys more skeletons, and Virien sets a string-and-bell alarm to protect the rear. Dreg and Malakai scout ahead, while Arden avoids another unnecessary fight by luring rats away from the tapestry room with rations.

Past the earlier rooms, the Bonebreakers find two glass eyes in a chamber of empty coffers, apparently giving them the missing pieces for the marble idol puzzle from [[sessions/session-002|Session 2]]. They continue through the old prison rooms, take a valuable tobacco box, and reach [[npcs/barrowshire/the-barrow/quintius|Quintius]]' decrepit quarters, where blood, bones, and rot underline how much necromancy still clings to the site.

The session's main success comes in the Barrow's largest chamber. Virien convinces a lone skeleton that he can restore it to life, then persuades it to drink holy water, destroying it without a full battle. With the room secured, the Bonebreakers haul out their first major treasure cache: gold, silver, crates of silk, gemstones, wine, and an ivory drinking horn. The company returns to town with its name established, its losses partly repaired, and the Barrow still not fully understood but no longer only a source of grief.

## Important events

- Virien is reincarnated at the Last Chance and returns in his original body.
- Ulrich joins the party.
- Bran "The Wolf," Grog, Rhian, and Dreg are hired as retainers.
- The party adopts the name "The Bonebreakers."
- Arden posts a notice promising 7 gold to adventurers who answer an alphorn call for aid at the Barrow.
- The Bonebreakers defeat another group of skeletons in the Barrow.
- Virien sets a bell-and-string warning trap against attacks from behind.
- The party finds two glass eyes, revealing the likely solution to the idol chamber puzzle.
- The Bonebreakers reach Quintius' room and then the Barrow's largest chamber.
- Virien convinces a skeleton to drink holy water, destroying it.
- The party hauls out a large cache of treasure and returns safely to town.
