## Narrative recap

Back in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], the party tries to turn the [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/silk-merchants|silk merchants]]' IOUs into usable expedition money. The merchants direct them to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/bean-counter|the Bean Counter]], where [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/ruby-sunbeam|Ruby Sunbeam]] produces the gold through pixie attendants, then watches with visible irritation as the party immediately redeposits it for safekeeping. With a bank ledger in hand, the group hires Stan Nice and [[characters/inactive-players/dom/retainers-followers/grog|Grog]], buys a draft horse, cart, and alphorn, and returns to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]] hoping this trip will finally pay out.

The plan is more organized, but the delve turns costly almost immediately. After scouting the outside and confirming only the main entrance and the narrow escape tunnel, the party leaves Grog at the entrance with the alphorn and explores the right-hand route. In a caved-in corridor, five skeletal arms wave from the rubble, one holding an intricate magical sword. Stan reaches for it and is killed by the trap, but [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] risks the same danger and recovers the +1 blade.

Deeper in, the party frees [[npcs/barrowshire/the-barrow/alfwine|Alfwine]], a pixie trapped under a cairn. She explains that she has lived in the Barrow for a long time and agrees to help after the party promises to aid her against the undead who imprisoned her. That alliance does not prevent more losses: skeletons ambush the next chamber, wounding [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] and killing [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ike|Ike]] before the survivors destroy them.

The group still presses on and finds the first clear proof of Quintius' hidden-chamber claim. Behind three tapestries, a pulley and secret door reveal a small room with a white marble idol whose eye sockets are empty. Magical white flames burn around a brazier holding three blue gems. Lacking whatever belongs in the sockets, Arden risks the flames directly and claims the gems, while the idol's full purpose remains unclear.

By then the expedition is down two companions and low on confidence, so the party decides to leave with Ike's body. At the entrance, two zombies ambush them as the stone rolls back. Virien is killed in the fight, struck with his own alphorn, though Barny, Arden, and Alfwine destroy the zombies. The survivors return to Barrow Spa with one magical sword, three blue gems, proof of hidden rooms, and the heavy cost of Stan, Ike, and Virien's deaths.
