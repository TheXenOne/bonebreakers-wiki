## Narrative recap

Back in Barrow Spa, the party cashes in the silk merchants' IOUs through the Bean Counter, where Ruby Sunbeam oversees the withdrawal with visible annoyance when the group immediately redeposits the gold. Better equipped and joined by Stan Nice and Grog, the party returns to the Barrow with a cart, horse, and alphorn in the hope of finally claiming real treasure.

Their second expedition goes badly. Stan is killed while reaching for a magical sword clutched by skeletal arms in a caved-in corridor, though Virien manages to recover the blade. Deeper inside, the party frees a pixie named Alfwine from a cairn, and she agrees to help if they will aid her in destroying the undead that trapped her.

The gains are costly. Ike is slain in a skeleton ambush, and the party discovers a hidden chamber behind three tapestries, where Arden braves magical white flames to seize three blue gems from a brazier beside a marble idol with empty eye sockets. As the survivors try to leave, two zombies ambush them at the entrance. Virien is killed in the fighting, and the party returns to town diminished and grieving.
