## Narrative recap

The return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] is quiet but momentous. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/admiral-nelson|Captain Nelson]] docks without fanfare, and one of the rescued nobles reveals herself to the party as a princess. She thanks them, says she will be in touch, and makes clear that they should tell no one. The Bonebreakers leave [[items/party-inventory/eleftherias-rudder|the Eleftheria]] in Nelson's care and spend the night at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/upper-class|the Upper Class]], where the session slows down long enough for the party to ask what they actually want from their growing power.

The resulting conversation lays out the party's long-term ambitions. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] wants to build a temple, find Mercy's Blade, and make Belenus a major force in East Agria. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] wants wealth, status, noble connections, and influence among those who need quiet work done. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] seeks the "one true god" while collecting religious relics and building a healing order. [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] wants legendary glory, a fortress, and a mercenary company. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] insists he was once the world's greatest wizard and wants immortality and his lost past.

The newer companions also make their motives clearer. [[characters/inactive-players/marco/characters/ehecat|Ehecat]] reveals that the Ramen Empire destroyed the Cassets, his home people, and that he wants vengeance on the responsible bloodline and ultimately the empire itself. [[characters/inactive-players/laurene/characters/durandale|Durandale]] explains that she was raised among many faiths, chose the light of Lathander, left temple life to test her ideals in the wider world, and still longs to return home despite the danger of that journey. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] speaks of a sheltered elven village she hated and her hunger for power, while keeping the darkest shape of those ambitions to herself. [[characters/inactive-players/matt/characters/athlestan-ghoulsbane|Athlestan]] reveals the strangest charge of all: he belongs to a hidden brotherhood of reincarnating priests sworn to destroy the King of Ghouls, a lich whose return is imminent and whose phylactery has never been found.

The next day, practical business resumes. At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]], [[npcs/flux-academy/herbert-nuel|Herbert Nuel]] identifies a potion of control gold dragon and a potion of gaseous form. [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]] receives the encoded documents from Jessum's material and the Asclepeion, while Barny learns that [[npcs/flux-academy/alric-zexerus|Alric Zexerus]] will not entertain his claims without formal Academy enrollment. Arden and Frank also begin shaping their public presence in Ikiria's docks, giving charity in Belenus's name while Frank quietly starts looking for informants among noble households.

The turning point comes at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/east-agria-company-headquarters|East Agria Company Headquarters]]. The rescued princess is formally revealed as [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], heir to the throne, Duchess of the Southlands, Mayor of Ikiria, and Governor of the East Agria Company. Flanked by Commander Smith and the newly elevated [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/admiral-nelson|Admiral Nelson]], she thanks the party, acknowledges their ambitions, and offers a secret patronage arrangement: she will give them high-profile missions that they may decline, but they must never reveal her involvement. Betrayal means death.

The Bonebreakers accept. [[npcs/flux-academy/ulna-glitterpatch|Ulna Glitterpatch]] pays the promised 5,000 GP for the Fos Imeras report and briefs them on Agatha's first mission: end the deathless plague afflicting the Southlands. Victims rot without dying and eventually rise as undead; [[factions/religions-and-cults/church-of-the-crimson-flame|the Church of the Crimson Flame]], once a healing sect of Bahal, now opens its gates only to Crimson Knights and appears increasingly cult-like. What began with a map in Draxas's book has carried the party into royal covert work, and their next target is the plague's cathedral.
