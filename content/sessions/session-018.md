## Narrative recap

The return to Ikiria is quiet but momentous. The rescued nobles and princess disappear into the city after thanking the party, while the Bonebreakers spend the night at the Upper Class and, for once, pause long enough to reflect on themselves. The session becomes a rare character-focused interlude, with Arden, Frank, Ulrich, Virien, Barny, Ehecat, Durandal, Luthien, and Athlestan each laying out long-term ambitions, loyalties, and grudges.

The next day, practical business resumes. Herbert Newell identifies more potions, Professor Morrel receives the encoded documents, and Barny learns that Aldrich Zaxarus will not indulge his claims without formal enrollment at the Academy. Meanwhile, Arden and Frank begin public acts of charity and quiet political networking in Ikiria’s docks.

The turning point comes at East Agria Company headquarters. The “rescued princess” is formally revealed as Princess Agatha, heir to the throne, Duchess of the Southlands, Mayor of Ikiria, and Governor of the East Agria Company. She thanks the party, rewards their expedition through Ulna Glitterpatch, and offers them a secret patronage arrangement. Their first mission under this arrangement is clear and grim: end the deathless plague centered on the Church of the Crimson Flame.

## Important events

- The party returns quietly to Ikiria.

- Ulna Glitterpatch provides the 5,000 GP reward for the Fos Imeras mission.

- The party accepts a mission to end the deathless plague.
