## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers continue exploring [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]], where the dead market behaves less like an abandoned ruin and more like a broken civic routine still trying to function. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] tests the rules by pretending to steal from one of the zombie merchants and getting caught. When he pays with imaginary money, the merchant still gives him a real imperial coin as change. Elsewhere, [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] finds a trader who unlocks a stone cabinet and exchanges Agria coin for silver instruments worth 260 gold pieces, while [[characters/inactive-players/marie/characters/fred|Fred]] asks who the emperor is. The merchants no longer know; too much time has passed.

The party learns that a counting house lies off to the side of the market and that fishermen's quarters lie farther ahead. Other transactions suggest the market may still hold valuable or significant goods. [[characters/inactive-players/valerio/characters/paul|Paul]] buys several casks of 2,000-year-old wine with imaginary coin. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] finds an intricately carved mask that the merchant refuses to sell because it is reserved for someone else. When she claims descent from that person, the merchant accepts the claim and says the mask is held on the lower floor rather than in the market above. [[characters/active-players/jennifer/characters/quak-ii|Quak II!]] finds a glass merchant with six imperial crystal vials.

The market's dead order becomes dangerous when [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] begins bartering for ivory statues and then destroys the zombie merchant with turning instead of paying. Several serpents erupt from nearby pools in response. One attacks Arden, while others seize Quak and Fred and drag them into the water. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] discovers that his sword does little against them, and the party realises that sharp weapons are ineffective while blunt force works normally. Fred and Quak both fall unconscious underwater before the Bonebreakers recover them: Ulrich heals Fred, Arden heals Quak, and the party kills the serpents with maces, magic, and coordinated blows.

After finding no treasure in the pool, the Bonebreakers move into the counting chamber. Inside are collapsed ledgers, overturned bookcases, and three suspiciously arranged locked chests. Paul inspects the first and discovers it is already open, with a hidden button in the bottom. Pressing it opens a false base and reveals the [[items/party-inventory/arch-iv-sunken-market-map|Arch IV Sunken Market Map]]. The map names the area as the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], marks several unvisited areas as flooded or entirely underwater, warns not to approach one channel after dark, and notes that the thing in the sump is not a fish.

The remaining chests are less subtle. Arden tests one with a ten-foot pole, causing the lock to snap sharply, and Steve then smashes it open by breaking the hinge. The chest contains 340 gold pieces. The party hammers open another chest as well, making enough noise to draw three snake-men into the chamber. Ulrich blesses the Bonebreakers, Arden casts Bran's Heroic Aura on Steve, and Fred uses Web to trap the attackers and pull their attention toward Steve. Luthien's charm magic and Paul's assassination strike have limited effect, suggesting the snake-men are too powerful for some of the party's usual tricks, but the fight turns once Arden blinds one with Light and the trapped enemies are worn down. Paul kills one, Steve kills another, and Quak kills the last.

Searching the bodies and nearby loot, the Bonebreakers recover 4,000 electrum pieces, an imperial seal worth 120 gold pieces, and jewellery worth 1,300 gold pieces. The final chest is empty. Before the party can push farther, chanting echoes through the market. Steve and Arden investigate and find strange dark druids moving through the stalls. Arden tries to speak to them in Common and preach kindness, but they do not respond warmly. Steve speaks in the chaotic tongue and smooths over the interruption. The druids say they worship [[factions/religions-and-cults/dark-druids-of-the-deep-one|the Deep One]], that they are not from here, and that they cannot guarantee safe passage past them. The Bonebreakers choose not to force a confrontation. They skirt around the druids, withdraw from the market, and return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] to rest and heal.
