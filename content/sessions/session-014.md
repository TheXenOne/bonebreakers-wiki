## Narrative recap

At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]], [[npcs/flux-academy/ulna-glitterpatch|Ulna Glitterpatch]] identifies the island on the map as [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]], once famous for miraculous healing and now cut off from contact. She is not a spellcaster but a scholar of physical healing, which makes the island's silence especially concerning to her. Glitterpatch offers the Bonebreakers operational expenses and 5,000 GP for a report on what happened there, then provides coordinates and a letter to support the expedition.

The party prepares quickly in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. [[npcs/flux-academy/herbert-nuel|Herbert Nuel]] identifies a potion of healing, a potion of speed, and a potion of poison, while Barny brews five more healing potions. At the docks, the Bonebreakers secure passage with [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/admiral-nelson|Captain Nelson]] and his navigator Winston, experienced coffee-traders who warn them about bad weather, pirates, and the renewed aggression of [[factions/governments/ramen-empire|the Ramen Empire]].

The voyage east shows the Bonebreakers how much larger the world has become since Barrow Spa. As Ikiria falls behind, they see gnomish lifts, automata, ship-handling magic, and a city fiercely proud of its future. At sea, Arden gives gold and blessings to sailors in Belenus's name and receives a divine sign that he is meant to go to "the temple." Frank learns from the crew, Ulrich suffers through seasickness, and Virien begins practicing the bone flute from the Jeweller's Sanctum.

Danger follows them across the water. Pirates give chase, but the party drives them off with a lie that the ship carries lupus-stricken plague victims bound for healing. Later, a golden dragon glides overhead, watches the ship, and leaves without revealing its purpose. When Fos Imeras comes into view, three Ramen ships surround Nelson's vessel. A Ramen captain grants only a conditional visa: the Bonebreakers may land if they investigate the disappearance of Callea, find the legendary Imperial [[npcs/fos-imeras/orsilochus|Orsilochus]], and recover his magical vessel, [[items/party-inventory/eleftherias-rudder|the Eleftheria]].

The party chooses the southern beach, where figures seem to be sunbathing. Up close, they find blistered patients in grey robes, white-clad attendants who appear to be physicians, and a harpy overseer with the body of an eagle and the torso of an old woman. Virien approaches and is immediately charmed by her song. The rest of the party plugs their ears with wax, attacks, and kills the harpy, breaking the charm and securing the beach. Fos Imeras is clearly not a functioning sanctuary anymore, and the healing expedition has become an investigation into collapse, captivity, and whatever waits inside [[locations/world/fos-imeras/golden-asclepeion|the Golden Asclepeion]].
