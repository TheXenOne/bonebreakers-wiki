## Narrative recap

Still trapped inside [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|the Invasion Fort]], [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] slips her hand out of her glove and escapes the mimic. She runs back toward the party, but another creature drops onto her head and paralyses her with its tentacles. Her cries bring the Bonebreakers racing through the secret entrance, yet they arrive too late: the five-foot-wide, beaked creature, identified as a Krell, has killed her.

The party fights from range while trying to recover Luthien's body. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] turns invisible, and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] casts Bran's Heroic Aura on himself in a desperate attempt to reach her remains. The Krell begins eating the corpse before Arden can intervene, but he resists its own attack and Barny eventually lands the killing blow. The Bonebreakers recover Luthien's belongings and what remains of her body. Too much has been eaten for Raise Dead to restore her; if she is to return, Reincarnation appears to be the party's only remaining option. Barny also harvests parts of the Krell and collects its brain fluids.

Grieving but still inside enemy territory, the party moves deeper into the fort. They surprise and kill two hobgoblins in the main room, then sabotage the portcullis by breaking the chain attached to its lever. Barny uses the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]] to look through the gryphon carrying it and sees the creature in a high mountain cave with other gryphons, a nest, and several eggs. On the way back to the western secret entrance, the party finds Luthien's abandoned glove stuck to the creature that first caught her. They provoke the slow-moving mimic with a ten-foot pole and destroy it from range, finding no treasure inside.

The Bonebreakers leave the fortress and travel northwest through [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] toward [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. Arden realises that their route may fail even if they reach it, because the party previously spiked shut the trapdoor into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] from below. They continue for three days as the grass thins into barren, rocky ground, eventually reaching a mapped ruin: an enormous hill containing a roughly thirty-metre-high [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|imperial temple-like structure]].

Using Luthien's recovered cloak and boots, [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] scouts a domed chamber guarded by twelve skeletal legionnaires. At its centre, an iron box rests on a plinth beneath soul-binding sigils and is secured by three locks that must be opened simultaneously. Barny reads the sigils and concludes that the box is connected to the binding of one particular person's soul, but conceals this from the party and claims it merely holds treasure.

Frank and Barny begin their approach, but Barny's attempt to tie up one skeleton while invisible alerts the whole guard. Arden destroys six with divine turning, [[characters/active-players/jennifer/characters/quak-ii|Quak II!]] drives three more away, and the party cuts down the rest. As Frank and Barny work on the locks, two transparent, pale-blue disenchanters creep up behind the group. One strips the enchantment from [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]]'s sword. During the fight, Barny aims the [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] at them and sees a dream of a soul contained within a codex. The party kills one disenchanter and effectively deals with the blinded survivor, leaving the locked box and the meaning of the soul-bound codex unresolved.

## Important events

- Luthien escapes the mimic but is paralysed and killed by a Krell before the party can reach her.
- The Bonebreakers recover Luthien's belongings and partial remains. Raise Dead cannot restore her because the Krell ate too much of her body; Reincarnation may still be possible.
- Barny kills the Krell and harvests body parts and brain fluids from it.
- The party kills two hobgoblins and disables the Invasion Fort's portcullis mechanism.
- Barny locates the escaped dagger-bearing gryphon in a high mountain cave containing other gryphons and several eggs.
- The mimic near the western secret entrance is identified through its behaviour and killed.
- The party leaves the fort and travels northwest toward the Necropolis.
- A mapped ruin proves to be a monumental imperial temple-like structure containing skeletal legionnaires and a three-locked iron box linked to soul-binding magic.
- Barny hides the true nature of the soul-binding sigils from the party.
- A disenchanter removes the enchantment from Selvar's sword.
- The Nightmare-Revealing Telescope shows the disenchanters dreaming of a soul contained within a codex.
