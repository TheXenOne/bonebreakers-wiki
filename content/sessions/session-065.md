## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers begin in the low-ceilinged servants' entrance of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]], continuing [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]]'s covert investigation. The air smells of beeswax. Bells ring above them and footsteps cross the floorboards, suggesting their arrival has been noticed. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] takes four sets of keys labelled for the boardroom, dining room, music room, and gallery. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess]] examines the delivery ledger and records recent arrivals and departures. Deliveries have dwindled since [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]]'s arrest; earlier entries suggest frequent parties and include the delivery of a portrait.

[[npcs/kingsport/elspeth-crane|Mistress Elspeth Crane]], the middle-aged mistress of the house, approaches and demands to know whose orders they follow. Tess claims [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]] sent them. Elspeth is suspicious, but allows them to continue after warning them not to disturb the officially sealed cabinets and furniture beneath the dust sheets.

In the kitchen, Steve offers to cook for a nervous young chambermaid, [[npcs/kingsport/nessa|Nessa]]. His imposing orcish appearance frightens her, although she enjoys his risotto. Asked about Arthur, she recalls serving drinks through the small door to his private salon; servants are forbidden to enter while guests are there. While serving drinks during one gathering, she overheard Arthur telling someone else in the salon, “Lirina understands what Agatha has taken from him.” Whom Arthur meant by “him” remains unclear.

The private salon is comfortable and empty. In an ashtray, the party finds a nearly destroyed letter whose only surviving words are “Smith already knows.” What Smith knew, and whether this means [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]], remain unclear. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] notices a plant and considers returning after resting and changing his prepared spells to use Speak with Plants; he does not cast it. Tess steals a bottle of fine brandy, while Steve searches beneath a sofa's dust cover and pockets the loose coins he finds.

The gallery contains royal portraits, including an informal painting of Arthur as a boy with the slightly older, tomboyish Agatha and [[npcs/agria/queen-audrey-ii|Audrey]]. Beside a more recent formal portrait of Arthur, Ulrich confirms that another picture has been removed comparatively recently. An anxious, hand-wringing butler enters. Caught off guard by Ulrich's question, he says the missing portrait was taken for the investigation. The claim that Wendell sent the party reassures him: rumours say the secretary has turned against Arthur.

The butler introduces himself as [[npcs/kingsport/hugh-malory|Hugh Malory]] but initially avoids naming the portrait's subject. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] secretly casts ESP behind him, learning that Hugh values Arthur's position and the security of his employment more than the prince personally. Hugh checks the corridor and locks the gallery door before confiding that he suspects [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], the portrait's subject, is connected to Arthur's predicament. He describes her as exceptionally charming and charismatic: she is Countess of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]], and rumours surround her relationship with Arthur. With the social season beginning, she will be hosting a masquerade at her palace in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/velur|Velur]], about a day's travel from Kingsport.

After Hugh leaves, Tess notices that the gallery has no portrait of the old king consort, who died before or around the births of Agatha and Arthur. The precise timing of his death remains unclear.

In the writing room, an ornate desk stands among books that appear unread. Steve steals an inkwell, while Tess discovers an incomplete letter beneath a drawer's false bottom. Its approximate wording expresses confidence in proposed actions, says “The con requires no knowledge on your part of any private action,” and promises that when Agatha is disgraced the Northlands will have “a duke fit to be king.” Tess pockets it. The party does not establish the letter's author, recipient, or the meaning of “con”.

Other correspondence spans years of Arthur's jealousy toward Agatha. Older letters are informal, while later drafts become disciplined, cautious, and political. As Tess prepares to take them, [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] enters. Neatly dressed, conspicuously polite, and otherwise unremarkable, he says he works for the Royal Chamberlain and has come to document evidence. He doubts the party's claim to act for Wendell and says this is his first inspection. Tess has already pocketed the hidden letter about “the con” and hands him the other correspondence. Corvin seems disappointed with the letters she gives him, but takes them and leaves.

The party turns to the wax-sealed cabinets. Selvar is beginning to lift a royal seal when Elspeth catches him. Steve uses his sword to cast Charm Person on Elspeth, then claims Selvar was repairing damage caused by Corvin. She remains suspicious and asks for written authority, saying the servants' accounts do not match the visitors' stories. Crucially, she reveals that Corvin has visited several times: his claim of a first visit was false. Steve persuades her to leave.

Using a knife warmed over a candle, Steve carefully removes the seal. The household archives contain letters to and from Arthur and records of repeated journeys to Veluria. Those trips increased markedly shortly before the Bonebreakers first encountered Arthur on the road. Financial records cover transport and hospitality, including a payment for discretion in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow|Harrow]]. A letter from Agatha tells her brother to take his responsibilities seriously.

The missing portrait is also inside. Unwrapping it reveals Lirina as an elegant, beautiful woman in fine clothing, giving the party a face to recognise. They take the financial records and Agatha's letters, return the portrait to the cabinet, then warm and replace the wax to conceal their search.

The thefts grow bolder in the empty ballroom. Steve lowers the chandelier, and the party strips it of crystals before raising it back into place. Moving through the corridors, they overhear Hugh and Nessa comparing what they have told the visitors. Nessa repeats a rumour that someone magically controlled Arthur in the Southlands. The Bonebreakers suspect this refers to their earlier charm, though the gossip does not identify them. Hugh repeats rumours that Lirina abandoned Arthur after his arrest and that the Queen intends to close the palace permanently. Neither claim is confirmed; closure could have wider consequences for the duchies.

After stealing instruments from the music room, the party searches Arthur's lavish bedroom. A bedside locket holds a miniature of Lirina; they take the locket but leave its picture behind. Steve steals all of Arthur's jewellery, while [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] puts on a fur cloak and bundles the expensive clothing into a sack. Tess finds love letters addressed to Lirina and a note from her to Arthur: “Do nothing rash. Commit nothing to paper.” The party also takes a coin pouch, then decides it has pressed its luck far enough and prepares to leave, with several rooms still unexplored.

The party hands all the stolen loot to Tess. Struggling under its weight, she casts Invisibility on herself. Meanwhile, Steve approaches the still-charmed Elspeth in feigned distress, accusing Corvin of stealing Arthur's jewellery. She accepts that he was suspicious, but insists the matter remain quiet and tells Steve to track him rather than immediately alert the guards.

The Bonebreakers leave the palace. Steve follows Corvin through [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]] to the Royal Chamberlain's building. Corvin pauses outside, checks for followers, then suddenly bolts. Steve chases him, tackles him, and ties him up. While Steve holds him, [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]] secretly plants some of Arthur's stolen jewels on him.

The party drags Corvin back to Elspeth, where the jewels fall from his pockets. She takes this as proof of his guilt and summons [[npcs/kingsport/sir-aldric-hale|Sir Aldric Hale]], Captain of the Queen's Guard. Steve maintains the lie, supported by Elspeth while she remains charmed. The guards arrest Corvin despite his muffled protests that he has been framed. His lies about his palace visits, suspicious activity there, and flight after returning to the Royal Chamberlain's building lead the Bonebreakers to suspect that he is not who he claims to be. His true identity and purpose remain unconfirmed. The jewellery accusation is the Bonebreakers' fabrication.

Thanking the party for its supposed service, the guards take the Bonebreakers back to the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/guardhouse|guardhouse at the docks]] to collect their weapons. The guard responsible for the key fumbles about, confused that he cannot find it. The party drops the stolen key behind him and tells him he dropped it. Still puzzled, he shrugs and returns their weapons, allowing them to keep them until their departure the following morning. The guards then pay for the Bonebreakers to stay at an inn. The party has left the palace with evidence pointing toward Lirina and Veluria, while Corvin is in custody for the theft it committed.

## Important events

- The party maintains its claim to be acting for Wendell while searching Arthur's household for Agatha.
- Hugh identifies Lirina as Countess of Veluria and provides the lead to her masquerade in Velur.
- Letters and travel records connect Arthur to Lirina, increasing visits to Veluria, and a payment for discretion in Harrow.
- Elspeth exposes Corvin's false claim that he is inspecting the palace for the first time.
- The Bonebreakers charm Elspeth, steal from the palace, and plant jewels on Corvin, securing his arrest and the guards' gratitude.

- The burned letter preserves only “Smith already knows.” Its context and the identity of Smith remain uncertain.
- Tess pockets the incomplete hidden letter; Corvin takes the other letters shown to him. The party takes financial records and Agatha's letters from the archives. The fate of the documents Corvin took remains unclear after his arrest.
- Lirina's full portrait is left in the resealed cabinet; her miniature is removed from the stolen locket and left behind. Her warning against rash action and written commitments survives in Arthur's bedroom.
- Selvar uses ESP on Hugh; Steve uses his sword to cast Charm Person on Elspeth; Tess casts Invisibility on herself while carrying all the stolen loot handed to her by the party. Speak with Plants is considered only.
- Stolen valuables include brandy, an inkwell, chandelier crystals, instruments, a locket, jewellery, clothes, and coins. Some jewels are planted on Corvin.
