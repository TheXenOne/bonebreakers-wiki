# Session 33 - The Taking of Tobold Took

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-032|Session 32 - The Heart of the Night]], [[sessions/session-034|Session 34 - When the Trap Is Yours]]

## Narrative recap

The Blood Moon crisis ends only for the campaign to pivot immediately into a new one. As the party escorts freed prisoners away from the ruined manor, Tobold is snatched by a giant hawk, and the Bonebreakers quickly learn that several villages have lost children to the same coordinated abductions.

Their search leads west toward Westfort and into a broader investigation of the Westmarsh. Along the way the party uncovers old history about the marsh, rumours of trained hawks, signs of organised monster activity, and evidence that Westfort itself has recently been sabotaged by human hands. By session end the rescue mission has widened into a frontier campaign involving kidnappings, mercenaries, and a failing border fort.

## Important events

- The party escapes the collapsing manor with rescued prisoners at dawn.
- Tobold is carried off by a giant hawk.
- Villagers confirm that children have been taken from the west and carried back in that direction.
- The party returns the former manor prisoners to safety before beginning a western expedition.
- Research in Ikiria points to highly trained hawks and to the long history of Westmarsh.
- The Bonebreakers learn Westmarsh was once a prosperous land before a necrotic plague.
- Akal kills a lone rider on the road, and the party finds a Gnollish record of raids and ransoms.
- The party reaches Westfort and learns the fort has recently suffered arson, poisoned water, and stolen weapons.
- Sergeant Dara grants the Bonebreakers use of Westfort as a base.
- Rumours at the Cracked Shield suggest the monsters of Westmarsh are being coordinated.
- Ulrich meets Sister Aldrith, and the party begins gathering local maps and intelligence.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- The party recovers an illegal dagger, a bottle of liquid, and a Gnollish record book from the slain rider.
- Research identifies the hawk kidnappings as something requiring unusual training.
- Westmarsh's history as a fallen pre-plague region becomes much clearer.
- Westfort's armoury is depleted, and a map of Westmarsh becomes a key asset.
- Sister Aldrith offers holy water, healing, and interest in relics and ancient texts.

## Threads raised this session

- Tobold's abduction becomes the party's most urgent personal goal. Status: Still unresolved.
- The wider kidnapping of children across the frontier is still ongoing. Status: Resolved in Session 40.
- The sabotage of Westfort points to organised enemies behind the frontier unrest. Status: Partially resolved.
- The exact source of the trained hawks is not yet known. Status: Resolved in Session 40.
