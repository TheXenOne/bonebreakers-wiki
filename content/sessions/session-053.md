## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers begin still in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]], with eleven zombies arriving after the death of [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]. The undead charge in with mason's tools and badly injure [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] in the opening assault, but [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] answer with turning magic that destroys many of them. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]], [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]], [[characters/active-players/jennifer/characters/quak-ii|Quak II!]], [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]], and the others finish the rest.

Searching the Bloated One's remains produces no treasure beyond a sash marking it as the Senior Administrator of Residential District V. That title gives the Bonebreakers a clearer way to read the Necropolis's archways: the numbered arches may correspond to administrative districts rather than simple dungeon doors. The party retraces its route to the shrine, where Ulrich's Locate Object points to a hidden chest in the plinth. Luthien finds and opens the concealed door, revealing an [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-shrine-locket|old locket]] painted with a family portrait and a vial of clear liquid. Arden later identifies the vial as holy water, apparently ordinary in power but unusually prayer-focused by its hidden placement.

Back in the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]], Arden again tests the ruby buttons in the imperial eagle statue's eyes and sees no obvious change in the archways. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] then pries the rubies from the eagle for loot, leaving the party with one fewer obvious control for the Nexus and another unresolved Necropolis mystery.

The Bonebreakers decide to return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], but the route above has changed. At the top of the stairwell, the new trapdoor over [[locations/world/agria/southlands/the-abyss|the Abyss]] cannot be opened from below. While the party bangs uselessly at the hatch, [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] uses the [[items/party-inventory/crystal-ball|crystal ball]] to look into the nave and sees that the Mercy's inhabitants have been imprisoned by orcs who appear to belong to the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]].

Unable to force the trapdoor, the Bonebreakers rest in the stairwell. Frank tries to cook food in the hope that the smell will lure the orcs below, but the trick fails. Steve and Selvar trade shouted insults through the trapdoor until Barny checks again with the crystal ball and sees only one orc directly above it. Barny charms that guard through the crystal ball, convinces him to open the hatch and send word that an invasion is coming from outside, then turns Frank invisible while Ulrich blesses the party.

The assault into the nave is quick. The Bonebreakers burst up from below, Arden and Ulrich hold two orcs in place, and Luthien puts the rest to sleep. After freeing the prisoners, Frank questions one surviving orc, Kronk, who says his force came from [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] over the mountains and was only following orders. Steve kills the other captives as the interrogation continues, including the squad leader Kronk says would know more. Before Steve kills Kronk as well, Kronk reveals that the first priest kidnapping from the Mercy months earlier was also carried out for the Grey Syndicate, suggesting that [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh One-Tusk]] may have been working for them.

While Arden is distracted, Barny secretly reanimates the dead squad leader and the party questions him too. The reanimated leader says the orcs were ordered to delay the Bonebreakers. The party cannot yet tell whether the intended delay was meant to keep them away from renewed trouble at [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] or from whatever lies deeper in the Necropolis below the Mercy.

After the fighting, the Bonebreakers rest in the Mercy. Ulrich casts Cure Disease on Steve and Micaelis, ending the rotting infection left by the Bloated One's death burst.

Barny then researches Bahal and the Mercy's history in the library. He confirms that the cathedral was built roughly 200 years ago as a church to Bahal, a healing god, and that the Abyss beneath it is older. The early worshippers knew of the Abyss, feared it, believed it held power, and built a stairway over it in the basement. One treatise describes the Abyss as a kind of stairway to heaven. This makes the party's suspicions about the [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]] sharper. Evidence now suggests something from the Necropolis may have possessed or corrupted him, though the matter remains unproven.

The Bonebreakers split their next move. Steve, Frank, Selvar, and Barny remain at the Mercy while the others travel toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] to restock and recover before another descent. On the road, they meet an unusually wary and intelligent bear. Luthien speaks with it, Arden offers food, and the bear takes a liking to Luthien, beginning to follow her.

In Barrow Spa, the party stays at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/red-herring|the Red Herring]], deposits loot at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/bean-counter|the Bean Counter]], and tries to gather news. No official word has reached town, but rumours say the Curia may have new business emerging and may now be open. Micaelis collects a springy spear that may help him vault obstacles. Luthien asks [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/aldrich|Aldrich]] about the crystal vial from Arch V, but he says a cleric would be better suited to identify it; Arden determines it is holy water. Frank orders specialised crossbow bolts by diagram, including barbed, crescent-headed, and grappling-hook designs, while Luthien orders a fine bow. On the road back, the returning group is ambushed by twelve insectoid humanoids.
