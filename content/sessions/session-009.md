# Session 9 - Tarantella's Tune

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-008|Session 8 - The Jeweller’s Sanctum]], [[sessions/session-010|Session 10 - Pentagrams and Pitfalls]]

## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers continue deeper into the Jeweller's Sanctum by solving the second checkered trap, confirming that black tiles are again the safe route. Beyond it they uncover a ruined library full of spider lore, trap manuals, and giant centipedes feeding on old books. The party clears the room and finds one especially useful book describing several mechanisms they have already encountered, along with references to a guardian and trapped water elsewhere in the dungeon.

A hinged bookcase reveals a narrow passage leading into one of the sanctum's strangest chambers: four silver flute-playing statues, three spike pits, and a giant spider hanging from the ceiling, humming a tarantella. Ulrich briefly distracts the creature by whistling back, but the room erupts into a difficult fight as centipedes burst from the walls.

Barny's Sleep spell saves the party from being overwhelmed, allowing them to kill the spider, claim its silver collar, and finish off the insects. Even so, poison takes its toll on several members, and the Bonebreakers choose to withdraw with the silver statues they can carry. Jessum is pleased with their progress and expects them back once they have recovered.

## Important events

- The Bonebreakers identify the second checkered floor as an electrical trap.
- The library beyond the trap is cleared of giant centipedes.
- Barny finds books on spiders, traps, and unusual dungeon mechanisms.
- A hinged bookcase conceals a narrow passage.
- The party discovers a chamber of silver flute-playing statues and spike pits.
- A giant silver-collared spider hums a tarantella from the ceiling.
- Ulrich briefly distracts the spider by whistling back.
- Barny's Sleep spell neutralizes most of the centipedes during the fight.
- The spider is slain, and Barny claims its silver collar.
- More centipedes emerge, leaving Barny and Rhian poisoned.
- The Bonebreakers retreat with the silver statues they can manage.
