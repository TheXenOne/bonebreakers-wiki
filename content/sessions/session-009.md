## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers continue deeper into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|the Jeweller's Sanctum]] by testing the second checkered floor. A burned rat suggests this trap is different from the blade corridor, and the party deliberately triggers it to confirm that the wrong tiles deliver an electrical shock. They still deduce that the black tiles are safe, crossing carefully and strengthening their sense that the carved face patterns mark the sanctum's trap logic.

Beyond the trap, [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]] spots movement in a ruined library. After a short rest and some mirror-scouting from [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]], the party rushes four giant centipedes feeding on old books and scrolls. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]], Bran, [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/rhian|Rhian]], and [[characters/inactive-players/dom/retainers-followers/grog|Grog]] all help clear the first wave. The room proves valuable for context as well as loot: Barny finds books on spiders and traps, including one that describes mechanisms like those already encountered and hints at both a summonable guardian and a trapped body of water deeper in the dungeon.

The library then opens the way forward. Ulrich discovers that one bookcase has hinges, eventually realizing it opens into a narrow passage. Bran and Arden lead the party through to one of the sanctum's strangest chambers: four silver statues of women playing flutes, three open spike pits, a shimmering magical ceiling, and a giant spider with a silver collar humming a tarantella from above. Ulrich briefly distracts the spider by whistling back while Arden finds a button on one statue, but the room turns hostile when centipedes begin emerging from the walls.

The fight nearly overwhelms the party as more centipedes arrive around the spider. Barny's Sleep spell stops the swarm at the critical moment, allowing the Bonebreakers to kill the spider, claim its silver collar, and execute the sleeping insects. The respite is short: another wave of centipedes bites Barny and Rhian, while Ulrich's immunity protects him. With several companions poisoned or out of action, the party abandons deeper exploration, hauls out the silver statues it can manage, and retreats to the entrance. [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum]] is pleased with their progress, but the western passages, the trap book's clues, and the unexplored rooms beyond the spider chamber remain open business.
