## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers press inland on Fos Imeras and enter the golden temple itself. Two new companions, Durandal and Luthien, join the group as they face a site already overrun by harpies and desperate captives. The party manages to rescue several people but cannot hold the temple and is forced into a hasty retreat.

The retreat turns disastrous. A storm destroys their ship on the rocks, taking both carts, Sirius the horse, and the Alphorn with it. The wreck leaves even the Bonebreakers’ name behind among the debris, underlining how badly the expedition has gone.

While the island falls deeper into chaos, Arden undergoes a more inward transformation. Throughout the session he is increasingly absorbed by visions from Belenus, receiving a clear calling to build a temple and heal the suffering on a much greater scale.
