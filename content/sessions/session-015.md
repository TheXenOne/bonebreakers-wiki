## Narrative recap

After securing the southern beach of [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]], the Bonebreakers press inland toward [[locations/world/fos-imeras/golden-asclepeion|the Golden Asclepeion]], the healing sanctuary that drew them across the sea. Two new companions join the expedition: [[characters/inactive-players/laurene/characters/durandale|Durandale]], a cleric, and [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]], an elf. Inside the golden temple, the party finds the island's crisis is not limited to one harpy on the beach. The complex is infested with harpies and holds desperate captives who need rescue.

The Bonebreakers manage to free several people from the harpy-infested complex, but the first push into the temple does not become a clean victory. Pressure inside the Asclepeion forces them into a hasty retreat before they can secure the site or fully understand what has happened there. The expedition's main objective is still ahead of them, but the party has confirmed that the famous healing sanctuary has become a dangerous prison.

The retreat turns disastrous on the shore. A storm drives the ship onto the rocks and destroys it, taking both carts, Sirius the horse, and the Alphorn with it. The wreck leaves even the Bonebreakers' name among the debris, a sharp reversal after the wealth and momentum of the Barrowshire expeditions. The party is now stranded on Fos Imeras without its old transport, its old signal, or an easy way home.

While the practical situation worsens, Arden undergoes a more inward transformation. Throughout the session he is increasingly absorbed by visions from Belenus, receiving a clear calling to build a temple and provide healing on a much greater scale. The failed first assault therefore changes two things at once: the Golden Asclepeion remains unresolved and dangerous, and Arden's personal religious ambitions become much more concrete.
