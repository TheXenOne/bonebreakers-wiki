## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers spend the aftermath of [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]'s confession trying to turn a dangerous roadside ambush into usable leverage. Arthur is expected to meet [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|the Grey Khan]] in Westmarsh, and the party intends to use that appointment to draw out the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] rather than simply expose him at once. Only Arthur's squires and secretary heard the worst of what he said, but passing caravans see enough to start rumours moving. The party sends [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/pell|Pell]] to [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] with a letter, has Arthur order his guards not to resist, and Steve stabilises the wounded [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/tomas-wren|Tomas Wren]].

Frank secures the most important evidence: [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]]'s ledger, which records meetings tied to the Syndicate. Back at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|The Mercy]], [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]] is already alarmed by reports from fleeing caravans, but Arden smooths the situation by presenting Arthur alive and contained. Frank also extracts an oath from Akal: if the Bonebreakers vanish, the evidence goes to [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]].

The party stores the recent treasure in the cathedral vault, then travels to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/index|Barrow Spa]] to shop and make arrangements. Steve commissions new armour, Frank and Arthur develop an unexpectedly friendly rapport, and Luthien has Arthur try on a mystery ring to identify it before taking back the [[items/party-inventory/ring-of-water-walking|Ring of Water Walking]]. From there the Bonebreakers move to [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/index|Westfort]], using [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/vexs-warehouse|Vex's Warehouse]] as a base while they try to use Arthur's expected meeting to reach the Syndicate through [[npcs/west-fort/gorm-the-fence|Gorm]].

The quiet approach collapses. After several nights of watching and waiting, a message arrives: the meeting is cancelled. The party concludes they have been seen with Arthur and that their cover is broken, so they turn toward Westmarsh instead. An ogre ambush on the road costs one of Arthur's guards but is otherwise beaten quickly by the party's magic. By the end of the session, the Bonebreakers reach [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|The Spider Market]], where its three rules still hold, and see a group of halflings approaching through the marsh.

- The party concludes that its contact plan has failed and heads back into Westmarsh.
