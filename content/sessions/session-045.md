## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers spend the session trying to turn their capture of Prince Arthur into usable leverage before rumour outruns them. They send warning to Commander Smith, keep Arthur and his men under control, and secure written evidence tying the prince to meetings with the Grey Syndicate.

For a while, that looks workable. The party smooths things over at the cathedral, lays contingency plans in the basement, and then tries to use Arthur's secretary to reopen a quiet line of contact through Gorm. That effort fails. By the end of the session the Bonebreakers know they have been seen with Arthur, their covert approach has collapsed, and the next move will have to happen in Westmarsh itself.

## Important events

- The party realises that only a few witnesses actually heard Arthur's confession.
- Pel is sent to Commander Smith with a letter explaining the situation.
- Arthur is made to order his guards not to resist when awakened.
- Steve stabilises the wounded squire Thomas.
- Frank takes a secretary's ledger showing meetings with the Grey Syndicate.
- The party returns to the cathedral and calms alarmed witnesses there.
- Aldus Fenwick is identified as a physician travelling between Westfort and Ikiria.
- Frank extracts an oath from Akal to deliver the evidence to the Princess if the party disappears.
- The treasure is stored in the cathedral vault.
- In Barrow Spa, Steve commissions armour and the party shops for curios.
- Luthien gifts Arthur a Ring of Water Walking.
- The Bonebreakers use Vex's warehouse in Westfort as a base while trying to reach the Syndicate through Gorm.
- After several nights, a message arrives that the meeting has been cancelled.
- The party concludes that its contact plan has failed and heads back into Westmarsh.
- Eight ogres ambush the group on the road, but the party defeats them quickly.
- The session ends with the Bonebreakers reaching the Spider Market and seeing a group of halflings approach.
