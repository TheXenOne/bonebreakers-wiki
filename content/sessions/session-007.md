# Session 7 - The Price of Greed

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-006|Session 6 - The Dragon's Deception]], [[sessions/session-008|Session 8 - The Jeweller’s Sanctum]]

## Narrative recap

Back in Barrow Spa, Barny is raised from the dead but spends time bedridden with resurrection sickness, using it to study new spells. The Bonebreakers regroup, hire a new thief named Neem, collect their refurbished cart, and part ways with Eric before returning to the shrine, where Gorfthistle is already waiting for them.

The new expedition focuses on the shrine catacombs. Guided by Gorfthistle, the party opens several iron caskets, triggering paralysing gas and finally capturing the floating blue automaton insect, only for Bran to smash it against a wall and make it explode. The caskets yield writing tools, ink, and robes, but the real prize comes when Gorfthistle opens the ornate casket of King Bomfluke. Arden quietly takes a red dagger with a moving eye in its pommel, along with coin and silver dust, while Gorfthistle greedily seizes the crown.

Greed ruins the partnership almost immediately. Neem tries to steal the crown by backstabbing Gorfthistle, then lies about what happened. Gorfthistle kills Neem, and the Bonebreakers retaliate by killing Gorfthistle. After discovering he had lied about paying them, the party follows a hidden corridor behind the royal casket into Sootmurk's former lair, only to realize it is a one-way exit. As they work free, Arden tries to claim Auto-Mulvis's head as a trophy, triggering an explosion that kills Dreg and nearly kills Bran and Arden as well.

## Important events

- Barny is resurrected but suffers resurrection sickness.
- The party hires Neem as a new thief.
- The Bonebreakers return to the shrine and rejoin Gorfthistle.
- Ghostly singing in the catacombs is translated as "Those who generously give, Mulvis protects."
- Several iron caskets are opened, releasing paralysing gas.
- The floating blue automaton insect is caught and then smashed, causing an explosion.
- The party recovers a writing implement, a purple ink well, and a blue robe from the catacombs.
- Gorfthistle opens the ornate casket of King Bomfluke.
- Arden quietly claims a red dagger, 100 GP, and a pouch of silver dust.
- Neem attempts to steal the crown and is killed by Gorfthistle.
- The Bonebreakers kill Gorfthistle.
- A hidden corridor behind Bomfluke's casket leads to a one-way exit into Sootmurk's lair.
- Auto-Mulvis explodes when Bran severs its head, killing Dreg.
