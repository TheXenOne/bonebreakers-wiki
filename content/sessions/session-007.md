## Narrative recap

Back in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], the Bonebreakers turn their shrine victory into survival logistics. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] is raised from the dead but suffers resurrection sickness, so he spends his enforced rest studying new spells while the rest of the party shops, reconnects with [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/dreg|Dreg]], and hires [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/neem|Neem]] as a new thief. They collect Sirius and their newly decorated cart, part ways with [[characters/inactive-players/james/characters/eric|Eric]], and return to the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]], where [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/gorfthistle|Gorfthistle]] is waiting for the promised search for King Bomfluke's treasure.

The return expedition focuses on the misty catacombs. Gorfthistle translates ghostly singing as "Those who generously give, Mulvis protects," but the warning does not stop the group from opening the iron caskets. The thieves check carefully and still trigger paralysing gas, briefly disabling both Gorfthistle and Neem before Arden cures Neem. Meanwhile, Arden finally nets the floating blue automaton insect that had eluded them before, only for [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]] to lose patience and smash it against a wall. It explodes, injuring Bran, Arden, and Gorfthistle, and makes clear that the shrine's small mechanisms can be as dangerous as its monsters.

The caskets yield useful but strange treasures, including a writing implement, a purple inkwell, and a blue robe, before the group reaches the ornate casket of King Bomfluke. Gorfthistle opens it and immediately grabs [[items/party-inventory/crown-of-king-bomfluke|the crown]], while Arden quietly pockets a red dagger with a moving eye in its pommel, coin, and silver dust. Arden also notices an odd backing in the casket and pokes through it, revealing a hidden corridor.

The fragile alliance collapses almost at once. Neem tries to steal the crown by backstabbing Gorfthistle in the mist, then shouts as though he were the victim. Virien and Arden rush to separate them, but Gorfthistle executes Neem with a crossbow bolt. Most of the Bonebreakers retaliate and kill Gorfthistle, then discover he had lied about having 500 GP to pay them. The treasure hunt has succeeded, but it leaves two more allies dead and the party holding the crown themselves.

The hidden corridor behind Bomfluke's casket leads into Sootmurk's former lair, but only one way, trapping the party until Virien can work them free. During the delay, Arden tries to pry the head from Auto-Mulvis as a trophy and eventually has Bran cut it off with a magic sword. The automaton explodes, killing Dreg and nearly killing Bran and Arden. The Bonebreakers leave the shrine with more treasure and more losses, haul everything back across the marsh, camp cautiously outside Barrow Spa, and deposit the loot the next morning.
