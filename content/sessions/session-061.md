## Narrative recap

Back at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] explains more of the nightmare he witnessed through the blinded spirit in the Necropolis. The figure handling [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] was a lizardfolk shaman from an unidentified clan, studying it closely. Barny then uses the [[items/party-inventory/crystal-ball|Crystal Ball]] to inspect [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. The siege remains stalemated, although conditions inside the fort appear better than when the party last saw them.

The Bonebreakers first travel to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], where they sell their accumulated loot to [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/ruby-sunbeam|Ruby Sunbeam]]. They also meet [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/michelangelo|Michelangelo]], a sculptor. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] commissions a stone statue of [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]] and asks the party what material would best honour [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]]. After being told that a tree has been planted for her, he commissions her statue in wood, although the material may still change. Michelangelo will need several days with each party member as a model, so the work must be completed during downtime.

At [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/minimarket|the Minimarket]], [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/wistor|Wistor]] buys a random ring from [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/amadeus-thud|Amadeus Thud]]. Once identified, it proves to grant fire resistance. Wistor gives it to [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]], who passes it to [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]]. With their business complete, the party travels west, camps overnight on the road, and reaches Westfort the following day.

The guards stop the Bonebreakers at the gates. [[npcs/west-fort/captain-rodrick|Captain Rodrick]] comes out and warns that many residents now blame the party for Westfort's troubles. At the party's request, he fetches [[npcs/west-fort/commander-valerius|Commander Valerius]] for a meeting outside the walls. Valerius reports that three ogres are operating two catapults and a ballista. Westfort has only about fifty defenders left—ten archers, twenty soldiers, and twenty militia—against roughly ninety hobgoblins.

The Bonebreakers propose approaching through [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]], circling behind the besieging army, and starting a large fire as the signal for Westfort to attack. While the garrison charges out, the party will destroy the siege engines and deal with the ogres. Valerius agrees and provides two barrels of oil for the signal fire.

Rather than cross the guarded bridge, the party heads north through extremely boggy wetlands with dense shrubs and almost no trees. Four dog-sized giant crabs approach on the first day. Wistor kills three with Lightning Bolt, while Arden blinds the last and drives it away. After resting and eating grilled crab, the Bonebreakers ford the [[locations/world/agria/west-flow-river|West Flow River]] westward into Westmarsh, turn south, and camp six to eight miles from the bridge.

Eleven [[factions/military-and-orders/monsters-of-westmarsh/bugbears-of-westmarsh|bugbears]] attack during the first watch. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] incapacitates three with Sleep, and [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] casts Sticks to Snakes, transforming fifteen sticks into fifteen snakes. The conjured snakes poison and wound the attackers while Barny blinds one; Ulrich, Wistor, and the snakes kill several more. Wistor attempts to question the last bugbear, but it refuses to talk and the party kills it.

An easterly wind the next day would drive the planned fire in the wrong direction, so the Bonebreakers wait. A northerly wind follows, and they decide to act. The party makes Micaelis invisible, Arden surrounds him with Silence, and Micaelis carries both oil barrels toward the enemy camp. Unseen and unheard, he pours one long line of oil from the north around the army's rear and a second line southward, leaving a narrow break between them.

At dawn, Micaelis ignites the northern oil line. Fire catches along the upper arc behind the hobgoblin army and begins advancing about ten feet every three rounds. The southern line remains laid but unlit, ready for Micaelis to ignite later. The besieging force is still led by the hobgoblin wearing a broken crown and includes about thirty archers, thirty melee troops, thirty further hobgoblins whose roles are unclear, and three ogres at the siege engines. Westfort's forces remain behind their gates as the Bonebreakers launch their part of the attack.

Ulrich casts Fire Resistance on himself and Selvar, Barny casts Fly on himself, and most of the party advances directly into the burning ground. Rose's opening arrow deals minor damage to one ogre, while Barny blinds another. Still invisible and surrounded by Arden's Silence, Micaelis reaches the first catapult and sabotages its mechanism. The nearest ogre can neither see nor hear him, but the furthest ogre spots that something is wrong.

One of Selvar's shots misses, and ten hobgoblin archers see enough to respond. Selvar's Mirror Image makes him an unmistakable cluster of targets, drawing their massed volley. The session closes on the Bonebreakers standing in the heart of the blaze, then emerging through fire and flames beneath a storm of hobgoblin arrows. Behind them, the northern oil line burns while the southern line waits unlit; ahead, one catapult lies disabled, two siege engines remain active, and Westfort's soldiers are still behind their gates.
