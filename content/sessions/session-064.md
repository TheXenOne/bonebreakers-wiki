## Narrative recap

While the Bonebreakers join the revelry in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow|Harrow]], [[npcs/harrow/fen-blackwater|Fen Blackwater]] leads several of them into a windowless back room at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow/anchor-and-chain|the Anchor & Chain]]. Ulrich asks for the room's key, which Fen gives him. Three hooded figures test the party by asking what the person they rescued aboard the ship first requested. The Bonebreakers correctly recall the demand to keep their association secret. The figures reveal themselves as [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/admiral-nelson|Admiral Nelson]], and [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]].

Agatha believes a deeper threat to her life lies behind the unrest across the Northlands. In her judgement, [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]] could not have organised everything himself, and something beyond even the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] may be involved. She must remain hidden and has little direct information, so she asks the Bonebreakers to investigate on her behalf. This takes precedence over the Necropolis: if she dies, Arthur would be the only surviving heir despite the proceedings against him. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/tess-tattercloak|Tess]] makes sure Agatha knows how much the party has done for Agria and her cause; Agatha confirms that she does.

Agatha recommends beginning with [[npcs/harrow/count-drago-ven|Count Drago Ven]], Count of Harrowmere, while establishing a convincing cover and maintaining it. The party says it has already presented itself to Rickart the Reeve as pilgrims. Agatha considers Ven selfish and effectively the ruler of a pirate town, but does not believe he is ambitious enough to overthrow her or involved in the attempt on her life. The Bonebreakers agree to investigate.

After leaving the secret meeting, the party climbs a rickety staircase from the docks through the warehouse district into Harrow's more developed upper city. The warehouses suggest that the port imports far more than it exports, with narcotics and exotic goods prominent among the cargo. They approach a wealthy inn that resembles an upper-class hotel. On entering, they find its luxurious interior full of raucous gambling and illicit activity. At Tess's suggestion, the party hires [[npcs/harrow/drakford|Drakford]], a dwarf trying to enforce justice amid the disorder. He agrees to provide muscle but does not believe their pilgrimage story.

Seeking influential contacts, the Bonebreakers enter the gambling room and play Deesix, a dice game with a 100-gold ante, and approach [[npcs/harrow/lord-elron|Lord Elron]]'s table as defeated players leave. They treat him antagonistically; [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] unsuccessfully tries to pick his pocket without being noticed. Most of the party folds with poor hands, while Nila has a strong one. Elron nevertheless wins by revealing a seven on a six-sided die. Drakford objects to the blatant cheating, and the others try to calm him. Elron hints that the game has bought them information: his opponents expect useful assistance in exchange for their losses.

The party then meets Elron privately and improvises a story about wanting to remove rival pilgrims supposedly seen in the warehouse district. Elron calls himself the lord of the town and claims to know everything happening in Harrow. He asks them to meet again the next morning. After a night at the inn, the Bonebreakers join him for breakfast at an upmarket café across the street. Following about half an hour of polite conversation, Elron tells them their rivals do not exist: he would have known about them. The party claims it was testing his information network and admits it wants an introduction to the Count. Elron says Ven is already waiting at the Anchor & Chain and that they could simply have approached him directly. He remains cordial and expects to meet them again.

The Bonebreakers return to the docks and enter the Anchor & Chain. Fen takes them back into the same room, where Count Ven is laughing uproariously beside a clerk with a ledger. Ven flirts with Fen, who brushes him off before locking the party inside with him. The Bonebreakers pose as sympathisers who regard Arthur's predicament as a tragedy and want political connections through which to support him.

Ven directs them to [[npcs/kingsport/brother-sedge|Brother Sedge]], a monk who formerly worked in Arthur's Palace in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]], where few people now remain. The Count asks for no payment except that they never reveal he sent them. The party decides to use Sedge to reach Arthur's supporters and boards its ship for the capital.

Three days of clear sailing carry them north along the coast past fishing villages. The journey helps orient them within the [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]]: the region comprises the city-state of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]], western [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria|Veluria]], central agricultural [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm|Grainholm]], and eastern [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere|Harrowmere]]. Fishing villages line the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/salt-coast|Salt Coast]] along the region's north. The party does not visit Grainholm directly.

As their ship approaches Kingsport, the Bonebreakers see a compact, opulent city-state overlooking the nearby Tyne. The Queen's Palace and [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]] dominate a city of ornate royal architecture and elaborate gardens, with no obviously dilapidated buildings. Domestic ships fill the harbour, but the party sees no naval fleet. Their merchant vessel stands out. After they disembark, dock workers and three friendly guards in gleaming armour approach and explain that visitors must register.

Sir Alric Hale, Captain of the Queen's Guard, takes them to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/guardhouse|a nearby guardhouse]], where they are asked to surrender their weapons. Tess is still invisible from the voyage and Nila is sneaking, leaving only [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] and Drakford subject to the demand. Drakford refuses. Ulrich persuades the guards to recognise him as his bodyguard, allowing the dwarf to remain armed; Ulrich's mace is confiscated. Nila secretly steals the key to the safe holding it. Sir Alric registers Ulrich's purpose as pilgrimage and directs him to Brother Sedge across the city.

The party follows Sir Alric's directions across the city to Sedge's simple home. Tess becomes visible before they enter. Wearing plain monastic robes, Sedge cautiously describes himself as a monk of the Furrow and denies personal political involvement. He says he can connect them with Arthur's supporters, while claiming to know nothing himself. Tess casts Glamour and takes the exact appearance of [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]], Arthur's secretary. Sedge's eyes widen. Speaking as Wendell, Tess demands immediate entry to Arthur's Palace to find supporters and help with the prince's trial. Sedge agrees. Tess resumes her own appearance but maintains the impersonation by claiming to be operating secretly.

Sedge escorts the Bonebreakers to the palace and unlocks its door with a key from an enormous ring. Nila secretly pockets the palace key. Sedge then leaves, and the Bonebreakers are alone inside Arthur's Palace, with access secured but its occupants and evidence still to be investigated.
