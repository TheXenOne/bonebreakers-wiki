## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers push farther west through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|the Jeweller's Sanctum]], following the trail of traps and odd rooms left beneath [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor|Tarantella Manor]]. They find a richly furnished study with an embroidered chair, a spider-themed desk, astrological diagrams, a bone flute, a purple glove, and a large checkered tome. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] learns that the tome is about traps, confirming that the party is now reading the dungeon's own design notes while still walking through it.

South of the study, the trap book's warning about water becomes immediate. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]] breaks through a door into a flooded chamber where the dead dwarf adventurer [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum]] previously sent below lies at the bottom, his armor corroded and his flesh ruined. [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] tries to cross by using the broken door as a raft, but a grey ooze climbs his arm, drags him into the water, dissolves his armor, and kills him before the party can save him. Elon eventually helps recover the body, but the expedition has turned from curiosity to another death.

The retreat nearly becomes a second disaster. Centipedes and rats attack on the way out, poisoning most of the group, but Bran cuts through enough of the attackers to keep the survivors moving. Back in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], the Bonebreakers trade Jessum's sealed letter for exclusive access to the sanctum, resurrect Virien, and spend two weeks recovering. [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/aldrich|Aldrich]] identifies the [[items/party-inventory/five-cursed-rings|five rings]] from Morlan's casket as curses rather than prizes, giving each ring's penalty a clearer shape.

When the Bonebreakers return to the manor, Jessum is sleeping better and gives them an [[items/party-inventory/encoded-gnomish-tome|encoded gnomish tome]] of local legends, phenomena, and locations. The party then pushes past the third checkerboard trap by stepping on white tiles, a shift from the earlier black-tile solutions. Beyond it, they enter a room of cages containing the skeletons of strange creatures, including sturges, two-headed snakes, owlbear cubs, and a zorn.

One cage contains a large mirror. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] approaches cautiously with a hand mirror and sees a bedraggled red-haired wizard in fine clothes reflected back. The figure introduces himself as [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]], former assistant to [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/draxas-tarantella|Draxas Tarantella]], and claims he was trapped after making a mistake long ago. The party suspects he is minimizing his guilt, but Arden smashes the mirror anyway. Morlan is freed only into the cage, and when Barny fails to charm him, he answers by throwing a knife.
