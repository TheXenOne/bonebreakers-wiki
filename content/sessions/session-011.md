# Session 11 - The Price of Curiosity

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-010|Session 10 - Pentagrams and Pitfalls]], [[sessions/session-012|Session 12 - A Queen’s Death]]

## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers push deeper into the Jeweller’s Sanctum and find a richly furnished study containing a bone flute, a purple glove, and a large tome on traps. South of it lies a flooded chamber with the corpse of the dwarf adventurer Jessum once sent below. Virien attempts to cross the water on a makeshift raft, but a grey ooze drags him in and kills him before the party can save him.

The retreat is nearly as dangerous as the discovery. Centipedes and rats ambush the party on the way out, leaving most of them poisoned, but Bran cuts a path to safety. Back in Barrow Spa, the Bonebreakers trade Jessum’s sealed letter for exclusive access to the sanctum, raise Virien from the dead, and have Aldrich identify the cursed rings from Morlan’s casket.

After resting, the party returns to the manor. Jessum gives them an encoded gnomish tome, and the Bonebreakers push past the third checkerboard trap by stepping on white tiles. In a room of cages full of monstrous skeletons, Arden looks into a large mirror and discovers Morlan, Draxas Tarantella’s former assistant, trapped inside. Arden smashes the mirror to free him, but Morlan remains confined in the cage and immediately turns hostile.
