## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers let the gloop pilgrims pass and turn back to the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|shrine]] machinery they had only partly understood. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] gathers tools and breaks into the locked side room near the statue chamber, while [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] and [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] remove two of the brass lightning bolts from the chained Mulvis statue. Behind the door, the party finds rotten shelves, automaton parts, moldy robes, and a smaller six-armed automaton version of Mulvis with a bolt-shaped socket in its back.

Back in the foundry, a cry for help draws them to a storeroom where gloop guards are tormenting [[characters/inactive-players/james/characters/eric|Eric]], a captured cleric and lone treasure-seeker. Arden tries to bluff the guards into handing Eric over as food, and the gloops reveal the threat's names before the party ambushes them: the serpent is [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/sootmurk|Sootmurk]], and her local commander is [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/captain-gulian|Captain Gulian]]. One captured gloop explains that Sootmurk's hunger has grown, forcing the gloops to take more travellers from the road.

With Eric freed and added to the expedition, Arden tests the shrine's old foundry system by hurling a brass lightning bolt into the solid iron pool. The bolt becomes real lightning, liquefying the iron long enough for the embedded ornate sword to sink. Virien works the bellows to cool the pool, and Arden manages to snatch the blade before it is lost. Virien takes the new weapon and passes his +1 sword to [[characters/inactive-players/dom/retainers-followers/grog|Grog]], giving the party a major reward before the central fight has even begun.

The Bonebreakers then push through the shrine's stranger inner rooms. They find embalming benches scented with funerary oils, tie up a sleeping gloop only for crab spiders to kill it, and enter cold misty crypt corridors marked by poor warnings about "gnome evil ghost." Iron caskets line the walls, including one larger ornate casket, but without a thief the party cannot open them. A floating blue automaton insect drifts past harmlessly, frustrating repeated attempts to catch it and leaving another ancient mechanism unexplained.

The session ends with the party turning the shrine's social order against itself. Arden frightens southern pilgrims by pretending to be a ghost, Virien confuses northern tribute guards by offering to pay for everyone, and the Bonebreakers set the greasy corridor ablaze as pilgrims and guards scatter. After looting the meagre tribute left behind, they force their way through the double doors into Sootmurk's vast cavern. There they find the serpentine Grease Dragon surrounded by eleven gloop guards, with Captain Gulian standing before her, just as the party prepares to throw lightning into the grease around them.
