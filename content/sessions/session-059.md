## Narrative recap

Inside the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]], the Bonebreakers kill the remaining disenchanter but still cannot move the soul-bound chest, which appears fixed to the floor. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] forces open the far doors, and [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] scouts onward beneath the light of glowing lichen. The passage reaches old sleeping quarters with stone slabs, a weapon rack, and an ash-filled brazier, but a wave of unnatural cold rolls through the area and begins freezing the party. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] protects himself by passing a torch over his body while relying on his ring of fire resistance. In the brazier, Frank finds a bronze button bearing a centurion's command insignia.

The party reaches a four-way junction centred on a stone plinth and an imperial eagle standard. Its inscription orders legionnaires to proceed in the direction the eagle faces, while the sound of marching approaches from every corridor. [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]] turns the eagle north, bringing two minotaurs down that passage. Frank's backstab and Steve's strength help the party kill both creatures, after which the Bonebreakers take 4,000 electrum pieces from them and Arden collects their horns. [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] then hears a soft male voice call her name from one of the corridors.

Turning the eagle east leads the party back to an identical barracks with the same centurion's button, confirming that the east and west passages loop. The northern route instead narrows into a prison lined with iron cells, restraints, and bones. Frank takes a corroded set of lockpicks and finds a warning scratched in Common: “Three days. They know about the vault. Do not trust the halfling.” Beyond the cells, he identifies a pressure plate on the far side of a raised portcullis. Suspecting that it requires several people's weight, the party crosses one at a time and reaches an empty guardroom without setting it off.

In the mess hall beyond, a wight officer waits at the head of a long stone table and orders the intruders to sit. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] answers his questions about the party's homeland and greatly exaggerates Agria's military power. Satisfied, the officer announces that he will leave, pointedly allowing the party to inspect an “unguarded” [[items/imperial-command-quarter-map|map of the surrounding quarter]]. The map labels the chamber through which they entered as the vault, proving they used a concealed back entrance into [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. It also confirms the east and west loops and marks secret passages in the barracks and mess hall, as well as treasure hidden in one of the prison cells.

The officer returns and introduces himself as [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]]. He explains that the emperor is long dead and says the priests did something evil in the vault while meddling with necromancy. When the party asks who the priest involved was, Varro refuses to speak the answer and instead writes [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]], implying a connection without explaining precisely what Valedictus did. The Bonebreakers recognise Valedictus as the author of the necromantic treatise they previously found in Arch VII. Returning to the prison, the party recovers a satchel holding 160 gold pieces and opens a secret route through a short sequence of reworked funerary chambers. The carvings there have been altered to depict a halfling, making the prison warning more troubling, but the passages contain no visible occupants.

The route leads to [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|an enormous domed family tomb]]. Twelve sarcophagi face inward from alcoves, each guarded by two wights. Barny uses the [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] and sees the twenty-four guardians dreaming of reliquaries they no longer wear; the occupant of one sarcophagus dreams of power and wealth. Faced with twenty-four wights, the party withdraws. Back in the command quarter, Varro confirms that the tomb belongs to a separate Necropolis district, showing that the short passage carried the party through a hidden connection between two parts of the dead city. The tomb holds a prominent imperial family's twelve most powerful members, but the family's identity remains unknown.

Further questions clarify other parts of the Necropolis. Varro says the Eighth Wing—[[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|Arch VIII]] in the Nexus—was sealed after something went wrong when the empire ended. He also identifies Valedictus as the high priest in the temple district reached through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]] and says that Valedictus “sees all.”

The Bonebreakers next enter another sleeping hall, where twelve skeletons sit among the personal effects of former soldiers. Micaelis approaches them as a fellow guard, but they attack immediately. Arden and [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] destroy ten with Turn Undead; Rose and Barny kill the remaining two. Among the dead soldiers' possessions, the party finds a child's letter containing only the word “Home.”

The Bonebreakers ask Varro for a tour, and he guides them into a well-maintained imperial armoury. A gated section contains superior weapons, bows, fine plate armour, and a chest. He says that he cannot grant access, but deliberately leaves the armoury unlocked and admits he is very tired after Frank promises that the Bonebreakers will lay him to rest. The party takes weapons and armour, and Frank opens the chest to find 800 gold pieces and a document that remains unread at the end of the session. Their looting triggers a trap, and the session ends as poisonous gas pours into the armoury.

Status: Partially resolved.
