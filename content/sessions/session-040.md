## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers refuse Ash's offer and force the Grey Syndicate's hidden stronghold into open conflict. Though Ash escapes, the party breaks her immediate force, opens the hidden route beyond her chamber, and pieces together written evidence that the Syndicate has been deliberately destabilising Agria through kidnapping, fire, and poison.

The real victory comes after the fight. The children are found alive in the hawks' nesting chamber, rescued, and carried back through the mountains to Westfort. The frontier army breaks, the fort survives, Tess is recovered from Barrow Spa, and the party returns at last to the Mercy, where another crisis is already waiting in the poisoned well and the furious crowd outside Arden's cathedral.

## Important events

- The party rejects Ash's offer despite sharp internal disagreement.
- Ash orders the Bonebreakers killed and escapes through a revolving door.
- The orcs in the chamber are defeated.
- Jeremy is spared and confirms little beyond the Grey Syndicate's secretive nature.
- The party forces open Ash's escape route and finds only a rough tunnel to the surface.
- Syndicate documents reveal a plan to destabilise Agria through poison, fire, and kidnapping.
- The Bonebreakers find the key to the central double doors and open the hawks' nesting chamber.
- The kidnapped children are discovered alive and rescued.
- Barny confirms through the crystal ball that Westfort still stands and the invading army has broken.
- Commander Valerius receives the children and praises the party's success.
- The Bonebreakers collect a 24,000 gold reward and reunite with Tess.
- At the Mercy, the party finds an angry mob and learns the cathedral well has been poisoned.
- Akal disperses the crowd by throwing gold into it after Arden's apology fails.
