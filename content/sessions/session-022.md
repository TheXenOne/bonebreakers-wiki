## Narrative recap

With the library and bishop's quarters finally breached, the Bonebreakers uncover the last clear evidence of how the Cathedral of the Crimson Flame was corrupted. The original bishop's writings describe a church sliding into torture, execution, and necrotic influence, and hidden mechanisms in the altar open a path down toward the source of the rot.

The confrontation with the Crimson Bishop is deadly. Barny is slain by Finger of Death, Frank risks himself in a desperate gaseous-form grapple, and the party only prevails by forcing the bishop out of the air and finishing him together. The session closes with the cathedral liberated, the plague lifted, massive treasure recovered, and both Barny and Frank restored to life in Barrow Spa, even as stranger depths below the church remain unexplored.

## Important events

- Timo claims a translucent sword from a fallen Son of Bahal.
- The bishop's diary confirms the cathedral's corruption deepened over the previous two years.
- Hidden mechanisms beneath the altar open a path downward.
- The Crimson Bishop offers an alliance before combat breaks out.
- Barny is killed by Finger of Death during the fight.
- Frank uses a potion of gaseous form to grapple the bishop in midair.
- Cedric and Athlestan help bring the bishop down.
- Ghoul prisoners are turned and overcome.
- The cathedral's treasure and library valuables are secured.
- A strange voice and an apparently endless descending stair are discovered below.
- Grave spiders in the library kill Frank after the main battle.
- Priests and Captain Fardr are freed, and the cathedral is reclaimed.
- Barny and Frank are resurrected in Barrow Spa the following morning.
