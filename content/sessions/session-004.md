## Narrative recap

With the first profitable [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|Barrow]] haul behind them, [[factions/other/bonebreakers|the Bonebreakers]] are summoned to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/guildhall|the Guildhall]] in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]]. [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/reginald|Reginald]], Guildmaster of the Tourism Merchants, explains that travellers are disappearing along [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kings-road|the King's Road]] as it crosses [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh|Kelfrek Marsh]]. He offers 600 GP on completion, plus two draft horses and a cart up front. [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] consider the pay low for the risk, but the party accepts, expecting the source of the disappearances may hold richer rewards.

Before leaving town, the Bonebreakers turn loot into resources and polish their public image. They commission [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/gus|Gus]] at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/pimp-my-ride|Pimp My Ride]] to repaint their cart black with red lettering, sell valuables through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/bean-counter|the Bean Counter]] and [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/minimarket|the Minimarket]], and buy supplies for the marsh. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] also buys a hunting dog, [[characters/active-players/ben/retainers-followers/fred|Fred]]. Rumors from young adventurers and a farmer on the King's Road point to a "Grease Dragon," blackened trails through the marsh, and toad-like people seen nearby.

The investigation quickly gives the rumors substance. The party follows oily soot-black trails, learns from the farmer that locals believe the creature has taken over a shrine a day's travel into the marsh, and pays him to watch their horses and cart. Ulrich tests the grease and confirms it catches fire violently, turning the trails from simple tracks into both hazard and possible weapon.

At sandstone cliffs on the far side of the marsh, the Bonebreakers find two entrances: a large archway where the oil trails converge and a partially collapsed iron gate. They choose the less obvious gate, squeeze through, and immediately trigger brass automaton hands when Barny prods a corpse. After destroying the hands, the party recovers golden gears and a brass crank box that first seems dangerous but proves to be a recording device shouting "For Mulvis!"

Inside the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|ruined shrine]], ancient gnomish machinery and religious imagery begin to reshape the problem. A chapel-like chamber holds a chained brass statue of Mulvis with three brass lightning bolts and a rancid basin, while murals show gnomes binding or commanding the same serpent-headed figure. Hidden passages lead to a foundry where a solidified pool of iron holds an ornate sword, but Arden cannot pull it free. The session ends when four gloop pilgrims enter calmly, explain that they are following the oil on pilgrimage to a great serpent, and confirm that travellers are being fed to it. The road disappearances are no longer just rumor: the party has found the route into the shrine and the first servants of the threat within.
