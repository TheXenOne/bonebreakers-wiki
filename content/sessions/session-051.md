## Narrative recap

Session 51 begins with a messenger from [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] bringing the Bonebreakers back into Ikiria's civic crisis. Some of the party seek rest in the taverns and brothel, while the rest report the events in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]]. Vaessa examines the coin recovered from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/public-well|the Public Well]] and says it should not exist there: Ikiria was first built by elves, then expanded by gnomes, with no known earlier civilisation beneath it. She suspects the poisoning and the planted coin may be separate acts, perhaps meant to frame someone.

The meeting turns the Bonebreakers' testimony into action. Witnesses from the Plats back their account of the inquisitors, and Vaessa accepts that the civilian inquisitors have been acting like lawful police despite having no such authority. She promises to deal with them, agrees to raise the quarantine problem with the [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]], and says the city will pay to rebuild [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|the Temple of Merciful Life]]. The party keeps its own cache of similar pre-imperial coins quiet, decides not to sell them, and stores them in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]'s cathedral vault before returning below.

The Bonebreakers descend once more through the Mercy route into [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]] Nexus. Through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vi|Arch VI]], they find a storm-lashed gatehouse and rampart that appears to be an outer entrance into the Necropolis, with skeleton archers patrolling the wall and [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] visible beyond. Imperial runes on the great doors reveal an opening mechanism, and [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] works out enough for the party to memorise it. Before they can leave, a false [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] steps out of the trapdoor; it fails to mimic him properly and is killed, carrying the same amount of gold as the real Barny.

The party then explores [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]], a roofed underground boulevard lit by faint lichen and maintained by silent zombie labourers. They loot old apartments, survive an ochre jelly that splits under attack, and learn that [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]]'s newly worn imperial armour is cursed. Deeper in, Barny bluffs a zombie foreman named [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]] with invented health-and-safety authority. Mortimer explains that the labourers are endlessly restoring [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain, then gives directions toward it. At a neighbourhood shrine, the party destroys another Barny-like double, recovers non-evil divine idols for [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]]'s research, and finds a hidden [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|map]] marking the children's quarters, the foreman's station, and the Bloated One's domain.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- The well coin confirms a historical contradiction: pre-imperial Westmarsh coinage should not naturally be present beneath Ikiria.
- The party hides its own pre-imperial coin cache in the Mercy vault rather than trying to sell it.
- An abyss door or hatch has been commissioned to cover the opening down into the Mercy basement.
- Arch VI appears to connect the Nexus to the Necropolis's outer gatehouse and wall.
- Imperial runes on the Arch VI doors explain the opening mechanism.
- False Barnys can appear in the Necropolis, imitate the real Barny imperfectly, and carry at least some matching possessions.
- [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] contains ancient homes, zombie labourers, and a looping restoration project.
- [[items/party-inventory/cursed-imperial-armour|Selvar's ancient imperial armour]] is cursed and worsens his armour class.
- Mortimer names "the restoration of the Bloated One's domain" as the labourers' purpose.
- The shrine contains many divine idols; Ulrich identifies most as non-evil, but three altered idols radiate evil.
- The hidden [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]] marks the areas already explored plus the children's quarters, the foreman's station, and the Bloated One's domain.
