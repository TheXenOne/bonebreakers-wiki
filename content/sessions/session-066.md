## Narrative recap

The morning after searching [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Prince Arthur's palace]] in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]], the Bonebreakers plan how to enter [[npcs/velur/lirina|Countess Lirina]]'s masquerade in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]]. Arthur is under arrest for plotting against his sister, [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]], the Bonebreakers' secret patron. She has asked them to investigate who else may be involved in the threat to her life. The palace search uncovered Arthur's love letters to Lirina and records of his repeated visits to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria|Velluria]], the county she rules. The Bonebreakers want to learn whether she was involved in his plans. They intend to enter the gathering as guests, entertainers, and a server.

[[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] will attend openly as a [[factions/religions-and-cults/belenites|Belenite]] bishop, accompanied by [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] as a fellow cleric. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] will pose as a foreign noble from the [[locations/world/ramen-empire|Ramen Empire]], with [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] as his bodyguard. [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] chooses “The Crimson Lotus” as the name of her acrobatic act. [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] plans to perform under his formal stage name, Barnobuck the Bizarre, but tells the others little about what his act will involve. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] intends to find work as a server.

Frank visits [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/captain-winston|Captain Winston]], who introduces him to a forger. Frank secures two forged invitations, and the party spends the week preparing for the masquerade. Arden acquires long robes decorated with gold, a tall gilt hat, and a ceremonial sceptre that can also serve as a weapon. His eye mask takes the shape of Belenus's sunburst. Ulrich polishes his armour and incorporates the [[items/party-inventory/crown-of-king-bomfluke|Crown of King Bomfluke]] into his helmet. He also casts Continual Light on the symbol of Belenus atop Arden's hat.

Frank dresses in a formal Ramen noble's toga with a gleaming pin and a slightly military appearance, completing the disguise with a lion mask. Steve hunts for skins and makes an animal-skin toga, dressing as a cultured Ramen barbarian with a bear's head for his mask. Rose prepares a silken performance outfit with ribbons and concealed daggers. Nila wears professional evening clothes suitable for a servant. Barny goes grave robbing and raises a minor undead as his female assistant, then dresses them both in multicoloured robes. The party rents an expensive carriage. Nila, Barny, and Rose hire horses and travel ahead to secure their places among the palace staff and performers.

The three reach Velur, a landlocked city filled with wealthy residents and celebrities. It is smaller than Kingsport but considerably more fashionable, with numerous estates, entertainment venues, and elaborate gardens. Lirina's [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] dominates the city, looking part fortress and part entertainment venue. Servants and performers queue to enter Velur through a checkpoint, where guards ask each visitor's name and occupation and record their arrival.

Once inside the city, Nila goes directly to the Gilt Palace. Her convincing servant's uniform gets her put to work immediately. Rose initially struggles to persuade the organisers that she belongs there, but wins a place as the opening act. Barny claims that the scheduled final act cannot attend and that he has been sent as the replacement magician. The organisers accept his story, giving all three a way into the masquerade before the other Bonebreakers arrive.

Later, the rented carriage brings Frank, Steve, Arden, and Ulrich to the Gilt Palace for the masquerade. Spectators have gathered outside to watch the arriving guests. Frank steps out first, immediately attracting attention with his foreign appearance. He presents his invitation and a gift of fine bear hide and leatherwork made by Steve. Frank and his bodyguard enter the palace together.

Arden and Ulrich then step from the carriage and approach slowly and ceremonially. The spectators fall quiet with a degree of reverence, even though many may not know who Arden is. Arden presents his invitation and blesses the assembled crowd. His gift, prepared by Ulrich, is a gem that dazzles with Continual Light. Once the clerics are admitted, all seven Bonebreakers are inside the masquerade.

The palace interior is exceptionally rich and luxurious. Before the assembled guests, the master of ceremonies introduces Lirina Kartashan, Countess of Velluria. She appears at the top of the stairs in a golden half-mask that does little to conceal her identity. The party recognises her immediately from the portrait in Arthur's Palace. Tall, slender, beautiful, and poised, she descends gracefully and pauses beside an ornate, empty ducal chair. She speaks about the political uncertainty facing Agria. As she surveys the guests, her gaze pauses on each Bonebreaker. She appears to know that they are present despite their disguises and cover stories.

Lirina claps her hands to begin the festivities. The Bonebreakers spread through the enormous ballroom, remaining close enough to work together. Its numerous alcoves offer places for private conversations, while attendants and eager guests quickly surround the countess. Frank seeks out an unmarried noblewoman and flirts with considerable success. She appears to be a distant cousin of the royal family. Arden approaches a physician to promote the Belenites' healing, but alarms him by saying that one of the party is almost capable of resurrecting the dead. The physician quickly withdraws from the conversation.

Rose opens the entertainment with an elaborate routine on long silken ribbons suspended above the ballroom. She climbs and pirouettes gracefully until a fumble sends her falling flat onto the floor. Embarrassed, she manages to play off the accident. Meanwhile, Nila circulates among the guests, listening for useful rumours. She learns that the woman Frank is flirting with is related to the royal bloodline and successfully pickpockets a letter from her. She also hears rumours that Lirina is trying to take Arthur's place. The letter's contents remain unknown.

Appearing as Barnobuck the Bizarre, Barny performs grotesque tricks involving knives and his undead assistant. He then turns the [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] on a noble and sees a nightmare of [[npcs/agria/queen-audrey-ii|Queen Audrey II]] being murdered in the streets. Barny incorporates it into his performance, provoking a vehement denial from the noble. He goes on to cut his assistant into pieces and put her back together, leaving the assembled crowd shocked and horrified.

## Important events

- Frank obtains two forged invitations through Winston's contact. The party enters the masquerade as guests, performers, and a server.
- Lirina appears to recognise the Bonebreakers despite their cover stories.
- Frank successfully approaches a noblewoman with royal family connections, and Nila steals a letter from her.
- Nila hears an unconfirmed rumour that Lirina wants Arthur's place.
- Barny publicly reveals a noble's nightmare about Audrey's murder and horrifies the audience with his assistant's dismemberment and reassembly.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- Two forged invitations help the party gain admission. Frank presents Steve's bear-hide and leatherwork gift; Arden presents Ulrich's illuminated gem.
- Ulrich uses Continual Light on Arden's hat and the gift gem. Arden's remark about resurrection describes a capability a party member is approaching, not a spell cast at the gathering.
- Nila takes a letter from the noblewoman Frank approaches. Its contents remain unread. The woman is later identified as [[npcs/velur/lady-miranda|Lady Miranda]].
- The Nightmare-Revealing Telescope shows the noble dreaming of Audrey's murder. This does not establish an actual attack, a plan, or the noble's intentions.
- Lirina addresses the guests beside an empty ducal chair. Nila hears rumours that she wants Arthur's place, but the party has not established what she intends.
