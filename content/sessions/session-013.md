## Narrative recap

With [[items/party-inventory/asclepeion-map|a map to the Golden Asclepeion]] in hand, the Bonebreakers begin looking for a way to identify the mysterious island and reach it. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Francis "Frank" Spencer Gray]] and [[characters/inactive-players/marco/characters/ehecat|Ehecat]] join the company, and the party pieces together the larger geography around them: [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire|Barrowshire]] lies in the western Southlands of East Agria, [[locations/world/westmarsh|Westmarsh]] lies to the west, and the nearest major port is [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] far to the east.

Before leaving, the party stops at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/steamy-smithy|the Steamy Smithy]] to stock up and gather travel information from visitors. Two unfriendly hooded strangers in the changing rooms soon reveal themselves as wererats when screams break out in the spa. The Bonebreakers rush in, kill one attacker, and capture the other, but not before a coffee merchant named James is badly wounded.

Arden heals James while crediting Belenus, earning the merchant's gratitude. James gives the party practical information about Ikiria: the road is dangerous, the city was first built by elves and later renovated by gnomes, and it stands on sea cliffs with an upper city above and docks below. He promises to look for them when they reach the city. The captured wererat says he came from the far southern Badlands in search of human flesh; Barny charms him, names him [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/rattius|Rattius]], and gains a strange new follower for the next month.

The journey east along the river is quieter than the party's recent dungeons, but it still adds future threads. On the first day, they see an ominous cathedral surrounded by dry, barren land and decide not to investigate. On the second night, glowing beetles ambush the camp; Barny's Sleep spell neutralizes them, and the party bottles their luminous cores. On the third day, Ikiria comes into view as a cliffside city of waterfalls, lower docks, open plazas, gnomish renovation, and heavy commerce.

Once inside the city, the Bonebreakers head for [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]], where a gnomish porter arranges meetings for the next morning with [[npcs/flux-academy/ulna-glitterpatch|Ulna Glitterpatch]], head of the College of Health, and [[npcs/flux-academy/herbert-nuel|Herbert Nuel]], master alchemist of the College of Invention. The group spends the evening looking around the docks for James and Jim, then rests at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/upper-class|the Upper Class]], having successfully moved the campaign from Barrowshire to Ikiria.
