## Narrative recap

The session opens with a flashback in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], where [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]] scouts the docks and recruits reinforcements at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/white-horse-inn|the White Horse Inn]]. He hires [[characters/inactive-players/dom/retainers-followers/kargor-viperhand|Kargor "Viperhand"]], a broad duelist with a huge shield and sword, for wages, treasure, and all the ale he can drink. [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] the elf also joins the company, setting up the larger force now fighting at the cathedral.

Back in the present, Timo's illusion still holds the first wave of [[factions/religions-and-cults/church-of-the-crimson-flame|Crimson Flame]] defenders in place. The party uses the breathing room to bind enemies, but two more Crimson Knights and another Son of Bahal arrive before they can fully withdraw. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] traps the new arrivals in Web, [[characters/inactive-players/matt/characters/athlestan-ghoulsbane|Athlestan]] Silences them, and the Bonebreakers grind the fight down through persistence. Athlestan withstands the undead knight's paralysing gaze long enough for him and [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]] to destroy it, while the rest of the trapped enemies are killed or captured.

The party loots fine swords and twisted armor from the fallen defenders, and Bran kills one tied initiate in retaliation for Arden's death. The Bonebreakers then push into the southern cathedral proper, where black marble walls reflect sourceless flames and chained prisoners beg not to be tortured again. The prisoners reveal that they were taken from nearby villages in the night. Frank frees two of them, and the party finds an empty barracks with only minor trinkets before deciding they have pushed far enough.

The withdrawal becomes another running fight. The Bonebreakers gather [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]]'s body, prisoners, and a bound Crimson Knight, then head for the southern gate. Virien and Selvar put two gate guards to sleep, but outside the walls a returning Son of Bahal appears with two more captives. Athlestan charges, is paralysed, and the group struggles through a messy fight until Selvar finally slays the undead escort. The two boys are freed, and Commander Smith is waiting when the survivors reach safety.

The aftermath matters as much as the combat. Bran rides Arden's body back to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], decides not to pay [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/judas|Judas]]'s night-time resurrection surcharge at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/second-chance|the Second Chance]], and has Arden raised the next morning after a comfortable night at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/red-herring|the Red Herring]]. The rest of the party returns the rescued boys to their farming village, earns food and shelter from their family, and interrogates the captured Crimson Knight.

Under Luthien's charm magic, the knight reveals how deeply the church has fallen. He believes everyone outside the Knights and Sons of Bahal is infected, says torture is necessary to find hidden undead, and names [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|the Crimson Bishop]] as the church's leader and source of purity. He also gives the party the first actionable map for the next assault: the Bishop has used a secret door in the northwest room of the northern dome, and the locked library in the northeast dome holds books on divine doubt and the dark arts. Each of the twelve priests has a key, as does [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]]. The first assault has cost the Bonebreakers dearly, but they now know where to strike.
