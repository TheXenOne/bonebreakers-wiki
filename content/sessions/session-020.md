# Session 20 - The Burned and the Bound

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-019|Session 19 - The Bell Tolls]]

## Narrative recap

The session opens with a flashback in Ikiria, where Virien recruits Selvar and the famed duelist Kargor “Viperhand” before the party’s next push. Back in the cathedral grounds, Timo’s illusion is still holding, giving the Bonebreakers time to bind enemies and defeat a fresh group of Crimson Knights and another Son of Bahal with Web, Silence, and sheer persistence.

The party then pushes into the southern cathedral proper. They find chained prisoners, empty barracks, and signs of routine torture and raids on nearby villages. Rather than press too far, they gather bodies, prisoners, and a bound Crimson Knight and fight their way out through the southern gate, even freeing two more captives from an undead escort outside the walls.

The aftermath matters as much as the combat. Bran rides Arden’s body back to Barrow Spa and raises him the next morning, while the rest of the party shelters with a farming family and interrogates the captured knight. Under magical pressure, he reveals the Crimson Bishop’s role, the existence of a secret door in the northern dome, and the locked library containing books on divine doubt and the dark arts. The Bonebreakers finally have a workable map of the next assault.

## Important events

- Virien recruits Kargor “Viperhand” and Selvar in a flashback.
- Timo maintains the illusion while the party defeats another wave of cathedral defenders.
- The party captures at least one enemy alive for questioning.
- Valuable weapons and armor are looted from slain Crimson Knights and Sons of Bahal.
- The southern cathedral reveals chained prisoners and evidence of abuse.
- Frank and Virien free several captives from chains.
- Two sleeping gate guards are bypassed and later killed.
- Another Son of Bahal is slain outside the grounds while escorting fresh prisoners.
- Bran rides Arden’s body back to Barrow Spa.
- Arden is resurrected at the Second Chance the following morning.
- The rescued boys are returned to their farming village.
- A charmed Crimson Knight reveals key information about the Bishop, library, and secret door.

- The party loots seven fine longswords, two large two-handed swords, and two suits of twisted plate armor.
