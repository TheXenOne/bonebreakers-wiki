## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers return to the Cathedral of the Crimson Flame with Sir Cedric and force their way back inside, pushing past the gates and cutting down defenders before making contact with a group of surviving priests. In exchange for food and protection, the priests hand over access to the library, giving the party a foothold deeper inside the occupied church.

The session turns into a mixture of stealth, improvisation, and hard fighting. Frank poisons the kitchen stew, the party uses a "Soup for the Abbott" ruse to move through the complex, and Selvar charms Captain Fardr into revealing more about the cathedral's corruption. By the end of the session the Bonebreakers have reached the bishop's quarters and the library, uncovered evidence that the original bishop feared he had been replaced, and disturbed something waiting above the bookshelves.

## Important events

- Sir Cedric joins the next cathedral assault.
- The party breaks back through the cathedral gates and defeats several defenders.
- Twelve surviving priests are found and agree to cooperate.
- Frank poisons the kitchen stew to weaken the enemy's position.
- The party uses a "Soup for the Abbott" disguise to move through the cathedral.
- Captain Fardr is charmed and reveals that the bishop became undead and that the corruption began about two years earlier.
- A second push reaches the nave and altar area.
- Red candles in the bishop's quarters paralyse several party members.
- A hidden holy symbol and the original bishop's diary and letter are discovered.
- Barny reaches the library and gathers books on dark arts as grave spiders stir overhead.
