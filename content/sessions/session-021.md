## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers return to the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|Cathedral of the Crimson Flame]] with [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Sir Cedric Dawnmere]], a knight whose own house was destroyed by undead and who has reason to hate what the [[factions/religions-and-cults/church-of-the-crimson-flame|Church of the Crimson Flame]] has become. Their aim is to force a second breach, find the source of the corruption, and decide whether anyone inside the cathedral can still be saved.

The assault begins violently. The party rides and fights through the gates, cuts down the first defenders, and is quickly forced into a running battle with Crimson Knights and Sons of Bahal inside the complex. After pushing through the first resistance, they turn east and find twelve ragged priests hidden on straw in rooms marked with blood-soaked religious slogans. The priests are desperate, hungry, and frightened; in exchange for food and protection, they give the Bonebreakers the library key and become the party's first real foothold inside the occupied church.

From there, the Bonebreakers shift into disguise and sabotage. In the kitchen, a cook mistakes them for cathedral troops and serves them gristle stew, giving Barny the opening to poison the cauldron. The party then dons stolen uniforms and pushes the bubbling food deeper into the complex under the cry of "Soup for the Abbott," using the ruse to move through enemy-held space without immediately drawing the whole garrison onto them.

The disguise carries them into the northern dome, a soot-darkened nave hung with crimson banners, and from there to a side chamber where [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]] is found raving, armed, and fighting imaginary enemies. Selvar charms him, but the escape nearly collapses when four undead Sons of Bahal patrol past and refuse to fall for the soup ruse. Virien seals the door long enough for the Bonebreakers to smash their way out through the windows and retreat with Fardr to the farmstead.

At the farm, Fardr's broken testimony gives the party its clearest lead yet: the bishop became an undead abomination around two years earlier, and Fardr believes the corruption can only end if the undead are purged. With that knowledge, the Bonebreakers return the next day for a more direct strike, charging through the nave and into the altar area. There they face Sons of Bahal and Crimson Knights around a bonfire of bones and seven blood-stained golden chalices, with Fardr and Cedric both helping clear the chamber.

The final discoveries come in the bishop's quarters and library. Behind a secret door, the party finds an immaculate room whose red candles flare and paralyse several Bonebreakers, forcing the still-mobile members to keep searching under pressure. They uncover a hidden magical holy symbol and writings from the original bishop, suggesting he feared something would take his place. Meanwhile, Barny reaches the library, gathers books on divine doubt and the dark arts, and disturbs grave spiders lurking above the shelves.
