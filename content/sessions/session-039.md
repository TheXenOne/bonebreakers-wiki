## Narrative recap

With siege looming, the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] spend a last calm morning gathering favours, gear, and blessings in [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. Steve receives a charm-deflecting shield from [[npcs/west-fort/mara-ironhand|Mara Ironhand]], Rose trades with [[npcs/west-fort/gorm-the-fence|Gorm the Fence]] for a [[items/party-inventory/necklace-of-strangulation|necklace of strangulation]], and Cedric prays with [[npcs/west-fort/sister-aldrith|Sister Aldrith]], receiving the [[items/party-inventory/book-of-sublime-holiness|Book of Sublime Holiness]]. [[npcs/west-fort/elara-swiftstring|Elara Swiftstring]] equips Luthien with Elven boots and a stealthy cloak, Selvar receives a Displacer Cloak, and Ulrich gets `+1` plate armour from Mara. Another message from the Mercy reports more deaths, and Barny privately confirms through the crystal ball that Arden is desperate for Cedric's help.

The bells interrupt any chance of responding to the church crisis. From the battlements the party sees the invasion force approaching: hobgoblin archers and infantry, ogre-drawn siege engines, and two dozen giant hawks carrying goblins. The Bonebreakers blunt the aerial attack with overlapping Sleep spells, heat, blindness, and other magic, dropping many hawks and scattering the rest before the main army reaches the walls.

Rather than wait for the siege, the party counterattacks. Cyder makes Steve invisible and Barny makes him fly, letting him sabotage the siege engines ahead of the army by cutting ropes and sowing confusion. The Bonebreakers then ride back to the invasion fort, slip inside through Akal's tiny-form work at the portcullis, and find the great hall nearly empty. Bugbears surprise Steve, but recognition from the earlier mine encounter keeps the moment from becoming a full fight.

The deeper search reveals the real stakes. A long corridor of empty cells contains toys, chalk markings, and other signs that the kidnapped children were held there recently. Barny uses the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]] to glimpse [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/jeremy|Jeremy]] speaking with [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]] somewhere deeper, and Ulrich finds the hidden passage leading underground.

The party follows the passage through the complex until orcs guide them to Ash instead of attacking. Calm and in control, she offers the Bonebreakers a bargain on behalf of the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]: investigate [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|The Necropolis]], share what they learn, and keep the treasure. By session end, the open siege has become entangled with a deeper criminal network, and the children are close but still not found.
