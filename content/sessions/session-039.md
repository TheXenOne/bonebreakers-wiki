## Narrative recap

With siege looming, the Bonebreakers spend a last calm morning gathering favours, gear, and blessings in Westfort. Those preparations are cut short when the invasion army finally appears and launches its aerial attack with giant hawks and goblin riders.

Rather than remain behind the walls, the party counterattacks on its own terms. After breaking the hawk assault and sabotaging the siege engines, the Bonebreakers return to the enemy stronghold, find the army gone, uncover signs that the kidnapped children were recently held there, and finally meet Ash in person. Her offer on behalf of the Grey Syndicate shifts the arc from open siege warfare toward a deeper conspiracy.

## Important events

- The party secures aid and magical gear from allies in Westfort.
- Another urgent message from Arden reports more priest deaths at the Mercy.
- The invasion army approaches Westfort with archers, infantry, siege engines, and giant hawks.
- Combined Sleep spells drop many hawks and goblins from the sky.
- Steve sabotages the siege engines under invisibility and flight.
- The Bonebreakers re-enter the fortress and find its main army gone.
- The long hall of cells reveals clear evidence that children were held there.
- Barny spies Jeremy speaking with Ash deeper inside the complex.
- Ulrich discovers a hidden passage leading underground.
- Orcs guide the party onward instead of attacking.
- Ash identifies herself as part of the Grey Syndicate and offers the party a bargain involving a nearby necropolis.
