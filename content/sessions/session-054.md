## Narrative recap

Back in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], the Bonebreakers regroup with two new player characters: [[characters/inactive-players/marie/characters/fred|Fred]] and [[characters/inactive-players/valerio/characters/paul|Paul]]. [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]], [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]], [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]], Fred, and Paul set out by road toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], intending to rejoin the rest of the party before another descent into [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]].

On the road near the broad river that runs from Barrow Spa toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], they come upon twelve four-armed insectoid humanoids carrying polearms and deep battle rucksacks. The group catches the creatures from behind, but the numbers look too dangerous for a clean fight. They try to flee instead, only to discover that the mantid-like warriors are impossibly fast. Arden's Hold Person fails completely, and even the party's horses cannot open enough distance.

The fight quickly turns disastrous. Fred charges in and lands a strong blow, while Bran fires accurately with his longbow, but the creatures dodge missiles with supernatural speed. They answer with claws and thrown crystal discs, cutting down Fred and Paul and wounding Arden. With no clear way to win and the others unable to escape cleanly, Bran makes the choice that defines the session: he sacrifices himself for the party, throwing himself into all twelve enemies and forcing their attention onto him.

Bran lands heavy blows on several of the creatures and survives long enough under the attacks of the whole group for Arden and Micaelis to get away with Fred and Paul's bodies. Bran dies in the melee, and his body cannot be recovered. His sacrifice saves the survivors, but leaves the road threat unidentified and still at large.

The survivors return to Barrow Spa, where Fred and Paul are resurrected. The party sends word by river trader to the Mercy, purchases a riverboat for 4,000 gold pieces, and hires eight oarsmen so it can travel 36 miles a day. They then rest for two weeks while Fred and Paul recover from resurrection.

During that downtime, the Bonebreakers turn grief into preparation. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] commissions a moat around the Mercy and has its walls raised to 30 feet, making the cathedral much harder to assault. Arden prays for a new spell in Bran's honour, Bran's Heroic Aura, which shields one ally and draws nearby enemies' attention for the rest of an encounter. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] researches a spell for locating nearby treasure, [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] improves the [[items/party-inventory/staff-of-withering|Staff of Withering]], and [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]], Frank, and [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] finish studying the [[items/party-inventory/manual-of-strength|Manual of Strength]], with Selvar and Steve gaining strength from it.

Ulrich asks for news of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] and learns that the renewed invasion has become a siege. The battle is stalemated: the enemy force is much reduced from its original size, but still has siege engines and includes hobgoblins, goblins, and ogres. Barny uses the [[items/party-inventory/crystal-ball|crystal ball]] to view the siege at the west gate, but does not share what he sees with the party.

When the party returns to the Mercy, they hold a large wake and ceremony for Bran and plan to commission a statue in his honour. Arden speaks of Bran as the Bonebreakers' shield, sword, and protector through many trials. The ceremony marks Bran's death as more than a battlefield loss: it becomes part of the Mercy's story and the party's own memory of survival.

After the wake, the Bonebreakers descend into the Necropolis again. The priest bolts the trapdoor behind them, and the party unspikes the lower trapdoor into the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis#The Nexus|Nexus]]. This time they choose [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]], the white arch with a faint green tinge and a smell of damp stone and stagnant water.

Beyond the arch, they enter a vast moisture-dripping hall covered in lichen and sloping downward. Farther in, they reach a street lined with rotted market stalls. Zombies stand at the sides, moving through the motions of market day: welcoming customers, holding out hands for payment, and handling goods that are no longer there. Frank plays along with one selling invisible art supplies. Steve gives two gold pieces to another zombie, which examines the unfamiliar coins, unlocks a display case, and hands over real gems worth 380 gold pieces.
