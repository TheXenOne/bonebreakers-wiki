## Narrative recap

With Gruumsh's warband broken and the church secured, the [[factions/other/bonebreakers|Bonebreakers]] leave [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/sir-cedric-dawnmere|Sir Cedric]] and the priests behind and return east to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]]. Frank uses the pause to establish the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|Temple of Merciful Life]] in a lower-city warehouse: public shrine in front, hidden information hub behind the curtains. He also hires [[characters/active-players/deri/retainers-followers/merrick|Merrick]] to spread a wildly embellished ballad about the party, including Frank's rescue of Princess Agatha and Athlestan's warnings about the [[npcs/westmarsh/king-of-ghouls|King of Ghouls]]. The publicity may help Frank, but it also creates risk; Ulrich later warns [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]], who is clearly unhappy.

That civic work collapses into panic when weapons across Ikiria begin talking. The party's own arms complain, threaten, or insult their bearers, and everyone who keeps hold of an awakened weapon starts feeling drained. Citizens who surrender their weapons seem to recover, while guards hurry people toward [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]] and mutter that the trouble must be caused by "him" again. The Bonebreakers follow the trail uphill, with minor chaos along the way: Frank steals from a noble, Arden redirects a frightened citizen toward the new temple, and Athlestan provokes an automaton badly enough that he and Arden have to flee.

At the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/precisely-measured-scroll|Precisely Measured Scroll]], [[npcs/ikiria/helvian|Helvian]] points the party toward [[npcs/flux-academy/screever-longtooth|Professor Screever Longtooth]] and [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]], a troublesome wizard protected by Academy politics and the merchant council led by [[npcs/ikiria/nixx-pocketbrain|Nixx Pocketbrain]]. Longtooth confirms the crisis is deadly: the weapons are draining their bearers' constitution and could leave people comatose or dead within hours. She believes Arkeus is the likely source, though she still sees him as a brilliant old friend, and asks the Bonebreakers to recover the original component or caster behind the effect, especially a translucent planar tuning fork later known as the [[items/party-inventory/astral-tuning-fork|Astral Tuning Fork]].

With Longtooth's recommendation, [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] authorises the party to enter [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/tower-of-arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus' tower]] under martial law and offers payment for proof of his involvement. She warns that previous groups vanished or returned babbling about clones and warped magic. By the end of the session, the Bonebreakers have reached the impossible cliffside tower, stepped through its hidden entrance, crossed an astral corridor on a stolen rug, and solved the first resonance puzzle. Ikiria's weapon crisis remains urgent, and the party is now inside the place most likely to decide it.

- Frank rents a warehouse in Ikiria and creates a Temple of Merciful Life front.
- Virien helps Frank secure an upper-city apartment.
- Merrick is hired to spread a heroic song about the party.
- Weapons across Ikiria awaken and begin speaking.
- Citizens who surrender their weapons seem to recover somewhat.
- Helvian points the party toward Professor Screever Longtooth and Arkeus Whizkeyrd.
- Longtooth warns that the sentient weapons are draining constitution and may kill within hours.
- Vaessa Silvertread allows the party to investigate Arkeus' tower and offers payment for proof.
- The tower's entrance puzzle leads to a hexagonal room with a shadow-based clue.
- The party crosses an astral-feeling corridor and solves a second puzzle involving crystal statues and wordplay.
