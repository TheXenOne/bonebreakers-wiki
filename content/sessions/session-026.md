# Session 26 - The Day the Steel Spoke

See also: [[Sessions]], [[sessions/session-025|Session 25 - A Deal with Gruumsh (And Other Bad Ideas)]], [[sessions/session-027|Session 27 - Reflections of the Mind]]

## Narrative recap

The Bonebreakers shift their attention to Ikiria and quickly stumble into a city-wide magical crisis. Sentient weapons begin speaking, draining their owners, and spreading panic across the city, while Frank sets up a warehouse and a front-facing Temple of Merciful Life that doubles as a new urban base of operations.

Their investigation points toward Arkeus Whizkeyrd and the Flux Academy. Professor Screever Longtooth explains that the awakened weapons are exhausting their users and may kill or cripple many people within hours, then directs the party toward Arkeus' tower in search of the original component behind the enchantment. By session end the Bonebreakers have found the tower, solved its first strange entry puzzles, and stepped into a much stranger environment than the streets outside.

## Important events

- Frank rents a warehouse in Ikiria and creates a Temple of Merciful Life front.
- Virien helps Frank secure an upper-city apartment.
- Merrick is hired to spread a heroic song about the party.
- Weapons across Ikiria awaken and begin speaking.
- Citizens who surrender their weapons seem to recover somewhat.
- Helvian points the party toward Professor Screever Longtooth and Arkeus Whizkeyrd.
- Longtooth warns that the sentient weapons are draining constitution and may kill within hours.
- Vaessa Silvertread allows the party to investigate Arkeus' tower and offers payment for proof.
- The tower's entrance puzzle leads to a hexagonal room with a shadow-based clue.
- The party crosses an astral-feeling corridor and solves a second puzzle involving crystal statues and wordplay.

## Items, magic, and discoveries

- Sentient weapons across Ikiria are magically active and dangerous to keep.
- Longtooth identifies a missing original component, especially a translucent tuning fork, as key to the crisis.
- The tower entrance clue reads: "The leap lies in shadow - step where none would."
- The party learns the crisis is magical, city-wide, and time-sensitive rather than a local haunting.
