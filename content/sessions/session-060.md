## Narrative recap

Still trapped in the command-quarter armoury beneath the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]], the Bonebreakers gain two new companions. [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]], a thief who followed the party inside, takes the document from the chest Frank opened and reads it. It states that a secret passage was sealed on [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune's]] orders. An arrow scratched where the poison-gas trap activated points down. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/wistor|Wistor]], a dragonborn who reveals himself after approaching invisibly, offers to buy the document from [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]], who sells it to him for fifteen gold pieces. Wistor then turns the mannequin that once held the looted sword and opens a second secret route, while [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] detects a hollow space beneath the floor.

[[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] pries up a loose flagstone and the party scavenges the hidden pay room below. Its chests and strongboxes hold military medals that were never awarded, unpaid wages, a silver eagle, cavalry tack, arm links, swords, and an imperial plumed helmet, together worth 1,980 gold pieces. The party then takes the mannequin passage. A sharp chemical smell prompts caution: Arden drops a torch down its first ladder to judge the depth, then extinguishes it when the party realises the vapour may be flammable and uses one of Ulrich's Continual Light stones instead. The group climbs down, follows the passage, and reaches a second ladder leading upward.

Arden climbs the second ladder and uses a crowbar to slide aside the stone slab blocking its top. It opens into a low resin-scented chamber resembling an embalming workshop. Eight channelled stone tables, sealed jars, iron instruments, funerary carvings, and a basin of adhesive black resin fill the room. Wistor opens powdered silver worth sixty gold pieces but releases a gas that nearly incapacitates everyone; when he breaks another jar, a second vapour paralyses Arden and [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]]. Ulrich's healing magic frees Arden, who then cures Selvar. Experiments with flame reveal that the carved mourners' eyes track the nearest light while other illumination dims.

An armadillo-like creature wanders into the workshop. [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] tries to feed it, but Wistor's lightning and Arden's first strike reveal it as a rust monster: Arden's magical mace rusts away completely, and the creature devours Rose's spear. Ulrich turns sticks into snakes, helping the party kill it before it can consume more equipment. He then collects several of the sealed jars.

North of the workshop, the Bonebreakers enter [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|the tomb of House Varrick]]. Twelve sarcophagi line a rectangular chamber around a raised black-marble coffin. Although Ulrich detects no evil intent, a voice inside the open coffin challenges the party's purpose. Arden explains that they only seek a route back to the Nexus and agrees to exchange information. [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]], an undead noble of the once-powerful [[factions/necropolis/house-varrick|House Varrick]], asks whether his family is remembered. Wistor tells him that the plague destroyed most civilisation in Westmarsh and that the house has been forgotten.

In return, Lord Varrick answers the party's questions. He suggests that the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]] may be seeking the necromantic power of [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|High Priest Valedictus]], or may instead intend to destroy him. Varrick remembers Valedictus as an expansionist necromancer who rejected his petitions three times, ignored heavy opposition within the Imperial City, and sought dominion across the continent. When the party confirms that undead activity is worsening in Agria, Varrick warns that it will continue while Valedictus remains in power. He advises the Bonebreakers to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]] and reveals that Valedictus possesses three phylacteries.

Wistor records the tomb's carved family history, which shows Varrick's three failed petitions. The party then reconsiders the skull found amid broken chains and stakes in Arch VII's Foundation Chamber and later traded away by [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]]. They suspect that [[items/glowing-skull|the glowing skull]] is one of Valedictus's phylacteries and that another may lie inside the three-locked, soul-bound chest at the Imperial Hill entrance.

After backtracking to the armoury, Nila fails to pick the locked gate, so Ulrich surrounds the group with Silence while they dismantle it using hammers, spikes, and crowbars. An incorporeal spirit wanders past despite the lack of noise. Its supernatural presence terrifies Nila and Rose, ages each of them ten years, and sends them fleeing. Neither Arden nor Ulrich can turn it, Wistor's breath cannot harm it, and its claws reach through the gate to drain four points of Wistor's Constitution. Ulrich averts a likely disaster by casting Continual Light directly upon the spirit and permanently blinding it.

Barny studies the blinded spirit through the [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] and sees it dreaming of a glowing skull in the hands of lizardfolk, strengthening the party's belief that the traded skull is a phylactery. The Bonebreakers wait motionless until the spirit wanders away, then finish dismantling the gate and continue north.

The route opens onto a balcony above an immense drill yard where a wight and three sentries oversee 300 skeletons repeating the same exercises. The [[items/imperial-command-quarter-map|command-quarter map]] identifies this route as District One, Arch One. Barny drinks a Potion of Control Undead and permanently takes command of the wight and six skeletons, using absurd drill orders to test their obedience while the party crosses the long bridge overhead. Beyond the far gallery, the route passes through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-i|Arch I]] and returns the Bonebreakers to the Nexus, finally proving which arch connects to the Imperial Hill command quarter.

The lower trapdoor into the Mercy remains spiked shut, but Wistor casts Knock and opens it from below. The party climbs into the well, closes and spikes the trapdoor again, and returns to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], where a waiting priest admits them and says preparations will be made for [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien's]] funeral.
