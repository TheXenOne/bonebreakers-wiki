The Bonebreakers through [[sessions/session-067|Session 67]]. [[#Sessions 61–67|Jump to the latest events]].

## Sessions 1–10

- [[sessions/session-001|Session 1]]

  **Adventurers explore the Barrow in search of treasure**

  Clues from the rescued necromancer [[npcs/barrowshire/the-barrow/quintius|Quintius]] draw more adventurers to the Barrow near [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]], where skeleton ambushes and sealed passages force them to find another way out.

- [[sessions/session-002|Session 2]]

  **A fairy is freed, but three adventurers die in the Barrow**

  The expedition frees the trapped pixie Alfwine and retrieves blue gems from a hidden chamber, but Stan, Ike, and Virien are killed before the survivors escape.

- [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]]

  **Virien returns to life and the Bonebreakers claim their first hoard**

  After recruiting retainers and adopting the company name, the survivors return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]], defeat its skeletons, and carry out a large cache of treasure.

- [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]]

  **Missing travellers lead the Bonebreakers to a gnomish shrine**

  A commission to investigate disappearances along the King's Road leads into Kelfrek Marsh and the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]], where gloop pilgrims reveal that captives are being taken deeper inside.

- [[sessions/session-005|Session 5]]

  **The shrine's kidnappers serve Sootmurk, the Grease Dragon**

  After rescuing the captive Eric, the Bonebreakers learn that the gloops feed travellers to Sootmurk and enter her cavern to stop the kidnappings.

- [[sessions/session-006|Session 6]]

  **The Bonebreakers escape captivity and kill Sootmurk**

  Captured by the dragon's magic, they bluff their guards and escape through fire, then return with a mechanical idol to win her hoard at the cost of Barny's life.

- [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]]

  **Barny is revived, but a return for treasure costs Dreg's life**

  Back at the shrine, a fight over [[items/party-inventory/crown-of-king-bomfluke|King Bomfluke's crown]] kills Neem and Gorfthistle, and an exploding automaton kills Dreg before the Bonebreakers leave with their loot.

- [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]]

  **A new commission opens Draxas's buried workshop**

  [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum Tarantella]] offers the treasure beneath his manor in return for reclaiming his grandfather's sanctum, where trapped floors and giant rats block the first rooms.

- [[sessions/session-009|Session 9]]

  **The Bonebreakers loot Draxas's sanctum and retreat poisoned**

  Clearing the library and a chamber of flute-playing statues yields silver treasure, but centipede venom forces the party back to Barrow Spa for treatment.

- [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]]

  **Cursed rings reveal the dangers of Draxas's experiments**

  After selling the silver and returning underground, the Bonebreakers overcome the sanctum's guardians and find an anti-magic casket whose rings curse Barny.

## Sessions 11–20

- [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]]

  **Virien is revived again, and Draxas's imprisoned apprentice is found**

  After an ooze kills Virien, the party resurrects him, receives an [[items/party-inventory/encoded-gnomish-tome|encoded tome]] from Jessum, and returns to release [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]] from a magical mirror.

- [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]]

  **The Rat Queen's defeat leaves a map to a distant healing temple**

  With Draxas's sanctum cleared, the Bonebreakers decipher a map hidden in Jessum's tome pointing to a [[locations/world/fos-imeras/golden-asclepeion|Golden Asclepeion]] and a marked lake on an unidentified island.

- [[sessions/session-013|Session 13]]

  **The map draws the Bonebreakers east to Ikiria**

  They travel to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] to identify the island, gaining the charmed wererat Rattius as a companion on the road and arranging meetings with scholars at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]].

- [[sessions/session-014|Session 14]]

  **The Bonebreakers sail to Fos Imeras to investigate its healing temple**

  [[npcs/flux-academy/ulna-glitterpatch|Ulna Glitterpatch]] sponsors the expedition to [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]], but an intercepting Ramen fleet also demands news of missing personnel and the magical ship Eleftheria before allowing them ashore.

- [[sessions/session-015|Session 15]]

  **Harpies guard the healing temple, and a storm strands the party**

  The Bonebreakers free captives from the Golden Asclepeion but must retreat, then lose their ship, carts, horse Sirius, and alphorn when a storm wrecks their vessel.

- [[sessions/session-016|Session 16]]

  **Fos Imeras's oracle falls, and a magical ship offers escape**

  After killing the oracle Sebastaeia and discovering her mutilated Purified, the party rescues the missing Orsilochus and solves a lake riddle to raise the Eleftheria.

- [[sessions/session-017|Session 17]]

  **The Bonebreakers rescue Princess Agatha and sail home**

  A final return to the Asclepeion frees [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Agatha]] and seven other Agrian nobles, while the recovery of the missing Imperials earns the Ramen fleet's escort back to Ikiria.

- [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]]

  **Agatha recruits the Bonebreakers to end the deathless plague**

  Back in Ikiria, the rescued princess reveals she is Agria's heir and secretly commissions the party to investigate the [[factions/religions-and-cults/church-of-the-crimson-flame|Church of the Crimson Flame]] and its cathedral.

- [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]]

  **The first assault on the Crimson Flame cathedral meets deadly resistance**

  Crimson Knights and undead Sons of Bahal counterattack after the alarm sounds, killing Arden and Durandale before Timo's illusion gives the survivors a chance to escape.

- [[sessions/session-020|Session 20]]

  **The Bonebreakers free the cathedral's prisoners and revive Arden**

  A captured knight reveals a secret route used by the bishop, while rescued villagers are returned home and Bran takes Arden to Barrow Spa for resurrection.

## Sessions 21–30

- [[sessions/session-021|Session 21]]

  **A charmed captain reveals the Crimson Bishop's undead rule**

  [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]] describes the bishop's corruption, and another assault reaches the altar and private quarters, where the Bonebreakers discover the original bishop's diary.

- [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]]

  **The Crimson Bishop falls, and the cathedral becomes the Mercy**

  His defeat ends the deathless plague and earns the Bonebreakers [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the cathedral]] as a permanent home, though Barny and Frank must be resurrected after the fighting.

- [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]]

  **The Bonebreakers cleanse the Barrow and claim it for magical research**

  Barny's search for lost magical knowledge brings the party back to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|its first dungeon]], where Arden purges the remaining necromancy and the group annexes the site.

- [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]]

  **Orcs raid the Mercy and abduct four priests**

  Tracing the kidnappers to Sootmurk's former shrine, the Bonebreakers buy one priest's release and plan to rescue the other three from [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh One-Tusk]]'s warband.

- [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]]

  **The Bonebreakers rescue the priests and force Gruumsh to surrender**

  An assault on the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]] destroys much of the warband, recovers the hostages and treasure, and leaves Gruumsh alive but defeated.

- [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]]

  **Talking weapons drain Ikiria's citizens, leading to Arkeus's tower**

  Flux Academy warns that the awakened weapons could kill within hours, and the Bonebreakers trace the crisis to the tower of the wizard [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]].

- [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]]

  **The tower hides a clone and the captive real Arkeus**

  A clone offers answers in exchange for a Harmonic Regulator, while the party unlocks a sealed chamber containing the real Arkeus and two crystal guardians.

- [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]]

  **The weapons crisis ends, and missing nobles lead to the Blood King's manor**

  Freeing Arkeus opens an astral portal that takes both wizards and the possessing motes, after which news of abducted nobles draws the party to the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king|Halls of the Blood King]].

- [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]]

  **A trapped vampire-hunter explains how to kill the Blood King**

  [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/selana|Selana]] allies with the Bonebreakers and reveals that the king's hidden heart must be destroyed, giving their search through the shifting manor a clear objective.

- [[sessions/session-030|Session 30]]

  **Disguised as vampires, the Bonebreakers gather clues inside the manor**

  A drunken vampire describes the Blood King's resurrection relic and [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/lord-demetrius|Lord Demetrius]]'s refusal to drink blood, before talking spiders attack the party in the conservatory.

## Sessions 31–40

- [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]]

  **A bargain with the Blood King's daughter helps free his prisoners**

  After killing Circander for the [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/princess-of-blood|Princess of Blood]], the Bonebreakers receive a wand, key, and map, then use the key to enter the dungeons and rescue captives.

- [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]]

  **Destroying the Blood King's hidden heart brings down his manor**

  A planetary mechanism reveals the heart in the observatory, and the Bonebreakers destroy it and escape with rescued prisoners, though their ally [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/selana|Selana]] dies.

- [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]]

  **Tobold's abduction leads to Westfort and signs of a wider attack**

  Giant hawks carry off [[characters/active-players/david/characters/tobold-took|Tobold]] and local children, sending the party to [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]], where arson, poisoned water, and stolen weapons suggest coordinated sabotage.

- [[sessions/session-034|Session 34]]

  **A failed ambush in Westmarsh costs Tess her life**

  The search for the kidnappers runs into a gnoll warband, and the survivors send Tess's body for resurrection while questioning captives about the marsh.

- [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]]

  **An inn fire exposes the saboteurs weakening Westfort**

  After rescuing patrons of the Cracked Shield, the Bonebreakers capture [[npcs/west-fort/grey-syndicate/vex|Vex]], recover stolen weapons, and use his warehouse to trap the hooded handler behind the attacks.

- [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]]

  **The captured handler leads the party to Ash at the Spider Market**

  Charmed into cooperation, Jeremy names [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]] as his employer, and the Bonebreakers follow him to the [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|market]] before tracing hawk clues into hobgoblin territory.

- [[sessions/session-037|Session 37]]

  **Hobgoblin preparations reveal an army gathering in Westmarsh**

  A charmed bugbear identifies a weapons depot, market rumours confirm mercenary recruitment, and Rose scouts a [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|fortress]] crowded with arms and officers.

- [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]]

  **Stolen invasion plans give Westfort warning of the coming siege**

  Rose steals plans naming Westfort as a target, but the party's raid exposes a much larger hidden army and forces a retreat to warn Commander Valerius.

- [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]]

  **The Bonebreakers disrupt the invasion and meet the Grey Syndicate's Ash**

  After grounding giant hawks and sabotaging siege engines, they find empty child cells in the fortress and meet Ash, who offers a [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Syndicate]] bargain to explore an ancient necropolis.

- [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]]

  **Westfort survives and the children are rescued, but Tobold is still missing**

  Rejecting Ash's offer, the party recovers the children and evidence of a plot to destabilise Agria, then returns to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] to find its well poisoned.

## Sessions 41–50

- [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]]

  **Voices beneath the Mercy lead into the Imperial Necropolis**

  With the well's poisoning traced to natural causes, the party follows Tobold's apparent voice into [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|Westmarsh's ancient Necropolis]] and recovers Valedictus's necromantic treatise and a [[items/glowing-skull|glowing skull]].

- [[sessions/session-042|Session 42]] and [[sessions/session-043|Session 43]]

  **The Necropolis defies ordinary space as plague worsens in Ikiria**

  Further delves into [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vii|Arch VII]] reveal warped chambers and dangerous guardians, while Steve's journey to Ikiria confirms that the outbreak there is real and worsening.

- [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]]

  **A roadside clash exposes Prince Arthur's plot against Agatha**

  After looting a chimera's hoard in the Necropolis, the party meets [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]] on the road and charms him into revealing a Syndicate plot against his sister Agatha.

- [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]]

  **The Bonebreakers secure evidence against Arthur and seek the Grey Khan**

  They take his secretary [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]]'s ledger and try to reach the Syndicate's leader, the Grey Khan, but a cancelled meeting sends them to the Spider Market with Arthur.

- [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]]

  **An assassin reports Tobold's death; Ash claims to be the Grey Khan**

  Tobold died under torture without betraying the party, according to an assassin wearing his face, while Ash offers 20,000 gold for Arthur's secretary and the Bonebreakers plan to capture her.

- [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]]

  **Arthur is arrested after the raid on Ash finds a decoy**

  The Bonebreakers seize the promised gold, secure Wendell's testimony, and bring their evidence to [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] in locked-down Ikiria, where he orders the prince's arrest.

- [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]]

  **Ikiria's plague investigation erupts into a battle with undead**

  Investigating missing bodies in the quarantined [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|Plats]], the Bonebreakers find [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|Frank's temple]] burned and meet [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]], who suspects poisoning, before undead rise from the corpse pit.

- [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]]

  **Maren identifies deliberate necromancy before a wraith kills her**

  As the corpse-pit battle continues, Maren says the undead were conjured, and her notebook links the crisis's earlier stages to poisoning followed by ordinary disease.

- [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]]

  **The undead are defeated and the Plats' poisoned well is purified**

  Ulrich neutralises the water's poison, but ancient Westmarsh coins at the bottom appear deliberately planted, leaving the culprits and any connection to the conjured undead unresolved.

## Sessions 51–60

- [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]]

  **Back in the Necropolis, the party finds an undead district still rebuilding**

  With their report delivered to Ikiria's authorities, the Bonebreakers explore the Necropolis's outer gate and [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|a residential district]] where zombie labourers still serve the Bloated One.

- [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]]

  **The Bloated One is slain, but his death leaves two companions diseased**

  A hidden maintenance passage lets the party ambush [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|Arch V's ruler]], but his necrotic death blast infects Steve and Micaelis with a disease that will turn them into zombies unless cured.

- [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]]

  **Returning home, the Bonebreakers drive Syndicate-linked orcs from the Mercy**

  Prisoners say the Syndicate ordered them to delay the party and also organised the earlier priest kidnappings, while Ulrich cures Steve and Micaelis of their disease.

- [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]]

  **Bran dies saving his companions on the road back to the Mercy**

  After [[characters/active-players/ben-l/retainers-followers/bran-the-wolf|Bran]] holds off insectoid attackers so Arden and Micaelis can escape, the survivors fortify the Mercy, hold his wake, and descend into the Necropolis's market district.

- [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]]

  **Flooded routes halt exploration of the Necropolis's Sunken Market**

  The party finds a map of the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]]'s underwater routes, defeats serpents and snake-men, and withdraws past worshippers of the Deep One to prepare for further exploration.

- [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]]

  **Gryphons interrupt the journey to Westfort for water-breathing potions**

  Travelling to equip themselves for the Sunken Market's submerged passages, the Bonebreakers lose their horses to nine gryphons, capture two of the attackers, and return to Barrow Spa.

- [[sessions/session-057|Session 57]]

  **Westfort faces a second siege and a demand to surrender the Bonebreakers**

  The party refuses the hobgoblins' demand and blocks their mountain raiding route, then heads for the [[locations/world/westmarsh/invasion-fort|Invasion Fort]], where Luthien is seized by a mimic.

- [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]]

  **Luthien dies at the fort; the party seeks another Necropolis entrance**

  A Krell kills her after she escapes the mimic, and the survivors' search for a western entrance leads to an [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|imperial temple]] with soul-binding sigils and a locked chest.

- [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]]

  **An undead officer reveals a Necropolis entrance and names the priest Valedictus**

  [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] confirms the temple's connection to the city, identifies [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] as Arch III's high priest, and links him to the vault's necromantic experiments without explaining his precise role.

- [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]]

  **An undead noble reveals Valedictus's three phylacteries**

  [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varrick|Lord Varrick]] warns that Agria's undead crisis will worsen while Valedictus remains in power and recommends finding [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]], while the party suspects the skull Steve traded away is a phylactery.

## Sessions 61–67

- [[sessions/session-061|Session 61]]

  **Back above ground, the Bonebreakers launch a relief attack on Westfort**

  Facing a larger hobgoblin army, they arrange a joint attack with the garrison, start a fire behind the enemy, and sabotage a catapult as the battle begins.

- [[sessions/session-062|Session 62]]

  **Kargaz is killed and Westfort's renewed siege is broken**

  Barny secretly reanimates the hobgoblin king [[npcs/westmarsh/kargaz-the-disciplined|Kargaz]] as the town rewards its defenders, and the Bonebreakers collect the water-breathing potions needed to resume exploring the Sunken Market.

- [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]]

  **Agatha's disappearance diverts the Bonebreakers north to Harrow**

  After Kargaz confirms Syndicate support for his war, the Bonebreakers learn Agatha and Smith have vanished and follow a letter apparently from her to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow|Harrow]].

- [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]]

  **Agatha is found alive; her investigation leads to Arthur's palace**

  Hiding with Smith in Harrow, she fears a wider threat despite Arthur's arrest and commissions an investigation that leads the party into [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|his palace in Kingsport]] under false pretences.

- [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]]

  **Arthur's letters lead the investigation to Countess Lirina's masquerade**

  Love letters and travel records link Arthur to [[npcs/velur/lirina|Velluria's countess]], whose masquerade in Velur offers a lead, while the party frames [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] for its own palace thefts.

- [[sessions/session-066|Session 66]]

  **The Bonebreakers infiltrate Lirina's masquerade, but she appears to recognise them**

  At [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|the Gilt Palace]], Nila steals a letter and hears rumours that Lirina wants Arthur's place, while [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Barny's telescope]] reveals a noble's nightmare of Queen Audrey's murder; Lirina's involvement remains unproven.

- [[sessions/session-067|Session 67]]

  **The Bonebreakers capture Lirina, who knows the Grey Khan's identity**

  Seized as she flees a blackout at her own masquerade and replaced by Rose in disguise, [[npcs/velur/lirina|the countess]] is revealed by ESP and her hidden armoury as an aspiring duchess and likely assassin whose coded message to the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]] warns that the party is coming.
