## Session Timeline

### The Traitor Prince

- [[sessions/session-047|Session 47 - Twenty Thousand in the Dark]]: the Bonebreakers raid a decoy cell at [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|the Spider Market]], steal 20,000 gold from Grey Syndicate operations, return through [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|West Fort]] and [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/index|Barrow Spa]], then bring the evidence and [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell]] to locked-down [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], where [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] orders [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] arrested.

### The Rot of Agria

- [[sessions/session-048|Session 48 - The Burning of Belenus]]: [[npcs/ikiria/vaessa-silvertread|Vaessa Silvertread]] sends the party into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] to investigate missing bodies, where they find [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|the Temple of Merciful Life]] burned down, evidence that the plague may involve both poison and disease, and ancient imperial undead rising from the corpse pits.
- [[sessions/session-049|Session 49 - Rot and Wraith]]: the corpse-pit battle continues, Quak! arrives to reinforce the party, [[npcs/ikiria/maren-sollow|Maren Sollow]] identifies the undead as unnatural conjurations, and a wraith kills her before the fight's full aftermath is shown.

## Major Threads by Timeline Position

- [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]] moves [[quests/major/active/prince-arthur-and-the-grey-khan|Prince Arthur and the Grey Khan]] from road intrigue and leverage into a formal arrest, closing the immediate Arthur handoff but not the larger Syndicate or succession problem.
- [[sessions/session-048|Session 48]] and [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]] turn [[quests/minor/active/plague-in-ikiria|Plague in Ikiria]] into a direct investigation of poisoned water rumours, overcrowding, missing bodies, anti-magic unrest, and open undead violence in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]].
- The same two sessions push [[quests/major/active/necropolis-beneath-the-mercy|Necropolis Beneath the Mercy]] upward into the city above, as imperial-marked undead appear in the corpse pits and Maren identifies the outbreak as unnatural conjuration.
- [[quests/major/active/princess-agathas-secret-patronage|Princess Agatha's Secret Patronage]] remains active in the background, but Agatha stays out of public view while her allies manage Arthur's arrest and Ikiria's wider crisis.

## Unresolved or Future Consequences

- [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] remains out of public view in Ikiria, though the current pages treat that secrecy as standard procedure rather than confirmed disappearance.
- Ash remains at large, and her claim to be [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|the Grey Khan]] is still unconfirmed.
- Maren's notebook preserves the poisoning and overcrowding theory, but the person or force conjuring undead in the corpse pits is still unknown.
- The exact link between the corpse-pit undead and the buried imperial past remains unresolved.
- Something neutral but active remains inside the automaton grave near the pits. Needs review.
