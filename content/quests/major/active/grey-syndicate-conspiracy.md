## Overview

The Grey Syndicate links Westfort sabotage, Westmarsh kidnappings, political manipulation, direct royal conspiracy, and attacks on the Mercy. It remains one of the clearest active enemy networks facing the Bonebreakers.

## How it began

The thread becomes visible when the party exposes Vex's sabotage cell in [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]], then traces the chain upward through Jeremy to Ash and the [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|Spider's Market]].

## Key developments

- Vex admits to poisoning, arson, and theft on behalf of a hidden employer.
- Jeremy leads the party to Ash and the Spider's Market network.
- Papers recovered in [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]] show the Syndicate destabilising Agria through fire, poison, and kidnapping.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] to [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], the Syndicate is tied directly to [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] and the struggle against Princess Agatha.
- In [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]], the Bonebreakers rob a decoy market cell of 20,000 gold, learn the Syndicate is dispersed rather than centralised, and hear of renewed invasion plans at a hobgoblin fort. Needs review.
- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], captured orcs in the Mercy say they came from Westmarsh, were ordered to delay the Bonebreakers, and that the first priest kidnapping from the Mercy was also Syndicate work.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], news confirms that the renewed invasion of Westfort has become a siege. Kargaz later confirms the Syndicate supported his campaign.
- In [[sessions/session-060|Session 60]], [[npcs/necropolis/lord-varek|Lord Varek]] suggests that the Syndicate may be seeking Valedictus's necromantic power or may instead intend to destroy him. This is informed speculation rather than proof of the Syndicate's objective.
- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], the reanimated [[npcs/westmarsh/kargaz-the-disciplined|Kargaz]] confirms that the Syndicate supported his war and promised him control of Westfort if he captured it. He says he knew little of its larger plans.
- In [[sessions/session-064|Session 64]], Agatha says she suspects a force beyond even the Syndicate and doubts Arthur could have organised the unrest alone. The party follows a lead through Count Venn and Brother Sedge into Arthur's Palace, without yet identifying a wider mastermind.
- In [[sessions/session-065|Session 65]], the palace search identifies [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of Velluria, as a lead through love letters, portraits, repeated journeys, and her warning against rash action and written commitments. Hugh Mallory points to her masquerade in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/vellure|Vellure]]. Corvin takes letters and lies about his previous visits. His suspicious palace activity and flight after returning to the Royal Chamberlain's building lead the party to suspect he is not who he claims to be. The Bonebreakers frame him for their own jewellery theft and secure his arrest.
- In [[sessions/session-067|Session 67 - Will the Real Countess Please Stand Up?]], the Bonebreakers capture Lirina at the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/vellure/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] and find encoded letters, a cipher book, and an assassin's armoury in her rooms. Her thoughts reveal that the letters belong to her spy network, that she knows who the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]] is without being the Khan, and that one message is meant to warn the Khan that the Bonebreakers are active in the Northlands and expected at her masquerade.
- In [[sessions/session-068|Session 68 - Ashes to Ashes]], at the Mercy, Selvar's ESP identifies Lirina as Ash. Her thoughts distinguish Lirina from the Grey Khan, but she affirms that Ash is the Khan; the Bonebreakers conclude that they have captured the Syndicate's leader. Lirina says her plans will continue without her and justifies the invasion as an attempt to replace Westfort's weak defenders with her own army. Her hatred of Valedictus is genuine, and a nightmare of plague ravaging Agria suggests fear of disaster. She offers an alliance, but the party cannot agree whether to accept.
- In [[sessions/session-069|Session 69 - A Cruel Bargain]], the Bonebreakers decide Lirina cannot be trusted, reject her alliance, and firmly side with Princess Agatha. They leave for Ikiria with Lirina, intending to leave her there and contact Agatha. Ninja orcs use two farmers and their captive daughter to demand her release. Akal sets the carriages alight before the party agrees on a response. Rose pursues the fleeing captor, but he kills the girl before she reaches him. Most attackers are killed and two escape. Their allegiance is **Needs review**.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales]], the Bonebreakers jail Lirina in Ikiria, inspect the prison, warn of a likely breakout, and prohibit contact with Arthur. Smith assures them that it is the safest available prison. Morel traces the poetry cipher to monastic copying and identifies [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] as a lead for another copy and the missing key. The party takes a concealed route north to avoid Syndicate orcs, but trees reveal recent orc activity; those orcs' allegiance is unconfirmed. The abbey has not been reached and no report to Agatha has been delivered.

## Current status

Active, with the leadership mystery resolved from the Bonebreakers' perspective. [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]], identified as Ash and the Grey Khan, is imprisoned in Ikiria. The party warns of a likely breakout and forbids her from speaking with Arthur. Smith assures them of the prison's security. Lirina says her plans can continue without her; the dispersed network has not been dismantled.

The Bonebreakers have rejected her alliance against Valedictus and firmly sided with Agatha, who has not yet received their findings. Morel identifies Thorngate Abbey as a lead for another copy of the poetry cipher and the missing key. The party is travelling there through the wooded highlands north of Ikiria. Trees describe recent orc movements, but neither those orcs' allegiance nor a connection to the earlier hostage-takers is established. The letters remain undeciphered.

## Consequences

- The party can no longer treat Westmarsh raids as isolated frontier problems.
- The conspiracy reaches from criminal handlers to royal succession.
- Arthur's arrest turns part of the conspiracy from hidden leverage into open political evidence.
- The priest kidnapping once attributed to Gruumsh's warband now appears to have been connected to the Syndicate, though the exact arrangement remains unclear.
- Corvin is in custody on fabricated theft evidence. Elswyth remains charmed when she supports the party; exposure of the frame-up could compromise its position with the Queen’s Guard.
- The party has identified the Syndicate leader and holds new documentary evidence, but has not yet reported to Agatha. Lirina's thoughts support her claim to answer to nobody; this does not establish the extent of every force involved in the unrest.
- One of Lirina's encoded messages is meant to warn the Grey Khan that the Bonebreakers are operating in the Northlands.
- Capturing the Khan removes her freedom without establishing that the dispersed network has stopped operating.
- Lirina's proposed alliance connects the Syndicate investigation to the campaign against Valedictus, but the Bonebreakers reject her help because they cannot trust her and firmly side with Agatha.
- Lirina knows the Bonebreakers have contact with Agatha, but not where the princess is hidden.
- The party has resisted arrest by the Vellurian Watch and removed a reigning countess from her palace; the political response remains unknown.
- Lirina's captivity has prompted a demand for her release backed by a civilian hostage. The girl is killed and two attackers escape, leaving a threat of uncertain allegiance at large.
- Lirina's placement in Ikiria secures a prison for the captured leader, but the anticipated breakout and risk of communication with Arthur require continuing precautions.
- The poetry book gives the investigation a new destination at Thorngate Abbey; the letters remain undeciphered until the missing key can be found.

## Loose ends

- Lirina is identified as Ash and the Grey Khan. How she concealed her identity as Ash: **Needs review**.
- The Syndicate's next move after losing Arthur's protection, Kargaz's army, and its leader's freedom remains unknown.
- Whether the Session 53 delay order targeted Westfort, the Necropolis, or another objective remains unresolved.
- Lirina says the invasion was meant to put her own army in Westfort; how that aim fits Kargaz's promised control and the network's other operations remains unclear.
- Lirina genuinely wants Valedictus destroyed, but the Bonebreakers have rejected cooperation. What her wider plans entail remains unresolved.
- Agatha has been found alive in hiding; Smith is now in Ikiria. The wider threat to Agatha, and any force beyond the Syndicate, remain unidentified.
- Lirina admits treating Arthur as a useful tool but blames him for his own mistakes. Her precise influence over his decisions and the means of her intended rise to Duchess remain unclear.
- Her encoded letters remain undeciphered. Morel traces the poetry cipher to monastic copying and identifies Thorngate Abbey, but another copy and the missing key remain unsecured.
- The burned message “Smith already knows,” the payment for discretion in Harrow, and Corvin's repeated visits need explanation.
- The hidden letter's author, recipient, and unclear reference to “con”: **Needs review**.
- Corvin's arrest does not establish his actual role; the fate of the letters he took is **Needs review**.
- Who sent the ninja orcs, how they knew to seek Lirina on the road, and whether they have any connection to the Syndicate: **Needs review**. Two escape; the fate of the hostage's killer is also **Needs review**.
- Whether anyone will attempt to break Lirina out of Ikiria's prison or circumvent the prohibition on contact with Arthur remains unresolved.
- Orcs have passed through the forest on the abbey route, killing a giant boar and attempting to hide the evidence. Their allegiance and any connection to the trapped trail are **Needs review**.
