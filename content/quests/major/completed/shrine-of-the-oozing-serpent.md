## Overview

Reginald's road-safety commission leads the Bonebreakers into the marsh shrine later known as the Shrine of the Oozing Serpent. The main threat is removed when Sootmurk the Grease Dragon is killed.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]], [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/reginald|Reginald]] hires the party to stop travellers disappearing along [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kings-road|the King's Road]].

## Key developments

- The party discovers that gloops have been feeding captives to [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/sootmurk|Sootmurk]].
- Ancient gnomish machinery, Mulvis imagery, and automata hint at a much older history in the shrine.
- In [[sessions/session-006|Session 6]], the party kills Sootmurk and breaks the immediate kidnapping cycle.
- In [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]], the Bonebreakers return for remaining treasure, caskets, and Bomfluke's relics.

## Current status

The shrine arc's main objective is complete.

## Resolution

Sootmurk is slain and the immediate danger to travellers on the road ends.

## Consequences

- The shrine remains important later when Gruumsh uses it as a stronghold.
- The party acquires several notable treasures, including the Sword of Mulvis and the Crown of King Bomfluke.
