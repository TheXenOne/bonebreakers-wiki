## Overview

The Barrow is the party's first major dungeon and the place where the Bonebreakers first make their name. What begins as an undead cleanup eventually ends with the site being cleansed and claimed as a research base.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-001|Session 1]], the party returns to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]] to investigate the undead threat around [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]].

## Key developments

- Early delves uncover [[npcs/barrowshire/the-barrow/quintius|Quintius]], trapped merchants, cursed chambers, hidden treasure, and heavy casualties.
- The party secures major loot in [[sessions/session-003|Session 3]], but does not fully finish the dungeon.
- In [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]], the Bonebreakers return, purge the remaining necromantic taint, and formally annex the Barrow for magical study.

## Current status

The Barrow is no longer an active undead crisis.

## Resolution

Arden's cleansing ends the Barrow's active necromantic corruption, and the spellcasters claim it as a controlled research site.

## Consequences

- The Barrow becomes one of the party's first long-term holdings.
- It establishes Barny's and the other magic users' interest in using cleared dungeons as bases.
