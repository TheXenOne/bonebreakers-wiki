## Overview

Princess Agatha's first secret mission sends the party to end the deathless plague by overthrowing the corrupted Church of the Crimson Flame. The mission succeeds and the cathedral becomes [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]].

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Agatha formally hires the party to deal with the plague centered on the cathedral.

## Key developments

- The first assault in [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]] is costly, killing Arden and Durandale before the survivors escape.
- Later returns uncover chained prisoners, priestly testimony, the bishop's diary, and the truth that the original bishop feared he had been replaced.
- In [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], the party kills the [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]], frees the surviving priests, and lifts the plague's local center.

## Current status

The main objective is complete.

## Resolution

The Church of the Crimson Flame falls, the deathless plague centered there is broken, and Princess Agatha grants the cathedral to the party, who rename it the Mercy.

## Consequences

- The Mercy becomes the party's main base near Ikiria.
- Hidden depths beneath the cathedral later open into the necropolis.
