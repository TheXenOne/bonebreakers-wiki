## Overview

An outbreak of sentient, life-draining weapons throws [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] into chaos and leads the party to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/tower-of-arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus' tower]]. The city-wide crisis ends, but Arkeus himself does not die or return safely.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], weapons across Ikiria begin speaking and draining their bearers. [[npcs/flux-academy/screever-longtooth|Screever Longtooth]] identifies [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]] as the most likely cause.

## Key developments

- The party receives legal backing to enter Arkeus' impossible tower.
- The tower reveals clones, magical distortions, and the translucent tuning fork Longtooth wanted.
- The Harmonic Regulator proves central to stabilising the crisis.
- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], the party frees the real Arkeus, but both Arkeus and his clone vanish into an astral disturbance.

## Current status

The sentient-weapon crisis is over.

## Resolution

The Harmonic Regulator is recovered, the tower crisis ends, and Ikiria's immediate weapon disaster is resolved.

## Consequences

- Ikiria avoids immediate collapse.
- The tower leaves behind dangerous artefacts, unexplained damage, and an astral loose end.
