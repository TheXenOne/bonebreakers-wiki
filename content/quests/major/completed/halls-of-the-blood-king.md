## Overview

The sudden appearance of the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king|Halls of the Blood King]] outside Ikiria draws the party into a vampire court full of captives, missing noble children, and hidden magical rules. The party destroys the Blood King's heart, but the arc leaves several unclear consequences behind.

## How it began

At the end of [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], Arun brings a rotting rose and news of a castle full of monsters and prisoners outside Ikiria.

## Key developments

- The party infiltrates the manor, meets [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/selana|Selana]], and learns that the Blood King's heart is hidden separately from his body.
- They navigate rivalries involving [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/princess-of-blood|the Princess of Blood]] and [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/lord-demetrius|Lord Demetrius]].
- Prisoners are rescued from the lower levels.
- In [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]], the party finds the hidden heart in the observatory model and destroys it as the manor collapses.

## Current status

The immediate manor threat is over.

## Resolution

The party destroys the Blood King's hidden heart, escapes with surviving prisoners, and ends the main crisis at the manor.

## Consequences

- Noble prisoners and other captives escape alive.
- [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/selana|Selana]] dies helping the party.
- The manor's magical legacy and some internal politics remain unclear.
