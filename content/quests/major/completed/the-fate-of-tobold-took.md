## Overview

Tobold's abduction begins as a personal rescue objective within the wider Westmarsh campaign, but the thread ends in tragedy rather than recovery.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-033|Session 33]], Tobold is carried away by a giant hawk shortly after the party escapes the Blood Moon manor.

## Key developments

- The search for Tobold becomes tied to the broader child-abduction and invasion plot.
- Even after the children are rescued in [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], Tobold is still missing.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], the party hears Tobold's voice from below the Mercy, but the meaning is unclear.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], an assassin posing as Tobold confirms that the real Tobold died under torture without betraying the party.

## Current status

This thread is resolved.

## Resolution

The party finally learns that Tobold is dead.

## Consequences

- The revelation sharpens the Grey Syndicate conflict.
- The voice heard below the Mercy remains unexplained, even though Tobold's fate is now known.
