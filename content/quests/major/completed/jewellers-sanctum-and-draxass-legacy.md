## Overview

Jessum Tarantella asks the party to clear the dangerous workshop beneath his manor. The result is a full dungeon clear, recovery of Draxas Tarantella's relics, and the discovery that points the campaign toward Fos Imeras.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], Reginald directs the Bonebreakers to [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum Tarantella]], who wants help reclaiming [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|the Jewellers' Sanctum]].

## Key developments

- The party survives layered trap design, giant vermin, and the legacy of Draxas's apprentice [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]].
- They defeat the Rat Queen, explore the workshop, and secure encoded texts and magical items.
- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], the dungeon is cleared and Jessum is finally able to reclaim his home properly.
- The most important long-term find is the hidden map in Draxas's writings leading toward [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]].

## Current status

The sanctum itself is cleared and the main objective is complete.

## Resolution

Jessum regains control of the manor and the Bonebreakers leave with the treasure, books, and map that launch the next expedition.

## Consequences

- The Tarantella estate is stabilised.
- Draxas's legacy directly leads into the Fos Imeras arc.
