## Overview

When the party returns from Westmarsh, the crowd outside the Mercy believes the cathedral well has been poisoned. The mystery is brief but important because it immediately threatens the party's authority at the site.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], the Bonebreakers return to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] to find unrest, sickness, and accusations around the well.

## Key developments

- Akal disperses the crowd after Arden's apology fails.
- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], the party tests the water directly.

## Current status

Resolved.

## Resolution

The poison turns out to be natural rather than deliberate sabotage.

## Consequences

- The party avoids wasting more time on a false enemy.
- Attention shifts immediately to the hidden passages beneath the cathedral.
