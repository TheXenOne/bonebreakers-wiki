## Overview

The Bonebreakers capture two wild gryphons after an attack on the road from [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa|Barrow Spa]] toward [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. The creatures are not tame enough to handle or ride, but the party intends to breed them and hopes eventually to establish gryphons as flying mounts.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]], nine gryphons kill the party's horses during a roadside ambush. The Bonebreakers kill six of the attackers, one escapes, and [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] and [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] each subdue one alive.

## Key developments

- Two gryphons are captured alive.
- The captives remain too wild to ride or handle safely.
- The party returns to Barrow Spa rather than continuing to Westfort.
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/gus|Gus]] is commissioned to make secure gryphon harnesses.
- The party forms a long-term plan to breed the gryphons as possible flying mounts.
- One escaped gryphon carries the [[items/party-inventory/red-seers-dagger|Red Seer's Dagger]], potentially allowing Barny to observe it.
- In [[sessions/session-058|Session 58]], Barny sees that the escaped gryphon is in a high mountain cave with other gryphons and several eggs.

## Current status

Active. The Bonebreakers hold two untamed gryphons and are waiting for purpose-built harnesses. Barny has located the escaped gryphon in a mountain nesting cave containing other gryphons and eggs, but the cave's location is unknown.

## Consequences

- Nearly all the party's horses are dead, and the final surviving horse is killed during the fight.
- The party needs safe containment, handling, and training before either gryphon can be used as a mount.
- The escaped gryphon and the embedded Red Seer's Dagger remain at large.
