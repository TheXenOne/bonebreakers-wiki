## Overview

Late in the published material, rumours of plague or sickness in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]] begin to spread again. The problem is real, but the exact nature of it is still unclear in the available sources.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], the party hears continued rumours of plague in Ikiria while investigating the Mercy and the necropolis.

## Key developments

- [[sessions/session-043|Session 43]] records Steve riding to Ikiria to confirm the reports.
- He returns convinced that the sickness is real and worsening.

## Current status
