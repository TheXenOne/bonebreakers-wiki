## Overview

Deep inside the [[locations/world/fos-imeras/golden-asclepieion|Golden Asclepion]], the party encounters faceless beings called the purified and a larger fused mass made from them. Their origin and exact nature are still unclear in the available player-facing sources.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-016|Session 16]], the party pushes beyond [[npcs/fos-imeras/sebastaeia|Sebastaeia]]'s grand hall and discovers rooms full of purified creatures and one horrific central aggregation.

## Key developments

- The purified appear connected to the temple's warped idea of healing.
- Entering their chamber causes severe ringing and bleeding before the creatures attack.
- More purified wait below a marked trapdoor, suggesting the problem was larger than one room.

## Current status

Needs review. The Fos Imeras expedition ends, but the available sources never fully explain what the purified were or how widespread they became.
