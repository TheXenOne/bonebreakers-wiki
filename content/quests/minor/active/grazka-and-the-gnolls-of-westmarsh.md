## Overview

The gnolls of Westmarsh are not the main enemy of the frontier arc, but they recur often enough to remain a live problem. Interrogations point to a matriarch named Grazka somewhere deeper in the marsh.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-034|Session 34]], the party's first major push out from [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]] is ruined when gnolls ambush them first.

## Key developments

- The surviving gnolls mention a matriarch named Grazka.
- Rumours in and around Westfort repeatedly describe gnolls as growing bolder.
- A later gnoll attack near [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|the Spider Market]] reinforces that they remain active even while hobgoblins and the Grey Syndicate take center stage.

## Current status

Active. The gnolls remain a recurring frontier threat, but the campaign has not yet pursued Grazka directly.
