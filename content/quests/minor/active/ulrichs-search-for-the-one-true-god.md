## Overview

[[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] openly states that he serves no single named god and wants to discover what he calls the one true god. That search becomes more concrete when he later receives a direct message from the Barrow's holy relics.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Ulrich explains that he has wandered from temple to temple, still receiving divine power, and that he wants to find the one true god.

## Key developments

- Ulrich also says he wants to found a group of healing clerics and gather relics from many faiths.
- In [[sessions/session-023|Session 23]], Ulrich claims the holy symbol of Sir Ignacious and hears a telepathic call from a righteous servant of the one true god.
- Sir Gallant immediately undercuts that claim by mocking Ignacious' strict moral code, leaving the meaning unresolved rather than confirmed.

## Current status

Active. Ulrich has found hints, but no final answer.
