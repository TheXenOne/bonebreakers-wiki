## Overview

After claiming a sentient sword tied to the lost Cassets, [[characters/inactive-players/marco/characters/ehecat|Ehecat]]'s hatred of the [[factions/governments/ramen-empire|Ramen Empire]] hardens into a lasting personal vow.

## How it began

In [[sessions/session-017|Session 17]], Ehecat learns that the sword recovered from the Nixies' treasure was made by a druid of his own people and urges vengeance against the Ramen Empire.

## Key developments

- The voyage around [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]] places the party directly inside renewed Agria-Ramen tensions.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Ehecat explains that the Casset tribe was wiped out by the Ramen Empire and that he wants to destroy the bloodline responsible.
- He also wants, eventually, to return to Cassets.

## Current status

Dormant. The vow remains central to Ehecat, but no sustained action has been taken against the Ramen Empire yet.
