## Overview

The Sword of Mulvis is a named blade recovered during the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]] arc, and it is now owned and wielded by [[characters/inactive-players/dom/characters/virien|Virien]]. Its importance comes from both its origin and its role as one of the party's earliest clearly identified magical weapons.

## Known properties

- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/aldrich|Aldrich]] identifies it as a sword carved from the spine of the demon Mulvis.
- The sword grants a `+1` bonus against ordinary enemies.
- Against demons or devils, the bonus improves to `+2`.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-005|Session 5]], the party recovers an ornate sword in the shrine, though its true nature is not yet known.
- In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], Aldrich identifies that weapon as the Sword of Mulvis.
- The sword remains one of the shrine arc's signature treasures.

## Current status

Virien is the current known bearer.
