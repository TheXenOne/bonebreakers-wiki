## Overview

The Rat Queen's Ring is the magical ring worn by [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/rat-queen|the Rat Queen]] in the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|Jeweller's Sanctum]]. It is now treated as a party-held item, though its exact current bearer is unclear, and it appears to enhance a rat's mind while working poorly or harmfully on human wearers.

## Known properties

- The ring is magical and clearly tied to the Rat Queen's unusual status.
- When [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] puts it on, it acts as a curse rather than a reward.
- It appears to raise a rat's intelligence while lowering or distorting a human's intelligence.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], the party kills the Rat Queen and takes the ring.
- Barny immediately tests it and is cursed with the intelligence of a very smart rat.

## Current status

The Bonebreakers retain the ring, but the exact current bearer is unclear. Needs review.
