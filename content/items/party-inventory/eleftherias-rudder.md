## Overview

Eleftheria's Rudder is a small magical version of the [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Eleftheria]]'s rudder. The Bonebreakers currently hold it as a shared party item connected to summoning, assembling, or otherwise calling forth the Eleftheria, Orsilochus's magical ship.

## What it does

- The rudder is tied directly to the Eleftheria rather than being only a symbolic token.
- The Eleftheria can be summoned or called forth through a ritual offering linked to the rudder.
- The ship is also known to be modular and unusually easy to conceal or reassemble once under the party's control.

## Summoning requirements

To summon the Eleftheria, the party knows the ritual requires:

- a villain's rib
- a pound of gold
- something purple
- something old

These were the same riddle components used to call the ship from the lake on [[locations/world/fos-imeras|Fos Imeras]].
