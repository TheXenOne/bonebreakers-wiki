## Overview

The Black Stone Ritual Knife is a valuable ritual object recovered from the Necropolis delve beyond the seventh arch, and it is currently owned by [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve "Ligma" Smith]].

## Known details

- The knife is made of black stone.
- It is described as a ritual knife rather than an ordinary weapon.
- It is worth about 600 gold pieces.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] recovers the ritual knife along with pre-imperial coinage.

## Current status

Steve is the last clearly known holder.
