## Overview

This sentient magic sword is recovered from the Nixies' treasure near [[locations/world/fos-imeras/index|Fos Imeras]] and claimed by [[characters/inactive-players/marco/characters/ehecat|Ehecat]], who is its known owner. It is one of the clearest item links between treasure recovery and a character's long-term personal quest.

## Known properties

- The sword is sentient.
- It was crafted by a druid of the Cassets.
- It presses its bearer toward vengeance against the [[factions/governments/ramen-empire|Ramen Empire]].

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-017|Session 17]], the party claims the Nixies' treasure and Ehecat discovers that the recovered sword is tied to his lost people.
- The sword deepens Ehecat's commitment to destroying the Ramen Empire and directly supports [[quests/minor/active/ehecats-vow-against-the-ramen-empire|Ehecat's Vow Against the Ramen Empire]].
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], Ehecat's goals are discussed more openly, keeping the sword's significance in view even when its exact powers stay private.

## Current status

The sword was in Ehecat's possession, but its current location after his retirement from the active roster is not confirmed. Needs review.
