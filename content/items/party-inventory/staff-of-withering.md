## Overview

The Staff of Withering is one of the rewards granted after the resolution of the Arkeus crisis, and it is currently owned and wielded by [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]]. It becomes one of his most dangerous offensive tools against living targets.

## Known properties

- The staff rapidly ages or withers living creatures it strikes.
- It is much less effective against undead, a limitation Barny discovers quickly.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-028|Session 28]], Barny chooses the Staff of Withering as his reward from Professor Longtooth.
- In [[sessions/session-029|Session 29]], he uses it against the undead hounds outside the Blood Moon castle and learns it is poorly suited to undead enemies.
- In [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]], Barny uses the staff to age a human guard to death inside the Halls of the Blood King.

## Current status

Barny is the known bearer in later sessions.
