## Overview

The Nightmare-Revealing Telescope is one of the strangest treasures taken from the collapsing Halls of the Blood King, and it is currently owned by [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]]. Rather than revealing distant places, it reveals a target's recent nightmares.

## Known properties

- The telescope must be aimed at a person rather than used for ordinary stargazing.
- It reveals the recent nightmares of whoever it is trained upon.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-032|Session 32]], the party finds the telescope on the balcony outside the observatory.
- Barny experiments with it, learns what it does, and later carries it out during the manor's collapse.

## Current status

Barny is the last clearly known bearer.
