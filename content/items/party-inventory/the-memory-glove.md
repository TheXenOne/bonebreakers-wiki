## Overview

The Memory Glove is one of the stranger exhibit-room curios found in [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]]'s tower, and it is currently associated with [[characters/inactive-players/matt/characters/athlestan-ghoulsbane|Athlestan]]. It grants a memory or vision that does not seem to belong to its wearer.

## Known properties

- The glove twitches before being worn.
- When [[characters/inactive-players/matt/characters/athlestan-ghoulsbane|Athlestan]] puts it on, he receives a memory that may not be his own.
- That memory points toward the King of Ghouls being somewhere in Westmarsh.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], Athlestan takes the glove from its display case and receives the vision tied to the King of Ghouls.

## Current status

Athlestan is the last clearly known bearer.
