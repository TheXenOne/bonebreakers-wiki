## Overview

The Crown of King Bomfluke is one of the more memorable relics recovered from the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent|Shrine of the Oozing Serpent]]. It is currently owned by [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]], and its two confirmed traits are unusual even by campaign standards: the crown cannot be easily damaged, and it protects its wearer from poison.

## Known properties

- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/aldrich|Aldrich]] demonstrates that the crown is effectively indestructible in ordinary handling.
- Aldrich also demonstrates that the crown renders poison ineffective against the wearer.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]], the crown is recovered after Gorfthistle opens the royal casket in the shrine, and Ulrich takes it afterward.
- In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], Aldrich identifies and demonstrates the crown's properties.
- In [[sessions/session-038|Session 38]], Ulrich sends the crown to [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden Sable]] at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] to help protect against the poisonings there.
- After the poisoning crisis is resolved, Ulrich recovers the crown from Arden and keeps it again.

## Current status

Ulrich currently holds the crown.
