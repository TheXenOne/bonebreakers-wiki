## Overview

The Wand of Daystars is a powerful wand later used against undead in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], and it is currently owned by [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]]. It appears to have come out of the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king|Halls of the Blood King]] expedition.

## Known properties

- The wand is especially effective against undead.
- In battle it can inflict heavy radiant-like damage, destroy weaker undead outright, and blind other targets.
- The exact number of charges and full limits of the wand are not yet documented. Needs review.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]], [[npcs/ikiria/halls-of-the-blood-king/princess-of-blood|the Princess of Blood]] rewards Barny with a wand, a basement key, and a map of the upper floor.
- In [[sessions/session-049|Session 49]], that wand is identified as the Wand of Daystars and used to obliterate one wraith outright while badly damaging and blinding other undead at the corpse pit.
- The wand from Session 31 is very likely the same Wand of Daystars used in Session 49. Exact identification timing: Needs review.

## Current status

Barny is the known bearer in later sessions.
