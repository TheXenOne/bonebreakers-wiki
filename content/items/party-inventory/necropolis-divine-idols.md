## Overview

The Necropolis Divine Idols are small religious statues taken from shrines and hidden places in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]]. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] identifies most of the neighbourhood shrine idols as non-evil and keeps them for research rather than sale, while three altered idols on that shrine radiate evil and appear to represent former gods reduced to lesser status. In the later residential streets, Ulrich also takes a hidden idol of an unofficial god.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], the party finds the shrine at a crossroads in Arch V while a false [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] prays there.
- Ulrich uses his holy symbol to distinguish the non-evil idols from three evil altered ones.
- Ulrich takes the non-evil idols, worth about 50 gold pieces in total, for research.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], the party finds another hidden idol of an unofficial god in an apartment area, and Ulrich takes it.

## Current status

Held by Ulrich for religious research.
