## Overview

The Encoded Gnomish Tome is a clue-bearing book given to the Bonebreakers by Jessum Tarantella after the midpoint of the Jeweller's Sanctum expedition. It is currently held as a shared party clue item, and it matters less as treasure than as the hidden route by which the campaign opens toward [[locations/world/fos-imeras/golden-asclepieion|Fos Imeras]].

## Known contents

- Jessum describes the tome as covering local legends, strange phenomena, and noteworthy places.
- The tome later yields a hidden map pointing toward the Golden Asclepieion.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], Jessum gives the tome to the party.
- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], research into the book reveals the hidden map that points toward the Golden Asclepieion and the marked lake nearby.

## Current status

The tome functions as a shared party clue object. Its later physical location is not tracked in detail. Needs review.
