## Overview

The Encoded Gnomish Tome is a book of local legends, strange phenomena, and noteworthy places given to the Bonebreakers by [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum Tarantella]]. A map hidden inside it leads the party towards [[locations/world/fos-imeras/golden-asclepeion|the Golden Asclepeion]] and the marked lake on Fos Imeras. The Bonebreakers entrust the book to [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]] for study at Flux Academy.

## Known contents

- Jessum describes the tome as covering local legends, strange phenomena, and noteworthy places.
- The tome later yields a hidden map pointing toward the Golden Asclepeion.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], Jessum gives the tome to the party.
- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], research into the book reveals the hidden map that points toward the Golden Asclepeion and the marked lake nearby.
- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], the party gives Morel the tome alongside an encoded temple transcription.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70]], Morel reports decoding a previously delivered book that describes an ancient gnomish island headquarters with a laboratory. Whether that account comes from this tome or another text is **Needs review**.

## Current status

Entrusted to Morel at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]] for study. Whether it is the book he has decoded, and whether the Bonebreakers have taken it back, are **Needs review**.
