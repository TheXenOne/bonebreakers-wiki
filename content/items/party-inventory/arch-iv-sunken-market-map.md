## Overview

The Arch IV Sunken Market Map is a hidden map recovered from a false base inside a counting-house chest in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv|Arch IV]]. It names the explored market district as the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]] and gives the Bonebreakers their first practical picture of the flooded routes and hazards beyond the zombie market street.

## Known contents

- Several unvisited areas are marked as flooded.
- Some areas are marked as entirely underwater.
- The fishermen's quarters lie farther ahead. Needs review.
- One channel is marked with a warning not to approach it after dark.
- The map notes that the thing in the sump is not a fish.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], [[characters/inactive-players/valerio/characters/paul|Paul]] inspects an already-open chest in the Sunken Market counting house, finds a hidden button in the base, and presses it.
- The button opens a false base containing the map.
