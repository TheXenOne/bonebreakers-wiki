## Overview

The Face-Changing Golden Coin is one of the divine curios found in Arkeus' tower, and it is currently owned by [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]]. Each turn reveals a different godly face.

## Known properties

- The face on the coin changes whenever it is turned or spun.
- At least one face is distinct enough that Ulrich thinks it is special.
- When prayed to, the coin gives the message: "Seek another path."

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] retrieves the coin from its display case and experiments with it.

## Current status

Ulrich is the last clearly known bearer.
