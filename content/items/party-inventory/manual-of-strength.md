## Overview

The Manual of Strength is one of the standout treasures recovered from the deep hoard beyond the seventh arch in the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|Necropolis]]. It is now treated as a party-held item, though its exact current bearer is unclear, and it appears to be a significant magical manual even though its exact effects are not yet clear.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], the party finds what appears to be a manual of strength in the hoard alongside a magical ring, a red crystal vial, a lion-faced shield, and Rose's imperial spear.

## Current status

The Bonebreakers retain the manual, but its exact bearer and confirmed effect are still unclear. Needs review.
