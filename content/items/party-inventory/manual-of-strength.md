## Overview

The Manual of Strength is one of the standout treasures recovered from the deep hoard beyond the seventh arch in the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|Necropolis]]. After extended study, it grants additional strength to [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] and [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] in [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]].

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], the party finds what appears to be a manual of strength in the hoard alongside a magical ring, a red crystal vial, a lion-faced shield, and Rose's imperial spear.
- In [[sessions/session-054|Session 54]], Selvar, Frank, and Steve finish reading the manual. Selvar and Steve gain additional strength.

Used by Selvar, Frank, and Steve by Session 54. Its exact remaining usefulness after that reading: Needs review.
