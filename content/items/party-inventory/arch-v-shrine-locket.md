## Overview

The Arch V Shrine Locket is a silver locket found in a hidden chest inside the shrine plinth in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]]. It bears fine artwork of a family, making it one of the more personal objects recovered from the Necropolis's residential district.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-053|Session 53]], [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]]'s Locate Object spell points to a hidden chest in the shrine.
- [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] finds and opens a concealed door in the plinth.
- The locket is found alongside a vial of clear liquid later identified by [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]] as holy water.

## Current status

Held by [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]].
