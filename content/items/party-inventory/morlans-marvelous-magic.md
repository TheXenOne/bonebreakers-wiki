## Overview

Morlan's Marvelous Magic is a spellbook recovered from a rat nest in the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|Jeweller's Sanctum]]. It is now treated as a Bonebreaker-held spellbook, though its exact current bearer is unclear, and it matters both as loot and as the first clear sign that [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]] was still part of the sanctum's story.

## Known contents

- The spellbook contains the first-level spells *Sleep* and *Light*.
- Barny also notes magical research in the book during the sanctum expedition.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]], the party recovers the spellbook from the rat nest.
- The book is the first major clue that Morlan still matters inside the sanctum, well before the party finds him trapped in a mirror.
- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], Morlan himself is finally encountered deeper in the dungeon.
