## Overview

The Laughing Blade is a named short sword found in Arkeus' tower, and it is currently owned by [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]]. It immediately shows signs of personality or enchantment when taken from its display.

## Known properties

- The blade twitches in its case before being taken.
- Once claimed, it fills its bearer with manic laughter and bad jokes.
- The behaviour strongly suggests a sentient or semi-sentient enchantment.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] takes the blade from its display case.
- She begins hearing it in her head at once.

## Current status

Luthien is the last clearly known bearer.
