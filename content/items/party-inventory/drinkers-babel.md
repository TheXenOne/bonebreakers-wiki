## Overview

Drinker's Babel is a named magical tankard found in a display case inside Arkeus' tower, and it is currently owned by [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]].

## Known properties

- The tankard is labeled "Drinker's Babel."
- When [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] drinks from it, the liquid vanishes mid-swallow.
- Its broader magical purpose is not yet clear. Needs review.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], Selvar smashes its display case and takes the tankard.

## Current status

Selvar is the last clearly known bearer.
