## Overview

The Astral Tuning Fork is the planar research tool Professor Longtooth specifically wants recovered from Arkeus' tower. It is held by the Bonebreakers as a party asset, though [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] is the party member most directly tied to its use, and it becomes the key that lets them move through barriers inside the tower and confirm Arkeus' involvement in the crisis.

## Known properties

- The fork is a translucent crystal tuning fork etched with runes.
- Striking it can make stone ripple and can dissolve magical barriers such as the Threshold Gate and the forcefields deeper in the tower.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-026|Session 26]], Longtooth identifies a translucent tuning fork as an important missing component tied to the crisis.
- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], Selvar finds the fork in the entrance room.
- The fork is then used repeatedly to cross the Threshold Gate and later forcefields within the tower.
