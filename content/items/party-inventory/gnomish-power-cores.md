## Overview

The Gnomish Power Cores were two power sources recovered from automaton remains in the old shrine of Mulvis. The Bonebreakers carried them for many sessions before rigging both to explode in [[sessions/session-057|Session 57]], causing a landslide that blocked a mountain route used to raid [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]].

## Known properties

- The cores powered gnomish automatons.
- Both could be rigged into a powerful explosive charge.
- Their other capabilities are unclear.

## History

- In [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], Frank recovers two cores alongside automaton arms and eyes.
- In [[sessions/session-057|Session 57]], the party detonates both cores in the mountains south of Westfort. The blast triggers a landslide and blocks the goblin raiding route.

## Current status

Destroyed in the Session 57 explosion.
