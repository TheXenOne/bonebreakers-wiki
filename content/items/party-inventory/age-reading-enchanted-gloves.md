## Overview

These enchanted gloves are found during the deeper exploration of the Halls of the Blood King, and they are currently owned by [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]]. Their gift is unusual but precise: they let the wearer know the age of whatever is touched.

## Known properties

- Once worn, the gloves grant knowledge of an object's age on contact.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-031|Session 31]], the gloves are found alongside a large dark key, scrolls, and an unknown potion.
- Barny puts them on and realizes what they do.

## Current status

Barny is the last clearly known bearer.
