## Overview

The Arch V Necropolis Map is a hidden map recovered from a shrine mechanism inside [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]]. It gives the Bonebreakers their clearest route deeper into the roofed underground district by marking the areas already explored and pointing toward the children's quarters, the foreman's station, and [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden Sable]] disturbs an evil idol on a neighbourhood shrine, revealing a pressure-plate mechanism.
- [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] knocks more idols away with arrows until a clay container rises from the shrine.
- Arden breaks open the container and finds the map inside.
