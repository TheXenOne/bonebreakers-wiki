## Overview

The Ring of Water Walking is a magical ring tied to the Bonebreakers' attempt to manage [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] after his capture, and it is now owned by [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]], who took it back.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]], [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]] buys a mystery ring in Barrow Spa.
- She gives the ring to Prince Arthur, at which point it is identified as a Ring of Water Walking.
- Luthien later takes the ring back and keeps it.

## Current status

Luthien now owns the ring.
