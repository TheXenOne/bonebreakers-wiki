## Overview

The Screaming Inkwell is a trembling glass inkwell found among the magical display cases in Arkeus' tower. It is currently in Bonebreaker hands, though the exact bearer is unclear, and it serves less as a tool than as an alarming guide object.

## Known properties

- When opened, it emits a piercing scream that stuns part of the party.
- It can then write messages of its own accord.
- Its best-known message directs the party back to search the entrance room more carefully.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-027|Session 27]], Arden takes the inkwell from its case and hands it to Barny, who opens it.
- The inkwell's message helps the party locate the tuning fork they had previously missed.

## Current status

The Bonebreakers still retain the inkwell, but the current bearer is not tracked clearly. Needs review.
