## Overview

The Five Cursed Rings are a matched set of harmful magic rings recovered from an anti-magic casket in the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|Jeweller's Sanctum]]. They are now treated as Bonebreaker-held cursed loot, though the exact current bearer or surviving full set is unclear, and they are important less for their power than for how thoroughly they punish anyone careless enough to wear them.

## Known curses

- Gold ring: reduces the wearer's armor class by 2.
- Silver ring: inflicts a 2-point penalty to saving throws and brings bad luck.
- Bronze ring: causes nightmares and slows recovery.
- Lead ring: prevents the use of magic items.
- Brass ring: inflicts a 2-point penalty to attacks made in daylight.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]], the party finds the rings in the anti-magic casket.
- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], Aldrich identifies the curse on each ring.
- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], Arden gives the lead ring to [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]] as part of a trick, and later offers cursed rings to [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/rat-queen|the Rat Queen]] during the failed negotiation in the parlor.
