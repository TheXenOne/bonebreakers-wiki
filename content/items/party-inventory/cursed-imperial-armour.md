## Overview

The cursed imperial armour is ancient armour recovered from undead in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] after the corpse-pit battle. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] identifies the armour as evil-aligned, and [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] later discovers that the suit he wears worsens his protection rather than improving it. Selvar remains affected by the curse.

## Known properties

- Evil-aligned aura.
- Selvar's worn suit gives him worse armour class.
- Whether every recovered suit has the same curse is unclear. Needs review.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-050|Session 50]], the Bonebreakers recover three suits of ancient imperial armour from the undead at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/corpse-pit|the Corpse Pit]].
- The suits are claimed by [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]], [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]], and [[characters/active-players/jennifer/characters/quak-ii|Quak II!]].
- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], Selvar realises during an ochre jelly fight in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] that his armour is cursed.

## Current status

Selvar is still affected by his cursed suit. The status of the other two suits needs review.
