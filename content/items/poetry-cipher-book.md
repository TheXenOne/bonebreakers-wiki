## Overview

The Poetry Cipher Book is a volume of poems about love and relationships by a rare half-orc poet from [[locations/world/agria|Agria]]. It provides the cipher for [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Lirina]]'s spy correspondence and links her private letters to the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]]'s communications. Missing pages prevent the Bonebreakers from reading the entire correspondence, but an intact page 19 in a second copy supplies the key for her matching letter, “On Love and Power.”

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-067|Session 67]], Ulrich finds a copy in Lirina's study at the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/vellure/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]]. Roughly its first fifth is missing. He recognises its use as a cipher and later bluffs Lirina with partially burned fragments that do not actually provide the key. Her thoughts reveal that one message warns the Grey Khan about the Bonebreakers.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70]], [[npcs/flux-academy/illitharien-morel|Illitharien Morel]] confirms publication in Agria and monastic copying. He identifies [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] as a lead for another copy and the missing pages.
- In [[sessions/session-071|Session 71]], the communications room in the [[locations/world/agria/grey-syndicate-caverns|Grey Syndicate Caverns]] yields another copy. Its earlier pages are removed, but page 19 survives intact and matches Lirina's letter numbered 19.

## Current status

Page 19 provides the key to Lirina's corresponding letter, which has not yet been decoded. Earlier cipher pages remain missing.

## Open questions

- The poet's name and the volume's title are unknown.
- Where complete copies or the other missing pages can be found.
- What the nineteenth letter will reveal once decoded.
