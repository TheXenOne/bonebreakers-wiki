## Overview

The Reserved Carved Mask is an intricately carved mask offered by a zombie merchant in the [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iv/sunken-market|Sunken Market]], but not freely for sale. The merchant says it is reserved for someone and later accepts [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/luthien|Luthien]]'s claim to be descended from that person.

The mask matters because it appears to have a specific intended recipient or lineage attached to it, and because it is held on a lower floor rather than in the market above.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-055|Session 55]], Luthien finds the mask at a zombie merchant's stall.
- The merchant refuses to sell it, saying it is reserved for someone.
- Luthien claims to be descended from that person, and the merchant accepts the claim.
- The merchant says the mask is held on the lower floor.

## Current status

Not held by the party. It remains somewhere on the lower floor of the Sunken Market. Needs review.
