## Overview

The Bloated One Work Ledger is a work order and inspection record found at a note-covered foreman's station inside [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]]. It records restoration work connected to [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]] and gives the Bonebreakers a clearer view of the district's strange undead bureaucracy.

## Known contents

- The ledger tracks numbered sections that have been worked on.
- The records appear to run back more than 2,000 years.
- The Bloated One seems to inspect the works once every year.
- One recent entry names Section 7, Junction 12 as having warm air and an anomaly.
- The same entry says not to report the anomaly to [[npcs/necropolis/the-tribune|the Tribune]].
- The anomaly is described as behind the school.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] reads the ledger after the party breaks the looping route inside Arch V.
- The party later finds a maintenance corridor behind the school and concludes that the warm-air anomaly is probably the Bloated One itself.
