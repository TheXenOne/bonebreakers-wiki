## Overview

Gobbo is the sole goblin survivor of the Bonebreakers' ambush on food raiders outside [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. After revealing the raiders' route through the southern mountains, he was hired for ten gold pieces per day and led the party toward [[locations/world/westmarsh/hobgoblin-mine|the Hobgoblin Mine]].

## Role in the campaign

- Gobbo provides first-hand knowledge of the mountain paths used to raid Westfort's food shipments.
- He currently serves as a paid guide rather than a prisoner.
