## Overview

Kargaz the Disciplined was the broken-crowned hobgoblin king who commanded the renewed siege of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/wistor|Wistor]] killed him with Lightning Bolt during the battle that broke the siege, but [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] secretly raised him as a zombie. Kargaz is now under Barny's command and was ordered to travel to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]], though whether he arrived remains unknown.

## Appearance

Kargaz wore a broken crown while commanding the siege army.

## Role in the campaign

- He commands approximately ninety hobgoblins and three ogres during the final relief attack on Westfort.
- Frank badly wounds him with a long-range shot, and Barny's attempt to charm him fails.
- Wistor kills him with a Lightning Bolt that also tears through the surrounding soldiers.
- Amid the battle's aftermath, Barny raises Kargaz as a zombie, makes him invisible, and orders him to hide until the Bonebreakers leave before travelling to the Barrow.

## Affiliations

- [[factions/military-and-orders/monsters-of-westmarsh/hobgoblins-of-westmarsh|Hobgoblins of Westmarsh]]
- Secretly controlled by Barny after his reanimation

## Current status

Killed, then reanimated as a zombie under Barny's control. He was last left invisible on the battlefield with orders to hide and later travel to the Barrow. Whether he completed that journey: Needs review.
