## Overview

Kargaz the Disciplined was the broken-crowned hobgoblin king who commanded the renewed siege of [[locations/world/agria/west-fort|Westfort]]. [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/wistor|Wistor]] killed him with Lightning Bolt during the battle that broke the siege, but [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] secretly raised him as a zombie and turned him invisible.

## Appearance

Kargaz wore a broken crown while commanding the siege army.

## Role in the campaign

- He commands approximately ninety hobgoblins and three ogres during the final relief attack on Westfort.
- Frank badly wounds him with a long-range shot, and Barny's attempt to charm him fails.
- Wistor kills him with a Lightning Bolt that also tears through the surrounding soldiers.
- Amid the battle's aftermath, Barny raises Kargaz as a zombie and makes him invisible.
- After the battle, Barny questions the reanimated Kargaz. Kargaz says the Grey Syndicate supported his war and promised him control of Westfort if he captured it, but says he knew little of the organisation's larger plan.

## Affiliations

- [[factions/military-and-orders/monsters-of-westmarsh/hobgoblins-of-westmarsh|Hobgoblins of Westmarsh]]
- Secretly controlled by Barny after his reanimation
