## Overview

The Grey Syndicate is the main covert organisation behind the Westfort sabotage arc and the later royal conspiracy around [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]. It remains dangerous after the Spider's Market decoy raid, even with [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]], identified as Countess Lirina and the Grey Khan, imprisoned in Ikiria.

## Key figures

- [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]: Orc handler and negotiator tied to the Syndicate. Identified as Countess Lirina and the Grey Khan through interrogation at the Mercy.
- [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/jeremy|Jeremy]]: Scar-lipped courier and paymaster captured after meeting Vex.
- [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|The Grey Khan]]: The organisation's leader, identified by the party as Ash/Lirina after ESP links her identities.

## Appearance

- Jeremy is a scar-lipped human.
- Ash's detailed appearance beyond being an orc woman needs review.

## Personality

- Ash is confident, transactional, and dangerous.
- Jeremy is more valuable as an information source than as a leader.

## Role in the campaign

- The Syndicate coordinates sabotage, kidnappings, mercenary networks, and political manipulation.
- It links local frontier crimes to the succession threat against Agatha.

## Campaign events

- The party exposes Vex's role in [[sessions/session-035|Session 35]].
- In [[sessions/session-036|Session 36]], Jeremy is captured and Ash is identified.
- In [[sessions/session-039|Session 39]] and [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]], the Syndicate remains tied to the wider siege and rescue effort.
- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] to [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], the Syndicate becomes openly linked to Arthur and the struggle over his secretary.
- In [[sessions/session-068|Session 68 - Ashes to Ashes]], at the Mercy, Selvar's ESP identifies Lirina as Ash. Her thoughts distinguish Lirina from the Grey Khan, but she affirms that Ash is the Khan; the Bonebreakers conclude that they have captured the Syndicate's leader.
- At the start of [[sessions/session-069|Session 69 - A Cruel Bargain]], the Bonebreakers reject Lirina's alliance because they cannot trust her and firmly side with Agatha.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales]], the Bonebreakers jail Lirina in Ikiria, warn of a likely breakout, and forbid her from speaking with Arthur. Morel identifies Thorngate Abbey as a lead for another copy of the poetry cipher and its missing key.

## Current status

Lirina/Ash is imprisoned in Ikiria, with guards warned of a likely breakout and ordered never to allow contact with Arthur. Smith assures the party of the prison's security. The Bonebreakers have rejected her alliance against Valedictus and firmly sided with Agatha, but have not yet delivered their findings. They are pursuing the poetry cipher towards [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]]. Lirina says her plans can continue without her; the wider network remains active.
