## Overview

The Grey Syndicate is the main covert organisation behind the Westfort sabotage arc and the later royal conspiracy around [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]. It remains dangerous after the Spider Market decoy raid, with [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]] still at large.

## Key figures

- [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]]: Orc handler and negotiator tied to the Syndicate. Later claims that she herself is the Grey Khan.
- [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/jeremy|Jeremy]]: Scar-lipped courier and paymaster captured after meeting Vex.
- [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|The Grey Khan]]: Supposed leader of the organisation. Ash's claim to hold that title remains unconfirmed.

## Appearance

- Jeremy is a scar-lipped human.
- Ash's detailed appearance beyond being an orc woman needs review.

## Personality

- Ash is confident, transactional, and dangerous.
- Jeremy is more valuable as an information source than as a leader.

## Role in the campaign

- The Syndicate coordinates sabotage, kidnappings, mercenary networks, and political manipulation.
- It links local frontier crimes to the succession threat against Agatha.

- [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]]

- networks operating through [[locations/world/westmarsh/spiders-market|the Spider Market]]

## Relationships

- Jeremy works under Ash.
- Arthur seeks the Grey Khan's support.
- Vex acts as a local embedded agent.

- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]] to [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], the Syndicate becomes openly linked to Arthur and the struggle over his secretary.
