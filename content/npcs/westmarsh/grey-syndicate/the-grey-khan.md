## Overview

The Grey Khan is the leader of the [[factions/criminal-and-underworld/grey-syndicate|Grey Syndicate]], whose support [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] sought in his plot against Agatha. The Bonebreakers identify the Khan with [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]] and [[npcs/vellure/lirina|Countess Lirina]] through their captive's answers and Selvar's ESP. Lirina keeps these identities distinct in her mind, explaining why a direct question about being the Khan produces an apparent denial. She is imprisoned in Ikiria, but says her plans will continue without her.

## Appearance

Ash appears to the party as an orc woman; Lirina is tall, slender, and beautiful. How she concealed her identity as Ash: **Needs review**.

## Personality

Ruthless, transactional, and ambitious. Lirina presents herself as a force for Agria's stability and justifies the invasions as a way to replace Westfort's defenders with her own army. She is responsible for killing a Bonebreaker, kidnapping children, arson, and widespread suffering. Her hatred of Valedictus is real.

## Role in the campaign

- The Grey Khan is the figure Prince Arthur believes he is travelling to meet for guidance on seizing the throne.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]], Arthur's confession identifies the Grey Khan as a major conspiratorial contact.
- In [[sessions/session-046|Session 46]], Ash claims that she is the Grey Khan, a claim the party cannot yet confirm.
- In [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]], a captured Syndicate recruiter cannot confirm who the Grey Khan is.
- In [[sessions/session-067|Session 67 - Will the Real Countess Please Stand Up?]], Michaelis asks the captive Lirina whether she is the Grey Khan. Her thoughts show that she knows who the Khan is but is not the Khan. One of her encoded messages is meant for the Khan, warning that the Bonebreakers are active in the Northlands and expected at her masquerade.
- In [[sessions/session-068|Session 68 - Ashes to Ashes]], at the Mercy, Selvar's ESP identifies Lirina as Ash. Her thoughts distinguish Lirina from the Grey Khan, but she affirms that Ash is the Khan; the Bonebreakers conclude that they have captured the Syndicate's leader.
- In [[sessions/session-069|Session 69 - A Cruel Bargain]], the Bonebreakers reject Lirina's alliance because they cannot trust her and firmly side with Agatha. Later, ninja orcs demand Lirina in exchange for a farming couple's captive daughter while the Bonebreakers travel towards Ikiria. The girl is killed, most attackers die, and two escape. Lirina remains captive; the ambushers' allegiance is **Needs review**.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales]], the Bonebreakers jail Lirina in Ikiria, warn the guards of a likely breakout, and prohibit contact with Arthur. They leave to pursue the poetry cipher towards Thorngate Abbey.

## Current status

Imprisoned as Lirina in Ikiria, with guards warned to expect a breakout attempt and ordered never to allow her to speak with Arthur. Commander Smith assures the Bonebreakers that the prison is the safest available place to hold her. Her plans may continue without her personal direction, leaving the Syndicate an active concern.

The Bonebreakers have rejected her alliance against Valedictus and firmly sided with Agatha. They are seeking another copy of the poetry cipher at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]]; her other letters remain undeciphered.
