## Overview

Lady Miranda is an unmarried noblewoman and a distant cousin of the royal family, attending [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]]'s masquerade at the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] with her own guards. [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] flirts with her successfully while posing as a Ramen noble. A letter [[characters/active-players/marta/characters/nila|Nila]] steals from her urges her to meet [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]] before he gives his evidence.

## Relationship with the Bonebreakers

Miranda knows Frank only in his assumed role. Nila learns of her royal blood while serving at the masquerade and picks her pocket. When the ballroom is plunged into darkness and Lirina cries that assassins are present, Miranda is frightened and tells Frank she expects the evening to have serious political consequences. He escorts her back to her guards. She is the only guest who chooses to stay overnight at the palace afterwards.

## The letter

The letter Nila takes from her urges Miranda to meet [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]], Arthur's secretary, before he gives his evidence. Its writer appears to be a third party who claims to be a friend of Arthur and to seek only the truth. Who the writer is, what Miranda makes of the letter, and whether she knows it is missing are unknown.

## Current status

Last known to have chosen to stay overnight at the Gilt Palace, unaware of the capture and impersonation at that point. Her whereabouts and knowledge after the Watch intervenes and the Bonebreakers escape are unknown.
