## Overview

Lirina Kartashan is Countess of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria|Velluria]], mistress of the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] in Velur, and the woman behind [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/ash|Ash]] and the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/the-grey-khan|Grey Khan]]. Tall, slender, and beautiful, she has a graceful, poised manner; [[npcs/kingsport/hugh-malory|Hugh Malory]] describes her as exceptionally charming and charismatic. She runs a spy network and pursues the ducal seat, treating [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Arthur]] as a useful tool. She claims that her violent campaigns serve Agria's stability.

The Bonebreakers know her as an enemy responsible for killing one of their companions, kidnapping children, burning an inn with the party inside, and orchestrating invasions that caused enormous suffering. She invokes Agria's stability to justify her violence while pursuing greater political power. Now their prisoner at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]], she offers cooperation against [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]], whom she hates. The party is divided over whether her usefulness against the lich can justify an alliance with her.

## Relationship with Arthur

Arthur repeatedly visited Velluria, with his journeys increasing shortly before the Bonebreakers first intercepted him. The Bonebreakers find love letters addressed to Lirina in his bedroom alongside her warning: “Do nothing rash. Commit nothing to paper.” Hugh suspects her involvement in Arthur's predicament; rumours that she abandoned him after his arrest are unconfirmed. She intends at least to become Duchess during his absence; whether she had any part in his fall is not established.

Lirina says Arthur adored her and had an intensely hostile relationship with Agatha. She admits treating him as a useful tool while insisting that his mistakes were his own. This does not establish how much she directed his individual decisions.

## Household

[[npcs/velur/opal|Opal]] attends Lirina during the masquerade at the Gilt Palace. She later recognises Rose's impersonation of the countess and alerts Dame Silka Voss during the Watch's search.

## Covert activities

Her study in the Gilt Palace holds encoded letters, which her thoughts identify as belonging to her spy network, and the poetry book used as their cipher. Her bedroom wardrobe has a false back concealing dark clothing for covert work, and a secret exit leads to an alley behind the palace. A locked closet contains daggers, poisons, and a disguise kit, all apparently sized for her. The evidence strongly suggests that she is an assassin herself.

Her network has gathered intelligence on the Bonebreakers' activities in the Northlands. One encoded message, meant for the Grey Khan, warns that they are expected at her masquerade. Charm Person and Sleep have both failed against her; ESP has not.

At the Mercy, Selvar's ESP identifies Lirina as Ash. Her thoughts distinguish Lirina from the Grey Khan, but she affirms that Ash is the Khan; the Bonebreakers conclude that they have captured the Syndicate's leader. How she concealed her identity as Ash: **Needs review**.

Her thoughts support her claim to answer to no superior. She says her plans will continue without her personal direction. She justifies the goblin invasion by saying Westfort's defenders were weak and her own army would have garrisoned it better.

## Valedictus and the feared plague

Lirina regards Valedictus as an arch-enemy she wants destroyed. Selvar's ESP confirms the sincerity of her hatred. Barny's [[items/party-inventory/nightmare-revealing-telescope|Nightmare-Revealing Telescope]] reveals her nightmare of a plague devastating Agria, suggesting a real desire to avert it. The dream does not establish its cause or predict an actual outbreak. She presents her opposition to the Necropolis's evil as grounds for an alliance while defending her own invasions. The Bonebreakers must weigh her potential help against the murder, kidnappings, arson, and suffering she has already caused.

## Current status

Captive at the Mercy after the Bonebreakers escape Velur with her. She offers them all treasure they find in the Necropolis in return for help destroying its evil and stabilising Agria, and says she could offer more as Duchess. No alliance or contract has been agreed.

She knows the party has been in contact with Agatha, but Micaelis refuses to disclose the princess's whereabouts. She says a contract involving cooperation with Agatha could be made, without committing to one.

## Open questions

- Whether the Bonebreakers will accept her alliance offer, and on what terms.
- The contents of the encoded letters and the extent of her spy network.
- Which plans and forces will continue operating while she is captive.
- Her precise role in Arthur's decisions and the continuing threat to Agatha.
- The cause and likelihood of the kingdom-wide plague she fears.
- How she concealed her identity as Ash, and why she wants back the dagger found in her armoury: **Needs review**.
- The Watch's response to her removal from Velur.
