## Overview

Opal is [[npcs/velur/lirina|Countess Lirina]]'s attendant, a small, slight woman associated with the [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] in Velur. She recognises [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] as an impostor when the Bonebreakers are concealing their capture of the countess. Her warning to [[npcs/velur/dame-silka-voss|Dame Silka Voss]] helps expose the deception and leads to the party's attempted arrest.

## Encounters with the Bonebreakers

The Bonebreakers see Opal attending Lirina during the [[sessions/session-066|masquerade at the Gilt Palace]], before learning her name during the Watch's intervention.

Opal accompanies Voss and eight guards into Lirina's private quarters. She whispers to the captain while [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]] reads her thoughts with ESP and discovers that she knows Rose is an impostor. Rose claims Opal attacked her the previous night, but Voss does not believe her.

When Voss tests Rose by asking the attendant's name, Selvar supplies “Opal” from their thoughts. Rose tries to maintain the disguise by claiming that Opal changed her name to deceive her. The bluff fails, and Voss orders the Bonebreakers' arrest.

Selvar puts Opal and the four crossbowmen to sleep when the party resists. Rose takes Opal's dagger and key before the Bonebreakers lock their prisoners in a room and escape with Lirina.
