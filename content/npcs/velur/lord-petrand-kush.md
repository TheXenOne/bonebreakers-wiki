## Overview

Lord Petrand Kush is the Master of Revels at [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]]'s [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]] in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur|Velur]]. [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]], disguised as the countess, charms him into accepting her as Lirina, helping the party conceal its capture of the real countess.

## Relationship with the Bonebreakers

After the masquerade breaks up in an assassination scare, Rose, dressed in Lirina's clothes, beckons Petrand upstairs from the top of the staircase. In the countess's private quarters she casts Charm Person on him through Steve's sword, and he becomes convinced that she is Lirina. He asks her for an update and accepts her assurance that everything is fine. He reports that most guests have decided not to stay overnight and that only [[npcs/velur/lady-miranda|Lady Miranda]] remains. The Bonebreakers are invited to stay at the palace as guests.

## Current status

Charmed, and treating Rose as the countess. He does not know that the real Lirina is the party's prisoner.
