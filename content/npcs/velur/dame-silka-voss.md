## Overview

Dame Silka Voss is Captain of the Vellurian Watch, an armoured officer who commands the guards sent to search [[npcs/velur/lirina|Countess Lirina]]'s [[locations/world/agria/northlands/velluria/velur/gilt-palace|Gilt Palace]]. She attempts to arrest the Bonebreakers after [[npcs/velur/opal|Opal]], Lirina's attendant, identifies [[characters/active-players/laura/characters/rose|Rose]] as an impostor. The party overpowers her and escapes with the countess, leaving its conflict with the Watch unsettled.

## Encounter with the Bonebreakers

Voss enters the private quarters with eight guards and Lirina's attendant, Opal. She initially conceals her suspicion, questions Rose's claim to be the countess, and then orders the party's arrest. [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] blinds her with Light, but she remains conscious and blows a whistle loud enough to summon reinforcements throughout the palace. Barny takes the whistle and uses it elsewhere to redirect the guards.

## Current status

Last left alive, restrained, and locked in a palace room with the other prisoners after the Bonebreakers resist arrest. Whether she has since been released, and how the Watch responds to the escape, are unknown.
