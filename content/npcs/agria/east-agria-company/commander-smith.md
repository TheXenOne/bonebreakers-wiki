## Overview

Commander Smith is head of security for the East Agria Company and one of [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]]'s main covert agents.

## Appearance

He often works in disguise. Further physical detail: Needs review.

## Personality

Efficient, discreet, and easily irritated by public loose ends.

## Role in the campaign

- Smith is the party's most direct contact with Agatha's covert operations.
- He helps launch the Mercy investigation and remains politically relevant during Arthur's exposure.
