## Overview

Princess Agatha is heir to the throne of [[locations/world/agria/index|Agria]], Duchess of Southlands, Mayor of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/index|Ikiria]], and head of the East Agria Company. The Bonebreakers rescue her from [[locations/world/fos-imeras/index|Fos Imeras]], after which she becomes their secret patron and assigns them covert missions.

## Appearance

Graceful. Further description: Needs review.

## Personality

Pragmatic, politically dangerous, and willing to reward useful service.

## Role in the campaign

- She covertly hires the party after the [[locations/world/fos-imeras/index|Fos Imeras]] expedition.
- She is the rescued Agrian princess from Fos Imeras.
- She secretly gives the Bonebreakers missions through her agents.
- She grants the Bonebreakers formal claim to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]] after the cathedral arc.
- Prince Arthur's conspiracy is partly aimed at removing her from the succession.
