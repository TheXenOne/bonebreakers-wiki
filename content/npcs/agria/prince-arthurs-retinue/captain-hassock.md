## Overview

Captain Brenn Hassock is [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur's]] captain and a senior armed member of his travelling retinue. Though loyal to Arthur, he ultimately obeys [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/commander-smith|Commander Smith]] and arrests the prince in [[sessions/session-047|Session 47]] when it becomes clear Arthur is in deep political trouble.
