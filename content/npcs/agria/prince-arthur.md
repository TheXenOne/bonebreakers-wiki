## Overview

Prince Arthur is son of [[npcs/agria/queen-audrey-ii|Queen Audrey II]] and Duke of [[locations/world/agria/northlands|Northlands]]. Later sources confirm that he has been plotting with the [[npcs/westmarsh/grey-syndicate/index|Grey Syndicate]] to have [[npcs/agria/princess-agatha|Princess Agatha]] removed so that he can become heir.
