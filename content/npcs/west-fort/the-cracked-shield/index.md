## Overview

This page groups the named civilians most closely tied to [[locations/world/agria/west-fort/cracked-shield|the Cracked Shield]] and its fire. Sarla and Lianna survive the attack.

## Key figures

- [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/osric-tam|Osric Tam]]: Owner of the Cracked Shield and a dependable local ally.
- [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/sarla|Sarla]]: Serving girl coerced by Vex into arson, then nearly murdered to keep her quiet.
- [[npcs/west-fort/the-cracked-shield/lianna|Lianna]]: Serving girl rescued from the fire.

## Appearance

- Sarla is described as flirtatious and watchful before the fire.
- Other physical details need review.

## Personality

- Osric is stubborn but loyal to the party after the inn is saved.
- Sarla is frightened and cornered rather than malicious.

## Role in the campaign

- The inn fire becomes the turning point that exposes Vex's embedded sabotage network.
