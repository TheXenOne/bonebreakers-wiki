## Overview

Morlan is Draxas Tarantella's former assistant, discovered trapped within the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|Jeweller's Sanctum]].

## Appearance

The session summaries describe him as a mad, red-haired wizard in opulent garb.

## Personality

Greedy, evasive, and immediately hostile when first freed.

## Role in the campaign

- Morlan explains the note found in the cursed-ring casket.
- He briefly joins the party out of self-interest before dying during the sanctum retreat.

## Affiliations

- [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/draxas-tarantella|Draxas Tarantella]]

## Associated locations

- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|The Jeweller's Sanctum]]

## Relationships

- Former assistant to Draxas Tarantella.
- Connected to the cursed rings and the spellbook _Morlan's Marvelous Magic_.

## Campaign events

- His name first appears in [[sessions/session-008|Session 8]] through the recovered spellbook.
- In [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]], the party finds the note warning him off the casket.
- In [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]], Arden discovers Morlan trapped in the mirror-cage complex.
- In [[sessions/session-012|Session 12]], Morlan is freed, briefly cooperates, and is then killed in a rat ambush.
