## Overview

This page groups the named Tarantella family figures tied to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/index|Tarantella Manor]] and the [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jewellers-sanctum|Jeweller's Sanctum]].

## Key figures

- [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum Tarantella (Jr.)]]: The last heir of the Tarantella line, humble and eager to restore the family name.
- [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/draxas-tarantella|Draxas Tarantella]]: Long-dead jeweller-magician whose traps and experiments define the sanctum.
- [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/morlan|Morlan]]: Draxas's former assistant, found alive deep inside the sanctum before dying during the retreat.
- [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/rat-queen|Rat Queen]]: Later menace tied to the manor arc and its aftermath.

## Appearance

- Jessum's detailed appearance needs review.
- Draxas is known mainly through his legacy and the dungeon he left behind.

## Personality

- Jessum is hopeful and cooperative.
- Draxas comes across as brilliant, secretive, and dangerously self-indulgent.

## Role in the campaign

- Jessum gives the party sanctioned access to the sanctum.
- Draxas functions as the absent architect of the entire Tarantella arc.
