## Overview

This page groups the recurring named proprietors and civic figures most strongly associated with [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/index|Barrow Spa]].

## Key figures

- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/calley-otoole|Calley O'Toole]]: Owner of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/red-herring|the Red Herring]].
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/agar|Agar]] and [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/esme|Esme]]: Co-owners of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/steamy-smithy|the Steamy Smithy]].
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/amadeus-thud|Amadeus Thud]]: Owner of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/minimarket|the Minimarket]], often accompanied by [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/spat|Spat]].
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/ruby-sunbeam|Ruby Sunbeam]]: Operator of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/bean-counter|the Bean Counter]] and one of the town's most consistently useful civilian contacts.
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/reginald|Reginal, Guildmaster]]: Self-important guildmaster of the Tourism Merchants.
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/aldrich|Aldrich]]: Scatter-brained operator of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/last-chance|the Last Chance]].
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/judas|Judas the Cleric]]: Cleric who runs [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/second-chance|the Second Chance]].
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/gus|Gus]]: Gruff owner of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow-spa/pimp-my-ride|Pimp My Ride]].
- [[npcs/barrowshire/barrow-spa-locals/silk-merchants|Silk Merchants]]: Wealthy merchants freed from captivity in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/barrow|the Barrow]] who help reward the party and point them toward trustworthy local establishments.

## Appearance

- Ruby's Feywild-styled cafe is the most visually distinctive element here.
- Most individual physical descriptions beyond that need review.

## Personality

- Calley, Ruby, and Amadeus are steady and practical local fixtures.
- Reginald is pompous.
- Aldrich is absent-minded.
- Gus is blunt and difficult to haggle with.

## Role in the campaign

- Together, these locals make Barrow Spa the party's first stable hub for lodging, shopping, money, resurrection, and rumours.

## Relationships

- Ruby, Judas, Aldrich, and Amadeus repeatedly intersect with the party's survival, money, and curse problems.
- [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/jessum-tarantella|Jessum Tarantella]] works in the same town but is covered on [[npcs/barrowshire/tarantella-manor/index|his own page]].
- The Silk Merchants briefly act as grateful civilian beneficiaries of the party's early Barrow victories.

## Campaign events

- The campaign opens around these businesses in [[sessions/session-001|Session 1]].
- Aldrich and Judas become especially relevant during the cursed rings and resurrection episodes around [[sessions/session-010|Session 10]] and [[sessions/session-011|Session 11]].
- Ruby later advises the party on imperial coinage in [[sessions/session-044|Session 44]].
- In [[sessions/session-056|Session 56]], Aldrich identifies new magical equipment while Gus accepts commissions for gryphon harnesses and a Bonebreaker-decorated wagon.
