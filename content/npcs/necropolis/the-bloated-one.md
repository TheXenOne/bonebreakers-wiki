## Overview

The Bloated One was the swollen undead authority or inspection figure behind the restoration works in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]] described the zombie labourers' purpose as "the restoration of the Bloated One's domain," and the [[items/bloated-one-work-ledger|work ledger]] shows the Bloated One inspecting that work annually for more than 2,000 years.

The Bonebreakers killed the Bloated One in [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], but its death released a necrotic explosion that infected [[characters/active-players/john/characters/micaelis|Micaelis]] and [[characters/active-players/jaykob/characters/steve-ligma-smith|Steve]] with a Constitution-draining disease.

## Appearance

Horse-sized, necrotic, grotesquely swollen with gas, wrapped in deep robes, and marked by tiny beady eyes in its bloated head.

## Role in the campaign

- The Bloated One appears to be the authority, object, or owner behind Arch V's endless restoration work.
- Its domain is important enough to be marked on the [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]].
- Mortimer directs the party toward it through Section 14, Junction 7, and Section 22.
- The work ledger tracks annual inspections by the Bloated One and a recent warm-air anomaly behind the school.
- The party concludes that the anomaly was probably the Bloated One itself, venting gas near the maintenance corridor.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-051|Session 51]], Mortimer names the Bloated One while explaining the purpose of the undead labourers' work. The party later finds a hidden map that marks the Bloated One's domain.
- In [[sessions/session-052|Session 52]], Cyder speaks with the Bloated One while pretending to come from Section 22. The Bloated One says Section 22 was demolished and reused for Section 12 because the restoration works have no fresh source of stone and keep reusing other parts of the city.
- The party finds a maintenance corridor behind the school that emerges behind the Bloated One, prepares an ambush, and kills it before it can properly respond.
- The Bloated One explodes on death, hitting Micaelis, Steve, Luthien, and Selvar. Micaelis and Steve are worst affected by a rotting disease.

## Current status

Dead. Its domain remains dangerous: eleven zombies arrive immediately after its death, and Micaelis and Steve need the rotting disease cured before it turns them into zombies.
