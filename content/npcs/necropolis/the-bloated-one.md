## Overview

The Bloated One is a named figure or power associated with [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The Bonebreakers have not yet met it, but [[npcs/necropolis/mortimer|Mortimer]] says the zombie labourers' purpose is "the restoration of the Bloated One's domain," and a hidden map marks the route toward that domain.

Unknown. Needs review.

## Role in the campaign

- The Bloated One appears to be the authority, object, or owner behind Arch V's endless restoration work.
- Its domain is important enough to be marked on the [[items/party-inventory/arch-v-necropolis-map|Arch V map]].
- Mortimer directs the party toward it through Section 14, Junction 7, and Section 22.
