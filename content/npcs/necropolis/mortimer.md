## Overview

Mortimer is a zombie foreman encountered in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. He supervises undead labourers who endlessly demolish and rebuild parts of the buried street, apparently as part of the restoration of [[npcs/necropolis/the-bloated-one|the Bloated One]]'s domain.

## Appearance

Zombie wearing an imperial sash.

## Personality

Rigid, bureaucratic, and still responsive to official-sounding procedure.

## Role in the campaign

- Mortimer gives the party the first named contact inside Arch V's restoration works.
- [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] convinces him that the Bonebreakers are inspectors from a health-and-safety department.
- He says he is 2,000 years old and that the restoration work is to be completed never.
- He directs the party toward Section 14, Junction 7, Section 22, and the Bloated One's domain.
