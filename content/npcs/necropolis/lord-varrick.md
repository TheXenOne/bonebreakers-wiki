## Overview

Lord Varrick is an undead noble interred in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/house-varrick-tomb|House Varrick Tomb]]. He challenges the Bonebreakers when they enter his family's burial chamber but agrees to exchange information rather than fight. He is distressed to learn that the once-powerful [[factions/necropolis/house-varrick|House Varrick]] has been forgotten, yet gives the party its clearest warning so far about [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|High Priest Valedictus]].

## Known history

Lord Varrick belonged to a powerful house during the ancient empire. The tomb carvings show him petitioning Valedictus three times and being rejected each time. Varrick remembers considerable opposition to Valedictus within the Imperial City, but says the high priest ignored it while pursuing the spread of his dark arts and dominion across the continent.

## Relationship with the Bonebreakers

Varrick is wary but not immediately hostile. After the Bonebreakers answer his questions about Westmarsh's plague and the fate of his house, he allows them to ask questions in return. He suggests that the Grey Syndicate may want Valedictus's necromantic power or may be trying to destroy him, warns that Agria's undead problem will worsen while Valedictus remains in power, and advises the party to find [[items/the-stylus|the Stylus]]. He also reveals that Valedictus has three phylacteries.

## Current status

Undead and sealed once more inside his black-marble sarcophagus. He allowed the party to leave peacefully.
