## Overview

Valedictus is an ancient imperial priest and necromancer connected to the deepest mysteries of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The Bonebreakers first know him as the author of an iron-bound treatise on souls, binding, compulsion, and necrotic arts. [[npcs/necropolis/captain-varro|Captain Varro]] later identifies him as the high priest in the temple district beyond [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]], saying that Valedictus “sees all.” Varro considers his name so unlucky that he refuses to speak it and writes it instead.

## Known history

- In [[sessions/session-041|Session 41]], the party finds Valedictus's treatise chained to a plinth in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-vii|Arch VII]].
- [[characters/active-players/callum/characters/barnobuck-the-bizarre|Barny]] reads the treatise and gains increased intelligence from it.
- In [[sessions/session-059|Session 59]], Varro describes priests meddling with necromancy in events that appear connected to the empire's downfall.
- Varro says the priests did something evil in the vault. When the party asks who the priest involved was, he considers the answer too unlucky to speak and instead writes “Valedictus,” implying a connection without explaining precisely what Valedictus did.
- Varro identifies Valedictus as the high priest in Arch III's temple district and says that he “sees all.”

## Current status

Varro places Valedictus in the temple district beyond Arch III, but the Bonebreakers have not entered that district. They do not know whether Valedictus is living, undead, or something else.
