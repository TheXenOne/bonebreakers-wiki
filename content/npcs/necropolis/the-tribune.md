## Overview

The Tribune is an unknown figure, title, or authority with power over at least two parts of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]]. The [[items/bloated-one-work-ledger|Bloated One work ledger]] in [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-v|Arch V]] says not to report a warm-air anomaly to the Tribune. A later document found in the Arch I command quarter says a secret passage was sealed on the Tribune's orders.

The Bonebreakers do not yet know whether the Tribune is a person, office, undead authority, record-keeping role, or something else.
