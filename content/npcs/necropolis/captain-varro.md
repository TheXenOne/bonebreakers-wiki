## Overview

Captain Varro is an ancient imperial officer who remains as a wight within a command quarter reached through the [[locations/world/westmarsh/imperial-hill-temple|Imperial Hill Temple]]. Unlike most of the Necropolis's undead, he speaks calmly with the Bonebreakers and quietly helps them explore. He is tired of his continued existence and appears to want the party to end it, though he remains bound by old duties and refuses to grant access openly.

## Role in the Necropolis

Varro waits in the command quarter's mess hall and still treats visitors according to imperial military expectations. He asks the Bonebreakers about their homeland and allows them to inspect an “unguarded” [[items/imperial-command-quarter-map|map]] after Barny answers his questions.

When the Bonebreakers ask Varro for a tour, he guides them through the nearby armoury. Although he says he cannot give them its contents, he deliberately leaves it unlocked.

## What he knows

- The empire and its emperor are long dead.
- The surrounding rooms form part of [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis|the Necropolis]].
- Priests meddled with necromancy in events that appear connected to the empire's downfall.
- Varro says the priests did something evil in the vault while meddling with necromancy. When the party asks who the priest involved was, he refuses to speak the answer, considering the name unlucky, and instead writes “[[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]].” This implies a connection without explaining it.
- [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/imperial-family-tomb|the family tomb]] lies in a separate district from Varro's command quarter and holds twelve powerful members of a prominent imperial family.
- [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-viii|the Eighth Wing]] was sealed after something went wrong when the empire ended.
- [[npcs/necropolis/valedictus|Valedictus]] is the high priest in the temple district reached through [[locations/world/westmarsh/necropolis/arch-iii|Arch III]] and “sees all.”

## Relationship with the Bonebreakers

Varro is guarded but cooperative. He gives information indirectly, leaves useful sites unguarded or unlocked, and admits that he is very tired after [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]] promises that the Bonebreakers will lay him to rest.

## Current status

Undead and still within the command quarter. The Bonebreakers have promised to lay him to rest, but have not yet done so.
