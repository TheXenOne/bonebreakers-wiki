## Overview

Fen Blackwater is the landlady of [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrow/anchor-and-chain|the Anchor & Chain]], the only inn on Harrow's docks. She becomes the Bonebreakers' first local contact when she agrees to spread word that they are adventurers seeking work.

## Appearance

Fen is a stocky woman missing two fingers.

## Personality

Unfazed by her rough customers and willing to connect newcomers with Harrow's opportunities.

## Role in the campaign

- Fen gives the party a way to make its presence known in Harrow.
- She indicates that there are many possible outlets for contraband in the town.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], Arden publicly introduces the Bonebreakers at the Anchor & Chain. Fen agrees to notify people that they are looking for work and answers Barny's question about selling contraband.

## Current status

Running the Anchor & Chain on Harrow's docks.
