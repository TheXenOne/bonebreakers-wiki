## Overview

Rickart is a local official in the County of Harrowmere who intercepts incoming ships near [[locations/world/agria/northlands/harrowmere/harrow|Harrow]]. The Bonebreakers know him as an apparently corrupt gatekeeper who demands an enormous payment from *Agatha's Favour* before allowing the ship to enter.

## Personality

Blatantly opportunistic in his dealings with Captain Winston and the Bonebreakers.

## Role in the campaign

- Rickart controls or claims authority over entry payments for Harrow.
- His demand gives the party its first indication of Harrowmere's corruption and the cost of operating there.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], Rickart initially demands 10,000 gold pieces to admit *Agatha's Favour*. [[npcs/agria/east-agria-company/captain-winston|Captain Winston]] negotiates the payment down to 7,500 gold pieces and pays it.
