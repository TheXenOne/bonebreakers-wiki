## Overview

Corvin Dace is a neatly dressed, extremely polite man who claims to work for the Royal Chamberlain documenting evidence at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]]. The Bonebreakers distrust him after [[npcs/kingsport/elspeth-crane|Mistress Elspeth Crane]] contradicts his account of his visits. He is now under arrest for a jewellery theft the party committed and falsely attributed to him.

## Evidence and arrest

Corvin claims this is his first inspection of Arthur's palace. Tess has already pocketed the hidden letter about “the con” when he arrives in the writing room. She hands over other correspondence, which he takes despite seeming disappointed with it. Elspeth says he has visited several times. His lies about his palace visits, suspicious activity there, and flight after returning to the Royal Chamberlain's building lead the Bonebreakers to suspect that he is not who he claims to be. His true identity and purpose remain unconfirmed.

Steve follows him to the Royal Chamberlain's building. Corvin checks for followers and then bolts, but Steve catches and binds him. Micaelis plants some of Arthur's stolen jewels on him. Elspeth, still charmed by Steve, supports the party's accusation, and [[npcs/kingsport/sir-aldric-hale|Sir Aldric Hale]] arrests Corvin despite his protests that he has been framed.

## Current status

In the Queen's Guard's custody on fabricated theft evidence. The Bonebreakers suspect his claimed identity is false, but have not established who he really is.
