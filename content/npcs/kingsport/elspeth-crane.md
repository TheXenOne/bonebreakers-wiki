## Overview

Mistress Elspeth Crane is the middle-aged mistress of the house at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]]. Protective of the sealed household and sceptical of visitors without written authority, she challenges the Bonebreakers' claim to act for Wendell. Steve uses his sword to cast Charm Person on her and exploits her distrust of [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] to secure her support for a fabricated theft accusation.

## Relationship with the Bonebreakers

Elspeth warns the party not to disturb official seals and later catches Selvar lifting one. Even under Steve's charm, she remains suspicious of conflicting accounts and asks for a letter of authority. She reveals that Corvin has visited repeatedly, contradicting his claim of a first inspection.

When Steve accuses Corvin of stealing Arthur's jewels, she insists on discretion and asks him to track Corvin. The party returns with Corvin and planted jewels. Believing this proves the accusation, she summons the Queen's Guard and supports Steve's story.

## Current status

Still charmed when she backs the Bonebreakers' account and secures Corvin's arrest. She does not know that the party stole the jewels and planted them on him.
