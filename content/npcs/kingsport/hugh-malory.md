## Overview

Hugh Malory is a butler at [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport/arthurs-palace|Arthur's Palace]] in Kingsport. Anxious and prone to wringing his hands, he values the comfort and security of his employment and the position of [[npcs/agria/prince-arthur|Prince Arthur]] more than the prince personally. He gives the Bonebreakers their lead to [[npcs/velur/lirina|Lirina]], Countess of Veluria, after accepting their claim to have been sent by [[npcs/agria/prince-arthurs-retinue/wendell|Wendell Prye]].

## Relationship with the Bonebreakers

Ulrich catches Hugh off guard by asking about a missing gallery portrait. Hugh says it was removed for the investigation, but initially withholds its subject's identity. The claim that Wendell sent the party reassures him: he has heard rumours that the secretary turned against Arthur. Selvar secretly uses ESP to learn how strongly Hugh fears losing his job.

After checking the corridor and locking the gallery door, Hugh identifies the portrait as Lirina and confides his suspicion that she is connected to Arthur's predicament. He describes her as exceptionally charming and tells the party about her masquerade at her palace in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/veluria/velur|Velur]]. His suspicion does not establish her involvement.

## Household gossip

The party later overhears Hugh and [[npcs/kingsport/nessa|Nessa]] comparing what they told the visitors. Hugh repeats rumours that Lirina abandoned Arthur after his arrest and that the Queen intends to close the palace permanently. Neither claim is confirmed.
