## Overview

Sir Aldric Hale is Captain of the Queen's Guard in [[locations/world/agria/northlands/kingsport|Kingsport]]. He registers the Bonebreakers on arrival and directs them to Brother Sedge. He treats them as helpful visitors after accepting Elspeth's account of Corvin's supposed theft, unaware that the party fabricated the evidence.

## Dealings with the Bonebreakers

At the harbour guardhouse, Hale registers Ulrich as a pilgrim and permits Drakford to remain armed as his bodyguard. Ulrich's mace is confiscated under the visitor weapons rules.

Summoned to Arthur's Palace by Elspeth, Hale arrests [[npcs/kingsport/corvin-dace|Corvin Dace]] after planted jewels and her testimony appear to support Steve's accusation. The guards escort the party to the dockside guardhouse to collect its weapons, grant permission to carry them until departure the following morning, and then pay for an inn stay.

## Current status

Commands the Queen's Guard and has taken Corvin into custody. His guards regard the Bonebreakers' intervention as a service to the city.
