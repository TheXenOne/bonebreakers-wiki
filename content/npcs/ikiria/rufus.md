## Overview

Rufus is a traumatised Ikirian guard found in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] during the plague and undead crisis.

## Appearance

Guard. Further description: Needs review.

## Personality

Shell-shocked and burdened by guilt.

## Role in the campaign

- Rufus gives the party one of their clearest human glimpses of the psychological damage spreading through Ikiria's guard ranks.
- Through [[characters/active-players/alessandro/characters/selvar|Selvar]]'s ESP, the party learns that Rufus is traumatised after accompanying an inquisitor while people were burned alive.
