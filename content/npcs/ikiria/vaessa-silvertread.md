## Overview

Vaessa Silvertread is the elven captain of the civil guard in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/index|Ikiria]]. She is visibly overstrained by the city's plague, unrest, missing bodies, and failing civic infrastructure, but she remains willing to act when the Bonebreakers bring witnesses and evidence from the Plats.

## Appearance

Elven. Further description: Needs review.

## Personality

Professional and practical.

## Role in the campaign

- She authorises the party's investigation into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/tower-of-arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus's tower]] during the sentient weapon crisis.
- In the plague crisis, she sends the party into [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]] to investigate missing bodies and the growing burial anomaly.
- She accepts testimony against the [[factions/other/civilian-inquisition|civilian inquisitors]], promises to deal with them, and agrees to raise the quarantine problem with the [[factions/guilds-and-companies/east-agria-company|East Agria Company]].
