## Overview

Gilly is Frank's paid informant in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria|Ikiria]], first contacted through [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/madame-gorvettis|Madame Gorvetti's]]. Her information helps connect the Bonebreakers to unrest around Princess Agatha, and Frank later funds her attempt to enter upper-city society on his behalf.

- She gathers information for [[characters/active-players/deri/characters/francis-spencer-gray|Frank]].
