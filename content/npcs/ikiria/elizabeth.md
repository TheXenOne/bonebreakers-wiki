## Overview

Elizabeth is an Ikirian woman whose sister was cured by [[characters/active-players/ben/characters/ulrich|Ulrich]] during the plague in the Plats. Grateful to the Bonebreakers, she accepts [[characters/active-players/ben-l/characters/arden-sable|Arden]]'s offer to serve Belenus and becomes the resident acolyte of the rebuilt [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats/temple-of-merciful-life|Temple of Merciful Life]].
