## Overview

Maren Sollow is a young gnomish cleric working among the dead in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]]. During the plague crisis, she keeps careful records of bodies sent to the pits and is the first named figure to tell the party that the numbers no longer add up.

## Personality

Diligent, observant, and calm under pressure.

## Role in the campaign

- Maren becomes the party's first direct source inside the burial crisis in [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/plats|the Plats]].
- She argues that Ikiria's plague may have at least two causes: an initial poisoning and later disease spread through overcrowding.
- She witnesses the first undead outbreak at the corpse pit seen directly by the party.
