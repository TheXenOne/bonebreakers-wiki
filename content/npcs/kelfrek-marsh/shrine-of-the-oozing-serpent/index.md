## Overview

This page groups the main named monster and wilderness figures tied to [[locations/world/agria/southlands/barrowshire/kelfrek-marsh/index|Kelfrek Marsh]] and the Shrine of the Oozing Serpent arc.

## Key figures

- [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/sootmurk|Sootmurk]]: Waning Grease Dragon at the shrine. Slain by the party.
- [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/captain-gulian|Captain Gulian]]: Gloop captain protecting Sootmurk. Killed during the escape.
- [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/gorfthistle|Gorfthistle]]: Treasure-hungry gnome adventurer who betrays the party and kills Neem before dying.
- [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/butch|Butch]]: Gorfthistle's automaton dog.
- [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/shrine-of-the-oozing-serpent/dreg|Dreg]]: Hired thief who dies during the party's retreat from the shrine while helping the expedition survive.
- [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/gruumsh-one-tusk|Gruumsh One-Tusk]]: Orc warlord of the marsh, later defeated and spared.
- [[characters/active-players/callum/retainers-followers/krazak-the-silent|Krazak the Silent]]: Gruumsh's lieutenant, later killed and reanimated as a party thrall in the character pages.
- [[npcs/kelfrek-marsh/orcs-of-the-marsh|The Orcs of Kelfrek Marsh]]: Gruumsh's disciplined warband.
- **The Gloops**: Marsh-dwelling followers tied to the shrine and road attacks.

## Appearance

- Sootmurk is a grease dragon.
- Gruumsh is an orc warlord.
- Gorfthistle travels with the automaton dog Butch.

## Personality

- Gorfthistle is deceitful and treasure-mad.
- Gruumsh is power-focused and militarily serious.
- Captain Gulian is loyal to strength rather than principle.

## Role in the campaign

- These figures drive the early Kelfrek Marsh arc and later the hostage rescue mission from the Mercy.

## Relationships

- Captain Gulian serves Sootmurk's local power.
- Gruumsh commands the marsh orcs and later loses Krazak the Silent, who becomes a party thrall in the character pages.
- Gorfthistle and Butch enter the shrine arc as false allies.
- Dreg and Neem are both part of the dangerous, unstable hired support orbiting the shrine expedition.

## Campaign events

- The marsh trouble begins around [[sessions/session-004|Session 4]].
- Sootmurk, Gulian, Gorfthistle, and the Gloops dominate [[sessions/session-005|Session 5]] through [[sessions/session-007|Session 7]].
- Gruumsh becomes central in [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]] and [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]].

- Gruumsh One-Tusk: alive after defeat and humiliation.
- Krazak the Silent: dead, then reanimated as a retainer-thrall in the character pages.
