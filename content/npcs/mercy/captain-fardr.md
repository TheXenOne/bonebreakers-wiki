## Overview

Captain Fardr is the once-great captain of the Crimson Knights, later broken by the cathedral's corruption and then repurposed into the Bonebreakers' new order at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]].

## Appearance

A bald, strapping man in plate, seen during the cathedral arc in a ruined mental state.

## Personality

Once martial and capable, later damaged but still useful once freed from the cathedral's control.

## Role in the campaign

- Fardr is the most important surviving named officer from the Mercy arc.
- He becomes a bridge between the old cathedral order and the party's claimed stronghold.

## Affiliations

- Formerly the Church of the Crimson Flame
- Later the reclaimed Mercy

## Associated locations

- [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|The Mercy]]

## Relationships

- Once served the [[npcs/mercy/church-of-the-crimson-flame|Crimson Bishop's regime]].
- Later works under the party's post-cathedral arrangement.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-021|Session 21]], Selvar charms Fardr and the party escapes with him.
- In [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], he is among the freed survivors when the cathedral is reclaimed.
- In [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]] and [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]], he helps guard rescued priests and support the next marsh operation.
- He is still relevant to Mercy affairs in later sessions including [[sessions/session-040|Session 40]] and [[sessions/session-045|Session 45]].
