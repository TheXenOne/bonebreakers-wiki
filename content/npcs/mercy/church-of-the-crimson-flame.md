## Overview

This page groups the named figures most closely tied to the fall and reclamation of [[locations/world/agria/southlands/mercy|the Mercy]]. The faction is broken after the Bonebreakers destroy the Crimson Bishop, Sergeant Grimlock falls, and Captain Fardr survives in service to the reclaimed cathedral.

## Key figures

- [[npcs/mercy/crimson-bishop|Crimson Bishop]]: Undead abomination who replaced the previous bishop and guarded the plague's core secrets. Slain in [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]].
- [[npcs/mercy/captain-fardr|Captain Fardr]]: Once-great captain of the Crimson Knights. Charmed, freed, and later repurposed into the Bonebreakers' post-cathedral order.
- [[npcs/mercy/sergeant-grimlock|Sergeant Grimlock]]: Ruthless drill sergeant to the initiates. Slain by the party.

## Appearance

- Fardr is described as a bald, strapping man in plate, reduced to a broken state during the cathedral arc.
- The Bishop and Grimlock's full appearances beyond their roles need review.

## Personality

- Fardr is damaged but useful once removed from the cathedral's control.
- Grimlock is presented as brutal and fanatical.
- The Crimson Bishop is manipulative, secretive, and monstrous.

## Role in the campaign

- This leadership cadre embodies the corruption of the Mercy.
- Fardr later becomes part of the Bonebreakers' attempt to reorganise the reclaimed cathedral.

## Relationships

- Fardr originally serves the Bishop.
- After the cathedral falls, Fardr is aligned with the Bonebreakers' new order there.

## Campaign events

- Commander Smith's investigation begins the Mercy arc in [[sessions/session-019|Session 19]].
- In [[sessions/session-021|Session 21]], Captain Fardr reveals key information under charm.
- In [[sessions/session-022|Session 22]], the Crimson Bishop is destroyed and the cathedral reclaimed.
- Fardr later helps oversee rescued priests and later missions from the Mercy in [[sessions/session-024|Session 24]] and [[sessions/session-025|Session 25]].

## Current status

- Sergeant Grimlock: dead.
- Captain Fardr: alive and serving the reclaimed cathedral.
