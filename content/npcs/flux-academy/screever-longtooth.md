## Overview

Screever Longtooth is a ratfolk scholar at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]] who specialises in magical disasters and is a long-time friend of [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]]. Exhausted by the sentient weapon crisis, she still gives the Bonebreakers the key explanation they need and directs them toward Arkeus's tower.

## Appearance

Ratfolk scholar. Further description: Needs review.

## Personality

Helpful, knowledgeable, and alarmed by the scale of the crisis.

## Role in the campaign

- Screever gives the party the clearest early explanation of the sentient weapon catastrophe, warning that the awakened weapons are draining their users and may soon kill or cripple people.
- She directs the investigation toward Arkeus's tower and the missing original component behind the enchantment.
