## Overview

Screever Longtooth is a ratfolk scholar at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]] who specialises in magical disasters and is a long-time friend of [[npcs/ikiria/arkeus-whizkeyrd|Arkeus Whizkeyrd]].
