## Overview

Illitharien Morel is the mild-mannered elven master of the Historium at [[locations/world/agria/southlands/ikiria/flux-academy|Flux Academy]]. Helpful and unassuming, he specialises in history and gnomish antiquity. The Bonebreakers turn to him to study encoded texts, identify ancient finds, and trace unfamiliar literature. His research explains Fos Imeras's gnomish origins and provides the lead to [[locations/world/agria/northlands/grainholm/thorngate-abbey|Thorngate Abbey]] in their effort to decipher Lirina's spy correspondence.

## Campaign events

- In [[sessions/session-018|Session 18]], the party gives him the encoded transcription from the temple and the encoded gnomish book obtained from Jessum.
- In [[sessions/session-063|Session 63]], he sorts the party's coin collection into old and very old Imperial currency and much more valuable pre-Imperial coins. The Flux Academy buys the Imperial coins at 110 percent of face value and the pre-Imperial coins at twice their normal gold value.
- In [[sessions/session-070|Session 70 - Dead Wyverns Tell No Tales]], he reports that Fos Imeras was an ancient gnomish shrine. He has decoded a previously delivered book describing an island headquarters with a laboratory. He also confirms that Lirina's poetry cipher was published in Agria, praises its writing despite not recognising its half-orc author, and traces it to monastic copying. He identifies Thorngate Abbey, giving the party a lead for another copy and the missing key.
