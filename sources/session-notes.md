# Session Notes

## Pre-Session History

**Campaign takes place in *Barrow Spa*, Capital of *Barrowshire***

* The story begins in *Barrow Spa*, an unusual town with hot springs.  
* Home to unique establishments like:  
* *The Steamy Smithy*: A combination spa and smithy run by Agar and Esme.  
* *Amadeus Thud’s Minimarket*: A general store accompanied by Spat, a one-eyed rat.  
* *The Lost Luggage*: A secure goods storage business.  
* *The Bean Counter*: A vegan cafe and cooperative bank.  
* *The Red Herring*: An opulent and upmarket inn.  
* *The Barrow*: An ancient burial site and partial namesake of the town, which has become a hub for undead activity.  
* Recently, skeletons and other undead have begun attacking merchants and kidnapping travelers.  
* Both an angry dwarf and a frightened merchant offered gold to the party in exchange for solving the problem.  
* The party’s first expedition into the Barrow:  
  * They fought a carcass crawler, leaving one ally paralyzed for the rest of the adventure.  
  * Explored cautiously, mapping much of the *Barrow’s* corridors.  
  * Found a central chamber filled with ten chatty skeletons.  
  * The skeletons revealed they had been resurrected by a necromancer named *Quintius* who had promised to restore them to human form.  
  * To avoid hostility, Virien created a distraction with a disembodied voice promising resurrection.  
  * The group pressed further into the dungeon, finding Quintius in its depths, who admits he could not deliver on his promises, angering the skeletons  
  * The group escorted Quintius out of the *Barrow* at the promise of treasure, freeing six imprisoned silk merchants on the way.  
  * They escaped the *Barrow*, with the angry Undead hot on their heels.

## Session 1

**Start in inn – Red Herring – opulent**

* Began in an opulent inn \- ‘The Red Herring’.  
* Virien and Barny left the necromancer in the inn.  
* He handed over his ruby amulet as promised in return for the escort.  
* He told the party of secret chambers and a fairy who had always lived in the *Barrow*.  
* Talked to the freed silk merchants, who, now back at town, rewarded Virien and Barny greatly (several thousand gold in IOUs).  
* Arden, Malakai, and Karsin approach Virien and Barny, offering their services after overhearing rumours of treasure. Arden assures his hatred of Undead.  
* The party goes to see Amadeus, owner of the general *Minimarket*, purchasing modest goods for their next outing.  
* They leave town, heading to the *Barrow*.  
* It is close to town \- a large burial mound housing long-dead heroic warriors.  
* They enter cautiously, and the door rolls closed behind them \- seemingly on its own.  
* Above the antechamber are words carved into the stone:  
  * All who enter are cursed.  
* Virien previously changed this to:  
  * All who enter are \*not\* cursed.  
* It has been changed to:  
  * All who enter are doomed.  
* The party turns left, but are ambushed by skeletons from behind.  
* Arden turns undead, forcing the skeletons to run away, grumbling as they do so.  
* The party continues past a tunnel leading off, finding a large, rough-cut marble slab with a big ‘X’ marked on it.  
* Arden taps loudly on it, attracting the attention of the carcass crawler \- a giant, worm-like creature with many limbs and tentacles.  
* Karsin is paralyzed, and the carcass crawler misses hitting Arden 7 times in a row.  
* Virien and Ike slay the creature, while Barny extracts paralysis venom from its corpse.  
* The party retreats to the entrance, failing to open it with a pole.  
* Karsin is eventually unparalysed, and the party ventures back to the tunnel that the carcass crawler came from.  
* They find a gigantic, sleeping, black widow spider in the middle of a web.  
* They set it on fire with two oil flasks, killing it before it has a chance to wake.  
* They rest, before moving further in.  
* Coming to a crossroad, they head straight, finding a very narrow, natural tunnel that they can escape the *Barrow* through.  
* Ike and Malakai scout ahead, but a zombie sneaks up on the remaining party.  
* Malakai deftly kills it with one decisive crossbow bolt.  
* The party crawls out, escaping with their lives \- but no loot.  
* They return to town.

## Session 2

* The party rests again at the *Red Herring*, enlisting the help of a gruff and sarcastic fighter \- Stan Nice.  
* Karsin and Malakai stay there to rest for several days, while the others visit the silk merchants, looking to cash in the IOUs, who assure them *the Bean Counter* is a trustworthy bank \- Calley O’Toole, owner of the *Red Herring* banks with them after all.  
* The lead merchant takes them to the coop \- a large cafe with a wispy, magical-feeling interior.  
* A large tree sits in the centre, giving the cafe a fey-wild feel.  
* Ruby Sunbeam, owner of the cafe, agrees to withdraw the funds for the party at the behest of the silk merchant \- they distrust not being able to see the gold themselves.  
* Several pixies emerge from the tree with a chest full of gold, before the party asks to redeposit it with the *coop*, drawing rolled eyes from Ruby.  
* With their bank ledger in hand, the party seeks to hire more help.  
* Virien enlists the aid of Grog, a gruff dwarf, while Arden attempts to hire a halfling.  
* Stan is put out, noting that he can handle what dangers lie in the *Barrow*, and the halfling leaves, insulted.  
* The party purchases more wares, including a draft horse, cart, and an alphorn, before leaving town, heading to the *Barrow*.  
* They scout around it this time, noting only the main entrance and the side tunnel they escaped from before.  
* Grog is left at the entrance, instructed to blow the Alphorn if he spots any movement.  
* They enter cautiously, taking the corridor to the right this time.  
* At the first corner, they find a caved-in corridor, with 5 skeletal arms waving from under rubble.  
* One arm holds an intricate blade, and Stan reaches daringly for it.  
* He is struck many times, and dies quickly.  
* The party destroys the arm holding the sword, trying to finesse it away from the danger with a long pole, but this fails.  
* Virien daringly reaches in, taking damage, but retrieving the magical \+1 sword.  
* The party drags the now-dead Stan back to the entrance where Grog, demoralised by the sight, abandons his post.  
* The party positions the cart and horse inside the antechamber, closing the entrance behind them.  
* They push further down the right path, going into the first room on the right.  
* A high-pitched voice shouts for help from a stone cairn in the centre of the room.  
* Arden pulls some rocks free, and Alfwine, a small pixie, pops out.  
* She thanks them for the help, explaining she’s lived here a long time, and wants their help eradicating the Undead that trapped her.  
* The party agrees, and together with Alfwine they continue on, exploring the next room right of the corridor.  
* As the party enters the room \- revealed as a storage room \- several skeletons attack, damaging Arden and killing Ike.  
* Arden turns them, and the party finishes the skeletons off, finding only food in the room.  
* The party briefly mourns their second fallen warrior, before continuing on, searching the next room to the left of the corridor.  
* It contains three tapestries: west \- a golden weave, middle \- a stoic warrior, east \- a monk knelt in prayer.  
* Arden, desperate for loot, tries to yank the golden tapestry free, but discovers it is actually covered in Yellow Mould.  
* The Mould emits clouds of harmful spores, but Arden successfully avoids it, instinctively holding his breath.  
* The party finds a pulley behind the eastern tapestry, which Alfwine pulls, and Virien discovers a secret door behind the middle one, unlocked by the pulley.  
* A small room beyond reveals a white marble idol of a saintly figure with empty eye sockets.  
* A burning white-hot brazier surrounds it in flame, and 3 blue gems sit, sparkling in the brazier.  
* The party surmises that something must go in the eye sockets, but lacking materials, Virient first attempts to flick the gems free of the brazier with first a pole \- burned to a crisp \- then his sword, failing and taking damage.  
* Arden greedily reaches in, grabbing the gems, surviving the flickering flames.  
* The party, down by 2, decides to make their exit, dragging Ike’s body back to the cart.  
* As they roll back the entrance stone, they are surprised by 2 Zombies.  
* In the ensuing battle, Virien is slain, bonked on the head with his own Alphorn, while the Zombies are slain by Barny, Arden, and Alfwine.  
* The remaining party members part from the barrow, leaving the Pixie behind, heading back to town.

## Session 3

* Mourning their dead party members, Arden and Barny read their wills. Ike leaves no will, and Stan wants to remain dead, while Virien wants a second chance at life, stating his wish not to disappoint his parents.  
* Arden and Barny find two establishments offering resurrection \- *The Second Chance*, offering to ‘Raise Dead’ for 1000GP, or *The Last Chance*, offering to ‘Reincarnate’ for 500GP.  
* Knowing Virien’s skinflint nature, the pair enter *The Last Chance*, finding a messy, haphazard shop.  
* Barny negotiates the terms of Virien’s resurrection with the absent-minded owner.  
* In a stroke of luck, Virien is reincarnated back into his own body, with the owner noting he’s never succeeded that well before.  
* The party reunites with Karsin and Malakai, before finding a new party member \- the cleric Ulrich \- and hiring several retainers:  
  * Arden hires Bran ‘The Wolf’, a fierce and wild fighter, giving him a stipend of 2 gold per day, and 60% of one treasure share.  
  * Virien hires Grog again, glossing over his disloyalty, earning respect from the Dwarf, giving him a stipend of 2 gold per day, and 50% of one treasure share.  
  * Barny hires Rhian, a novice magic user, giving her a stipend of 2 gold per day, and 50% of one treasure share.  
  * Malakai hires Dreg, a cunning thief seeking riches, giving him a stipend of 2 gold per day, and 60% of one treasure share.  
* The party goes shopping at the *Minimarket*, buying more equipment, upgrading armour, and preparing for their next outing.  
* They discuss the party name, dubbing themselves ‘**The Bonebreakers**’.  
* Arden puts up a notice in town alerting any adventurers that they will receive 7 gold for aid received at the *Barrow* if the Alphorn is blown.  
* The party returns to the *Barrow*, briefly scouting to assess that the coast is clear.  
* As they enter, Virien chalks over the foreboding curse carved into the antechamber.  
* The party heads to the right again, encountering 3 skeletons.  
* All 3 clerics fail to turn the skeletons, but Bran obliterates one with a mighty swing, and the party dispatches the others.  
* Virien lures 2 more skeletons out, using his Ventriloquism spell, and the party gets the jump on them, destroying them.  
* Virien sets a trap \- a string with a bell on, ensuring any coming behind the party will alert them.  
* Dreg and Malakai sneak ahead, seeing 4 rats in the tapestry room.  
* While the party deliberates how to tackle the issue, Arden throws rations ahead, luring the rats safely in the opposite direction.  
* Pushing ahead, the party encounters a room with empty coffers, finding two glass eyes, realising these are the answer to the idol’s puzzle.  
* Pressing on, they come to another empty set of rooms \- the guard and prison rooms that housed the previously trapped merchants.  
* They rest up, before taking a valuable tobacco box, pushing further into the *Barrow*.  
* They come across Quintius’ decrepit room \- a rotten bed, blood and bones lining the floor.  
* Before them lies the largest chamber of the *Barrow*, about 60 ft deep, with a large arched ceiling.  
* They enter very cautiously, on guard, while Virien confidently strides in, assuring any skeletons that he has found a way to bring them back to life.  
* The one skeleton in the room is convinced, and Virient pushes his luck, asking them to bring him gold.  
* The skeleton acquiesces, before taking instruction from Virien, drinking a vial of Holy Water in the hopes of coming back to life.  
* The skeleton is destroyed, and the party ensures the room is secure, resting before distributing the loot to carry back.  
* The party hauls out: 500GP, 1000SP, 4 crates of silk, 10-20 gemstones, an ivory drinking horn, and 2 casks of wine.  
* The party very slowly hauls their loot back the way they came, finding Sirius \- the draft horse \- and cart intact.  
* They set off back to town, with their loot in tow.

## Session 4

* The Bonebreakers return to town, and are approached by a young messenger requesting their immediate presence at the *Guildhall*.  
* The *Guildhall* is grandiose and lavish in construction.  
* Ulrich enters on behalf of the party.  
* He meets Reginald, the Guildmaster of the Tourism Merchants \- clearly viewing himself as part of ‘upper society’ \- who tells him that tourism in *Barrow Spa* is struggling.  
* Travelers are being attacked on the *Kingsroad* as they pass through the *Kelfrek Marsh*.  
* He asks Ulrich for the Bonebreaker’s aid in solving whatever issue plagues the road, offering 600GP on completion, and 2 draft horses \+ a cart up front.  
* He offers 3 more bits of information: rumors say a creature is taking the travelers, the last travelers went missing yesterday, and the marsh is a day’s travel away.  
* The group deliberates over taking the job, Virien and Arden arguing that 600 GP is meagre pay for something so dangerous, though they ultimately decide to take the offer, hoping for bountiful treasures.  
* The party heads to *Pimp My Ride*, a horse and carriage customisation service \- the only in the Barony.  
* They meet the proprietor, Gus, a middle-aged tattooed man exuding ex-con vibes.  
* Gus is working on a carriage with intricate filigree and luxurious detail as they enter.  
* He is initially gruff, but loosens up once Virien describes the party’s plan to paint their cart black with red letters, though he refuses to negotiate the price.  
* The party accepts, leaving Sirius and the first cart with Gus, promising to pay 875 GP on completion, before prying him for information on the disappearance of travelers nearby.  
* He tells them he has heard of something taking travelers from the road to feed a large creature, but knows no more than that.  
* The party sell off their treasure \- the gems and silks to Ruby at the *Bean Counter*, the tobacco and tapestries to Amadeus at *The Minimarket*.  
* Virien returns the drinking horn to Grog’s friend, who can’t remember promising the reward, but gives it after Grog affirms the deal.  
* After purchasing more supplies at *The Minimarket*, Ulrich purchases a hunting dog \- a lurcher \- naming it Fred.  
* Seeking information, the Bonebreakers find a group of inexperienced adventurers, fresh in town, who tell them that they met a homesteader telling of a large shape slithering through the marsh, leaving trails of black grass that lead into the marsh.  
* The party rests at the Red Herring, before heading out in the morning.  
* They follow the *Kingsroad*, finding large trails of oil slick with soot in it \- clearly containing the remnants of something burnt.  
* They come across a farmer \- likely the aforementioned homesteader \- who says he’s lived there for 10 years.  
* The party pry him for information, learning:  
  * Locals say a ‘Grease Dragon’ has invaded a shrine across the marsh, a day’s travel away.  
  * It has been abducting people taking them back to the shrine.  
  * Toad people have been spotted in the area too by his friend, Ted.  
  * The farmer is worried, but likes his home, and doesn’t want to move.  
* The party pays him 2 SP to look after their horses and cart, and sets off.  
* Ulrich experiments with the grease, separating a small section of it, and setting it alight; it goes up in a blaze instantly \- it is very flammable.  
* The Bonebreakers travel for a day to the east, across the Marsh.  
* As evening settles in, great Sandstone cliffs come into view, and as they get closer, they see two entrances in the base of the cliff.  
* The northern entrance is a large, open archway, where all of the oil trails from across the marsh converge, leading inside.  
* The southern entrance is a set of broken iron gates leading in.  
* The party reasons that the left entrance is the more public one, so they opt for the iron gates, discovering it is partially caved in.  
* They clamber through the rubble, finding a rotting corpse, which Barny pokes with his staff.  
* As he does, two intricate brass automaton hands emerge behind him and Arden, trying to grab them.  
* Thankfully they both miss, and the party dispatches them both, with Arden pocketing them.  
* In the rubble, they also find 3 golden gears, and a palm-sized brass box with a crank, red button, and small holes as if for a speaker.  
* They move on, entering a chapel-like room containing a plinth with a 12ft brass statue.  
* The statue depicts a large being with a serpent-like head, and 6 shackled arms.  
* 3 of the arms hold brass lightning bolts, and a basin lies in front of the statue, filled with now-rancid water.  
* There is a mural on a nearby wall depicting a gnome army fighting a large demon \- clearly the figure depicted by the statue.  
* The party notices thick cobwebs above, and Arden asks Grog to point the lantern up so they can see.  
* Two crab spiders drop, one on each of them, but the party thankfully dispatches them both quickly.  
* The Bonebreakers continue to investigate \- Virien searches for a secret door behind the statue, convinced there is something there, but failing to find anything.  
* Meanwhile, Ulrich discovers a set of spikes along the back of the statue, with one in particular standing out.  
* He pulls it, and a secret door opens in front of Virien, who thinks he opened it.  
* The party retrospectively sees a gnome leader binding the large demon via a big spike on its back on the wall mural.  
* Ulrich discovers an engraving on the basin in gnomish, but none of the party can read it, and Virien discovers that the lightning bolts are removable.  
* Satisfied for the moment, they push on, through the secret door into a corridor.  
* They find a locked door to the left, and with no thief or knock spell, have to leave it for the moment.  
* They head on, turning right at a fork in the corridor, coming to a small room with two rotten sofas.  
* They discover an accordion, a bag of bolts, and a set of iron rations \- all in good condition \- and find another doorway blocking their path \- a smooth sheet of brass with no hinge or crack in it.  
* Virien pulls out the brass box, turning the crank, and a small voice comes from within, shouting “For Mulvis\!”.  
* He presses the red button, and it changes to a ticking “tch” sound.  
* The party backs into a corner, suspicious that it may explode, as Virien cautiously presses it again, revealing it as a recording device.  
* They backtrack, continuing in the previous corridor, finding it comes to an abrupt end.  
* Virien reasons there must be a hidden door, but cannot find it, as Ulrich presses on the wall, revealing the secret passageway.  
* The Bonebreakers enter into a large foundry where 5 anvils surround a forge \- now solid iron.  
* Large bellows and workbenches litter the room, housing scattered tools and brass automaton body parts, and a giant oil slick runs through it all.  
* On the northern wall lies another mural \- gnomish smiths surrounding a forge, with the demon \- Mulvis \- blasting it with lightning.  
* In the centre of the cooled iron is an intricate and ornate sword, clearly of great value.  
* As Ulrich peeks down the hall, Virien inspects the workbenches, finding a gold ingot, 2 spears, a brass bell, and a tuba.  
* Arden, cautious of any danger, pokes the sword with a long pole, before tentatively stepping onto the solid iron pool, and tries to grab the sword, failing to pull it free.  
* Mid-action, 4 toad headed humanoids \- Gloops \- enter the room, their flabby, hunched forms radiating a vicious vibe.  
* They ignore the Bonebreakers, seemingly intent on following the oil trails.  
* Arden breaks them from their reverie, asking them where they’re going.  
* They explain they are here on a pilgrimage from their home in the Kelfrek Marsh, having paid lots of money to see the great serpent who will grant them their desires.  
* They affirm that the Gloops have been feeding the creature travelers.  
* The session closes as there is a pause in the conversation.

## Session 5

* The session picks up as the 4 pilgrims continue past the party to the south.  
* Ulrich gathers some tools, while Arden creates a break in the grease.  
* Virien and Barny test the bellows, discovering they work as expected.  
* The party doubles back, Ulrich beginning work dismantling the locked door, as Virien and Barny retrieve two of the brass lightning bolts from the statue.  
* Ulrich breaks through the door after much clattering, and the party finds a small room with rotten shelves full of automaton parts and moldy robes.  
* At the back of the room is a 6ft automaton version of the demon Mulvis, wielding a shortsword in each of its 6 arms.  
* Arden inspects it carefully, finding a hole in its back the same size and shape as the lightning bolts.  
* As the Bonebreakers rest, Ulrich ponders why it is 6ft tall if the door is 5ft high.  
* They return to the foundry, where they hear a cry for help from the north-east.  
* Investigating, they find a small storeroom housing 4 gloop guards throwing darts at a dead crab spider and a human cleric \- Eric.  
* Arden strikes up a conversation, unconvincingly trying to persuade them that they’ve been ordered to take the prisoner as food for the creature.  
* The gloops are fairly stupid, and give up that the creature is a female called Sutmark, and their commander is called Gullian.  
* Right on cue, Sutmark can be heard rumbling through the walls of the shrine, and the gloops are convinced, marching the cleric into the foundry.  
* The party jump them, killing all but one gloop, who they shackle in the storeroom, plying them for more information.  
* Sutmark eats travellers, but has grown more hungry recently, so the gloops have been taking even more travellers.  
* The party gag the gloop, before Eric introduces himself \- an adventurer seeking treasure on his own.  
* The Bonebreakers welcome him, but before they press on, Arden returns to the foundry, hurling one of the lightning bolts at the pool of iron.  
* The bolt comes alive, turning into a real lightning bolt, liquifying the pool instantly.  
* Arden hastily tries to loop the sword with a grappling hook, but fails.  
* With quick thinking, Virien cools the pool with the bellows, stopping the sword’s descent, and Arden barely manages to catch it this time.  
* Virien takes the new sword, and passes his \+1 sword to Grog.  
* The new sword is ornately decorated, razor sharp, and looks as if carved from a creature of some kind.  
* Just then, a pack of giant soot snails enters, as the party watches on in bewilderment.  
* They pass through without incident, and the party heads south from the store room.  
* They find carved gnomish workbenches, depicting embalming, and smell the distinct aroma of oils used in preparation of the dead.  
* On one workbench lies a gloop, fast asleep \- the party ties it up, just before two crab spiders enter the room.  
* Virien tries to lure them with food, but they drop on the gloop and Ulrich.  
* Before they can act, the gloop is dead, but the party slays them before any others are hurt.  
* Virien approaches a door to the south with poorly written text scrawled on it, “gnome evil ghost keep out”.  
* The group enters a cold, misty set of corridors, and as they reach a crossroad, a pulsing blue light emerges from the south, hovering.  
* The party freezes, and it passes harmlessly, revealed to be a small automaton insect.  
* The Bonebreakers find a loop of iron caskets, all upright, secured to the wall, and with gnomish text on each.  
* They also pass a proverb scrawled on the wall, but no-one speaks gnomish.  
* Ulrich tries every casket, but they are all locked, until they discover one large one, more ornate than the rest.  
* Without a thief to open locks, the party tries to capture the flying bug multiple times, but fails at every turn, almost comically.  
* They abandon the cavern, finding an exit to the west that leads into the main corridor south of the foundry.  
* To the south lie 3 pilgrim gloops, covering themselves in grease as tribute to Sutmark, while to the north lies 4 gloop guards, stopping a dozen pilgrims from passing.  
* The pilgrims are trying to pass, but the guards demand tribute.  
* As the gloops spot them, Arden pretends to be a ghost, causing those to the south to flee.  
* The guards to the north are unconvinced, and Virien lures them with the promise of gold as tribute.  
* As they approach, he causes chaos by saying he’ll pay for all the pilgrims.  
* The pilgrims run amok as the guards are confused.  
* In the chaos, the Bonebreakers retreat, setting the hall aflame, forcing the guards and pilgrims out of the shrine.  
* The party quickly loots a meagre 24 GP and 46 SP from the existing tributes, before entering the large double doors to the south, revealing a massive cavern, at least 50ft in diameter and height.  
* Inside, a ferocious sight greets them \- 11 gloop guards surround Sutmark, the strange, serpentine Grease Dragon, and the gloop captain, Gullian, stands in front of them.  
* Shocked, the session ends as the party is about to hurl a lightning bolt towards Sutmark and the grease surrounding it.

## Session 6

* As the party enters the cavern, Sutmark growls at them calling them fools.  
* Barny looses the lightning bolt, which strikes the Grease Dragon, damaging it and setting it alight.  
* The party acts quickly, cutting the path to them off with flames, slamming the doors in front of them, but not before Sutmark casts sleep.  
* The party all retreat to the hallway, but fall into a slumber for over an hour.  
* The Bonebreakers awake in shackles \- the same room they had found Eric strung up in earlier.  
* All 11 gloop guards are squashed in, watching them, and Captain Goolian starts discussions,  
* Eric tries to convince them the party are emissaries from a more powerful dragon \- Goldmark \- and that we’d been sent as a test of Sutmark’s worthiness.  
* Virien doubles down, saying Goldmark has sent a bar of gold as a gift.  
* He directs Goolian to his pack, where he finds the gold bar, blowing the gigantic Alphorn along the way.  
* Just then, a deep rumbling voice echoes through the dungeon, as if emphasising the point.  
* Arden chips in, saying the party could show even more magic like the lightning bolt earlier if Goolian sets them free \- he could have the power.  
* Goolian sends the devout guards out, and strikes a private deal to set the party free if they take him to Goldmark \- along with his guards of course.  
* The party agrees, and are set free \- albeit without their weapons.  
* They walk to the entrance, accompanied by the guards, where Virien and Arden fake a fight over the mishap in the cavern.  
* Using the distraction, Arden flings his torch at the grease trail, burning several Gloops, with the rest fleeing back inside.  
* Captain Goolian is dead, however two Gloops with the party’s weapons flee.  
* Ulrich slays one, while Barny heroically fights the other.  
* Barny tussles with the Gloop, and Arden catches up, using his only spell to cast Cause Light Wounds, not wanting his friend to perish.  
* The party collects their weapons and regroups before heading back into the shrine via the hidden entrance.  
* They purify the water in the basin in the temple, filling their water flasks, before taking the last lightning bolt.  
* In the small room with the automata, they encounter a somewhat grumpy and treasure hungry gnome, Gorthistle, and his automaton dog, Butch.  
* He questions Virien about the sword the party retrieved, and tells the party of the treasure of King Bonflute, though he doesn’t know where it is.  
* The party agrees to help him, knowing where it is, if he helps them defeat Sutmark.  
* He agrees, and the party enacts their plan.  
* Arden investigates the automata version of Mulvis, activating it briefly.  
* Its arms whirl, and he barely escapes with his life, very luckily managing to deactivate it before it eviscerates him with its 6 blades as it whirls in place.  
* The party head back to the foundry with the Auto-Mulvis, where 2 crab spiders attack, but are luckily dealt with in a bit of a shit show combat.  
* The bonebreakers load Auto-Mulvis onto the dolly in the foundry, wheeling it to the doors into Sutmark’s lair,  
* They burst through, initiating combat and surprising Sutmark and the 5 remaining guards.  
* Bran & Eric push the cart in, hacking into Sutmark to devastating effect.  
* The rest of the party follow, and soon finish the already-wounded dragon off.  
* The remaining gloops, enraged, attack.  
* Barny is unfortunately slain, and the group finishes the rest off.  
* The party finds a host of loot, and well over 9000 gold, of which they can only carry 3200 (plus a bunch of gems).  
* Arden attempts to intimidate the remaining Gloop into never kidnapping travelers again, trying to use the same deception of the golden dragon, Goldmark.  
* The Gloop, convinced, follows the party, leading through the marsh at Virien’s behest, thinking they might lead him to this powerful dragon.  
* Hauling the loot and their fallen member, they head back through the marsh, collecting their wagon from the farmer, and finally arriving back in Barrow Spa,  
* The Bonebreakers, ever cautious (lol), rough it for the night with their loot.  
* They swiftly deposit it in the morning, and head for the Last Chance to resurrect Barny.

## Session 7

* Barny is successfully resurrected, but is now suffering from resurrection sickness.  
* He spends his time while bed bound to study intensely, learning new spells in the process.   
* The party do some shopping, find Dreg, and hire a new thief \- a shady looking man named Neem.   
* They collect Sirius and the newly decorated cart as Eric leaves to rest up.   
* They take the carts and head back to the shrine, where they find Gorthistle waiting.   
* They lead him into the catacombs, where they hear a ghostly singing.  
* He translates, “Those who generously give, Mulvis protects.”
